_"Every line carved upon them became a law unto itself."_

The Runic form exists in perfect harmony with the hidden structure of magic. Intricate glyphs woven throughout their bodies channel power with remarkable efficiency, allowing them to manipulate Essence with exceptional precision. Where others rely upon instinct, the Runic form thrives through understanding.

Some scholars speculate the gods created the Runics not as warriors, but as living demonstrations that even magic obeys immutable laws.

## Attribute Modifiers
- +2 Intelligence and Wisdom
- -2 Strength

## Passive Ability: Runic Matrix

**Runic Matrix** Whenever you complete an Awakening or Essence Development Tier, choose one:
- Increase your maximum **Mana by 1**, or
- Increase your maximum **Stamina by 1**.

## Activated Ability: Runic Stabilization

**Runic Stabilization** — Free Action  
**Frequency:** Once per encounter.

Choose Mana or Stamina. Until the end of your next turn, abilities that use the chosen resource cost **1 less** (minimum 0).

This doesn't scale with Character Rank, but it becomes more valuable naturally as you chain multiple abilities together.
