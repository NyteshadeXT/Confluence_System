_"Before the skies shattered, they were the breath between worlds."_

The Auran form was fashioned from living wind and perfect order. They served as the first Aetherial Wardens, preserving the balance between the planes and guiding the flow of magical currents through the earliest Dungeons. Though they vanished long before the Shattering, their ideals endured within the trials they helped shape.

No true Auran have walked Aetheria for countless ages. Only within the deepest Dungeons can a worthy soul still awaken in their image, carrying forward the forgotten purpose of the first Wardens.

## Passive Ability: Windborne

**Windborne**

Whenever you complete an Awakening or Essence Development Tier, increase either your maximum Mana or Stamina by 1.

Additionally, your movement ignores difficult terrain created by non-magical effects.

## Activated Ability: Gale Step

**Gale Step** — Free Action  
**Frequency:** Once per encounter.

Move up to your Speed. This movement doesn't trigger reactions. You can move across liquids, through occupied spaces, and over gaps during this movement, provided you end on a surface capable of supporting you.
