_"Within every ember waited a fire that could remake the world."_

The Smolder form is born from passion made manifest. Powerful emotions kindle extraordinary reserves of magical energy, allowing moments of breathtaking brilliance that few other forms can match. Yet like any flame, their strength burns brightest when embraced without hesitation.

Dungeon records often portray the Smolder form as a reminder that creation and destruction are born from the same fire.

## Passive Ability: Burning Spirit

**Burning Spirit**

Whenever you complete an Awakening or Essence Development Tier, increase your maximum Mana by 1.

Additionally, whenever you gain a Character Rank, increase your maximum Hit Points by 1.

## Activated Ability: Inner Inferno
**Inner Inferno** — Free Action  
**Frequency:** Once per encounter.

Gain 1 + your Character Rank Mana. This Mana may exceed your normal maximum, but any excess Mana is lost at the end of your next turn.

Until the end of your turn, the first ability you use that costs Mana deals +1 damage.
