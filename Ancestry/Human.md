_"From countless possibilities, they became the measure of all others."_

The Human form embodies adaptability above all else. It possesses no singular mastery, yet no inherent weakness. Those reborn as Human within a Dungeon find themselves capable of shaping their destiny through determination, learning, and perseverance rather than innate gifts.

Many scholars believe this was the first form the gods perfected—a vessel capable of becoming whatever the trials demanded.

## Passive Ability: Adaptable

**Adaptable**  When you complete an awakening or Essence development-tier advancement, choose Mana or Stamina. Increase the chosen maximum by 1.

## Activated Ability: Push Beyond

**Push Beyond** — Free Action  
**Frequency:** Once per encounter

Choose Mana or Stamina. Regain an amount of the chosen resource equal to 1 + your Character Rank.

You can use Push Beyond immediately before paying an ability’s resource cost. This allows you to use an ability even when you would otherwise lack the necessary resource.
