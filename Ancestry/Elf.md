_"They listened to the world's hidden rhythms until they became part of its song."_

The Elven form was created for those whose strength lies in precision rather than force. Every movement is deliberate, every thought clear, and every thread of mana resonates with effortless harmony. Within the Dungeon, Elves excel not because they possess greater power, but because they waste nothing.

Some believe the gods designed the Elven form to teach patience—that mastery comes not from abundance, but from perfect control.

## Attribute Modifiers
- +2 Dexterity and Intelligence
- -2 Strength

## Passive Ability: Arcane Affinity

**Arcane Affinity** Whenever you complete an Awakening or Essence Development Tier, increase your maximum Mana by 1.

## Activated Ability: Elven Focus

**Elven Focus** — Free Action  
**Frequency:** Once per encounter

The next ability you use this turn costs 1 less Mana (minimum 0).

If the ability has no Mana cost, you instead gain a +1 status bonus to any attack roll, saving throw DC, or skill check made as part of that ability.
