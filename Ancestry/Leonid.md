_"Every heartbeat carried the thrill of the hunt."_

The Leonid form was shaped for relentless pursuit. Swift, fearless, and driven by instinct, they thrive when momentum is theirs to command. Their bodies are built for decisive action, rewarding boldness and punishing hesitation.

Among the oldest Dungeon murals, Leonids are always depicted in motion, forever chasing prey that symbolizes the challenges every Seeker must overcome.

## Passive Ability: Hunter's Instinct

**Hunter's Instinct**

Whenever you complete an Awakening or Essence Development Tier, increase your maximum Stamina by 2.

## Activated Ability: Relentless Hunter

**Relentless Hunter** — Free Action  
**Frequency:** Once per encounter.

Immediately gain 1 + your Character Rank Stamina. Until the end of your turn, your movement doesn't provoke reactions.
