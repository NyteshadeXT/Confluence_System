_"Their light was never meant to illuminate the world, only the soul."_

The Celestine form is deeply attuned to the currents of spirit that flow through every living being. Mana gathers within them naturally, and wounds of body and soul alike are slower to diminish their resolve. They are often chosen by those whose trials demand wisdom, compassion, or unwavering conviction.

Ancient inscriptions describe the Celestines as vessels through which divine purpose could be expressed without corruption.

## Attribute Modifiers
- +2 Wisdom and Charisma
- -2 Strength

## Passive Ability: Inner Radiance

**Inner Radiance** Whenever you complete an Awakening or Essence Development Tier, increase your maximum Mana by 1 or Healing Surges by 1.

## Activated Ability: Soul's Grace

**Soul's Grace** — Free Action  
**Frequency:** Once per encounter.

When you would spend Mana to activate an ability, reduce that Mana cost by 1 + your Character Rank (minimum 0).

If the ability's Mana cost is reduced to 0, you gain temporary hit points equal to your Character Rank until the start of your next turn.
