### Myconid Sporekin

**Level 4 Minion Controller**  
Medium natural humanoid (plant) • **XP 1**

**Initiative** +3  
**Senses** Perception +4; low-light vision  
**HP** 1; a missed attack never damages a minion.  
**AC** 18; **Fortitude** 16, **Reflex** 14, **Will** 16  
**Speed** 5

**Traits**

**Fungal Communion**  
The myconid gains a +2 bonus to saving throws while adjacent to another myconid.

**Standard Actions**

**✦ Fungal Slam (standard; at-will)**  
+7 vs. AC; **4 damage.**

**✦ Numbing Spores (standard; encounter) ✦ Poison**  
**Close blast 2**; creatures in blast; +5 vs. Fortitude; **Hit:** The target is slowed until the end of the myconid's next turn.

**Skills** Nature +9  
**Str** 12 (+3) **Dex** 10 (+2) **Wis** 15 (+4)  
**Con** 16 (+5) **Int** 10 (+2) **Cha** 13 (+3)  
**Alignment** Unaligned • **Languages** Common

---

There are a couple design choices in there that I particularly like.

**Numbing Spores should be Encounter, not At-Will.** If Location 12 has three or four of these, repeated blast attacks that slow could become obnoxious very quickly. Each lesser Myconid getting one defensive burst creates the fungal-colony feel without turning combat into condition management.

I also deliberately made the spores **non-damaging**. The Sporekin's job isn't to kill the PCs; it's to hinder them while the more substantial Myconids do the fighting. That's particularly appropriate for the enclave, where combat occurs because they're trying to defend their home rather than hunt the party.

**Fungal Communion** is mostly flavor with a small mechanical effect. I considered giving them an attack bonus while adjacent to another Myconid, but that encourages you to constantly worry about their positioning. A saving-throw bonus is almost invisible until relevant and reinforces their communal nature. With 1 HP, it also won't come up very often—which is fine.

One thing I'd consider changing for **this specific encounter** is the name. Rather than putting "Myconid Minion" on the table, I'd use:

> **Myconid Sporekin**

That makes _Sporekin_ an in-world term for ordinary members of this manifested Myconid community, while mechanically they're just Level 4 minions.

For the five-person enclave we outlined, I could see **2 Myconid Sporekin + 3 existing 4e Myconids** being a good starting composition. That gives you enough mechanical variety to assign the Speaker, Tender, Watcher, Gatherer, and Young One different capabilities without creating five custom stat blocks.
