# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Attack ✦ Core Concept

**AT-WILL MARTIAL WEAPON**

**Eligible Essences:** Eye, Hunt, Oath, Zeal, Momentum  

**Slot:** 1 – Core Concept  
**Tier:** At-Will

You bait your foe into movement that exposes them to punishment.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Stable
**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Range:** Melee weapon
**Target:** One creature

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Strength vs. AC

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 1[W] + Strength modifier damage. Until the start of your next turn, if the target shifts, it provokes an opportunity attack from an ally of your choice.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Marked Motion** Increase the damage to 1[W] + Strength modifier + 2.
>> - **Rank 6 — Punishing Step** If the target shifts and provokes the opportunity attack, it takes additional damage equal to your Wisdom modifier.
>> - **Rank 9 — Locked Engagement** If the target shifts and is hit by the opportunity attack, it is slowed until the end of its next turn.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Damage increases to 2[W] + Strength modifier.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** Damage increases to 3[W] + Strength modifier. If the target shifts, it also takes damage equal to your Strength modifier (whether or not the opportunity attack hits).
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Damage increases to 4[W] + Strength modifier. If the opportunity attack hits, the target is knocked prone.
