# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Attack ✦ Apex Ability

**Daily Martial Weapon**

**Eligible Essences:** Blade, Order, Wolf, Oath, Hunt, Resolute, Zeal, Reckoning

**Slot:** 5 – Apex Ability  
**Tier:** Daily

You single out your foe and make them your problem—pressing them relentlessly with measured violence and unyielding focus.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Provocative. This power always escalates Dungeon attention.
**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Attack Type and Range:** Melee weapon
**Target:** One creature
**Cost:** Cost: 2 Stamina

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Power Rank vs. AC

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 2[W] + your Essence ability modifier damage. Until the end of the encounter, you gain a +2 bonus to attack rolls and a +4 bonus to damage rolls against the target.

> [!Miss|background-color-yellow]
> - Until the end of the encounter, you gain a +1 bonus to attack rolls and a +2 bonus to damage rolls against the target.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 —  Focused Threat** Increase the hit damage to 3[W] + your Essence ability modifier.
>> - **Rank 6 — Unrelenting Pressure** Increase the hit bonuses to +3 to attack rolls and +5 to damage rolls against the target.
>> - **Rank 9 — Crushing Pursuit** While the bonuses from this power are active, you gain a +2 power bonus to AC against the target’s attacks.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Hit damage increases to 4[W] + Essence ability modifier. Hit bonuses become +3 to attack rolls and +6 to damage rolls.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** Hit damage increases to 5[W] + Essence ability modifier. Hit bonuses become +4 to attack rolls and +8 to damage rolls. If the target is bloodied, it takes a –2 penalty to attack rolls against creatures other than you.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Hit damage increases to 6[W] + Essence ability modifier. If you reduce the target to 0 HP, you may immediately shift up to your speed and make a melee basic attack against another creature (once per use).
