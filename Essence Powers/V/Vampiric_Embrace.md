# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Attack ✦ Signature Strike

**Encounter Arcane Implement**

**Eligible Essences:** Blood, Shadow, Zeal, Gathering, Devouring

**Slot:** 3 – Signature Strike  
**Tier:** Encounter

A stream of siphoning darkness feeds your strength from your enemy’s fading vitality.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Predatory
**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Range:** Ranged 5
**Target:** One creature
**Cost:** 1 Mana

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Power Rank vs. Will (Constitution)

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 2d8 + your Constitution modifier necrotic damage, and you gain 5 temporary hit points. If aligned with Blood or Zeal, increase temporary hit points to  5 + your Intelligence modifier.

> [!Miss|background-color-yellow]
> - Half damage. No temporary hit points.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Deepened Drain** Increase the damage to 3d8 + your Constitution modifier.
>> - **Rank 6 — Gluttonous Siphon** Increase the base temporary hit points to 8 (or 8 + Intelligence modifier if using the Infernal Expression).
>> - **Rank 9 — Feeding Frenzy** If the target is bloodied when hit, you gain additional temporary hit points equal to your Constitution modifier.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Damage increases to 4d8 + Constitution modifier. Temporary hit points become 10 (or 10 + Intelligence modifier with Infernal Expression).
>> - **Silver / Epic:** Damage increases to 5d8 + Constitution modifier. If the target is reduced to 0 HP by this power, you regain hit points equal to your Constitution modifier.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Damage increases to 6d8 + Constitution modifier. While you have temporary hit points from this power, you gain a +2 power bonus to attack rolls against the target (if still alive).
