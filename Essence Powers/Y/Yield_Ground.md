# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Utility ✦ Tactical

**Encounter Martial**

**Eligible Essences:** Swift, Cat, Hunt, Wind  

**Slot:** 2 – Utility / Passive  
**Tier:** Encounter

You refuse to remain where danger expects you to be.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Neutral
**Action Type:** Immediate Reaction  
**Range:** Personal
**Trigger:** An enemy damages you with a melee attack.

> [!Effect|background-color-blue]
> You shift a number of squares equal to your Wisdom modifier.  You gain a +2 bonus to all defenses until the end of your next turn.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Fluid Withdrawal** Increase the defense bonus to +3.
>> - **Rank 6 — Tactical Repositioning** After shifting, you may choose one ally within 5 squares; that ally gains a +2 power bonus to AC until the end of your next turn.
>> - **Rank 9 — Elusive Recovery** If you end the shift outside the triggering enemy’s reach, you gain temporary hit points equal to your Wisdom modifier.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** The defense bonus increases to +4.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** The defense bonus increases to +5. You may shift up to Wisdom modifier + 1 squares.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** The defense bonus increases to +6. If the triggering enemy misses you with its next attack before the end of your next turn, it falls prone.
