# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Utility ✦ Tactical Command

**ENCOUNTER MARTIAL**

**Eligible Essences:** Eye, Oath, Momentum, Swift, Balance, Harmony

**Slot:** 2 – Utility / Passive  
**Tier:** Encounter

A gesture and a word reposition the battlefield.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Stable
**Action Type:** Move Action  
**Range:** Ranged 10
**Cost:** 1 Stamina
**Target:** One ally

> [!Effect|background-color-blue]
> The target takes a move action as a free action.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Decisive Signal** The ally gains a +2 power bonus to AC until the end of its movement.
>> - **Rank 6 — Tactical Follow-Through** If the ally ends this movement adjacent to an enemy, it gains a +2 power bonus to its next attack roll before the end of its next turn.
>> - **Rank 9 — Seamless Coordination** After the ally completes the movement, you may shift 1 square as a free action.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** The ally may shift up to 2 squares as part of the granted movement without provoking opportunity attacks.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** If the ally uses the granted movement to engage an enemy, it may make a melee basic attack as a free action (once per use).
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** You may target two allies with this power (resolve each movement separately).
