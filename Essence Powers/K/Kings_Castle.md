# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Attack ✦ Tactical Strike

**Encounter Martial Weapon Mobility**

**Eligible Essences:** Fox, Wolf, Hunt, Oath, Order, Harmony

**Slot:** 4 – Tactical Strike  
**Tier:** Encounter

You slip behind a sturdier ally, striking from cover while switching places in one fluid motion.

**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Range:** Melee or Ranged weapon
**Requirement:** You must be wielding a crossbow, a light blade, or a sling.
**Target:** One creature
**Cost:** 1 Stamina

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Power Rank vs. Reflex (Dexterity)

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 2[W] + your Dexterity modifier damage. After the attack, switch places with one willing adjacent ally.

> [!Miss|background-color-yellow]
> - Half damage (round down). You may still switch places with the ally.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Fluid Exchange** Increase the weapon damage to 3[W] + your Dexterity modifier.
>> - **Rank 6 — Covered Position** After switching places, you and the chosen ally gain a +2 power bonus to AC against the target until the end of your next turn.
>> - **Rank 9 — Perfect Reversal** If the attack hits, the target grants combat advantage to the ally you switched with until the end of your next turn.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Damage increases to 4[W] + Dexterity modifier. The switch does not provoke opportunity attacks from the target.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** Damage increases to 5[W] + Dexterity modifier. After switching, the ally may shift 1 square as a free action.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Damage increases to 6[W] + Dexterity modifier. If both you and the ally are adjacent to the target after the switch, the target is slowed until the end of its next turn.
