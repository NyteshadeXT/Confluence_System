# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`

Attack ✦ Restorative Flame

**DAILY FIRE HEALING**

**Eligible Essences:** Fire, Life, Renewal, Growth, Sun

**Slot:** 5 – Apex Ability
**Tier:** Daily

Living flame engulfs your enemies, feeding upon them until its purpose is fulfilled. As the fire fades, its stolen vitality leaps outward to restore your allies.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Elevated
**Action Type:** Standard Action
**Range:** Area burst 1 within 10 squares
**Cost:** 1 Mana
**Target:** Each enemy in the burst

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Power Rank vs. Reflex

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 1d6 + Wisdom modifier fire damage, and the target takes ongoing 5 fire damage (save ends). If the target drops to 0 Hit Points before saving against the ongoing damage, choose one creature within 5 squares of the target. That creature regains Hit Points equal to 5 + your Constitution modifier.
> 
> When the target saves against the ongoing fire damage, choose one creature within 5 squares of the target. That creature regains Hit Points equal to your Constitution modifier.

> [!Miss|background-color-yellow]
> - Half damage. The target does not take ongoing damage and there is not healing effect.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Vital Flame** When an ally regains Hit Points from Fires of Life, it also gains temporary Hit Points equal to your Constitution modifier.
>> - **Rank 6 — Sustaining Embers** Increase the ongoing fire damage to 7.
>> - **Rank 9 — Life from Ash** When a target drops to 0 Hit Points while suffering the ongoing damage, you may divide the healing of 5 + your Constitution modifier between two creatures within 5 squares of the target.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Increase the initial damage to 2d6 + Wisdom modifier and the ongoing fire damage to 10.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** Whenever Fires of Life causes a creature to regain Hit Points, that creature may also spend a Healing Surge.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Increase the initial damage to 3d6 + Wisdom modifier. When a target affected by the ongoing damage drops to 0 Hit Points, every ally within 2 squares of that target regains Hit Points equal to your Constitution modifier.
