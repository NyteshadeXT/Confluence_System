# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Attack ✦ Apex Ability

**Daily Arcane Fire Implement**

**Eligible Essences:** Fire, Blood, Zeal, Omen, Devouring, Fracture

**Slot:** 5 – Apex Ability  
**Tier:** Daily

Liquid flame engulfs your foe, clinging and burning long after the initial blast fades.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Escalating
**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Range:** Ranged 10
**Target:** One creature
**Cost:** 2 Mana

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Power Rank vs. Reflex (Constitution)

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 3d10 + your Constitution modifier fire damage. The target takes ongoing 5 fire damage (save ends).

> [!Miss|background-color-yellow]
> - Half damage. No ongoing damage.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Intensified Blaze** Increase the initial damage to 4d10 + your Constitution modifier.
>> - **Rank 6 — Clinging Inferno** Increase the ongoing fire damage to ongoing 7 fire damage (save ends).
>> - **Rank 9 — Devouring Flame** The first time the target fails its saving throw against the ongoing fire damage, it takes additional fire damage equal to your Constitution modifier.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Initial damage increases to 5d10 + Constitution modifier. Ongoing damage becomes ongoing 10 fire damage (save ends).
>> - **Silver / Epic:** Initial damage increases to 6d10 + Constitution modifier. If the target fails two saving throws against the ongoing damage, it is weakened until the end of its next turn.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Initial damage increases to 7d10 + Constitution modifier. The ongoing fire damage persists even if the target succeeds on its first saving throw; it must succeed on two saving throws to end the effect.
