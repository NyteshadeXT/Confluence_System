# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Utility ✦ Mobility

**At-Will Martial**

**Eligible Essences:** Swift, Cat, Fox, Wind  

**Slot:** 2 – Utility / Passive  
**Tier:** At-Will

You move with practiced silence, never sacrificing speed for subtlety.

**Action Type:** Move Action  
**Range:** Personal
**Prerequisite:** You must be trained in Stealth.

> [!Effect|background-color-blue]
> You move up to your speed and make a Stealth check as part of this action.  You do not take the normal penalty to Stealth for moving during this check.  You may utilize even when enemies are watching you.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Whispering Step** You gain a +2 power bonus to the Stealth check made as part of this power.
>> - **Rank 6 — Passing Shadow** If you have cover or concealment at any point during this movement, you may remain hidden at the end of the movement even if you end in light cover.
>> - **Rank 9 — Unseen Flow** If you are hidden at the end of this movement, you gain a +2 power bonus to your next attack roll before the end of your turn.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** You may move up to **your speed + 2 squares**.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** You ignore difficult terrain during this movement.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Once per turn, if you begin this movement hidden, you may become invisible until the end of your turn instead of merely hidden.
