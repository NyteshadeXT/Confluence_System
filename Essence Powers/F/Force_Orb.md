# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Attack ✦ Signature Strike

**ENCOUNTER ARCANE FORCE IMPLEMENT**

**Eligible Essences:** Force, Glass, Echo, Magic

**Slot:** 3 – Signature Strike  
**Tier:** Encounter

A compact sphere of force detonates in a violent burst.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Neutral
**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Range:** Ranged 20
**Cost:** 1 Mana
**Primary Target:** One creature or object

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Intelligence vs. Reflex

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 2d8 + Intelligence modifier force damage. Each enemy adjacent to the primary target must also succeed on a Intelligence vs. Reflex or suffer 1d10 + Intelligence modifier force damage.

> [!Miss|background-color-yellow]
> - No mark.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Resonant Detonation** Increase the primary damage to 3d8 + Intelligence modifier.
>> - **Rank 6 — Shattering Pulse** Increase the secondary damage to 1d12 + Intelligence modifier.
>> - **Rank 9 — Concussive Backwash** If at least one secondary target is hit, you may push one secondary target 1 square.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Primary damage becomes 4d8 + Intelligence modifier. Secondary damage becomes 2d8 + Intelligence modifier.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** Primary damage becomes 5d8 + Intelligence modifier. Secondary damage becomes 2d10 + Intelligence modifier. If the primary target is bloodied, secondary targets grant combat advantage until the end of your next turn.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Primary damage becomes 6d8 + Intelligence modifier. Secondary damage becomes 3d8 + Intelligence modifier. If three or more enemies are hit (primary + secondary), the primary target is dazed until the end of your next turn.
