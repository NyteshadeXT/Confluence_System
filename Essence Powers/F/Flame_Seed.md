# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`

Attack ✦ Elemental Growth

**AT-WILL FIRE ZONE**

**Eligible Essences:** Fire, Growth, Plant, Wood

**Slot:** 1 – Core Concept  
**Tier:** At-Will

You form a seed infused with volatile Essence and hurl it toward your foe. On impact, it erupts into a brief garden of supernatural flame.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Stable  
**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Range:** Ranged 10  
**Cost:** None  
**Target:** One creature

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Power Rank vs. Reflex

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 1d6 fire damage. The squares adjacent to the target become a fiery zone until the end of your next turn. An enemy that enters the zone or starts its turn there takes fire damage equal to your Wisdom modifier. A creature can take this damage only once per turn.

> [!Miss|background-color-yellow]
> - Half damage. No ongoing damage.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Lingering Embers** The fiery zone also includes the target's square.
>> - **Rank 6 — Hungry Flame** Zone damage increases to **2 + your Wisdom modifier**.
>> - **Rank 9 — Spreading Flame** When an enemy takes damage from the zone, you may extend the zone into one unoccupied square adjacent to that enemy. The expanded zone lasts for the original duration.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Increase the initial damage to **2d6 fire damage**.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** When you create the fiery zone, choose one creature hit by Flame Seed. That creature gains Vulnerable 5 Fire until the zone ends.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Increase the initial damage to **3d6 fire damage**, and the zone persists until the end of your following turn.
