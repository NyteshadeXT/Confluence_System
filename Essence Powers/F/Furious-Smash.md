# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Attack ✦ Core Concept

**AT-WILL MARTIAL WEAPON**

**Eligible Essences:** Zeal, Oath, Shield, Momentum, Storm

**Slot:** 1 – Core Concept  
**Tier:** At-Will

Your strike carries more fury than force, igniting aggression in your allies.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Stable
**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Range:** Melee weapon
**Target:** One creature

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Strength vs. Fortitude

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - The target takes damage equal to your Strength modifier. Choose one ally adjacent to you or the target. That ally gains a power bonus equal to your Charisma modifier to their next attack roll and damage roll against the target before the end of their next turn.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Rallying Fury** Increase the damage to 1[W] + your Strength modifier.
>> - **Rank 6 — Driving Momentum** If the ally hits the target with the empowered attack, that ally gains temporary hit points equal to your Charisma modifier.
>> - **Rank 9 — Crushing Rebuke** If the ally hits the target with the empowered attack, the target takes a –2 penalty to attack rolls until the end of its next turn.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Damage becomes 2[W] + Strength modifier. The ally’s power bonus to attack and damage increases by +1.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** Damage becomes 3[W] + Strength modifier. If the ally’s empowered attack hits, you may slide the target 1 square.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Damage becomes 4[W] + Strength modifier. If the ally’s empowered attack hits a bloodied target, it deals additional damage equal to your Charisma modifier.
