# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Attack ✦ Apex Ability

**DAILY ARCANE FIRE CONJURATION IMPLEMENT**

**Eligible Essences:** Fire, Light, Momentum, Convergence, Storm  

**Slot:** 5 – Apex Ability  
**Tier:** Daily

A rolling inferno answers your command.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Escalating
**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Range:** Ranged 10
**Cost:** 2 Mana
**Target:** One creature adjacent to the sphere

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Intelligence vs. Reflex  

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - You conjure a Medium flaming sphere in an unoccupied square within range. 2d6 + Intelligence modifier fire damage. Any creature that starts its turn adjacent to the sphere takes 1d4 + Intelligence modifier fire damage. 
> 
> - Move Action: You may move the sphere up to 6 squares.
> 
> - Sustain Minor:  You may sustain the sphere until the end of the encounter. As a standard action, you may make another attack with it.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Intensified Core** Increase the attack damage to 3d6 + your Intelligence modifier.
>> - **Rank 6 — Scorching Aura** Increase the start-of-turn damage to 1d6 + Intelligence modifier.
>> - **Rank 9 — Relentless Orbit** When you move the sphere, it may enter enemies’ squares. The first enemy whose space it enters during that movement takes fire damage equal to your Intelligence modifier.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Attack damage becomes 4d6 + Intelligence modifier. The sphere’s move increases to 8 squares.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** Attack damage becomes 5d6 + Intelligence modifier. Creatures that take start-of-turn damage are slowed until the end of their turn.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Attack damage becomes 6d6 + Intelligence modifier. The sphere occupies a Large space instead of Medium, and its start-of-turn damage applies to creatures within 2 squares.
