# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Attack ✦ Tactical Strike

**Encounter Martial Weapon Reaction**

**Eligible Essences:** Fox, Cat, Swift, Hunt, Eye  

**Slot:** 4 – Tactical Strike  
**Tier:** Encounter

You turn your enemy’s aggression against them, slipping aside and answering their overreach with a sudden counterstrike.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Neutral
**Action Type:** Immediate Reaction  
**Trigger:** An enemy makes a melee attack against you
**Range:** Melee or Ranged weapon
**Cost:** Cost: 1 Stamina

> [!Effect|background-color-blue]
> You shift 1 square, then make a melee or ranged basic attack against the triggering enemy. You gain a power bonus to the attack roll equal to your Wisdom modifier.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Slippery Footing** Increase the shift to 2 squares.
>> - **Rank 6 — Exploit the Opening** If the triggering attack misses you, your counterattack deals additional damage equal to your Wisdom modifier.
>> - **Rank 9 — Turn the Blade** If your counterattack hits, the triggering enemy takes a –2 penalty to attack rolls until the end of its next turn.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** You may shift before or after the basic attack. The power bonus to the attack roll increases by +1.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** If the triggering attack misses you, the counterattack gains combat advantage and deals additional damage equal to your Wisdom modifier (once per use).
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** If the counterattack hits, the triggering enemy is knocked prone or slid 1 square (your choice).
