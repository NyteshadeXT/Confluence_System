# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`

Attack ✦ Elemental Restraint

**ENCOUNTER COLD**

**Eligible Essences:** Ice, Water, Storm, Elemental

**Slot:** 3 – Signature Strike
**Tier:** Encounter

A sudden eruption of supernatural cold engulfs your foe, freezing it in place before it can react.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Stable
**Action Type:** Standard Action
**Range:** Ranged 10
**Cost:** 1 Mana
**Target:** One creature

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Power Rank vs. Fortitude

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 1d6 + Wisdom modifier cold damage, and the target is Immobilized until the end of your next turn.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Deep Freeze** Add your Constitution modifier to the damage.
>> - **Rank 6 — Numbing Frost** While Immobilized by Frost Flash, the target also takes a -1 penalty to attacks.
>> - **Rank 9 — Frozen Solid** The target cannot shift until the end of your following turn, even after the Immobilized condition ends.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Increase the damage to 2d6 + Wisdom modifier + Constitution modifier.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** The target is Immobilized until it succeeds on a saving throw. It makes its first saving throw against this effect at the end of your next turn.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Increase the damage to 3d6 + Wisdom modifier + Constitution modifier. A target that fails its first saving throw also grants Combat Advantage while Immobilized.
