# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Utility ✦ Durability

**Daily Arcane**

**Eligible Essences:** Blood, Zeal, Resolute, Renewal, Bond

**Slot:** 2 – Utility / Passive  
**Tier:** Daily

Otherworldly strength hardens your flesh and fortifies your will.

**Action Type:** Minor Action  
**Range:** Personal
**Cost:** 2 Mana

> [!Effect|background-color-blue]
> You gain temporary hit points equal to 5 + your Constitution modifier.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Hardened Flesh** Increase the temporary hit points to 8 + your Constitution modifier.
>> - **Rank 6 — Infernal Endurance** While you have temporary hit points from this power, you gain a +2 power bonus to Fortitude.
>> - **Rank 9 — Refuse the Abyss** If the temporary hit points are reduced to 0 before the end of the encounter, you regain temporary hit points equal to your Constitution modifier (once per use).
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Temporary hit points become 10 + your Constitution modifier.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** Temporary hit points become 15 + your Constitution modifier.  While you have these temporary hit points, you gain resistance to all damage equal to your Constitution modifier.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Temporary hit points become 20 + your Constitution modifier. The first time you would be reduced to 0 HP while these temporary hit points remain, you instead drop to 1 HP and the temporary hit points end.
