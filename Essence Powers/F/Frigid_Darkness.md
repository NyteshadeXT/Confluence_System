# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`

**Attack** **✦** **Signature Strike**  
ENCOUNTER ARCANE COLD FEAR IMPLEMENT

**Eligible Essences:** Ice, Shadow, Omen, Deep, Revelation, Star, Magic, Suppression

**Slot:** 3 – Signature Strike  
**Tier:** Encounter

You conjure a freezing mantle of starless darkness that clings to your foe, numbing both body and awareness.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Escalating 
**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Range:** Ranged 10  
**Cost:** 1 Mana
**Target:** One creature

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Essence Attack vs. Fortitude (Constitution-based)

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 2d8 + your Essence ability modifier cold damage. The target grants combat advantage to you and your allies until the end of your next turn.

> [!Miss|background-color-yellow]
> - Half damage.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Clinging Void** Increase damage to 3d8 + ability modifier.
>> - **Rank 6 — Numbing Suppression** The target takes a –2 penalty to Reflex until the end of your next turn.
>> - **Rank 9 — Frozen Stillness** If the target is already granting combat advantage, it is also slowed until the end of your next turn.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Damage increases to 4d8 + ability modifier.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** Damage increases to 5d8 + ability modifier. The target grants combat advantage to all allies until the end of your next turn.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Damage increases to 6d8 + ability modifier. If the target is bloodied, it is also immobilized until the end of your next turn.
