# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`

Utility ✦ Predatory Movement

**DAILY MOVEMENT**

**Eligible Essences:** Swift, Pursuit, Momentum, Wind, Cat, Wolf, Fox, Dog

**Slot:** 2 – Utility / Passive  
**Tier:** Daily

Essence surges through your limbs, sharpening every movement into the tireless stride of a pursuing predator.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Elevated  
**Action Type:** Minor Action  
**Range:** Personal  
**Cost:** 1 Stamina  
**Target:** Self

> [!Effect|background-color-blue]
> Until the end of the encounter, you gain a power bonus to **Speed equal to your Dexterity modifier**. The physical expression of this power reflects the Essence through which it is manifested. A Wolf or Cat Essence might alter your stance and gait, Wind may carry you forward on rushing currents, while Momentum drives each step into the next.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Bounding Stride** Once per turn, ignore the movement cost of the first square of Difficult Terrain you enter.
>> - **Rank 6 — Unbroken Pace** When you begin your turn with no enemy adjacent to you, gain an additional +1 power bonus to Speed until the end of that turn.
>> - **Rank 9 — Relentless Chase** Once per round, when an enemy you can see willingly moves farther away from you, you may shift 1 square toward that enemy as a Free Action.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Difficult Terrain does not reduce your Speed while moving toward an enemy you can see. 
>> - **Silver / Epic:** Once per turn, when you move at least half your Speed, you may shift 2 squares at the end of that movement.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Once per round, when an enemy you can see moves, you may immediately move up to half your Speed toward that creature as a Free Action.
