# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Attack ✦ Tactical Strike 

**ENCOUNTER ARCANE FIRE IMPLEMENT**

**Eligible Essences:** Fire, Ruin, Magic, Ash, Force, Ranged

**Slot:** 4 – Tactical Strike  
**Tier:** Encounter

You hurl a blazing bolt of flame that erupts outward, scorching all who stand too close.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Escalating (fire spreads instability)
**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Range:** Ranged 10  
**Cost:** 1 Mana
**Target:** One creature

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Power Rank vs. Reflex

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 3d6 + your Essence ability modifier fire damage. Creatures adjacent to the target take 1d6 + your Essence ability modifier fire damage.

> [!Miss|background-color-yellow]
> - Half damage to the target. No splash damage.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Lingering Flame** Creatures hit by the splash damage take a –1 penalty to Reflex until the start of your next turn.
>> - **Rank 6 — Spreading Inferno** Increase the splash damage dice to 2d6.
>> - **Rank 9 — Burning Pressure** If two or more creatures take splash damage, the primary target takes additional damage equal to your Constitution modifier.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Damage increases to 4d6 + ability modifier. Splash damage increases to 2d6 + ability modifier.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** Damage increases to 5d6 + ability modifier. Creatures hit by splash damage take ongoing fire damage equal to your Intelligence modifier (save ends).
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Damage increases to 6d6 + ability modifier. The area around the target becomes a close burst 1 of flame until the end of your next turn.
