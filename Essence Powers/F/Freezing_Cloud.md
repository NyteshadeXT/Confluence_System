# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Attack ✦ Apex Ability

**DAILY ARCANE COLD IMPLEMENT**

**Eligible Essences:** Ice, Water, Balance, Omen  

**Slot:** 5 – Apex Ability  
**Tier:** Daily

An expanding mist of killing frost blankets the battlefield.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Escalating
**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Range:** Area burst 2 within 10 squares
**Cost:** 2 Mana
**Target:** Each creature in burst

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Intelligence vs. Fortitude

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 1d8 + Intelligence modifier cold damage. The cloud lasts until the end of your next turn. Any creature that enters the area or starts its turn there is subject to another attack. You may dismiss the cloud as a minor action.

> [!Miss|background-color-yellow]
> - Half damage.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Deepening Frost** Increase the initial damage to 2d8 + your Intelligence modifier.
>> - **Rank 6 — Numbing Chill** Creatures hit by this power are slowed until the end of their next turn.
>> - **Rank 9 — Killing Cold** Increase the damage of the secondary attacks (entering or starting turn in the area) to 2d8 + Intelligence modifier.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Initial damage becomes 3d8 + Intelligence modifier. The cloud’s burst increases to burst 3.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** Initial damage becomes 4d8 + Intelligence modifier. Creatures hit by the secondary attack are immobilized until the end of their turn.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Initial damage becomes 5d8 + Intelligence modifier. If a creature fails two consecutive saving throws against effects caused by this power, it is restrained until the end of its next turn.
