# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`

Attack ✦ Radiant Enchantment

**DAILY RADIANT CHARM**

**Eligible Essences:** Light, Sun, Revelation, Magic

**Slot:** 5 – Apex Ability
**Tier:** Daily

A burst of shifting, luminous color settles over your enemies. The supernatural radiance muddles movement and exposes every weakness before flaring brilliantly as its hold finally breaks.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Elevated
**Action Type:** Standard Action
**Range:** Area burst 1 within 10 squares
**Cost:** 1 Mana
**Target:** Each creature in the burst

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Power Rank vs. Will

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - The target is Slowed and grants Combat Advantage (save ends both). 
> 
> When the target saves against this effect, it takes 3d6 + Wisdom modifier radiant damage and grants Combat Advantage until the end of your next turn.

> [!Miss|background-color-yellow]
> - The target takes 1d6 + Wisdom modifier radiant damage and grants Combat Advantage until the end of your next turn.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Lingering Glamour** A target hit by Faerie Fire takes a -1 penalty to its first saving throw against the power.
>> - **Rank 6 — Revealing Light** While affected by Faerie Fire, the target cannot benefit from concealment and cannot become Hidden.
>> - **Rank 9 — Blinding Flare** When the Aftereffect triggers, the target also takes a -2 penalty to attack rolls until the end of its next turn.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Increase the Aftereffect damage to 4d6 + Wisdom modifier and the Miss damage to 2d6 + Wisdom modifier.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** A target affected by the Aftereffect is also Dazed until the end of its next turn.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Increase the burst to Area burst 2 within 10 squares. A creature that fails its first saving throw against Faerie Fire takes radiant damage equal to your Wisdom modifier.
