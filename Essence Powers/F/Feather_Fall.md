# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Utility ✦ Arcane Reaction

**DAILY ARCANE**

**Eligible Essences:** Wind, Light, Balance, Echo  

**Slot:** 2 – Utility / Passive  
**Tier:** Daily

Gravity loosens its hold at your command.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Stable
**Action Type:** Free Action  
**Range:** Ranged 10
**Cost:** 2 Mana
**Trigger:** You or one creature in range falls

> [!Effect|background-color-blue]
> The target takes no damage from the fall and does not fall prone.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Soft Descent** The target may land in any square within 2 squares of the original landing point.
>> - **Rank 6 — Guided Drift** After landing, the target may shift 2 squares as a free action.
>> - **Rank 9 — Suspended Moment** When triggered, you may instead cause the target to hover harmlessly until the end of its next turn before descending safely.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** You may target up to two creatures within range with the same trigger.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** You may target any number of creatures within range that are falling from the same event.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** If a creature protected by this power would fall as the result of being pushed, pulled, or knocked prone, you may negate the forced movement once during the encounter.
