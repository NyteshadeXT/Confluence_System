# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Utility ✦ Utility / Passive  

**PSIONIC • MOVEMENT**

**Eligible Essences:** Air, Dimension, Freedom, Swift, Water, Wind, Wing

**Slot:** 2 – Utility / Passive
**Tier:** At-Will

With focused thought, you lift yourself upon a current of psionic energy and move across hazards as lightly as a drifting feather.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Wind

**Action Type:** Move Action  
**Range:** Personal  
**Cost:** None

> [!Effect|background-color-blue]
> - Move up to 3 squares. During this movement, you ignore difficult terrain and can move across liquid or stand on it as though it were solid ground.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Swift Current** Increase the movement to 4 squares.
>> - **Rank 6 — Airborne Step** You can move across open gaps up to 1 square wide during this movement.
>> - **Rank 9 — Feathered Flight** Increase the movement to 6 squares.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** You may move across open gaps up to 3 squares wide during this movement.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** You may use this power as a free action once per encounter.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** You gain a fly Speed equal to your land Speed until the end of your turn when you use this power.
