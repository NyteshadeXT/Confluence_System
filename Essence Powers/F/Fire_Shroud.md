# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Attack ✦ Signature Strike  

**ENCOUNTER ARCANE FIRE IMPLEMENT**

**Eligible Essences:** Fire, Ash, Smoke, Magic, Gathering, Entropy, Ruin

**Slot:** 3 – Signature Strike  
**Tier:** Encounter

You cloak your enemies in a mantle of flame that clings and burns long after the initial ignition.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Escalating
**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Range:** Close burst 3  
**Cost:** 1 Mana
**Target:** Each enemy in burst

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Power Rank vs. Fortitude (Intelligence-based)

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 1d8 + your Essence ability modifier fire damage, and ongoing 5 fire damage (save ends).

> [!Miss|background-color-yellow]
> - Half damage. No ongoing damage.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Lingering Flames** Increase ongoing damage to 7.
>> - **Rank 6 — Smothering Heat** Creatures taking ongoing damage take a –2 penalty to Reflex.
>> - **Rank 9 — Consuming Fire** If a creature fails its first save against the ongoing damage, it takes additional fire damage equal to your Intelligence modifier.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Damage increases to 2d8 + ability modifier. Ongoing damage increases to 10.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** Damage increases to 3d8 + ability modifier. Creatures taking ongoing damage also grant combat advantage.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Damage increases to 4d8 + ability modifier. If a creature is bloodied, the ongoing damage cannot be reduced.
