# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Attack ✦ Tactical Strike

**Encounter Martial Weapon**

**Eligible Essences:** Wolf, Bear, Blade, Momentum, Might, Claw

**Slot:** 4 – Tactical Strike  
**Tier:** Encounter

Surrounded and bloodied, you lash out in every direction with feral savagery, punishing any foe foolish enough to press too close.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Neutral
**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Attack Type and Range:** Close burst 1
**Requirement:** You must be wielding two melee weapons.
**Target:** Each enemy in the burst you can see
**Cost:** Cost: 1 Stamina

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Power Rank vs. AC

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 1[W] + your Essence ability modifier damage.

> [!Miss|background-color-yellow]
> - Half damage.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Press the Pack** If you hit two or more enemies, you gain a +2 power bonus to AC until the start of your next turn.
>> - **Rank 6 — Raking Blades** If you hit the same enemy with both weapons (GM adjudication based on attack representation), increase that enemy’s damage taken from this power by your Essence ability modifier.
>> - **Rank 9 — Blood Frenzy** If you are bloodied when you use this power, increase weapon damage to 2[W].
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Damage increases to 2[W]. If you hit at least three enemies, you may shift 1 square after resolving the attacks.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** Damage increases to 3[W]. If you are bloodied, enemies hit by this power grant combat advantage until the end of your next turn.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Damage increases to 4[W]. If you reduce a creature to 0 HP with this power, you may immediately make a basic attack against a different enemy in the burst (once per use).
