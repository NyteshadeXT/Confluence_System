# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Attack ✦ Core Concept

**At-Will Martial Weapon Mobility**

**Eligible Essences:** Swift, Cat, Fox, Blade, Ranged  

**Slot:** 1 – Core Concept  
**Tier:** At-Will

You dart forward with perfect timing, striking as you slip into a better position.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Neutral
**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Attack Type and Range:** Melee or Ranged weapon
**Requirement:** You must be wielding a crossbow, a light blade, or a sling.
**Target:** One creature

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Power Rank vs. AC

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 1[W] + your Dexterity modifier damage. You may move up to 2 squares before making this attack.

> [!Miss|background-color-yellow]
> - No damage.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Fluid Footwork** If you move at least 2 squares before the attack, you gain a +1 power bonus to the attack roll.
>> - **Rank 6 — Slip the Gap** After resolving the attack, you may shift 1 square.
>> - **Rank 9 — Uncatchable** If you hit, you do not provoke opportunity attacks from the target until the end of your turn.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Damage increases to 2[W]. You may move up to 3 squares before the attack instead of 2.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** Damage increases to 3[W]. If you move at least 2 squares before the attack, you gain combat advantage for the attack.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Damage increases to 4[W]. Once per turn, if this power reduces a creature to 0 HP, you may immediately move up to your speed.
