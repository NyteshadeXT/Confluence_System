# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Utility ✦ Tactical

**ENCOUNTER DIVINE**

**Eligible Essences:** Oath, Order, Light, Revelation, Harmony

**Slot:** 2 – Utility / Passive
**Tier:** Encounter

You channel divine certainty into an ally’s strike, ensuring their blow does not falter.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Neutral
**Action Type:** Immediate Interrupt
**Range:** Close burst 10
**Cost:** 1 Mana
**Trigger:** An ally within range makes an attack roll against your sworn target
**Target:** The triggering ally

> [!Effect|background-color-blue]
> The target rolls the attack twice and uses either result. If another effect allows the ally to roll twice and choose a result (higher or lower), this power does not apply to that attack. If the attack is rerolled, both dice are rerolled.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Shared Precision:** The ally gains a +1 bonus to the attack roll when resolving the chosen result.
>> - **Rank 6 — Directed Strike:** If both rolls would hit, the attack deals +2 damage.
>> - **Rank 9 — Unerring Aid:** The ally may treat one of the two rolls as a minimum of 10.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** The effect may trigger on any attack against your sworn target, not just allies.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** If both rolls would hit, the attack gains +1[W] or +1d10 damage (matching the attack type).
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Once per round, when used, the ally’s attack is treated as if it rolled a natural 20 on one die (before modifiers), if both rolls would hit.
