# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Attack ✦ Signature Strike

**Encounter Arcane Implement**

**Eligible Essences:** Star, Omen, Shadow, Eye, Domination, Mind

**Slot:** 3 – Signature Strike  
**Tier:** Encounter

You whisper a fragment of cosmic truth, and your foe’s certainty fractures.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Disturbing
**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Range:** Ranged 5
**Target:** One creature
**Cost:** 1 Mana

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Essence Attack vs. Will

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 2d8 + Intelligence modifier acid damage, and ongoing 5 acid damage (save ends).
> 
> - **Secondary Attack:** 2d8 + your Charisma modifier psychic damage, and the target takes a –1 penalty to Will defense until the end of your next turn. If aligned with Star or Omen, the penalty becomes  1 + your Intelligence modifier.

> [!Miss|background-color-yellow]
> - Half damage.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Fractured Insight** Increase the base penalty to **–2 to Will** until the end of your next turn.
>> - **Rank 6 — Lingering Corrosion** Add your Intelligence modifier to the ongoing damage dealt to the primary target (applied once per round).
>> - **Rank 9 — Collapse of Certainty** If the target fails a saving throw against one of your psychic effects before the end of your next turn, it takes psychic damage equal to your Charisma modifier.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Damage increases to 3d8 + Charisma modifier. The base Will penalty becomes –2 (before Star/Omen scaling).
>> - **Silver / Epic:** Damage increases to 4d8 + Charisma modifier. Once per encounter, if the target fails a saving throw while affected by this power’s penalty, it is dazed until the end of its next turn.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Damage increases to 5d8 + Charisma modifier. If the target fails a saving throw against one of your psychic powers while its Will is reduced by this effect, it also takes ongoing psychic damage equal to your Intelligence modifier (save ends).
