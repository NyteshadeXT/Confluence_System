# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Utility ✦ Sacred Intervention

**ENCOUNTER DIVINE**

**Eligible Essences:** Life, Balance, Oath, Light  

**Slot:** 2 – Utility / Passive  
**Tier:** Encounter

A brief plea grants strength to overcome weakness.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Stable
**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Range:** Ranged 5
**Cost:** 1 Mana
**Target:** You or one ally

> [!Effect|background-color-blue]
> The target makes a saving throw with a power bonus equal to your Charisma modifier.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Steadfast Prayer** If the saving throw fails, the target gains temporary hit points equal to your Charisma modifier.
>> - **Rank 6 — Guiding Light** If the saving throw succeeds, the target gains a +2 power bonus to its next attack roll before the end of its next turn.
>> - **Rank 9 — Unwavering Faith** Once per use, if the saving throw fails, the target may reroll the saving throw but must use the second result.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** The saving throw gains a minimum result equal to your Charisma modifier (if the die result is lower, treat it as that value before modifiers).
>> - **Silver / Epic:** If the saving throw succeeds, the target may also end one additional effect that a save can end.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** If the saving throw succeeds against a save-ends condition imposed by an enemy, that enemy takes radiant damage equal to your Charisma modifier.
