# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Attack ✦ Tactical Strike

**ENCOUNTER DIVINE RADIANT IMPLEMENT**

**Eligible Essences:** Light, Life, Oath, Balance  

**Slot:** 4 – Tactical Strike  
**Tier:** Encounter

Radiant brilliance scorches your foes and guides your allies’ blades.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Stable
**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Range:** Close blast 3
**Cost:** 1 Mana
**Target:** Each enemy in blast

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Wisdom vs. Reflex

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 1d8 + Wisdom modifier radiant damage. Allies in the blast gain a +2 power bonus to attack rolls until the end of your next turn.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Lingering Brilliance** Increase the damage to 1d10 + your Wisdom modifier.
>> - **Rank 6 — Guiding Radiance** Allies who gain the +2 power bonus also gain a +2 power bonus to damage rolls on their next attack before the end of your next turn.
>> - **Rank 9 — Cleansing Light** Enemies hit by this power shed bright light and take a –2 penalty to saving throws against radiant effects until the end of your next turn.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Damage increases to 2d8 + Wisdom modifier. The ally attack bonus remains +2.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** Damage increases to 3d8 + Wisdom modifier. The ally attack bonus increases to +3.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Damage increases to 4d8 + Wisdom modifier. If at least two enemies are hit, allies in the blast also gain temporary hit points equal to your Wisdom modifier.
