# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Attack ✦ Core Concept

**AT-WILL DIVINE WEAPON**

**Eligible Essences:** Oath, Shield, Light, Life, Harmony

**Slot:** 1 – Core Concept  
**Tier:** At-Will

A brief prayer hardens faith into protection.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Stable
**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Range:** Melee weapon
**Target:** One creature

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Strength vs. AC

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 1[W] + Strength modifier damage. You and one adjacent ally gain a +1 power bonus to AC until the end of your next turn.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Shared Faith** Increase the AC bonus to +2.
>> - **Rank 6 — Guarding Presence** If the attack hits, you may choose to grant the AC bonus to either one adjacent ally or yourself and one adjacent ally.
>> - **Rank 9 — Bastion of Oath** If you are wielding a shield and hit with this attack, the AC bonus increases by +1 against opportunity attacks until the end of your next turn.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Damage increases to 2[W] + Strength modifier**. The AC bonus remains +2.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** Damage increases to 3[W] + Strength modifier. You and the chosen ally also gain a +1 power bonus to Fortitude until the end of your next turn.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Damage increases to 4[W] + Strength modifier. If the attack hits, you may mark the target until the end of your next turn.
