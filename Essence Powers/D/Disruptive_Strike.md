# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Attack ✦ Tactical Strike

**ENCOUNTER MARTIAL WEAPON**

**Eligible Essences:** Hunt, Eye, Swift, Blade, Ranged, Trap, Insight

**Slot:** 4 – Tactical Strike  
**Tier:** Encounter

You interrupt your foe at the perfect moment, turning a deadly strike into a failed attempt.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Neutral
**Action Type:** Immediate Interrupt  
**Range:** Melee or Ranged weapon  
**Cost:** 1 Stamina
**Trigger:**  You or an ally is attacked by a creature
**Target:** The attacking creature

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Essence Attack vs. AC; Strength (melee); Dexterity (ranged)

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 1[W] + your Essence ability modifier damage. The target takes a penalty to its triggering attack roll equal to 3 + your Wisdom modifier.

> [!Miss|background-color-yellow]
> - The target still takes a –2 penalty to the triggering attack roll.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Sharpened Timing** Increase the penalty to **4 + Wisdom modifier** on a hit.
>> - **Rank 6 — Defensive Instinct** The ally targeted by the triggering attack gains a +1 bonus to AC against that attack.
>> - **Rank 9 — Perfect Interruption** If the triggering attack misses, the attacker takes a –2 penalty to attack rolls until the end of its next turn.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Damage increases to 2[W] + ability modifier.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** Damage increases to 3[W] + ability modifier. If the triggering attack misses, the attacker is off-balance (grants combat advantage).
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Damage increases to 4[W] + ability modifier. If the triggering attack misses, you may shift 2 squares as a free action.
