# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Attack ✦ Signature Strike

**ENCOUNTER MARTIAL WEAPON**

**Eligible Essences:** Blade, Polearm, Steel, Swift, Momentum, Wind

**Slot:** 3 – Signature Strike  
**Tier:** Encounter

Your weapon flows in a fluid arc, turning motion into lethal precision before your foe can react.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Neutral
**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Range:** Melee weapon  
**Cost:** 1 Stamina
**Target:** One creature
**Requirement:** You must be wielding a melee weapon.

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Power Rank vs. AC

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 2[W] + your Essence ability modifier damage. If you are wielding a polearm or a heavy blade, the target is immobilized until the end of your next turn.

> [!Miss|background-color-yellow]
> - Half damage.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Flowing Continuity** If you moved at least 2 squares before the attack, gain a +1 bonus to the attack roll.
>> - **Rank 6 — Binding Arc** If you are wielding a polearm or heavy blade, the target takes a –2 penalty to Reflex while immobilized.
>> - **Rank 9 — Relentless Motion** If the target is already slowed or immobilized, deal additional damage equal to your Dexterity modifier.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Damage increases to 3[W] + ability modifier.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** Damage increases to 4[W] + ability modifier. Immobilized target also grants combat advantage.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Damage increases to 5[W] + ability modifier. If wielding a polearm or heavy blade, immobilization becomes (save ends).
