# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Attack ✦ Signature Strike

**Encounter Arcane Implement**

**Eligible Essences:** Shadow, Blood, Force, Omen, Reckoning

**Slot:** 3 – Signature Strike  
**Tier:** Encounter

A claw of infernal force seizes your foe and drags them where you will.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Coercive
**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Range:** Ranged 10
**Target:** One creature Large or smaller
**Cost:** 1 Mana

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Essence Attack vs. Fortitude

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 2d8 + your Constitution modifier damage, and you slide the target 2 squares. If aligned with Blood or Shadow, increase the slide distance to  2 + your Intelligence modifier.

> [!Miss|background-color-yellow]
> - Half damage. No slide.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Crushing Pull** If the target collides with a creature or solid object during the slide, it takes additional force damage equal to your Constitution modifier.
>> - **Rank 6 — Binding Drag** If the target ends the slide adjacent to you or an ally, it takes a –2 penalty to attack rolls until the end of its next turn.
>> - **Rank 9 — Relentless Grip** If the target is bloodied, increase the base slide distance by 1 square.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Damage increases to 3d8. Slide distance increases by 1 square.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** Damage increases to 4d8. Once per encounter, if the target collides with a creature or object during the slide, it is knocked prone at the end of the movement.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Damage increases to 5d8. If the target fails a saving throw against one of your arcane effects before the end of your next turn, you may slide it 2 squares as a free action.
