# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Attack ✦ Apex Ability

**Daily Arcane Light Implement**

**Eligible Essences:** Star, Light, Omen, Gathering, Suppression

**Slot:** 5 – Apex Ability  
**Tier:** Daily

A burning star coalesces around your foe, hemming them in with lances of relentless radiance.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Dominant
**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Range:** Ranged 10
**Target:** One creature
**Cost:** 2 Mana

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Essence Attack vs. Will

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 3d6 + your Charisma modifier radiant damage, and the target is immobilized until the end of your next turn. The target takes a –2 penalty to Will defense (save ends).

> [!Miss|background-color-yellow]
> - Half damage. No immobilization.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Gravitic Corona** Increase the radiant damage to 3d8 + your Charisma modifier.
>> - **Rank 6 — Relentless Orbit** If the target is immobilized by this power and attempts to teleport or shift before the immobilization ends, it takes radiant damage equal to your Charisma modifier.
>> - **Rank 9 — Crushing Constellation** If the target fails its first saving throw against the –2 Will penalty, it also takes a –2 penalty to attack rolls until the end of its next turn.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Damage increases to 4d6 + Charisma modifier. Immobilization lasts until the end of your next turn (save ends thereafter).
>> - **Silver / Epic:** Damage increases to 5d6 + Charisma modifier. While the target suffers the –2 Will penalty, it also grants combat advantage.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Damage increases to 6d6 + Charisma modifier. If the target fails two saving throws against this power’s effects, it is blinded until the end of its next turn.
