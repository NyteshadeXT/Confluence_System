# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Attack ✦ Core Concept  

**FEAR • PSIONIC • PSYCHIC • WEAPON**

**Eligible Essences:** Discord, Dream, Malign, Mind, Omen, Shimmer

**Slot:** 1 – Core Concept
**Tier:** At-Will

As your weapon strikes home, you plant the image of howling demons in your foe's mind, making it hesitate to exploit an opening.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Mind

**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Range:** Melee weapon  
**Cost:** None

**Target:** One creature

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Power Rank vs. AC

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 1[W] + your Essence ability modifier psychic damage. Until the end of your next turn, the target takes a –2 penalty to opportunity attack rolls.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Screaming Chorus** The penalty to opportunity attack rolls increases to –3.
>> - **Rank 6 — Frightened Reach** Until the end of your next turn, the target cannot use threatening reach.
>> - **Rank 9 — Demon Panic** Until the end of your next turn, the target cannot make opportunity attacks.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** The target is off-guard until the start of its next turn.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** On a critical hit, the target is frightened 1.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** You may target up to two creatures with this power, making a separate attack roll against each.
