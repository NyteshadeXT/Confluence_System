# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Utility ✦ Channel Divinity

**ENCOUNTER DIVINE**

**Eligible Essences:** Light, Oath, Balance, Life  

**Slot:** 2 – Utility / Passive  
**Tier:** Encounter

In peril, faith steadies your hand.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Stable
**Action Type:** Free Action  
**Range:** Personal
**Cost:** 1 Mana

> [!Effect|background-color-blue]
> You gain a +1 power bonus to your next attack roll or saving throw before the end of your next turn.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Guiding Spark** Increase the bonus to +2.
>> - **Rank 6 — Timely Providence** If you use this power before making an attack against a bloodied enemy or before attempting a saving throw against a fear or charm effect, increase the bonus by an additional +1.
>> - **Rank 9 — Divine Certainty** If the roll benefiting from this power fails by 2 or less, you may reroll the die but must use the second result.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** The base bonus becomes +2. You may apply it to an ally within 5 squares instead of only yourself.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** The base bonus becomes +3. If used on a saving throw and the save succeeds, the target gains temporary hit points equal to your Charisma modifier.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** The base bonus becomes +4. Once per encounter, when this power causes an attack to hit that would otherwise have missed, the attack deals additional radiant damage equal to your Charisma modifier.
