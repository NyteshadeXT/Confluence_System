# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Utility ✦ Tactical

**ENCOUNTER DIVINE**

**Eligible Essences:** Might, Order, Light, Oath, Force

**Slot:** 2 – Utility / Passive  
**Tier:** Encounter

You call upon divine power to strike with overwhelming force.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Escalating
**Action Type:** Minor Action
**Range:** Personal
**Cost:** 1 Mana

> [!Effect|background-color-blue]
> Add your Strength modifier as extra damage to your next attack this turn.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> -  **Rank 3 — Empowered Strike:**  Gain a +1 bonus to the attack roll on the empowered attack.
>> - **Rank 6 — Driving Force:** Add an additional +2 damage.
>> - **Rank 9 — Crushing Blow:** If the attack hits, the target is knocked prone.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Add Strength modifier + 1d6 damage.
>> - **Silver / Epic:**  Add Strength modifier + 2d6 damage and push the target 2 squares on hit.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Add Strength modifier + 3d6 damage and stun the target until the end of your next turn on hit.
