# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Attack ✦ Signature Strike

**ENCOUNTER DIVINE RADIANT IMPLEMENT**

**Eligible Essences:** Light, Sun, Revelation, Eye, Equilibrium, Oath  

**Slot:** 3 – Signature Strike  
**Tier:** Encounter

A searing column of brilliance descends from above, exposing your foe to righteous punishment.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Neutral
**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Range:** Ranged 10  
**Cost:** 1 Mana
**Target:** One creature

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Wisdom vs. Reflex

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 2d10 + Wisdom modifier radiant damage. One ally you can see gains combat advantage against the target until the end of your next turn.

> [!Miss|background-color-yellow]
> - Half damage.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Blinding Radiance** Increase damage to 3d10 + Wisdom modifier.
>> - **Rank 6 — Exposed to Judgment** The target takes a –2 penalty to AC until the end of your next turn.
>> - **Rank 9 — Searing Vulnerability** The first ally who hits the target before the end of your next turn deals extra radiant damage equal to your Wisdom modifier.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Damage increases to 4d10 + Wisdom modifier.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** Target grants combat advantage to all allies until the end of your next turn.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Target is blinded until the end of your next turn.
