# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Utility ✦ Channel Divinity

**ENCOUNTER DIVINE**

**Eligible Essences:** Oath, Light, Life, Balance  

**Slot:** 2 – Utility / Passive  
**Tier:** Encounter

Faith steels the spirit against affliction.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Stable
**Action Type:** Minor Action  
**Range:** Close burst 10
**Cost:** 1 Mana
**Target:** One creature in burst

> [!Effect|background-color-blue]
> The target makes a saving throw with a power bonus equal to your Charisma modifier.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Steeled Resolve** If the saving throw succeeds, the target gains temporary hit points equal to your Charisma modifier.
>> - **Rank 6 — Sacred Endurance** If the saving throw fails, the target gains a +2 power bonus to its next saving throw before the end of your next turn.
>> - **Rank 9 — Unbreakable Spirit** If the saving throw fails by 2 or less, the target may reroll the saving throw but must use the second result.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** The saving throw gains a minimum result equal to your Charisma modifier (if the die result is lower, treat it as that value before modifiers).
>> - **Silver / Epic:** If the saving throw succeeds, the target may also end one additional effect that a save can end.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** If the saving throw succeeds against a charm, fear, or radiant effect, the source of that effect takes radiant damage equal to your Charisma modifier.
