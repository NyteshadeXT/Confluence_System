# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Utility ✦ Core Concept

**AT-WILL DIVINE RADIANT**

**Eligible Essences:** Oath, Light, Reckoning  
Oath, Order, Light, Suppression, Retribution
**Slot:** 1 – Core Concept  
**Tier:** At-Will

You invoke divine judgment and compel your foe to face you.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Escalating
**Action Type:** Minor Action  
**Range:** Close burst 5
**Target:** One creature in burst

> [!Effect|background-color-blue]
> You mark the target. While marked:
> - The target takes a –2 penalty to attack rolls for attacks that do not include you
> - The first time it makes such an attack before the start of your next turn, it takes light damage equal to 3 + your Charisma modifier.
> The mark lasts until you use this power on another target, or you fail to engage the target (attack it or end adjacent to it on your turn). You may use this power once per turn.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Burning Rebuke** Increase the radiant damage to **4 + your Charisma modifier**.
>> - **Rank 6 — Sacred Pressure** If the marked target is bloodied, it takes a –3 penalty instead of –2 when attacking a creature other than you.
>> - **Rank 9 — Relentless Judgment:** The target takes damage even on a miss when triggering the effect
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Radiant punishment becomes 6 + your Charisma modifier.  If the target triggers the radiant damage, it sheds bright light until the end of its next turn.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** Radiant punishment becomes 9 + your Charisma modifier. Once per encounter, when the target triggers the radiant damage, you may pull it 1 square.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Radiant punishment becomes 12 + your Charisma modifier. If the target triggers the radiant damage while bloodied, it is slowed until the end of its next turn.
