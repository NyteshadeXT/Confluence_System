# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Attack ✦ Signature Strike

**Encounter Martial Weapon Control**

**Eligible Essences:** Blade, Snake, Adept, Fox  

**Slot:** 3 – Signature Strike  
**Tier:** Encounter

A sudden, perfectly timed strike leaves your foe reeling and unfocused.

**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Attack Type and Range:** Melee weapon
**Requirement:** You must be wielding a light blade.
**Target:** One creature
**Cost:** 1 Stamina

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Power Rank vs. AC

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 1[W] + your Dexterity modifier damage, and the target is **dazed** until the end of your next turn.

> [!Miss|background-color-yellow]
> - Half damage.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Surgical Timing** You gain a +2 power bonus to the attack roll if the target is granting combat advantage.
>> - **Rank 6 — Disorienting Cut** If the target is already slowed, marked, or suffering a movement penalty, you may slide it 1 square before it becomes dazed.
>> - **Rank 9 — Exploit the Opening** If the target is bloodied, increase the weapon damage to 2[W].
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Damage increases to 2[W]. The dazed condition lasts until the end of your next turn as normal. If the target is bloodied, it also takes a –2 penalty to attack rolls until the end of its next turn.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** Damage increases to 3[W]. Once per encounter, if the target fails a saving throw against one of your effects while dazed by this power, it is instead stunned until the end of its next turn.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Damage increases to 4[W]. If the target is dazed by this power and attempts to shift or teleport before the condition ends, it takes Dexterity modifier psychic damage.
