# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Attack ✦ Core Concept

**At-Will Arcane Light Implement**

**Eligible Essences:** Light, Force, Star, Sun, Omen  

**Slot:** 1 – Core Concept  
**Tier:** At-Will

A lance of searing starlight punishes those who dare close the distance.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Forewarning
**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Range:** Ranged 10
**Target:** One creature

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Essence Attack vs. Fortitude

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 1d6 + your Constitution modifier radiant damage. If the target moves closer to you before the start of your next turn, it takes an additional 1d6 + your Constitution modifier radiant damage.

> [!Miss|background-color-yellow]
> - No damage.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Brilliant Sting** Increase the initial damage to 1d8.
>> - **Rank 6 — Relentless Glow** If the target triggers the additional damage, it sheds bright light in a 2-square radius until the end of its next turn.
>> - **Rank 9 — Punishing Advance** If the target triggers the additional damage, increase that damage by your Constitution modifier (applies once per turn).
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Initial damage becomes 2d6. Triggered damage becomes 2d6.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** Initial damage becomes 3d6. If the target triggers the additional damage, it takes a –2 penalty to attack rolls until the end of its next turn.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Initial damage becomes 4d6. If the target triggers the additional damage, you may push it 1 square after resolving the damage.
