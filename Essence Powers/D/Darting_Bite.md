# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`

Attack ✦ Bestial Skirmish

**ENCOUNTER BEAST**

**Eligible Essences:** Swift, Wolf, Snake, Fox, Cat

**Slot:** 3 – Signature Strike
**Tier:** Encounter

You dart between your foes with predatory speed, striking with tooth or fang before slipping beyond retaliation.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Stable
**Action Type:** Standard Action
**Range:** Melee touch
**Cost:** 1 Stamina
**Target:** One or two creatures


> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Power Rank vs. Reflex made separately against each target.

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 1d10 + Wisdom modifier damage. If at least one attack hits, you may shift 2 squares after resolving all attacks.
> 
> If your current form does not possess an appropriate bite, fangs or equivalent natural weapons manifest for the attacks and disappear immediately afterward.

> [!Miss|background-color-yellow]
> - Half damage.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Darting Retreat** Increase the shift after the attack to 3 squares.
>> - **Rank 6 — Predator's Footwork** Your movement from Darting Bite does not provoke opportunity attacks, even from effects that would normally restrict shifting.
>> - **Rank 9 — Flashing Fangs** If both attacks hit, you may shift a number of squares equal to your Dexterity modifier instead of the normal distance.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Increase the damage to 2d10 + Wisdom modifier.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** You may target up to three creatures, provided each is within your melee reach when its attack is made.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Increase the damage to 3d10 + Wisdom modifier. After resolving the power, you may shift up to your Dexterity modifier even if none of the attacks hit.
