# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Attack ✦ Apex Ability

**DAILY MARTIAL WEAPON**

**Eligible Essences:** Eye, Zeal, Oath, Momentum, Hunt  

**Slot:** 5 – Apex Ability  
**Tier:** Daily

Under your direction, every strike finds weakness.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Escalating
**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Range:** Melee weapon
**Cost:** 2 Stamina
**Target:** One creature

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Attack: Strength vs. AC

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 3[W] + Strength modifier damage. Until the end of the encounter, you and each ally within 5 squares gain a power bonus to attack rolls against the target equal to 1 + your Intelligence modifier.

> [!Miss|background-color-yellow]
> - Until the end of the encounter, you and each ally within 5 squares gain a +1 power bonus to attack rolls against the target.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Crushing Opening** Increase the weapon damage to 4[W] + your Strength modifier.
>> - **Rank 6 — Tactical Insight** If you hit, increase the attack bonus granted to allies by +1.
>> - **Rank 9 — Relentless Coordination** If the target is bloodied, allies benefiting from this power also gain a +2 power bonus to damage rolls against the target.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Damage increases to 5[W] + Strength modifier. The granted attack bonus increases by +1.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** Damage increases to 6[W] + Strength modifier. If an ally hits the target while benefiting from this power, the target takes additional damage equal to your Intelligence modifier (once per round).
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Damage increases to 7[W] + Strength modifier. If the target drops to 0 HP while this effect is active, you may immediately designate a new target within 5 squares; the attack bonus transfers to that target until the end of the encounter.
