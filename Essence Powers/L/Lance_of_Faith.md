# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`

**Attack** **✦ Core Concept**

At-Will Implement Radiant Psychic Sonic Force

**Eligible Essences:** Light, Eye, Sound, Magic, Harmony

**Slot:** 1 – Core Concept  
**Tier:** At-Will

You hurl a focused lance of power that pierces certainty itself, leaving your target exposed to what follows.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Neutral
**Action Type:** Standard Action
**Range:** Ranged 5
**Target:** One creature

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Essence Attack vs. Reflex

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 1d6 + your Essence ability modifier damage. The damage type is determined by the Essence that unlocked this power:
> 	- **Light:** Radiant damage
> 	- **Eye:** Psychic damage
> 	- **Sound:** Sonic damage
> 	- **Magic:** Force damage
> 
> **Expose** Before the end of your next turn, the next attack made against the target gains a +2 bonus to the attack roll. If the target is concealed or hidden, that condition is suppressed until the end of your next turn.

> [!Miss|background-color-yellow]
> - No damage. The Expose effect still applies.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Focused Beam** Increase the damage to 1d8 + your Essence ability modifier.
>> - **Rank 6 — Piercing Clarity** Increase the Expose bonus to +3.
>> - **Rank 9 — Shattered Veil** If the next attack benefiting from Expose hits, the target takes additional damage equal to your Essence ability modifier.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Damage increases to 2d6 + Essence ability modifier. Expose bonus remains +3.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** Damage increases to 3d6 + Essence ability modifier. If the Expose-enhanced attack hits, the target takes a –2 penalty to AC until the end of its next turn.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Damage increases to 4d6 + Essence ability modifier. If the Expose-enhanced attack reduces the target to 0 HP, you may immediately apply Expose to a different creature within 5 squares.
