# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Attack ✦ Tactical Strike

**ENCOUNTER MARTIAL WEAPON**

**Eligible Essences:** Wind, Momentum, Swift, Balance, Oath  

**Slot:** 4 – Tactical Strike  
**Tier:** Encounter

Your strike flows through the battlefield, displacing your enemy like a leaf in a gale.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Stable
**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Range:** Melee weapon
**Cost:** 1 Stamina
**Target:** One creature

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Strength vs. AC

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 2[W] + Strength modifier damage. You or one ally adjacent to the target swaps places with the target.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Flowing Exchange** Increase the damage to 3[W] + your Strength modifier.
>> - **Rank 6 — Disorienting Gust** After the swap, the target takes a –2 penalty to attack rolls until the end of its next turn.
>> - **Rank 9 — Balanced Reversal** After the swap, the creature that switched places with the target gains a +2 power bonus to AC until the end of its next turn.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Damage increases to 4[W] + Strength modifier. The swap does not provoke opportunity attacks from the target.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** Damage increases to 5[W] + Strength modifier. After the swap, the target is slowed until the end of its next turn.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Damage increases to 6[W] + Strength modifier. If the swapped creature is an ally, that ally may make a melee basic attack as a free action (once per use).
