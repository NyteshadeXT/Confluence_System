# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Utility ✦ Sacred Touch

**AT-WILL DIVINE HEALING**

**Eligible Essences:** Life, Oath, Light, Harmony 

**Slot:** 2 – Utility / Passive  
**Tier:** At-Will

Your touch carries divine restoration.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Stable
**Action Type:** Minor Action  
**Range:** Melee touch
**Cost:** Expend one of your Healing Surges.
**Target:** One creature

> [!Effect|background-color-blue]
> You spend a Healing Surge and regain no hit points.  Instead, the target regains hit points as if it had spent a Healing Surge. You must have at least one Healing Surge remaining to use this power.
> 
> You may use this power a number of times per day equal to your Wisdom modifier (minimum 1), but only once per round.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Gentle Restoration** The target regains additional hit points equal to your Charisma modifier.
>> - **Rank 6 — Cleansing Touch** When you use this power, the target may make a saving throw against one effect that a save can end.
>> - **Rank 9 — Radiant Vitality** If the target is bloodied, it gains temporary hit points equal to your Wisdom modifier.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** The target regains additional hit points equal to your Wisdom modifier.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** When the target regains hit points from this power, it also gains a +2 power bonus to all defenses until the start of your next turn.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** If the target is reduced to 0 HP before the end of the encounter, you may use Lay on Hands on that target as an immediate reaction without expending a daily use (once per encounter).
