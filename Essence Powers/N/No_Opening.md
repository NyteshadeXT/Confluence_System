# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Utility ✦ Defensive

**Encounter Martial**

**Eligible Essences:** Shield, Order, Iron, Adept, Mind

**Slot:** 2 – Utility / Passive  
**Tier:** Encounter

You close ranks instantly, denying your enemy the opening they thought they had.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Neutral
**Action Type:** Immediate Interrupt  
**Range:** Personal
**Trigger:** An enemy attacks you and has combat advantage against you.

> [!Effect|background-color-blue]
> The triggering enemy loses combat advantage against you for that attack.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Tight Guard** You gain a +2 power bonus to AC against the triggering attack.
>> - **Rank 6 — Counter Poise** If the triggering attack misses, you gain a +2 power bonus to your next attack roll against that enemy before the end of your next turn.
>> - **Rank 9 — Iron Discipline** If the triggering attack misses, the enemy grants combat advantage to you until the end of your next turn.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** After negating combat advantage, you also gain resistance to all damage from the triggering attack equal to your Constitution modifier.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** If the triggering attack misses, you may shift 1 square as a free action.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** If the triggering attack misses, the enemy takes damage equal to your Strength or Intelligence modifier (chosen when this power is unlocked).
