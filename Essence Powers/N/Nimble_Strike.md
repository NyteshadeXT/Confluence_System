# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Attack ✦ Core Concept

**At-Will Martial Weapon Mobility**

**Eligible Essences:** Swift, Cat, Ranged, Wind, Fox  

**Slot:** 1 – Core Concept  
**Tier:** At-Will

You weave past danger, striking while constantly adjusting your position.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Neutral
**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Range:** Ranged weapon
**Target:** One creature

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Essence Attack vs. AC (Dexterity)

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 1[W] + your Essence ability modifier damage. You may shift 1 square before or after making this attack.

> [!Miss|background-color-yellow]
> - No damage.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Fluid Motion** Increase the damage to 1[W] + Essence ability modifier + 2.
>> - **Rank 6 — Evasive Rhythm** If you shift before the attack, you gain a +2 power bonus to AC against opportunity attacks until the end of your turn.
>> - **Rank 9 — Constant Repositioning** If the attack hits, you may shift 1 additional square.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Damage increases to 2[W] + Essence ability modifier.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** Damage increases to 3[W] + Essence ability modifier. If you shift before the attack, you gain combat advantage for that attack.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Damage increases to 4[W] + Essence ability modifier. If you reduce the target to 0 HP, you may shift up to your speed.
