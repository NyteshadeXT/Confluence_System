# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Attack ✦ Apex Ability

**DAILY ARCANE COLD**

**Eligible Essences:** Ice, Armor, Water, Shield, Force, Elemental, Shadow, Reckoning
**Slot:** 5 – Apex Ability  
**Tier:** Daily

Black ice encases you in a sheath of merciless cold, punishing those who dare approach.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Escalating
**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Range:** Personal
**Cost:** 1 Mana

> [!Effect|background-color-blue]
> You gain temporary hit points equal to 10 + your Intelligence modifier.
> 
> Until the end of the encounter, an enemy that starts its turn adjacent to you takes 1d6 + your Constitution modifier cold damage.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Deepening Frost** Increase the temporary hit points to 12 + your Intelligence modifier.
>> - **Rank 6 — Numbing Aura** Increase the cold damage to 1d8 + your Constitution modifier.
>> - •      
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Temporary hit points increase to 15 + Intelligence modifier. Cold damage becomes 1d8 + Con.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** If a creature takes cold damage from this effect, it is slowed until the end of its turn.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** If your temporary hit points are reduced to 0, each adjacent enemy takes cold damage equal to your Constitution modifier.
