# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Attack ✦ Apex Ability

**DAILY DIVINE FIRE WEAPON**

**Eligible Essences:** Light, Oath, Zeal, Reckoning  

**Slot:** 5 – Apex Ability  
**Tier:** Daily

Sacred fire brands your foe, and divine wrath answers every act of defiance.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Escalating
**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Range:** Melee weapon
**Cost:** 2 Mana
**Target:** One creature

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Attack: Strength vs. AC

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 2[W] + Strength modifier damage, and ongoing 5 fire damage (save ends).
> 
> **Special:** If the target makes an attack on its turn, it cannot attempt a saving throw against the ongoing damage that turn.

> [!Miss|background-color-yellow]
> - Half damage; no ongoing damage.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Intensified Wrath** Increase the ongoing fire damage to 7.
>> - **Rank 6 — Relentless Judgment** When the target makes an attack while affected, it takes additional fire damage equal to your Wisdom modifier. (This damage occurs whether or not it can attempt a saving throw.)
>> - **Rank 9 — Scouring Brand** If the target fails its first saving throw against this power, it takes fire damage equal to your Strength modifier.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Ongoing damage increases to 10.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** If the target makes two attacks in one turn, it takes fire damage equal to your Wisdom modifier for each attack.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** When the target is reduced to 0 HP while affected, each enemy adjacent to it takes fire damage equal to your Charisma modifier.
