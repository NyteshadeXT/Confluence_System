# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Attack ✦ Apex Ability  

**HEALING POLYMORPH PSIONIC WEAPON**

**Eligible Essences:** Adept, Balance, Harmony, Life, Renewal, Serene, Soul

**Slot:** 5 – Apex Ability
**Tier:** Daily

You attain a moment of complete harmony between mind, body, and spirit, restoring yourself and turning each subsequent strike into a lesson in perfect form.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Harmony

**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Range:** Melee weapon  
**Cost:** 3 Mana

**Target:** One creature

> [!Effect|background-color-blue]
> - You regain Hit Points equal to 2d6 + your highest mental ability modifier. You then assume the aspect of elevated harmony until the end of the encounter.

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Power Rank vs. AC

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 2[W] + your Essence ability modifier damage.

> [!Miss|background-color-yellow]
> - Half damage.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Restorative Balance** Increase the healing to 3d6 + your highest mental ability modifier.
>> - **Rank 6 — Harmonious Assault** Once per round while in this aspect, when you hit with a Slot 1 power, you gain temporary Hit Points equal to your highest mental ability modifier and deal the same amount of extra damage to that target.
>> - **Rank 9 — Perfect Accord** The first time each round you would take damage while in this aspect, reduce that damage by your highest mental ability modifier.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** While in this aspect, you gain a +1 bonus to AC and all saving throws.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** You may use the Rank 6 expression twice per round, but no more than once per creature.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** When the aspect ends, regain Hit Points equal to your highest mental ability modifier.
