# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Attack ✦ Apex Ability  

**CHARM PSIONIC PSYCHIC WEAPON**

**Eligible Essences:** Corrupt, Discord, Dream, Malign, Mind, Subjugation

**Slot:** 5 – Apex Ability
**Tier:** Daily

Your attack fills your foe with a vision of betrayal, driving it to lash out at an apparent traitor before the illusion fades.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Mind

**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Range:** Melee weapon  
**Cost:** 3 Mana

**Target:** One creature

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Power Rank vs. AC

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 2[W] + your Essence ability modifier psychic damage. Choose one creature the target can reach. The target immediately makes a melee Strike against that creature as a free action. You choose the target of this Strike, but the target chooses all other details.

> [!Miss|background-color-yellow]
> - Half damage.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Paranoid Vision** The target is off-guard until the end of its next turn.
>> - **Rank 6 — Treacherous Blow** If the compelled Strike hits, it deals extra psychic damage equal to your highest mental ability modifier.
>> - **Rank 9 — Shattered Trust** The target is frightened 1 after it resolves the compelled Strike.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** If no creature is within the target's reach, it may Shift up to 1 square before making the compelled Strike.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** On a critical hit, the target makes two melee Strikes against the chosen creature instead of one.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** The target is also stupefied until the end of its next turn.
