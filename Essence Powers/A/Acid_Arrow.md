# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Attack ✦ Apex Ability

**DAILY ARCANE ACID IMPLEMENT**

**Eligible Essences:** Alchemy, Entropy, Venom, Ruin, Magic, Water, Elemental, Acid
**Slot:** 5 – Apex Ability  
**Tier:** Daily

A lance of corrosive power detonates in searing ruin.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Escalating
**Action Type:** Single Action  
**Range:** Ranged 20
**Cost:** 2 Mana
**Primary Target:** One creature

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Intelligence vs. Reflex

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 2d8 + Intelligence modifier acid damage, and ongoing 5 acid damage (save ends).
> 
> - **Secondary Attack:** Make an attack roll for each creature adjacent to the primary target. 1d8 + Intelligence modifier acid damage, and ongoing 5 acid damage (save ends).

> [!Miss|background-color-yellow]
> - Half damage to the primary target and ongoing 2 acid damage (save ends). No secondary attack.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Corrosive Potency** Increase ongoing acid damage against the primary target to 7.
>> - **Rank 6 — Lingering Corrosion** Add your Intelligence modifier to the ongoing damage dealt to the primary target (applied once per round).
>> - **Rank 9 — Spreading Dissolution** When the primary target fails its first saving throw against this power, one creature adjacent to it takes acid damage equal to your Intelligence modifier.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Ongoing damage increases to 10 (primary) and 5 (secondary).
>> - **Silver / Epic:** Creatures taking ongoing acid damage grant combat advantage.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** If a creature fails two saves against this power, it is weakened until the ongoing damage ends.
