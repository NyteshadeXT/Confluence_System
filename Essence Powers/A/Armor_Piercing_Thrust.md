# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Attack ✦ Signature Strike

**ENCOUNTER MARTIAL WEAPON**

**Eligible Essences:** Blade, Polearm, Snake, Hunt, Adept, Steel

**Slot:** 3 – Signature Strike
**Tier:** Encounter

You drive your weapon into a gap in your foe’s guard with practiced precision.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Neutral
**Action Type:** Standard Action
**Range:** Melee weapon
**Requirement:** You must be wielding a melee weapon.
**Cost:** 1 Stamina
**Target:** One creature

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Strength vs. Reflex

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 1[W] + Strength modifier damage.
> 
> If you are wielding a light blade or a spear, you also gain a bonus to the damage roll equal to your Dexterity modifier.

> [!Miss|background-color-yellow]
> No damage.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Precise Entry** If you are wielding a light blade or a spear, you gain a +1 power bonus to the attack roll in addition to the normal Dexterity bonus.
>> - **Rank 6 — Exploited Weakness** On a hit, the target takes a –2 penalty to AC against the next attack made against it before the start of your next turn.
>> - **Rank 9 — Unerring Thrust** If you miss by 2 or less while wielding a light blade or a spear, deal damage equal to your Dexterity modifier.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Damage increases to 2[W] + Strength modifier. If wielding a light blade or a spear, add Dexterity modifier to the damage roll.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** Damage increases to 3[W] + Strength modifier. On a hit, the target grants combat advantage until the end of your next turn.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Damage increases to 4[W] + Strength modifier. If wielding a light blade or a spear, the target also takes a –2 penalty to Reflex until the end of your next turn.
