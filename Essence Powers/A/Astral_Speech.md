# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Utility ✦ Utility / Passive  

**DAILY DIVINE MIND**

**Eligible Essences:** Oath, Light, Revelation, Zeal, Order, Equilibrium

**Slot:** 2 – Utility / Passive  
**Tier:** Daily

Your words carry the weight of divine truth, bending doubt into belief.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Neutral → Escalating (if used to dominate social outcomes repeatedly)
**Action Type:** Minor Action  
**Range:** Personal
**Cost:** 1 Mana + Quintessence

> [!Effect|background-color-blue]
> Until the end of the encounter, you gain a +4 power bonus to Diplomacy checks. In addition, creatures who can hear you take a –2 penalty to checks made to oppose your social influence (such as Insight or counter-arguments), at the GM’s discretion.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Unshakable Conviction** Increase the bonus to Diplomacy checks to +5.
>> - **Rank 6 — Words of Authority** Once during the effect, you may treat a failed Diplomacy check as a success.
>> - **Rank 9 — Irrefutable Truth** Creatures who fail to oppose your Diplomacy are briefly overwhelmed, granting you advantage on your next interaction with them.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Bonus increases to +6.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** Your words influence even hostile creatures unless they are immune to charm effects.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Once per encounter, you may impose a command-like effect through speech alone (GM adjudicated).
