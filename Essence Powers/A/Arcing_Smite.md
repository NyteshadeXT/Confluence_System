# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Attack ✦ Tactical Strike

**ENCOUNTER DIVINE WEAPON**

**Eligible Essences:** Oath, Order, Zeal, Reckoning, Blade, Bludgeon, Polearm, Light, Momentum
**Slot:** 4 – Tactical Strike  
**Tier:** Encounter

You sweep your weapon in a wide arc, calling divine judgment upon multiple foes and binding them to your presence.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Neutral → Escalating (marking multiple enemies draws attention)
**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Range:** Melee weapon  
**Cost:** 1 Mana
**Targets:** One or two creatures

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Power Rank vs. AC (one attack per target)

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 1[W] + your Essence ability modifier damage, and the target is marked until the end of your next turn.

> [!Miss|background-color-yellow]
> - No mark.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Binding Judgment** Marked targets take a –2 penalty to attack rolls against creatures other than you.
>> - **Rank 6 — Pressing Presence** If both attacks hit, you gain a +1 bonus to AC until the start of your next turn.
>> - **Rank 9 — Relentless Claim** If a marked target shifts or moves away from you before the end of your next turn, it takes radiant damage equal to your Charisma modifier.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Damage increases to 2[W] + ability modifier.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** Damage increases to 3[W] + ability modifier. Marked targets grant combat advantage to you.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Damage increases to 4[W] + ability modifier. Marked targets are slowed while marked.
