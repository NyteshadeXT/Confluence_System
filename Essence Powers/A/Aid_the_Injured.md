# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Utility ✦ Signature Support

**ENCOUNTER MARTIAL HEALING**

**Eligible Essences:** Life, Renewal, Bond, Balance, Harmony, Oath, Zeal, Shield, Serene, Resolute, Growth
**Slot:** 2 – Utility / Passive  
**Tier:** Encounter

Your presence steadies the wounded and restores their will.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Stable
**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Range:** Melee touch
**Cost:** 1 Stamina
**Target:** You or one adjacent ally

> [!Effect|background-color-blue]
> The target can spend a Healing Surge.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Steadying Presence** The target gains temporary hit points equal to your Constitution modifier.
>> - **Rank 6 — Bolstered Resolve** The target gains a +1 power bonus to all defenses until the end of your next turn.
>> - **Rank 9 — Shared Endurance** If the target was bloodied before spending the Healing Surge, it gains resistance to all damage equal to your Wisdom modifier until the end of your next turn.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** The target gains additional hit points equal to your Charisma modifier.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** If the target was bloodied, it may shift 1 square as a free action.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** The target also gains a +1 power bonus to all defenses until the end of its next turn.
