# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Utility ✦ Apex Ability

**DAILY MARTIAL FORCE**

**Eligible Essences:** Shield, Armor, Steel, Stone, Resolute, Iron, Earth, Turtle, Might, Oath, Force, Rune, Siege, Suppression, Tree
**Slot:** 5 – Apex Ability  
**Tier:** Daily

You plant your stance and become an immovable bulwark, your will and steel merging into an unbreakable defense.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Neutral
**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Range:** Personal
**Cost:** 2 Stamina

> [!Effect|background-color-blue]
> You gain a +2 power bonus to AC and Fortitude until the end of the encounter.
> 
> While this effect persists:
> - Whenever an enemy hits you with a melee attack, it takes force damage equal to your Constitution modifier.
> - You cannot be pushed, pulled, or slid unless you choose to be.
> - If you are marked by an enemy, that mark immediately ends.
> 
> If you are wielding a shield when you activate this power, you also gain temporary hit points equal to 5 + your Constitution modifier.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Reinforced Guard** Increase the AC and Fortitude bonus to +3.
>> - **Rank 6 — Punishing Steel** Retaliation damage increases to Constitution modifier + 2.
>> - **Rank 9 — Relentless Anchor** The first time each round you would be knocked prone, you may ignore it.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** The bonus to AC and Fortitude increases to +3. Temporary hit points increase to 10 + Constitution modifier.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** Enemies that hit you with melee attacks are knocked prone if they are Large or smaller (once per creature per round).
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** The first time you would be reduced to 0 hit points while this power is active, you instead remain at 1 hit point and each adjacent enemy takes force damage equal to twice your Constitution modifier.
