# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Attack ✦ Tactical Strike

**ENCOUNTER ARCANE COLD IMPLEMENT**

**Eligible Essences:** Ice, Water, Magic, Ranged, Momentum, Suppression

**Slot:** 4 – Tactical Strike  
**Tier:** Encounter

Twin lances of freezing energy streak toward your foes, locking their movement in place as frost spreads beneath them.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Escalating (movement denial disrupts flow)
**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Range:** Ranged 10  
**Cost:** 1 Mana
**Targets:** One or two creatures

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Essence Attack vs. Reflex (Intelligence-based; one attack per target)

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 1d10 + your Essence ability modifier cold damage, and the target is immobilized until the end of your next turn.

> [!Miss|background-color-yellow]
> - Half damage. No immobilization.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Deep Freeze** Increase damage to 2d10 + ability modifier.
>> - **Rank 6 — Crippling Frost** Immobilized targets take a –2 penalty to Reflex.
>> - **Rank 9 — Binding Ice** If a target is already slowed or immobilized, it is also knocked prone.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Damage increases to 3d10 + ability modifier.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** Damage increases to 4d10 + ability modifier. Immobilization lasts until the end of the target’s next turn after yours.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Damage increases to 5d10 + ability modifier. If both attacks hit the same target, it is also slowed (save ends) after the immobilization ends.
