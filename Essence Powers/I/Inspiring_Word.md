# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Utility ✦ Encounter

**Encounter Martial Healing**

**Eligible Essences:** Zeal, Resolute, Oath, Order, Renewal, Bond

**Slot:** 2 – Utility / Passive  
**Tier:** Encounter

Your voice cuts through pain and doubt, rekindling the will to fight.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Neutral
**Action Type:** Minor Action
**Range:** Close Burst 5
**Cost:** 1 Stamina
**Target:** You or one ally in burst

> [!Effect|background-color-blue]
> The target can spend a Healing Surge and regain additional hit points equal to 1d6 + your Charisma modifier. You can use this power twice per encounter, but only once per round. 

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Stirring Command** Increase the additional healing to 1d8 + your Charisma modifier.
>> - **Rank 6 — Rallying Presence** The target gains temporary hit points equal to your Charisma modifier.
>> - **Rank 9 — Unbreakable Resolve** If the target is bloodied when they receive this healing, they gain a +2 power bonus to attack rolls until the end of their next turn.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Additional healing becomes 2d6 + Charisma modifier.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** Additional healing becomes 3d6 + Charisma modifier. You can use this power three times per encounter, but only once per round. 
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Additional healing becomes 4d6 + Charisma modifier. If the target is below half HP after the healing resolves, they gain a +2 power bonus to all defenses until the start of your next turn.
