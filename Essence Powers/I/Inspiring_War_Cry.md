# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Attack ✦ Signature Strike

**ENCOUNTER MARTIAL WEAPON**

**Eligible Essences:** Sound, Zeal, Resolute, Oath, Order, Renewal, Revelation, Harmony

**Slot:** 3 – Signature Strike  
**Tier:** Encounter

Your strike is punctuated by a commanding cry that steels the will of your allies and drives weakness from their bodies.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Neutral
**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Range:** Melee weapon  
**Cost:** 1 Stamina
**Target:** One creature

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Essence Attack vs. AC (Strength-based)

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 2[W] + your Essence ability modifier damage. One ally within 5 squares who can hear you immediately makes a saving throw.

> [!Miss|background-color-yellow]
> - Half damage. No saving throw.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Rallying Voice** The ally gains a +1 bonus to the saving throw.
>> - **Rank 6 — Strengthened Resolve** If the saving throw succeeds, the ally gains temporary hit points equal to your Charisma modifier.
>> - **Rank 9 — Inspiring Surge** If the saving throw succeeds, the ally gains a +1 bonus to attack rolls until the end of your next turn.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Damage increases to 3[W] + ability modifier. You may target yourself with the saving throw.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** Damage increases to 4[W] + ability modifier. The ally may roll twice and take the better result.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Damage increases to 5[W] + ability modifier. You may grant saving throws to two allies instead of one.
