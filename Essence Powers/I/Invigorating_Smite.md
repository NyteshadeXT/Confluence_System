# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Attack ✦ Signature Strike  

**ENCOUNTER DIVINE HEALING WEAPON**

**Eligible Essences:** Life, Oath, Zeal, Light, Renewal, Beacon, Equilibrium

**Slot:** 3 – Signature Strike  
**Tier:** Encounter

Your strike channels divine vitality, restoring strength to you and those who fight beside you.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Neutral
**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Range:** Melee weapon  
**Cost:** 1 Mana
**Target:** One creature

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Power Rank vs. Will

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 2[W] + your Essence ability modifier damage. If you are bloodied, you regain hit points equal to 5 + your Wisdom modifier. Bloodied allies within 5 squares regain hit points equal to 5 + your Wisdom modifier.

> [!Miss|background-color-yellow]
> - Half damage. No healing.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Strength Renewed** Increase the healing to 7 + Wisdom modifier.
>> - **Rank 6 — Shared Vitality** You and affected allies gain a +1 bonus to saving throws until the end of your next turn.
>> - **Rank 9 — Surging Faith** If you are bloodied when you use this power, you gain temporary hit points equal to your Charisma modifier.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Damage increases to 3[W] + ability modifier. Healing increases to 10 + Wisdom modifier.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** Damage increases to 4[W] + ability modifier. Healing applies whether or not the target is bloodied.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Damage increases to 5[W] + ability modifier. All affected allies also gain resistance to damage equal to your Wisdom modifier until the end of your next turn.
