# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Attack ✦ Tactical Strike

**ENCOUNTER ARCANE COLD IMPLEMENT**

**Eligible Essences:** Ice, Water, Balance, Wind  

**Slot:** 4 – Tactical Strike  
**Tier:** Encounter

A treacherous glaze of ice spreads beneath your enemies’ feet.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Stable

**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Range:** Area burst 1 within 10 squares
**Cost:** 1 Mana
**Target:** Each creature in burst

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Intelligence vs. Reflex

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 1d6 + Intelligence modifier cold damage. The target is knocked prone. The area becomes difficult terrain until the end of your next turn. You may dismiss the terrain as a minor action.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Spreading Frost** Increase the damage to 2d6 + your Intelligence modifier.
>> - **Rank 6 — Slippery Collapse** Creatures that stand up while within the affected area must make a saving throw or fall prone again.
>> - **Rank 9 — Encroaching Chill** The difficult terrain created by this power lasts until the end of your next turn + 1 round.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Damage increases to 3d6 + Intelligence modifier. Area becomes burst 2.
>> - **Silver / Epic:**  Damage increases to 4d6 + Intelligence modifier. Creatures that enter the affected area are slowed until the end of their turn.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Damage increases to 5d6 + Intelligence modifier. If a creature falls prone in the area, it is also immobilized until the end of its next turn.
