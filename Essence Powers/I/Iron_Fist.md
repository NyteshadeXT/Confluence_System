# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Attack ✦ Core Concept  

**PSIONIC • WEAPON**

**Eligible Essences:** Armor, Bear, Bone, Earth, Iron, Shield, Steel, Stone

**Slot:** 1 – Core Concept
**Tier:** At-Will

You densify your striking limb into unyielding psychic iron, delivering a heavy blow while hardening yourself against reprisal.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Iron

**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Range:** Melee weapon  
**Cost:** None

**Target:** One creature

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Power Rank vs. AC

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 1[W] + your Essence ability modifier damage. Until the end of your next turn, you gain resistance to all damage equal to your highest mental ability modifier.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Tempered Fist** Increase the resistance to 1 + your highest mental ability modifier.
>> - **Rank 6 — Fire-Hardened** While the resistance is active, you gain fire resistance equal to 3 + your highest mental ability modifier.
>> - **Rank 9 — Heavy Blow** Increase the damage to 2[W] + your Essence ability modifier.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** While the resistance is active, you gain a +1 bonus to AC.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** The first enemy that damages you while the resistance is active takes force damage equal to your highest mental ability modifier.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** On a critical hit, the resistance lasts until the end of your next two turns.
