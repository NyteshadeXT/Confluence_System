# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`

Utility ✦ Stance

**Daily Martial Healing Stance**

**Eligible Essences:** Bear, Iron, Shield, Bone  

**Slot:** 2 – Utility / Passive  
**Tier:** Daily

You steady your breathing and ignore pain that would cripple a lesser warrior.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Escalating
**Action Type:** Minor Action  
**Range:** Personal
**Cost:** 2 Stamina

> [!Effect|background-color-blue]
> You enter the Boundless Endurance stance.  While in this stance, when you are bloodied, you gain regeneration equal to 2 + your Constitution modifier. The stance lasts until the end of the encounter or until you fall unconscious.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Hardened Resolve** Increase the regeneration to 3 + your Constitution modifier while bloodied.
>> - **Rank 6 — Relentless Will** The first time each encounter you would fall unconscious while in this stance, you remain at 1 HP instead. This ends the stance. (Once per activation.)
>> - **Rank 9 — Iron Recovery** While bloodied and benefiting from this stance, you gain a +1 power bonus to Fortitude and Will.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Regeneration becomes 4 + Constitution modifier while bloodied.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** While bloodied in this stance, you gain resistance to weapon damage equal to your Constitution modifier.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** When you enter the stance, you immediately gain temporary HP equal to your Constitution modifier.
