# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Utility ✦ Utility Passive 

**PSIONIC  MIND  CONTROL**

**Eligible Essences:** Mind, Dominion, Oath, Order, Subjugation, Suppression

**Slot:** 2 – Utility / Passive  
**Tier:** At-Will

You seize your foe's attention with a focused psychic challenge, making it costly to ignore you.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Mind

**Action Type:** Minor Action  
**Range:** Close burst 3  
**Cost:** None

**Target:** One creature in the burst

> [!Effect|background-color-blue]
> The target is marked by you until you use this power again or until the end of the encounter. A creature marked by you takes a –2 penalty to attack rolls for attacks that do not include you as a target.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Divided Demand** You can target up to two creatures in the burst.
>> - **Rank 6 — Unrelenting Demand** The penalty from your mark increases to –3.
>> - **Rank 9 — Dominating Demand** You can target up to three creatures in the burst.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Increase the burst to 5.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** CA creature that critically fails an attack against you while marked by you is off-guard until the end of its next turn.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** You can maintain this power's mark on up to five creatures at once.
