# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Attack ✦ Signature Strike

**ENCOUNTER ARCANE FIRE IMPLEMENT**

**Eligible Essences:** Fire, Light, Momentum

**Slot:** 3 – Signature Strike  
**Tier:** Encounter

Flame erupts from your hands in a roaring wave.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Stable
**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Range:** Close blast 5
**Cost:** 1 Mana
**Target:** Each creature in blast

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Intelligence vs. Reflex

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 2d6 + Intelligence modifier fire damage.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Intensified Flames** Increase the damage to 2d8 + Intelligence modifier.
>> - **Rank 6 — Lingering Heat** Creatures hit by this power take fire damage equal to your Intelligence modifier at the end of their next turn. (Occurs once.)
>> - **Rank 9 — Surging Inferno** If you hit at least two creatures, you gain a +2 power bonus to your next damage roll before the end of your next turn.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Damage increases to 3d6 + Intelligence modifier.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** Creatures hit are slowed until the end of your next turn.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** The first creature that enters the blast area before the end of your next turn takes fire damage equal to your Intelligence modifier.
