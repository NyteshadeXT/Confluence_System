# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Attack ✦ Apex Ability

**DAILY MARTIAL WEAPON**

**Eligible Essences:** Oath, Shield, Stone, Zeal, Balance  

**Slot:** 5 – Apex Ability  
**Tier:** Daily

Honorable warriors never fall while you still stand.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Neutral → Escalating
**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Range:** Melee weapon
**Cost:** 2 Stamina
**Target:** One creature

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Strength vs. AC

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 3[W] + Strength modifier damage. Allies within 5 squares gain temporary hit points equal to 5 + your Charisma modifier and a +1 power bonus to all defenses until the end of the encounter.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Reinforcing Cry** Increase the temporary hit points granted to 7 + your Charisma modifier.
>> - **Rank 6 — Steadfast Formation** While an ally benefiting from the defense bonus is adjacent to you, increase their bonus to +2.
>> - **Rank 9 — Unbroken Line** The first time each ally benefiting from this power would be bloodied during the encounter, they gain temporary hit points equal to your Constitution modifier. (Triggers once per ally.)
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Defense bonus increases to +2.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** Allies also gain +1 to saving throws while within 5 squares.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** When an ally within 5 squares drops to 0 HP, they instead remain at 1 HP (once per encounter).
