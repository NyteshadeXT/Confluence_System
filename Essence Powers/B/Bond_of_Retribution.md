# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Attack ✦ Core Concept

**AT-WILL DIVINE WEAPON**

**Eligible Essences:** Light, Order, Oath, Reckoning

**Slot:** 1 – Core Concept
**Tier:** At-Will

You mark a foe with divine consequence—others who strike you invite its punishment.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Neutral
**Action Type:** Standard Action
**Range:** Melee weapon
**Cost:** —
**Target:** One creature

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Power Rank vs AC

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 1[W] + your Essence ability modifier damage.
> 
> - The first time an enemy other than the target hits or misses you before the end of your next turn the target takes damage equal to your Intelligence modifier.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Imposed Consequence:** Gain a +1 bonus to the attack roll
>> - **Rank 6 — Amplified Retribution:** Increase the triggered damage by +2
>> - **Rank 9 — Shared Punishment:** The triggering enemy also takes damage equal to half your Intelligence modifier
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** The triggered damage becomes Intelligence modifier + 1d6
>> - **Silver / Epic:** The triggered damage becomes Intelligence modifier + 2d6, and the target takes a –2 penalty to attack rolls until the end of its next turn
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** The triggered damage becomes Intelligence modifier + 3d6, and you may apply the effect twice per round
