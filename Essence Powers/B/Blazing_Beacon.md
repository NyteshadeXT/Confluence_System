# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Attack ✦ Signature Strike  

**ENCOUNTER DIVINE RADIANT WEAPON**

**Eligible Essences:** Light, Sun, Oath, Revelation, Rune, Eye, Order, Dominion

**Slot:** 3 – Signature Strike  
**Tier:** Encounter

Holy radiance crowns your foe in a blazing sigil, guiding the judgment of your allies.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Neutral
**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Range:** Melee weapon  
**Cost:** 1 Mana
**Target:** One creature

> [!Effect|background-color-blue]
> Strength vs. AC

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 1[W] + Strength modifier radiant damage. Until the end of your next turn, ranged attack rolls against the target gain a +2 power bonus.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Intensified Beacon** Increase damage to 2[W] + Strength modifier.
>> - **Rank 6 — Guiding Radiance** The bonus to ranged attacks increases to +3.
>> - **Rank 9 — Mark of Illumination** If a ranged attack hits the target before the end of your next turn, that attacker gains temporary hit points equal to your Wisdom modifier.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Damage increases to 3[W] + Strength modifier. Bonus becomes +4.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** Damage increases to 4[W] + Strength modifier. The target grants combat advantage to ranged attacks.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Damage increases to 5[W] + Strength modifier. Ranged attacks against the target score a critical hit on a 19–20.
