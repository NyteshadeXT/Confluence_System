# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Attack ✦ Tactical Strike  

**ENCOUNTER MARTIAL WEAPON**

**Eligible Essences:** Swift, Momentum, Ambush, Adept, Eye, Wind, Fox, Cat
**Slot:** 4 – Tactical Strike  
**Tier:** Encounter

You strike and slip past your foe in one seamless motion, leaving them off-balance and out of position.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Neutral
**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Range:** Melee weapon  
**Cost:** 1 Stamina
**Requirement:** You must be wielding a light blade.
**Target:** One creature

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Essence Attack vs. Will (Dexterity-based)

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 2[W] + your Essence ability modifier damage. You switch places with the target, then shift 1 square.

> [!Miss|background-color-yellow]
> - No position swap. You may shift 1 square.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Fluid Exchange** After switching places, you gain a +1 bonus to AC until the start of your next turn.
>> - **Rank 6 — Disorienting Feint** The target takes a –2 penalty to Reflex until the start of your next turn.
>> - **Rank 9 — Perfect Misdirection** After resolving the power, you may become concealed until the end of your next turn if you have cover or concealment.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Damage increases to 3[W] + ability modifier.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** Damage increases to 4[W] + ability modifier. After switching places, the target is slowed until the end of your next turn.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Damage increases to 5[W] + ability modifier. After switching places, you may immediately make a Stealth check to hide.
