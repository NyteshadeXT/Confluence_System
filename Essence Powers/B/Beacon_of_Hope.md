# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Attack ✦ Apex Ability

**DAILY DIVINE HEALING IMPLEMENT**

**Eligible Essences:** Life, Light, Oath, Balance  

**Slot:** 5 – Apex Ability  
**Tier:** Daily

Radiant assurance weakens your enemies and strengthens your faithful.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Neutral
**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Range:** Close burst 3
**Cost:** 2 Mana
**Target:** Each enemy in burst

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Wisdom vs. Will

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - The target is weakened until the end of your next turn.
> - You and all allies in the burst regain 5 hit points. Until the end of the encounter, your healing powers restore +5 additional hit points.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Renewed Faith** Increase the immediate healing to 7 hit points.
>> - **Rank 6 — Sustained Grace** Increase the healing bonus granted to your healing powers to +7 hit points.
>> - **Rank 9 — Radiant Assurance** If at least one target is weakened by this power, you gain temporary hit points equal to your Wisdom modifier.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Immediate healing increases to 10 hit points.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** Enemies weakened by this power take a –2 penalty to saving throws until the end of your next turn.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** The first time each ally in the burst drops to 0 HP during the encounter, they regain hit points equal to your Wisdom modifier.
