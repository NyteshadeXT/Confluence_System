# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Utility ✦ Utility / Passive  

**PSIONIC  MIND  TELEPORTATION**

**Eligible Essences:** Air, Dimension, Freedom, Shimmer, Swift, Wind, Wing

**Slot:** 2 – Utility / Passive  
**Tier:** Conditional

You fold a fraction of the distance between yourself and a fleeing foe, keeping them within your reach.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Dimension

**Action Type:** Opportunity Action  
**Range:** Personal  
**Cost:** None

**Trigger:** An adjacent enemy Shifts or otherwise willingly moves away from a space adjacent to you.

> [!Effect|background-color-blue]
> You Shift 1 square. You must end this movement closer to the triggering enemy.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Lingering Blur** You may Shift 2 squares when you use this power. You must still end closer to the triggering enemy.
>> - **Rank 6 — Flash Step** You may teleport instead of Shifting when you use this power.
>> - **Rank 9 — Pursuing Blur** You Shift or teleport up to 3 squares when you use this power.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** You can use this power once per round without expending your opportunity action.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** When you teleport with this power, you may end in any unoccupied space adjacent to the triggering enemy.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** You may teleport up to 5 squares when you use this power.
