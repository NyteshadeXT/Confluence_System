# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`

Utility ✦ Living Defense

**ENCOUNTER PROTECTION**

**Eligible Essences:** Tree, Plant, Armor, Growth, Wood

**Slot:** 2 – Utility / Passive
**Tier:** Encounter

Living bark spreads across flesh and equipment, layering the target in resilient natural armor.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Stable
**Action Type:** Minor Action
**Range:** Ranged 5
**Cost:** 1 Mana
**Target:** You or one ally

> [!Effect|background-color-blue]
> Until the end of your next turn, the target gains a power bonus to AC equal to your Constitution modifier.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Thickened Bark** The target also gains a +1 power bonus to Fortitude for the duration.
>> - **Rank 6 — Living Bulwark** If the target takes damage while Barkskin is active, reduce the first instance of damage it takes by your Constitution modifier.
>> - **Rank 9 — Ironwood Skin** Barkskin lasts until the end of your following turn.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** The target also gains Resistance to physical damage equal to your Constitution modifier while Barkskin is active.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** When Barkskin prevents or reduces damage, the target gains temporary Hit Points equal to your Constitution modifier.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** You may target yourself and one ally within range with the same use of Barkskin.
