# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Utility ✦ Utility / Passive  

**PSIONIC  MIND  PROTECTION**

**Eligible Essences:** Armor, Bear, Earth, Iron, Resolute, Shield, Stone, Turtle

**Slot:** 2 – Utility / Passive  
**Tier:** Conditional

You sheath yourself in disciplined psychic force as an enemy's first assault reveals its intent.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Mind

**Action Type:** Free Action  
**Range:** Personal  
**Cost:** None

**Trigger:** The first time an enemy's attack hits or misses you during an encounter.

> [!Effect|background-color-blue]
> Until the end of your next turn, you gain resistance to all damage equal to 2 + your highest mental ability modifier.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Hardened Will** Increase the resistance to 4 + your highest mental ability modifier.
>> - **Rank 6 — Steadfast Defense** When this power triggers, you also gain temporary Hit Points equal to your highest mental ability modifier.
>> - **Rank 9 — Unbreakable Focus** Increase the resistance to 6 + your highest mental ability modifier.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** While this resistance is active, you cannot be forcibly moved unless the effect's source succeeds at a Power Rank check against you.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** The first time each round an enemy damages you while this resistance is active, that enemy takes psychic damage equal to your highest mental ability modifier.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** When this power triggers, you also gain temporary Hit Points equal to twice your highest mental ability modifier.
