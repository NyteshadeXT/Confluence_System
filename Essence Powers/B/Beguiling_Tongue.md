# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Utility ✦ Social Influence

**ENCOUNTER ARCANE**

**Eligible Essences:** Light, Eye, Oath, Zeal, Revelation

**Slot:** 2 – Utility / Passive  
**Tier:** Encounter

You channel otherworldly charm and dangerous eloquence through your voice.

**Action Type:** Minor Action  
**Range:** Personal

> [!Effect|background-color-blue]
> You gain a +5 power bonus to your next Bluff, Diplomacy, or Intimidate check during this encounter.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Silvered Words** Increase the bonus to +6.
>> - **Rank 6 — Compelling Presence** If the check succeeds, you gain a +2 power bonus to your next Charisma-based skill check before the end of your next turn.
>> - **Rank 9 — Irresistible Conviction** If you fail the empowered check, you may reroll it. You must use the second result. (Usable once per activation.)
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Bonus increases to +8.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** If you succeed by 5 or more, you may also shift the target’s attitude one additional step (GM adjudicated).
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Once during the encounter, you may treat a Charisma-based skill check as though you rolled a 15 on the die before modifiers.
