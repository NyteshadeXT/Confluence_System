# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`

Attack ✦ Apex Ability

**Daily Martial Weapon Reliable**

**Eligible Essences:** Bludgeon, Might, Steel, Bear, Resolute, Zeal  
**Slot:** 5 – Apex Ability  
**Tier:** Daily

You commit to a single, devastating blow, shattering defenses through sheer force and certainty.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Escalating
**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Attack Type and Range:** Melee weapon
**Target:** One creature
**Cost:** 2 Stamina

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Power Rank vs. AC

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 3[W] + your Essence ability modifier damage.

> [!Miss|background-color-yellow]
> - Half damage (round down). If this power misses, it is not expended.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Shattering Impact** If the target is bloodied, add your Constitution modifier to the damage.
>> - **Rank 6 — Crushing Commitment** Increase the damage to 4[W] + ability modifier.
>> - **Rank 9 — Unstoppable Force** If the attack hits, you may push the target 1 square and knock it prone.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Damage becomes 5[W] + ability modifier.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** Dam If the target is marked by you, increase damage by your Wisdom modifier.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** On a hit, the target grants combat advantage until the end of your next turn.
