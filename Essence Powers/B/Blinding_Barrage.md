# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
**Attack** **✦ Apex Ability**

**Daily Martial Weapon Control**

**Eligible Essences:** Ranged, Fox, Adept, Swift, Light

**Slot:** 5 – Apex Ability  
**Tier:** Daily

A storm of precise strikes lashes out in every direction, leaving foes clutching at ruined sight and shattered focus.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Escalating
**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Attack Type and Range:** Close blast 3
**Requirement:** You must be wielding a crossbow, a light thrown weapon, or a sling.
**Target:** Each enemy in the blast you can see
**Cost:** 2 Stamina

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Power Rank vs. AC (Dexterity)

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 2[W] + your Dexterity modifier damage, and the target is **blinded** until the end of your next turn.

> [!Miss|background-color-yellow]
> - Half damage (round down).   The target is **not blinded**.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Sharpened Volley** Increase the damage to 3[W] + Dexterity modifier.
>> - **Rank 6 — Relentless Precision** If at least two enemies are blinded by this power, you may shift 2 squares.
>> - **Rank 9 — Disorienting Storm** Creatures blinded by this power take a –2 penalty to attack rolls until the end of your next turn.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Blind duration becomes “until the end of your next turn; if the target is bloodied, it is also slowed until the end of its next turn.”
>> - **Silver / Epic:** If a blinded target misses an attack, it grants combat advantage until the end of its next turn.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** The first time each blinded creature takes damage from you before the end of your next turn, it takes additional damage equal to your Dexterity modifier.
