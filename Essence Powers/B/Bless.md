# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Utility ✦ Sacred Benediction

**DAILY DIVINE**

**Eligible Essences:** Light, Zeal, Oath, Balance, Harmony

**Slot:** 2 – Utility / Passive  
**Tier:** Daily

You call down a steady blessing that guides every strike.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Neutral
**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Range:** Close burst 20
**Cost:** 1 Mana
**Targets:** You and each ally in burst

> [!Effect|background-color-blue]
> Until the end of the encounter, all targets gain a +1 power bonus to attack rolls.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Strengthened Benediction** Increase the bonus to +2 for allies within 5 squares of you. (Those outside 5 squares remain at +1.)
>> - **Rank 6 — Guided Strike** Once per round, when an ally benefiting from this power misses with an attack, they may reroll the attack roll. They must use the second result. (Only one ally per round.)
>> -  **Rank 9 — Radiant Unity** When an ally benefiting from this power scores a critical hit, one ally within 5 squares gains temporary hit points equal to your Wisdom modifier.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** The bonus increases to +2.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** Allies within 5 squares also gain +1 to saving throws.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** The first time each ally hits during the encounter, they deal additional radiant damage equal to your Charisma modifier.
