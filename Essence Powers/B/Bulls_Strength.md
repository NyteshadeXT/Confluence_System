# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Attack ✦ Core Concept  

**PSIONIC • WEAPON**

**Eligible Essences:** Ape, Bludgeon, Earth, Gravity, Might, Momentum, Stone

**Slot:** 1 – Core Concept
**Tier:** At-Will

You drive your weapon forward with a surge of impossible strength, batting your foe aside.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Gravity

**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Range:** Melee weapon  
**Cost:** None

**Target:** One creature

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Power Rank vs. AC

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 1[W] + your Essence ability modifier damage, and you push the target 1 square.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Long-Arm Blow** Your reach increases by 1 for this attack.
>> - **Rank 6 — Driving Strength** You push the target up to 2 squares instead.
>> - **Rank 9 — Sweeping Strength** You may target up to two creatures within your reach, making a separate attack roll against each.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** A creature you push with this power is off-guard until the start of your next turn.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** You may push each target up to 3 squares.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** If you push a target into an obstacle or another creature, it takes extra damage equal to your highest physical ability modifier.
