# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Utility ✦ Arcane Enhancement

**ENCOUNTER ARCANE**

**Eligible Essences:** Wind, Momentum, Balance, Storm

**Slot:** 2 – Utility / Passive  
**Tier:** Encounter

A surge of arcane force carries you beyond mortal limits.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Stable
**Action Type:** Move Action  
**Range:** Ranged 10
**Cost:** 1 Mana
**Target:** You or one creature

> [!Effect|background-color-blue]
> The target makes an Athletics check to jump with a +10 power bonus and does not need to move to make a running jump.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Empowered Launch** Increase the power bonus to the Athletics check to +12.
>> - **Rank 6 — Aerial Grace** The target does not provoke opportunity attacks during the jump.
>> - **Rank 9 — Arcane Descent** The target takes no falling damage from this jump and does not fall prone if it lands safely.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> -  **Bronze / Paragon:** The power bonus becomes +15.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** If the jump ends adjacent to an enemy, the target gains a +2 power bonus to its next attack roll before the end of its next turn.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** The jump may be treated as teleportation for the purpose of ignoring terrain and obstacles during the movement.
