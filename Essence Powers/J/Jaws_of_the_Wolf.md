# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Attack ✦ Apex Ability

**Daily Martial Weapon Multiattack**

**Eligible Essences:** Wolf, Momentum, Blade, Hunt  

**Slot:** 5 – Apex Ability  
**Tier:** Daily

You pressure your enemy from multiple angles, forcing an opening through relentless, coordinated strikes.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Provocative
**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Range:** Melee weapon
**Requirement:** You must be wielding two melee weapons.
**Target:** One creature
**Cost:** 2 Stamina

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Essence Attack vs. AC (main-hand and off-hand weapon). Make two attacks.

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 2[W] + your Essence ability modifier damage per attack.

> [!Miss|background-color-yellow]
> - Half damage per attack (round down).

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Relentless Pairing** Increase the damage to 3[W] + Essence ability modifier per attack.
>> - **Rank 6 — Pack Assault** If both attacks hit, the target is knocked prone.
>> - **Rank 9 — Blood in the Water** If both attacks hit a bloodied target, it takes additional damage equal to your Essence ability modifier.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Damage becomes 4[W] + Essence ability modifier per attack.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** Damage becomes 5[W] + Essence ability modifier per attack. If both attacks hit, the target is slowed (save ends).
>> -  **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Damage becomes 6[W] + Essence ability modifier per attack. If both attacks hit and the target fails its first saving throw against this power’s effects, it is dazed until the end of its next turn.
