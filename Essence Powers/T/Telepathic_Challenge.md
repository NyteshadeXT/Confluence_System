# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Utility ✦ Utility / Passive  

**PSIONIC • MIND • CONTROL**

**Eligible Essences:** Dominion, Mind, Oath, Order, Subjugation, Suppression

**Slot:** 2 – Utility / Passive
**Tier:** Encounter

You loom large in your enemies' minds, demanding their attention with an inescapable psychic challenge.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Mind

**Action Type:** Minor Action  
**Range:** Close burst 2  
**Cost:** 1 Mana

**Target:** Each enemy in the burst

> [!Effect|background-color-blue]
> - You mark each target until the end of your next turn. A creature marked by you takes a –2 penalty to attack rolls for attacks that do not include you as a target.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Expansive Challenge** Increase the burst to 3.
>> - **Rank 6 — Pressing Presence** The penalty from your mark increases to –3.
>> - **Rank 9 — Inescapable Demand** The mark lasts until the end of the encounter or until you mark another creature with this power.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** A creature marked by this power is off-guard until the start of your next turn.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** The first time a creature marked by this power damages an ally before the mark ends, it takes psychic damage equal to your highest mental ability modifier.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** You may use this power once per encounter without paying its Mana cost.
