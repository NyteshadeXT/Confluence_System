# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Utility ✦ Mobility

**Encounter Martial**

**Eligible Essences:** Swift, Cat, Wind  

**Slot:** 2 – Utility / Passive  
**Tier:** Encounter

You slip past danger in a blur of motion, denying your enemies a clean strike.

**Action Type:** Move Action  
**Range:** Personal
**Prerequisite:** You must be trained in Acrobatics.

> [!Effect|background-color-blue]
> You shift a number of squares equal to one-half your speed (rounded down).

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Fluid Motion** Increase the shift distance to one-half your speed + 1 square.
>> - **Rank 6 — Untouchable Flow** During this movement, you gain a +2 power bonus to AC against opportunity attacks.
>> - **Rank 9 — Blurred Passage** After completing the shift, you gain concealment until the start of your next turn.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** You may shift one-half your speed (rounded up).
>> - **Silver / Epic:** You may shift up to your speed.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** You may shift up to your speed and ignore difficult terrain during the movement.
