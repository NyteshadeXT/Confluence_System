# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Attack ✦ Apex Ability

**Daily Martial Weapon Positioning**

**Eligible Essences:** Fox, Cat, Adept, Blade  

**Slot:** 5 – Apex Ability  
**Tier:** Daily

Through misdirection and relentless pressure, you turn your foe into a piece on the board—one you keep moving.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Provocative. This power always draws Dungeon attention.
**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Range:** Melee or Ranged weapon
**Requirement:** You must be wielding a crossbow, a light blade, or a sling.
**Target:** One creature
**Cost:** 2 Stamina

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Power Rank vs. AC (Dexterity)

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 3[W] + your Dexterity modifier damage, and you slide the target 1 square. Until the end of the encounter, each time you hit the target, you may slide it 1 square.

> [!Miss|background-color-yellow]
> - Half damage (round down).  The persistent slide effect does not apply.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Sharpened Misdirection** Increase the initial damage to 4[W] + your Dexterity modifier.
>> - **Rank 6 — Extended Control** Increase the initial slide to 2 squares.
>> - **Rank 9 — Master Manipulator** When you slide the target using the persistent effect, you may also shift 1 square.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Initial damage increases to 5[W] + Dexterity modifier.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** Initial damage increases to 6[W] + Dexterity modifier. The persistent slide increases to 2 squares per hit.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Initial damage increases to 7[W] + Dexterity modifier. Once per round, when you slide the target with the persistent effect, it grants combat advantage until the end of your next turn.
