# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`

Attack ✦ Verdant Control

**ENCOUNTER PLANT ZONE**

**Eligible Essences:** Plant, Tree, Growth, Trap

**Slot:** 3 – Signature Strike
**Tier:** Encounter

Roots and grasping vines erupt across the battlefield, striking nearby creatures and leaving the ground tangled beneath them.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Stable
**Action Type:** Standard Action
**Range:** Area burst 1 within 10 squares
**Cost:** 1 Mana
**Target:** Each creature in the burst

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> Power Attack vs. Reflex

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 1d8 + Wisdom modifier damage. Each square adjacent to a creature hit by Twisting Vines becomes Difficult Terrain until the end of your next turn. Overlapping affected squares remain a single area of Difficult Terrain.

> [!Miss|background-color-yellow]
> - No damage for that attack.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Spreading Roots** The square occupied by each target you hit also becomes Difficult Terrain.
>> - **Rank 6 — Grasping Undergrowth** A creature hit by Twisting Vines is Slowed while it remains within Difficult Terrain created by this power.
>> - **Rank 9 — Choking Growth** Difficult Terrain created by Twisting Vines lasts until the end of your following turn.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Increase the damage to 2d8 + Wisdom modifier.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** The affected terrain becomes a zone. Enemies that enter the zone or begin their turn there are Slowed until the end of that turn.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Increase the damage to 3d8 + Wisdom modifier, and increase the initial burst to Area burst 2 within 10 squares.
