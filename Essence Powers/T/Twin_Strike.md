# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Attack ✦ Core Concept

**At-Will Martial Weapon Multiattack**

**Eligible Essences:** Wolf, Hunt, Momentum, Blade, Ranged  

**Slot:** 1 – Core Concept  
**Tier:** At-Will

You attack twice in rapid succession, overwhelming your foe through relentless pressure.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Neutral
**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Range:** Melee or Ranged weapon
**Requirement:** You must be wielding either two melee weapons or a ranged weapon.
**Targets:** One or two creatures

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> Make two attacks, each against the same target or different targets.
> - Melee: Essence Attack vs. AC (main weapon and off-hand weapon) 
> - Ranged: Essence Attack vs. AC (two attacks)

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 1[W] damage per attack.

> [!Miss|background-color-yellow]
> - No damage on a miss for that attack.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Relentless Pairing** Add your Essence ability modifier to the damage of one of the two attacks (your choice after rolling).
>> - **Rank 6 — Focused Assault** If both attacks hit the same target, that target takes additional damage equal to your Essence ability modifier.
>> - **Rank 9 — Predatory Rhythm** If you hit two different creatures, you gain a +2 power bonus to AC until the start of your next turn.

>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Each hit deals 1[W] + Essence ability modifier damage.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** Each hit deals 2[W] + Essence ability modifier damage.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Each hit deals 3[W] + Essence ability modifier damage.  If both attacks hit the same target, it grants combat advantage until the end of your next turn.
