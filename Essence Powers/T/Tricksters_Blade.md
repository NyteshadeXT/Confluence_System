# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Attack ✦ Signature Strike

**ENCOUNTER MARTIAL WEAPON**

**Eligible Essences:** Blade, Fox, Shadow, Eye, Swift, Ambush

**Slot:** 3 – Signature Strike  
**Tier:** Encounter

You strike true, then blur into motion and feints that leave your enemies guessing and off-balance.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Neutral
**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Range:** Melee or Ranged weapon  
**Cost:** 1 Stamina
**Requirement:** You must be wielding a crossbow, a light blade, or a sling.
**Target:** One creature

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Power Rank vs. AC (Dexterity-based)

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 2[W] + your Essence ability modifier damage. You gain a bonus to AC equal to your Charisma modifier until the start of your next turn.

> [!Miss|background-color-yellow]
> - Half damage. You gain a +1 bonus to AC until the start of your next turn.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Defensive Flourish** Increase the AC bonus by +1.
>> - **Rank 6 — Elusive Movement** While the AC bonus is active, you gain a +1 bonus to Reflex.
>> - **Rank 9 — Untouchable Trickster** If an attack misses you while this effect is active, you may shift 1 square.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Damage increases to 3[W] + ability modifier.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** Damage increases to 4[W] + ability modifier. AC bonus applies against all attacks, including area effects.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Damage increases to 5[W] + ability modifier. If you are not bloodied, increase the AC bonus by your Dexterity modifier.
