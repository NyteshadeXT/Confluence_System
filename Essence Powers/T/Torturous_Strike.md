# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Attack ✦ Signature Strike

**Encounter Martial Weapon Pain**

**Eligible Essences:** Blade, Snake, Might, Adept, Blood  

**Slot:** 3 – Signature Strike  
**Tier:** Encounter

You twist the blade just enough to turn pain into something far worse.

**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Range:** Melee weapon
**Requirement:** You must be wielding a light blade.
**Target:** One creature
**Cost:** 1 Stamina

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Power Rank vs. AC (Dexterity)

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 2[W] + your Dexterity modifier damage.

> [!Miss|background-color-yellow]
> - Half damage (round down).

**Attack:** 
> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Agonizing Twist** Increase the weapon damage to 3[W] + your Dexterity modifier.
>> - **Rank 6 — Deep Wound** If you have combat advantage against the target, it takes additional damage equal to your secondary ability modifier.
>> - **Rank 9 — Lingering Torment** If the target is bloodied when hit, it takes ongoing damage equal to your secondary ability modifier (save ends).
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Damage increases to 4[W] + Dexterity modifier.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** Damage increases to 5[W] + Dexterity modifier. If the target is bloodied and hit, it takes a –2 penalty to attack rolls until the end of its next turn.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Damage increases to 6[W] + Dexterity modifier. If you have combat advantage and hit, the target is weakened until the end of your next turn.
