# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`

Attack ✦ Verdant Control

**AT-WILL PLANT**

**Eligible Essences:** Plant, Tree, Growth, Hunt, Trap

**Slot:** 1 – Core Concept  
**Tier:** At-Will

A length of barbed vegetation erupts into being, lashing around your prey before contracting and dragging it toward you.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Stable  
**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Range:** Ranged 10  
**Cost:** None  
**Target:** One creature

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Power Attack vs. Fortitude

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 1d8 + Wisdom modifier damage, and you pull the target 2 squares.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Clinging Thorns** Increase the pull to 3 squares.
>> - **Rank 6 — Barbed Snare** If the target ends the pull adjacent to you, it is Slowed until the end of your next turn.
>> - **Rank 9 — Relentless Vine** Increase the pull to 4 squares. You may choose to pull the target fewer squares.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Increase the damage to 2d8 + Wisdom modifier.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** If the target ends the pull adjacent to you, you may Immobilize it instead of Slowing it until the end of your next turn.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Increase the damage to 3d8 + Wisdom modifier. After pulling the target, you may shift it 1 square.
