# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Attack ✦ Core Concept  

**PSIONIC • WEAPON**

**Eligible Essences:** Discord, Dream, Eye, Mind, Shadow, Shimmer, Veil

**Slot:** 1 – Core Concept
**Tier:** At-Will

You flood your foe's mind with distorted images, making every nearby threat difficult to judge.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Mind

**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Range:** Melee weapon  
**Cost:** None

**Target:** One creature

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Power Rank vs. AC

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 1[W] + your Essence ability modifier damage. Until the end of your next turn, the target takes a penalty to attack rolls equal to the number of your allies adjacent to it (maximum –3).

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Warped Perception** The penalty's maximum increases to –4.
>> - **Rank 6 — Reflexive Distortion** You may use this power in place of a melee basic attack when making an opportunity attack.
>> - **Rank 9 — Blinding Torrent** If at least two of your allies are adjacent to the target, it is blinded until the start of its next turn. A creature can be blinded by this expression only once per encounter.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** The target is also off-guard while affected by the attack penalty.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** The penalty applies even if no allies are adjacent to the target; in that case, it is –1.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** On a critical hit, the target is blinded until the end of its next turn instead.
