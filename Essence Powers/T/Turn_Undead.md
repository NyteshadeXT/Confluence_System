# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Attack ✦ Channel Divinity

**ENCOUNTER DIVINE RADIANT IMPLEMENT**

**Eligible Essences:** Light, Oath, Balance, Life  

**Slot:** 3 – Signature Strike  
**Tier:** Encounter

Radiant judgment sears the restless dead.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Stable
**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Range:** Close burst 2
**Cost:** 1 Mana
**Target:** Each undead creature in burst

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Wisdom vs. Will

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 1d10 + Wisdom modifier radiant damage. You push the target a number of squares equal to 3 + your Charisma modifier. The target is immobilized until the end of your next turn.

> [!Miss|background-color-yellow]
> - Half damage. The target is not pushed or immobilized.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Blazing Rebuke** Increase the damage to 2d10 + your Wisdom modifier.
>> - **Rank 6 — Overwhelming Radiance** Increase the push distance to 4 + your Charisma modifier.
>> - **Rank 9 — Lingering Condemnation** If an undead target is bloodied when hit, it is also weakened until the end of your next turn.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Damage increases to 3d10 + Wisdom modifier.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** Damage increases to 4d10 + Wisdom modifier. Undead hit by this power are slowed (save ends) after the immobilization ends.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Damage increases to 5d10 + Wisdom modifier. If an undead target fails its first saving throw against an effect imposed by this power, it is stunned until the end of its next turn.
