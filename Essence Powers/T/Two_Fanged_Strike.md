# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Attack ✦ Signature Strike

**Encounter Martial Weapon Multiattack**

**Eligible Essences:** Wolf, Hunt, Momentum, Blade, Ranged  

**Slot:** 3 – Signature Strike  
**Tier:** Encounter

You strike twice in rapid succession, driving both blades—or both shots—into the same vulnerable opening.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Neutral
**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Range:** Melee or Ranged weapon
**Requirement:** You must be wielding either two melee weapons or a ranged weapon.
**Target:** One creature
**Cost:** Cost: 1 Stamina

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> Make two attacks against the target.
> - Melee: Essence Attack vs. AC (main-hand and off-hand weapon)  
> - Ranged: Essence Attack vs. AC (two attacks)

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 1[W] + your Essence ability modifier damage per attack.  If both attacks hit, deal extra damage equal to your Wisdom modifier.

> [!Miss|background-color-yellow]
> - No damage for that attack.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Driving Pair** Increase the damage per hit to 2[W] + your Essence ability modifier.
>> - **Rank 6 — Coordinated Pressure** If both attacks hit, increase the extra damage to your Wisdom modifier + your Essence ability modifier.
>> - **Rank 9 — Hunter’s Lock** If both attacks hit, the target is slowed until the end of your next turn.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Damage per hit increases to 3[W] + Essence ability modifier.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** Damage per hit increases to 4[W] + Essence ability modifier. If both attacks hit, the target grants combat advantage until the end of your next turn.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Damage per hit increases to 5[W] + Essence ability modifier. If both attacks hit a bloodied target, it is weakened until the end of your next turn.
