# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Attack ✦ Tactical Strike  

**ENCOUNTER MARTIAL WEAPON**

**Eligible Essences:** Ape, Bear, Might, Momentum, Force, Hunt

**Slot:** 4 – Tactical Strike  
**Tier:** Encounter

You charge through your enemies with unstoppable force, battering them backward with each blow.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Escalating (forced movement chains draw attention)
**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Range:** Melee or Ranged weapon  
**Cost:** 1 Stamina
**Requirement:** You must be wielding two melee weapons or a ranged weapon.
**Targets:** One or two creatures

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Essence Attack vs. AC (two attacks). 

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 1[W] + your Essence ability modifier damage per hit. With each hit, you push the target 1 square. If both attacks hit the same target, you instead push it 1 + your Wisdom modifier squares.

> [!Miss|background-color-yellow]
> - No push.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Driving Assault** Increase the push distance by 1 square on each hit.
>> - **Rank 6 — Disruptive Charge** If both attacks hit the same target, it takes a –2 penalty to attack rolls until the end of its next turn.
>> - **Rank 9 — Overrun Force** If the target collides with a creature or obstacle, it takes additional damage equal to your Strength modifier.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Damage increases to 2[W] + ability modifier per hit.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** Damage increases to 3[W] + ability modifier per hit. If both attacks hit the same target, it is knocked prone.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Damage increases to 4[W] + ability modifier per hit. If the target is bloodied and both attacks hit, it is also dazed until the end of your next turn.
