# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Attack ✦ Core Concept

**AT-WILL ARCANE THUNDER IMPLEMENT**

**Eligible Essences:** Sound, Force, Wind, Reckoning, Balance, Storm  

**Slot:** 1 – Core Concept  
**Tier:** At-Will

A concussive wave explodes outward, scattering your foes.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Stable
**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Range:** Close blast 3
**Target:** Each creature in blast

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Power Rank vs. Fortitude

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 1d6 + Intelligence modifier thunder damage. You push the target a number of squares equal to your Wisdom modifier.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Resonant Force** Increase the damage to 1d8 + your Intelligence modifier.
>> - **Rank 6 — Concussive Impact** Creatures pushed by this power take additional thunder damage equal to your Wisdom modifier.
>> - **Rank 9 — Shattering Wave** If a target is pushed 2 or more squares, it takes a –2 penalty to attack rolls until the end of its next turn.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Damage increases to 2d6 + Intelligence modifier.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** Damage increases to 3d6 + Intelligence modifier. Creatures pushed by this power are also knocked prone if they collide with a solid surface.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Damage increases to 4d6 + Intelligence modifier. If three or more creatures are hit, you may slide one creature an additional number of squares equal to your Wisdom modifier (once per use).
