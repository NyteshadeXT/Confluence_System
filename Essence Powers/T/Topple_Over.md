# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Attack ✦ Tactical Strike

**ENCOUNTER MARTIAL WEAPON**

**Eligible Essences:** Blade, Cat, Swift, Momentum, Snake, Balance, Storm

**Slot:** 4 – Tactical Strike  
**Tier:** Encounter

You lunge with perfect timing, using your opponent’s movement against them to send them crashing to the ground.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Neutral
**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Range:** Melee weapon  
**Cost:** 1 Stamina
**Requirement:**  You must be wielding a light blade.
**Target:** One creature

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Power Rank vs. AC (Dexterity-based)

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 1[W] + your Essence ability modifier damage, and the target is knocked prone.

> [!Miss|background-color-yellow]
> - No prone. You may shift 1 square.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Leveraged Motion** If the target is already off-balance (slowed or flanked), gain a +1 bonus to the attack roll.
>> - **Rank 6 — Disruptive Fall** The target takes a –2 penalty to attack rolls while prone from this power.
>> - **Rank 9 — Opportunistic Collapse** If the target stands before the end of your next turn, it provokes a reaction from you.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Damage increases to 2[W] + ability modifier.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** Damage increases to 3[W] + ability modifier. Prone target also grants combat advantage until the end of your next turn.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Damage increases to 4[W] + ability modifier. If the target is bloodied, it is also slowed after being knocked prone.
