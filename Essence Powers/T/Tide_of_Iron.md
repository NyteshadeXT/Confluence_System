# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Attack ✦ Core Concept

**At-Will Martial Weapon**

**Eligible Essences:** Shield, Order, Might

**Slot:** 1 – Core Concept  
**Tier:** At-Will

You drive your foe backward with steel and shield, claiming the ground they abandon.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Neutral
**Action Type:** Standard Action
**Range:** Melee weapon
**Requirement:** You must be wielding a shield.
**Target:** One creature

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Essence Attack vs. AC

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> -1[W] + Strength modifier damage, and you push the target 1 square if it is your size, smaller, or one size larger. You may shift into the space the target vacated.


> [!Miss|background-color-yellow]
> - No damage.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Driving Advance** Increase the damage to 1[W] + Strength modifier + 2.
>> - **Rank 6 — Press the Line** Increase the push distance to 2 squares.
>> - **Rank 9 — Hold the Ground** If you shift into the vacated space, you gain a +2 power bonus to AC against the target’s next attack before the end of its next turn.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Damage increases to 2[W] + Strength modifier.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** Damage increases to 3[W] + Strength modifier. If the target is bloodied, it is slowed until the end of your next turn.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Damage increases to 4[W] + Strength modifier. If the target cannot be pushed, it instead takes additional damage equal to your Strength modifier.
