# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Attack ✦ Tactical Strike  

**ENCOUNTER MARTIAL WEAPON PRECISION**

**Eligible Essences:** Blade, Adept, Hunt, Eye, Steel, Focus

**Slot:** 4 – Tactical Strike  
**Tier:** Encounter

You sacrifice force for certainty, committing fully to a strike that must land.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Neutral
**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Range:** Melee weapon  
**Cost:** 1 Stamina
**Requirement:** You must be wielding a melee weapon.
**Target:** One creature

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Essence Attack +4 vs. AC

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 1[W] + your Essence ability modifier damage.

> [!Miss|background-color-yellow]
> - No damage.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Disciplined Aim** Increase the attack bonus to +5.
>> - **Rank 6 — Near-Certain Contact** If the attack misses by 2 or less, deal damage equal to your Strength modifier.
>> - **Rank 9 — Surgical Precision** On a hit, the target takes a –2 penalty to AC against the next attack made against it before the end of your next turn.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Damage increases to 2[W] + ability modifier. Attack bonus remains +5.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** Damage increases to 3[W] + ability modifier. If you hit, you gain a +2 bonus to your next attack roll before the end of your next turn.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Damage increases to 4[W] + ability modifier. This attack cannot be reduced below a hit by reactions or penalties.
