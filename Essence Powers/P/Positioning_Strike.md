# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Attack ✦ Tactical Strike

**Encounter Martial Weapon Control**

**Eligible Essences:** Fox, Cat, Snake, Adept, Order  

**Slot:** 4 – Tactical Strike  
**Tier:** Encounter

A feint, a shove, and a flash of steel put your enemy exactly where you want them.

**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Range:** Melee weapon
**Requirement:** You must be wielding a light blade.
**Target:** One creature
**Cost:** 1 Stamina

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Essence Attack vs. Will (Dexterity)

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 1[W] + your Dexterity modifier damage, and you slide the target 1 square. 

> [!Miss|background-color-yellow]
> - Half damage (round down).

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Tactical Precision** Increase the weapon damage to 2[W] + your Dexterity modifier.
>> - **Rank 6 — Guided Placement** If the target ends the slide adjacent to one of your allies, that ally gains a +2 power bonus to their next attack roll against the target before the end of your next turn.
>> - **Rank 9 — Artful Control** After sliding the target, you may shift 1 square.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Damage increases to 3[W] + Dexterity modifier. Base slide distance increases by 1 square.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** Damage increases to 4[W] + Dexterity modifier. If the target is slid into difficult terrain or adjacent to an ally, it is slowed until the end of its next turn.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Damage increases to 5[W] + Dexterity modifier. If the slide moves the target at least 2 squares, it grants combat advantage until the end of your next turn.
