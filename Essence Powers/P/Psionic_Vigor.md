# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Utility ✦ Utility / Passive  

**PSIONIC • VITALITY**

**Eligible Essences:** Flesh, Life, Lizard, Renewal, Serene, Soul, Water

**Slot:** 2 – Utility / Passive
**Tier:** Encounter

You focus your mind, converting determination into a protective reserve of psionic vitality.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Soul

**Action Type:** Minor Action  
**Range:** Personal  
**Cost:** 1 Mana

> [!Effect|background-color-blue]
> - You gain temporary Hit Points equal to 4 + your highest mental ability modifier.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Enduring Vigor** Increase the temporary Hit Points to 6 + your highest mental ability modifier.
>> - **Rank 6 — Focused Recovery** If you are bloodied when you use this power, you also end one fear or mental effect affecting you.
>> - **Rank 9 — Overflowing Vigor** Increase the temporary Hit Points to 8 + your highest mental ability modifier.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** The temporary Hit Points last until the end of the encounter.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** When the temporary Hit Points are depleted, you may Shift 1 square as a free action.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** You may use this power twice per encounter.
