# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`

Attack ✦ Predatory Assault

**AT-WILL BEAST**

**Eligible Essences:** Cat, Wolf, Fox, Dog, Bear, Ape, Hunt, Pursuit, 

**Slot:** 1 – Core Concept  
**Tier:** At-Will

You spring upon your prey with sudden bestial ferocity, striking before it can recover from your assault.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Stable  
**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Range:** Melee touch  
**Cost:** None  
**Target:** One creature

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Power Rank vs. Reflex

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 1d8 + Wisdom modifier damage. The target grants Combat Advantage to the next creature that attacks it before the end of your next turn.
>
> If your current form does not possess appropriate natural weapons, they manifest as part of the attack and disappear immediately afterward. You may use Pounce in place of a Melee Basic Attack when charging.

> [!Miss|background-color-yellow]
> - No damage.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Sudden Pounce** When you charge using Pounce, you may shift 1 square before beginning the charge.
>> - **Rank 6 — Predatory Opening** The creature that consumes the Combat Advantage granted by Pounce gains a +1 power bonus to the attack roll.
>> - **Rank 9 — Relentless Pounce** After charging with Pounce, you may shift 1 square after resolving the attack.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Increase the damage to **2d8 + Wisdom modifier**.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** On a hit during a charge, the target grants Combat Advantage to all creatures until the end of your next turn.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Increase the damage to **3d8 + Wisdom modifier**. After a successful charge, you may shift up to 2 squares.
