# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Attack ✦ Apex Ability

**DAILY MARTIAL WEAPON**

Eligible Essences: Hunt, Oath, Stone, Momentum, Cage  

**Slot:** 5 – Apex Ability  
**Tier:** Daily

No escape. No retreat. Only reckoning.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Escalating
**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Range:** Melee weapon
**Cost:** 2 Stamina
**Target:** One creature

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Strength vs. AC

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 3[W] + Strength modifier damage. Until the end of the encounter, the target cannot shift while at least two of your allies (or you and one ally) are adjacent to it.

> [!Miss|background-color-yellow]
> - Half damage to the primary target and ongoing 2 acid damage (save ends). No secondary attack.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Crushing Lock** Increase the weapon damage to 4[W] + your Strength modifier.
>> - **Rank 6 — Anchored Prey** While the condition is active, the target takes a –2 penalty to attack rolls against creatures other than you.
>> - **Rank 9 — Iron Circle** If the target attempts to move (other than shifting) while at least two of your allies are adjacent to it, it takes damage equal to your Strength modifier (once per round).
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Damage increases to 5[W] + Strength modifier. While the condition is active, the target is slowed if at least two allies remain adjacent.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** Damage increases to 6[W] + Strength modifier. If the adjacency condition is met, the target also grants combat advantage.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Damage increases to 7[W] + Strength modifier. If the target fails two saving throws against effects imposed by you while pinned, it is immobilized until the end of its next turn.
