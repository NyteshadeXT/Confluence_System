# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Attack ✦ Core Concept

**At-Will Martial Weapon Precision**

**Eligible Essences:** Blade, Snake, Adept, Hunt  

**Slot:** 1 – Core Concept  
**Tier:** At-Will

You slip your blade through gaps in armor, striking where protection is weakest.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Neutral
**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Range:** Melee weapon
**Requirement:** You must be wielding a light blade.
**Target:** One creature

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Power Rank vs. Reflex (Dexterity)

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 1[W] + your Dexterity modifier damage.

> [!Miss|background-color-yellow]
> - No damage.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Needlepoint Accuracy** Increase the damage to 1[W] + your Dexterity modifier + 2.
>> - **Rank 6 — Exploit Weakness** If you have combat advantage against the target, this attack deals additional damage equal to your Dexterity modifier.
>> - **Rank 9 — Precise Entry** If the attack hits, the target takes a –2 penalty to AC against your next attack before the end of your next turn.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Damage increases to 2[W] + Dexterity modifier.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** Damage increases to 3[W] + Dexterity modifier.  If you have combat advantage, this power ignores 2 points of resistance.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Damage increases to 4[W] + Dexterity modifier. If the target is bloodied and you have combat advantage, it takes additional damage equal to your Dexterity modifier (once per turn).
