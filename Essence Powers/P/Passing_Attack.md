# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Attack ✦ Signature Strike

**Encounter Martial Weapon**

**Eligible Essences:** Blade, Momentum, Swift, Hunt, Polearm  

**Slot:** 3 – Signature Strike  
**Tier:** Encounter

You strike through one foe and carry your momentum forward, flowing seamlessly into a second attack without breaking stride.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Neutral
**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Range:** Melee weapon
**Primary Target:** One creature
**Cost:** Cost: 1 Stamina

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - **Primary Target** Essence Attack vs. AC
> - **Secondary Target** Essence Attack +2 vs. AC

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 1[W] + your Essence ability modifier damage. You may shift 1 square and make a secondary attack doing 1[W] + your Essence ability modifier damage to the secondary target.

> [!Miss|background-color-yellow]
> - No damage. You may still shift 1 square, but cannot make the secondary attack.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Driving Momentum** Increase the primary and secondary damage to 2[W] + your Essence ability modifier.
>> - **Rank 6 — Relentless Advance** Increase the shift to 2 squares.
>> - **Rank 9 — Unbroken Flow** If the primary attack hits, the secondary attack no longer requires the +2 bonus; instead, it gains a +2 power bonus to the attack roll and deals additional damage equal to your Essence ability modifier on a hit.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Primary and secondary damage become 3[W] + Essence ability modifier.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** Primary and secondary damage become 4[W] + Essence ability modifier. If both attacks hit, the secondary target is knocked prone.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Primary and secondary damage become 5[W] + Essence ability modifier. If both attacks hit and at least one target is bloodied, you may make a melee basic attack against a third creature adjacent to you (once per use).
