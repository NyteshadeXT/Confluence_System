# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Attack ✦ Apex Ability  

**PSIONIC • TELEPORTATION • WEAPON**

**Eligible Essences:** Cage, Chain, Crocodile, Dimension, Gravity, Pursuit, Spider, Suppression

**Slot:** 5 – Apex Ability
**Tier:** Daily

You bind your foe to a psionic anchor, allowing you to drag it back to your side whenever it tries to escape.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Dimension

**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Range:** Melee weapon  
**Cost:** 3 Mana

**Target:** One creature

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Power Rank vs. AC

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 2[W] + your Essence ability modifier damage. The target is anchored until the end of the encounter.

> [!Miss|background-color-yellow]
> - Half damage. The target is anchored until the end of its next turn.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Long Tether** The anchor functions while the target is within 15 squares of you instead of 10 squares.
>> - **Rank 6 — Dimensional Snare** An anchored target cannot teleport unless it first succeeds at a Power Rank check against your Power Rank.
>> - **Rank 9 — Crushing Recall** Whenever you teleport an anchored target with this power, it takes force damage equal to your highest mental ability modifier.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** You may target up to two creatures within your reach, making a separate attack roll against each.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** Whenever you teleport an anchored target, you may also Shift 1 square as a free action.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** An anchored target is slowed while it is more than 3 squares from you.

> [!note] Anchor Recall
> At the end of an anchored target's turn, if it is within 10 squares of you and there is an unoccupied space adjacent to you, you may teleport it to that space as a free action. The target can be teleported this way only once per round.
