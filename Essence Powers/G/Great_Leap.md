# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Utility ✦ Movement

**At-Will Martial**

**Eligible Essences:** Swift, Cat, Might, Wind, Storm

**Slot:** 2 – Utility / Passive  
**Tier:** At-Will

You launch yourself with explosive grace, clearing impossible gaps without hesitation.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Neutral
**Action Type:** Move Action  
**Range:** Personal
**Prerequisite:** You must be trained in Athletics.

> [!Effect|background-color-blue]
> Make a High Jump or Long Jump Athletics check.  Determine the DC as if you had a running start, even if you did not.  The distance you jump can exceed your speed.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Soaring Form** You gain a +2 power bonus to the Athletics check made as part of this power.
>> - **Rank 6 — Unbroken Arc** You ignore difficult terrain during the jump and do not provoke opportunity attacks for leaving a square as part of the jump.
>> - **Rank 9 — Cat’s Landing** You do not fall prone if you fail the jump check by 5 or less. If you succeed on the jump, you may shift 1 square after landing.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** You gain a +5 power bonus to the Athletics check instead of +2.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** If you succeed on the jump, you gain a +2 power bonus to AC and Reflex until the start of your next turn.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Once per turn, if this jump carries you adjacent to an enemy, you may make a melee basic attack as a free action.
