# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Utility ✦ Tactical

**Encounter Martial**

**Eligible Essences:** Order, Shield, Might, Wolf  

**Slot:** 2 – Utility / Passive  
**Tier:** Encounter

You seize the moment, hauling an ally into a position where they can fight beside you.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Neutral
**Action Type:** Move Action  
**Range:** Melee 1
**Target:** One willing adjacent ally.

> [!Effect|background-color-blue]
> Slide the target up to 2 squares to a square adjacent to you.  This movement does not provoke opportunity attacks.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Steady Pull** Increase the slide distance to 3 squares.
>> - **Rank 6 — Shielding Step** After being slid, the ally gains a +2 power bonus to AC against the next attack made against them before the start of your next turn.
>> - **Rank 9 — Coordinated Advance** After the slide, you may shift 1 square as a free action.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Slide distance increases to 4 squares. After being slid, the ally gains a +2 power bonus to their next attack roll before the end of their next turn.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** After being slid, the ally may make a melee basic attack as a free action.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** You may target two willing adjacent allies with this power (resolve each slide separately).
