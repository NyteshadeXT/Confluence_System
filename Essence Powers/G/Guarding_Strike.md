# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Attack ✦ Signature Strike

**ENCOUNTER MARTIAL WEAPON**

**Eligible Essences:** Oath, Shield, Zeal, Stone, Balance  

**Slot:** 3 – Signature Strike  
**Tier:** Encounter

With a disciplined blow, you destabilize your foe and shield an ally from retaliation.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Stable
**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Range:** Melee weapon
**Cost:** 1 Stamina
**Target:** One creature

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Strength vs. AC

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 2[W] + Strength modifier damage. Choose one ally adjacent to you or the target. Until the end of your next turn, that ally gains a +2 power bonus to AC against the target’s attacks.
> 
> If you possess a Zeal or Oath Essence, the bonus to AC equals 1 + your Charisma modifier instead.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Reinforcing Blow** Increase the weapon damage to 3[W] + your Strength modifier.
>> -  **Rank 6 — Anchored Defense** If the target attacks the chosen ally before the end of your next turn, it takes damage equal to your Strength modifier.
>> - **Rank 9 — Steadfast Wall** If the target is bloodied, the chosen ally also gains a +2 power bonus to Fortitude until the end of your next turn.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Damage increases to 4[W] + Strength modifier. The AC bonus remains +2 (or 1 + Charisma modifier if using Presence Expression).
>> - **Silver / Epic:** Damage increases to 5[W] + Strength modifier. If the chosen ally is attacked by the target, they may shift 1 square as an immediate reaction (once per use).
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Damage increases to 6[W] + Strength modifier. If the target misses the chosen ally while this power is active, you may mark the target until the end of your next turn.
