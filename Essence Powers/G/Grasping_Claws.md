# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`

Attack ✦ Bestial Assault

**AT-WILL WILD SHAPE**

**Eligible Essences:** Bear, Cat, Ape, Lizard, Hunt, C

**Slot:** 1 – Core Concept  
**Tier:** At-Will

Your Wild Shape manifests vicious claws that tear into your foe, leaving it unable to escape your next assault.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Stable  
**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Range:** Melee touch  
**Cost:** None  
**Target:** One creature  
**Requirement:** You must be using Wild Shape.

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Power Rank vs. Reflex

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 1d8 + Wisdom modifier damage, and the target is Slowed until the end of your next turn. Grasping Claws can be used as a Melee Basic Attack.
> 
> **Effect:** When you use this power, you manifest the claws or equivalent natural weapons of your Essence for the duration of the attack. If you are using Wild Shape, those weapons are instead part of your current form.

> [!Miss|background-color-yellow]
> - Half damage (round down). If this power misses, it is not expended.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Hooked Claws** If the target attempts to shift while Slowed by Grasping Claws, you may shift 1 square as a Free Action to remain adjacent to it.
>> - **Rank 6 — Rending Pursuit** When you hit a target already Slowed by Grasping Claws, deal additional damage equal to your Wisdom modifier.
>> - **Rank 9 — No Escape** A creature Slowed by Grasping Claws also cannot shift until the end of your next turn.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Increase the damage to 2d8 + Wisdom modifier. While in True Wild Shape, you may shift 1 square before making this attack.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** On a hit, you may choose to Immobilize the target until the end of your next turn instead of Slowing it.>> 
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Increase the damage to 3d8 + Wisdom modifier. A target Immobilized by Grasping Claws grants Combat Advantage to you until the effect ends.
