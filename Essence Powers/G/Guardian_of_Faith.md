# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Attack ✦ Apex Ability

**DAILY DIVINE RADIANT CONJURATION IMPLEMENT**

**Eligible Essences:** Oath, Light, Balance, Life  

**Slot:** 5 – Apex Ability  
**Tier:** Daily

A spectral guardian stands watch in your deity’s name.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Neutral
**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Range:** Ranged 5
**Cost:** 2 Mana

> [!Effect|background-color-blue]
> You conjure a guardian that occupies one square within range. The guardian lasts until the end of the encounter. As a move action, you may move the guardian up to 3 squares. Any creature that ends its turn adjacent to the guardian is subject to an attack:
> 
> **Attack:** Wisdom vs. Fortitude
> **Hit:**  1d8 + Wisdom modifier radiant damage.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Vigilant Sentinel** Increase the guardian’s damage to 2d8 + your Wisdom modifier.
>> - **Rank 6 — Consecrated Ground** Enemies that take damage from the guardian take a –2 penalty to attack rolls until the end of their next turn.
>> - **Rank 9 — Unyielding Watcher** If a creature takes damage from the guardian and is bloodied, it is slowed until the end of its next turn.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Guardian damage becomes 3d8 + Wisdom modifier. The guardian may be moved up to 5 squares as a move action.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** Guardian damage becomes 4d8 + Wisdom modifier. Enemies damaged by the guardian grant combat advantage until the end of their next turn.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Guardian damage becomes 5d8 + Wisdom modifier. If a creature takes damage from the guardian twice in the same encounter, it is immobilized until the end of its next turn.
