# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Utility ✦ Skill

**Encounter Martial**

**Eligible Essences:** Fox, Adept,  Cat

**Slot:** 2 – Utility / Passive  
**Tier:** Encounter

Your hands move faster than thought.

**Action Type:** Minor Action  
**Range:** Personal
**Prerequisite:** You must be trained in Thievery.

> [!Effect|background-color-blue]
> You may make a Thievery check as part of this action, even if the check would normally require a standard action.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Sleight of Hand** You gain a +2 power bonus to the Thievery check made as part of this power.
>> - **Rank 6 — Seamless Theft** If the Thievery check succeeds, you may shift 1 square as a free action.
>> - **Rank 9 — Hidden Motion** If you succeed on the Thievery check, you may immediately make a Stealth check to become hidden (if you have cover or concealment).
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** You may use this power **twice per encounter**, but only once per round.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** When you succeed on the Thievery check by 5 or more, the action does not provoke opportunity attacks.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** When you succeed on the Thievery check, you may take an additional minor action this turn (once per use).
