# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Attack ✦ Core Concept

**AT-WILL DIVINE WEAPON**

**Eligible Essences:** Swift, Wind, Balance, Oath, Motion

**Slot:** 1 – Core Concept
**Tier:** At-Will

You flow through your enemy’s space, displacing them with precise momentum.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Neutral
**Action Type:** Standard Action
**Range:** Melee weapon
**Cost:** —
**Target:** One creature

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Essence Attack vs AC

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 1[W] + your Essence ability modifier damage**. You shift 1 square. You slide the target 1 square into the space you occupied

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Fluid Motion:** Gain a +1 bonus to the attack roll
>> - **Rank 6 — Extended Displacement:** Increase the slide distance by +1 square
>> - **Rank 9 — Controlled Repositioning:** You may choose to shift before or after the attack
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** If the slide occurs, the target takes a –2 penalty to AC until the start of your next turn
>> - **Silver / Epic:** Increase the slide by +2 squares, and you may shift 2 squares
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** You may slide the target into any adjacent square you could occupy
