# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`

Utility ✦ Concealing Mist

**DAILY ZONE**

**Eligible Essences:** Veil, Shimmer, Water, Air, Shadow

**Slot:** 2 – Utility / Passive
**Tier:** Daily

Mist rolls into existence and thickens rapidly, swallowing shapes and breaking clear lines of sight across the battlefield.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Elevated
**Action Type:** Standard Action
**Range:** Area burst 1 within 10 squares
**Cost:** 1 Mana
**Target:** Area

> [!Effect|background-color-blue]
> The burst creates a zone of **Lightly Obscured** squares until the end of your next turn.
> 
> **Sustain** The zone persists until the end of your next turn. Each time you sustain Obscuring Mist, you may increase its burst size by 1, to a maximum of burst 5. The zone expands from its original center.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Gathering Fog** Allies within the zone gain a +1 power bonus to Stealth checks.
>> - **Rank 6 — Rolling Mist** When you sustain the zone, you may also move its center 1 square before increasing its size.
>> - **Rank 9 — Cloying Fog** Enemies more than 2 squares away from you while both you and they are inside the zone treat creatures in the zone as having concealment.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** When you first create the zone, it begins as Area burst 2 instead of burst 1.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** Allies inside the zone may attempt to become Hidden with only partial concealment from the mist.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** When sustained at maximum size, the outermost squares of the zone may extend around blocking terrain as the mist flows through available openings.
