# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Utility ✦ Tactical

**ENCOUNTER DIVINE**

**Eligible Essences:** Oath, Order, Revelation, Hunt, Suppression

**Slot:** 2 – Utility / Passive
**Tier:** Encounter

You bind your will to a single foe, narrowing your purpose until nothing else exists.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Neutral
**Action Type:** Minor Action
**Range:** Close burst 10
**Cost:** 1 Mana
**Target:** One enemy you can see in burst

> [!Effect|background-color-blue]
> You swear an oath against the target. Until the end of the encounter or until the target drops to 0 HP, your attacks against that target gain heightened precision:
> - When you make a melee attack against the target and it is the only enemy adjacent to you, roll twice for the attack and use either result.
> - If another effect allows you to roll twice and choose a result (higher or lower), this effect does not apply to that attack.
> - If you reroll an attack made under this effect, reroll both dice.
> 
> If the target drops to 0 HP before the end of the encounter, you regain the use of this power.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Relentless Focus:** Gain a +1 bonus to Essence Attack rolls against the sworn target.
>> - **Rank 6 — Isolated Prey:** You gain a +1 bonus to damage rolls against the target while no other enemies are adjacent to you.
>> - **Rank 9 — Unerring Judgment:** When you roll twice from this power, you may treat one die as if it rolled a minimum of 10.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** The accuracy benefit applies to all attacks (not just melee) against the target while the isolation condition is met.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** When the condition is met, you also ignore concealment and gain a +2 bonus to damage rolls against the target.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Once per round, when both attack rolls would hit, treat the attack as a critical hit.
