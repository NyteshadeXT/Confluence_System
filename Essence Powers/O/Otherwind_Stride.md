# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Attack ✦ Tactical Strike

**ENCOUNTER ARCANE IMPLEMENT TELEPORTATION**

**Eligible Essences:** Air, Wind, Swift, Magic, Wing, Freedom, Paradox

**Slot:** 4 – Tactical Strike  
**Tier:** Encounter

You unleash a violent, unseen current that lashes your enemies as you step through its flow and reappear elsewhere.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Erratic
**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Range:** Close burst 1  
**Cost:** 1 Mana
**Target:** Each creature in burst

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Essence Attack vs. Fortitude (Charisma-based)

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 1d8 + your Essence ability modifier damage, and the target is immobilized until the end of your next turn. You teleport 5 squares.

> [!Miss|background-color-yellow]
> - No immobilization.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Flowing Displacement** Increase teleport distance by 2 squares.
>> - **Rank 6 — Disorienting Current** Targets hit take a –2 penalty to Reflex until the end of your next turn.
>> - **Rank 9 — Spatial Slip** After teleporting, you gain a +1 bonus to AC until the start of your next turn.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Damage increases to 2d8 + ability modifier.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** Damage increases to 3d8 + ability modifier. You may teleport before or after the attack.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Damage increases to 4d8 + ability modifier. After teleporting, you become concealed until the end of your next turn.
