# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Attack ✦ Core Concept  

**PSIONIC • WEAPON**

**Eligible Essences:** Dog, Dominion, Iron, Oath, Polearm, Resolute, Shield, Steel

**Slot:** 1 – Core Concept
**Tier:** At-Will

You strike with a flurry of defensive motion, compelling your foe to focus on you rather than your allies.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Mind

**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Range:** Melee weapon  
**Cost:** None

**Target:** One creature

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Power Rank vs. AC

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 1[W] + your Essence ability modifier damage, and you mark the target until the end of your next turn.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Pressing Defense** The penalty from your mark increases to –3.
>> - **Rank 6 — Punishing Focus** The first time the marked target damages an ally before the end of your next turn, it takes psychic damage equal to your highest mental ability modifier.
>> - **Rank 9 — Whirling Guard** You may target up to two creatures within your reach, making a separate attack roll against each.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** A target marked by this power is off-guard until the start of your next turn.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** When a target marked by this power damages an ally, you may Shift 1 square as a free action.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** You may target each enemy within your reach, making a separate attack roll against each.
