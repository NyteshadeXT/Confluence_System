# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Attack ✦ Core Concept

**AT-WILL MARTIAL WEAPON**

**Eligible Essences:** Hunt, Momentum, Zeal, Oath, Swift, Harmony

**Slot:** 1 – Core Concept  
**Tier:** At-Will

You reposition the pack before the strike lands.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Stable
**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Range:** Melee weapon
**Target:** One creature

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Strength vs. AC

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 1[W] + Strength modifier damage. Before the attack, choose one ally adjacent to you or the target. That ally shifts 1 square as a free action.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Coordinated Step** Increase the damage to 1[W] + Strength modifier + 2.
>> - **Rank 6 — Press the Advantage** If the ally ends the shift adjacent to the target, your attack deals additional damage equal to your Wisdom modifier.
>> - **Rank 9 — Encircling Formation** If the ally ends the shift flanking the target with you, the target grants combat advantage until the end of your next turn.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Damage increases to 2[W] + Strength modifier.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** Damage increases to 3[W] + Strength modifier. The chosen ally may shift 2 squares instead of 1.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Damage increases to 4[W] + Strength modifier. If the ally hits the target before the start of your next turn, it deals additional damage equal to your Strength modifier.
