# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`

Attack ✦ Elemental Containment

**DAILY WIND**

**Eligible Essences:** Wind, Air, Storm, Force, Cage, Trap, Elemental

**Slot:** 5 – Apex Ability
**Tier:** Daily

Violent currents coil around your foe, forming an invisible prison of compressed wind. The moment your captive attempts to escape, the prison ruptures outward in a devastating blast.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Elevated
**Action Type:** Standard Action
**Range:** Ranged 10
**Cost:** 1 Mana
**Target:** One creature

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Power Attack vs. Reflex

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 2d10 + Wisdom modifier damage. Whether the attack hits or misses, the target grants Combat Advantage until it moves or until the end of the encounter. 
>  
> The first time the target willingly moves before the end of the encounter, the Wind Prison ruptures. When it ruptures, each enemy within 5 squares of the target is knocked Prone. The Wind Prison then ends.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Pressurized Cage** When the Wind Prison ruptures, each affected enemy also takes damage equal to your Wisdom modifier.
>> - **Rank 6 — Violent Escape** The creature carrying the Wind Prison is also knocked Prone when the prison ruptures.
>> - **Rank 9 — Concussive Release** When the Wind Prison ruptures, you may also push each affected enemy 1 square away from the target after knocking it Prone.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Increase the initial damage to 3d10 + Wisdom modifier.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** When the Wind Prison ruptures, affected enemies are pushed up to 2 squares away from the target and knocked Prone.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Increase the initial damage to 4d10 + Wisdom modifier. When the Wind Prison ruptures, enemies within 5 squares are also **Dazed** until the end of their next turn.
