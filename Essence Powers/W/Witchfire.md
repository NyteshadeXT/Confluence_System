# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Attack ✦ Signature Strike

**Encounter Arcane Fire Implement**

**Eligible Essences:** Light, Shadow, Moon, Zeal, Suppression

**Slot:** 3 – Signature Strike  
**Tier:** Encounter

Argent flame erupts in your foe’s mind and body, searing flesh and unraveling focus.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Disruptive
**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Range:** Ranged 10
**Target:** One creature
**Cost:** 1 Mana

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Essence Attack vs. Reflex (Charisma)

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 2d6 + your Charisma modifier fire damage, and the target takes a –2 penalty to attack rolls until the end of your next turn. If aligned with Moon or Light, increase the penalty to  2 + your Intelligence modifier.

> [!Miss|background-color-yellow]
> - Half damage.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Scouring Flame** Increase the damage to 3d6 + your Charisma modifier.
>> - **Rank 6 — Unraveled Focus** If the target is bloodied when hit, the penalty to attack rolls increases by 1.
>> - **Rank 9 — Lingering Brand** If the target misses with an attack while under this penalty, it takes fire damage equal to your Intelligence modifier.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Damage increases to 4d6 + Charisma modifier.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** Damage increases to 5d6 + Charisma modifier. The attack penalty lasts until the end of the target’s next turn after yours.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Damage increases to 6d6 + Charisma modifier. If the target is bloodied and hit, it is weakened until the end of your next turn.
