# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Attack ✦ Signature Strike

**ENCOUNTER DIVINE THUNDER WEAPON**

**Eligible Essences:** Oath, Light, Sound, Reckoning, Lightning, Storm

**Slot:** 3 – Signature Strike  
**Tier:** Encounter

Your strike lands with a thunderclap of divine judgment.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Neutral
**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Range:** Melee weapon
**Cost:** 1 Mana
**Target:** One creature

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Strength vs. AC

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 1[W] + Strength modifier thunder damage. The target is dazed until the end of your next turn.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Resounding Judgment** Increase the damage to 2[W] + Strength modifier.
>> - **Rank 6 — Cracking Verdict** If the target is bloodied when hit, it also takes a –2 penalty to attack rolls until the end of your next turn.
>> - **Rank 9 — Shuddering Impact** If the target fails its first saving throw against an effect you impose before the end of your next turn, it is knocked prone.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Damage increases to 3[W] + Strength modifier.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** Damage increases to 4[W] + Strength modifier. The dazed condition lasts until the end of the target’s next turn after yours.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Damage increases to 5[W] + Strength modifier. If the target is bloodied and hit, it is stunned until the end of your next turn instead of dazed.
