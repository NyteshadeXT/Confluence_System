# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Attack ✦ Signature Strike

**ENCOUNTER MARTIAL WEAPON**

**Eligible Essences:** Blade, Order, Hunt, Oath, Zeal, Revelation, Bond

**Slot:** 3 – Signature Strike  
**Tier:** Encounter

A single, decisive strike exposes your enemy’s weakness and calls your allies to exploit it.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Neutral
**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Range:** Melee weapon  
**Cost:** 1 Stamina
**Target:** One creature

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Power Rank vs. AC (Strength-based)

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 2[W] + your Essence ability modifier damage. Until the end of your next turn, all allies gain a +2 bonus to damage rolls against the target. 

> [!Miss|background-color-yellow]
> - Half damage. No bonus.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Exposed Weakness** Allies also gain a +1 bonus to attack rolls against the target.
>> - **Rank 6 — Press the Advantage** The first ally to hit the target before the end of your next turn deals additional damage equal to your Strength modifier.
>> - **Rank 9 — Coordinated Assault** If two or more allies hit the target, it takes a –2 penalty to AC until the end of your next turn.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Damage increases to 3[W] + ability modifier. Damage bonus increases to +3 (or 2 + Charisma modifier).
>> - **Silver / Epic:** Damage increases to 4[W] + ability modifier. Allies gain the damage bonus even on missed attacks (minimum 1 damage).
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Damage increases to 5[W] + ability modifier. If the target is bloodied, allies gain an additional +2 damage.
