# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Attack ✦ Apex Ability

**DAILY MARTIAL WEAPON**

**Eligible Essences:** Zeal, Momentum, Oath, Swift, Convergence  

**Slot:** 5 – Apex Ability  
**Tier:** Daily

Your strike ignites a chain of motion, and unity becomes unstoppable.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Escalating
**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Range:** Melee weapon
**Cost:** 2 Stamina
**Target:** One creature

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Strength vs. AC

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 3[W] + Strength modifier damage. Slide one adjacent ally 1 square. Until the end of the encounter, whenever you or an ally within 10 squares hits with an attack, the attacker may slide one adjacent ally 1 square.

> [!Miss|background-color-yellow]
> - HChoose one ally within 10 squares. Until the end of the encounter, that ally gains the slide effect.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Rallying Surge** Increase the damage to 4[W] + Strength modifier.
>> - **Rank 6 —  Expanding Formation** Increase the slide distance granted by this power to 2 squares.
>> - **Rank 9 — Relentless Momentum** When you slide an ally using this power’s effect, that ally gains a +2 power bonus to AC until the start of your next turn.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Damage increases to 5[W] + Strength modifier.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** Damage increases to 6[W] + Strength modifier. When an ally slides another ally via this effect, the sliding ally may also shift 1 square.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Damage increases to 7[W] + Strength modifier. Once per round, when an ally hits and triggers the slide effect, you may instead slide two adjacent allies 1 square each.
