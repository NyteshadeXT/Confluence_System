# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Utility ✦ Bestial Transformation

**AT-WILL POLYMORPH WILD SHAPE**

**Eligible Essences:** Ape, Bear, Wolf, Snake, Spider, Bat, Fox, Cat, Dog, Crocodile, Lizard, Turtle, Wasp

**Slot:** 2 – Utility / Passive  
**Tier:** At-Will

You draw upon the physical nature of your Essence, allowing its bestial traits to emerge through your body. With experience, these manifestations deepen until there is little distinction between embodying the beast and becoming it.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Stable  
**Action Type:** Triple Action  
**Range:** Personal  
**Cost:** None  
**Target:** Self

### Effect

You manifest a **Bestial Aspect** associated with the Essence through which this power is expressed or dismiss an aspect you have already manifested. You may use Wild Shape only once per round.

The nature of your Bestial Aspect is determined by the Essence expressing Wild Shape. Bear might manifest thick fur, powerful claws, and an ursine build; Wolf might produce fangs, predatory eyes, and lupine features; Spider might develop chitinous skin, additional eyes, or altered limbs.

The exact appearance is yours to determine but must remain consistent with the identity of the Essence.

While manifesting a Bestial Aspect:
- You remain recognizably your normal ancestry and retain your normal size.
- Your attributes, defenses, Hit Points, Mana, and Stamina do not change.
- Your movement speeds and movement modes do not change.
- You retain your normal senses.
- Your equipment remains equipped and functions normally.
- You may use powers with the **Wild Shape** keyword.
- Your Bestial Aspect does not inherently grant natural weapons, special senses, movement modes, or other capabilities associated with the represented creature. These abilities must be granted by another power, Awakening Stone, or effect.

Wild Shape represents your ability to manifest the physical identity of your Essence. Other powers determine what those manifestations allow you to do.

### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
- **Rank 3 — Deepening Aspect** Your connection to the beast strengthens. When you enter Wild Shape, choose Athletics, Acrobatics, or Perception. You gain a +1 power bonus to checks using the chosen skill while Wild Shape remains active. The chosen skill should reflect the nature of the Essence and the form being manifested.
- **Rank 6 — Hybrid Form** Your Bestial Aspect can now reshape your body extensively, creating a hybrid of your natural form and the creature represented by your Essence. When you activate Wild Shape, you may assume either your Bestial Aspect or Hybrid Form. While in Hybrid Form, equipment you cannot physically use merges harmlessly into your body while equipment you can reasonably use remains available. Merged equipment continues to provide passive benefits but cannot be accessed or activated. Your statistics, size, and movement modes otherwise remain unchanged.
- **Rank 9 — Swift Transformation** Your Wild Shape becomes more natural to you. Once per round, you may activate or dismiss Wild Shape as a Double Action instead of a Triple Action. When you return completely to your normal form, you may shift 1 square.

### TIER EXPRESSIONS
- **Bronze / Paragon — True Wild Shape:** Your mastery allows you to surrender your natural form completely. When you activate Wild Shape, you may assume your Bestial Aspect, Hybrid Form, or **True Wild Shape**. In True Wild Shape, you fully transform into a natural or Essence-infused version of the creature represented by the Essence expressing this power. Your equipment merges into your form and cannot be accessed, though you continue to receive its passive benefits. You retain your attributes, defenses, Hit Points, Mana, Stamina, and other game statistics. True Wild Shape does not inherently grant new movement modes, natural attacks, special senses, or other abilities unless another power or effect grants them.
- **Silver / Epic — Essence Incarnate:** Your transformed body is no longer constrained to an ordinary representation of the creature. Your Wild Shape may display supernatural characteristics reflecting your other Essences and Awakening Stones. When you activate Wild Shape or change between its forms, you may shift up to 2 squares.
- **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional) — Primal Apotheosis:** Your physical form becomes an expression of bestial Essence rather than fixed anatomy. You may freely transition between your normal form, Bestial Aspect, Hybrid Form, and True Wild Shape once per round without using an action.
