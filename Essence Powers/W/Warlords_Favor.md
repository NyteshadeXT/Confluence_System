# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Attack ✦ Tactical Strike

**ENCOUNTER MARTIAL WEAPON**

**Eligible Essences:** Eye, Oath, Zeal, Hunt, Balance, Harmony

**Slot:** 4 – Tactical Strike  
**Tier:** Encounter

You open a precise weakness and direct your ally to exploit it.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Stable
**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Range:** Melee weapon
**Cost:** 1 Stamina
**Target:** One creature

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Strength vs. AC

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 2[W] + Strength modifier damage. One ally within 5 squares gains a +2 power bonus to attack rolls against the target until the end of your next turn. If you possess an Intelligence-aligned Essence (Eye, Balance), the bonus equals 1 + your Intelligence modifier instead.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Directed Exploit** Increase the damage to 3[W] + Strength modifier.
>> - **Rank 6 — Tactical Precision** If the ally hits the target while benefiting from this power, they deal additional damage equal to your Intelligence modifier.
>> - **Rank 9 — Coordinated Assault** The chosen ally also gains a +2 power bonus to damage rolls against the target until the end of your next turn.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Damage increases to 4[W] + Strength modifier.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** Damage increases to 5[W] + Strength modifier. The granted attack bonus increases by +1.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Damage increases to 6[W] + Strength modifier. If the ally hits the target while benefiting from this power, the target is slowed until the end of its next turn.
