# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Attack ✦ Signature Strike  

**PSIONIC  FORCE  PSYCHIC**

**Eligible Essences:** Mind, Force, Pain, Discord, Gravity

**Slot:** 3 – Signature Strike  
**Tier:** Encounter

You make a foe feel a psychic echo of the pain it inflicts on those you protect.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Escalating
**Action Type:** Immediate Reaction  
**Range:** Melee 1  
**Cost:** 1 Mana

**Trigger:** An adjacent enemy damages an ally with an attack that does not include you as a target.
**Target:** The triggering enemy

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Power Rank vs. Will

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 2d6 + your highest mental ability modifier force and psychic damage.

> [!Miss|background-color-yellow]
> - Half damage.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 —Shared Agony** Increase the damage to 3d6 + your highest mental ability modifier.
>> - **Rank 6 — Disruptive Spike** The target is off-guard until the start of its next turn. If the target is marked by you, it also cannot Shift until then.
>> - **Rank 9 —  Crushing Reprisal** Increase the damage to 4d6 + your highest mental ability modifier.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** The triggering ally gains temporary Hit Points equal to your highest mental ability modifier.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** If the triggering attack was a critical hit, the target is also stunned until the end of its next turn.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** You may use this power once per encounter without paying its Mana cost.
