# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Utility ✦ Skill

**Encounter  Martial**

**Eligible Essences:** Fox, Adept, Eye, Mind

**Slot:** 2 – Utility / Passive  
**Tier:** Encounter

You know exactly when to lie again.

**Action Type:** Free Action  
**Range:** Personal
**Trigger:** You roll a Bluff check and dislike the result.
**Prerequisite:** You must be trained in Bluff.

> [!Effect|background-color-blue]
> Reroll the Bluff check.  You decide to use this power before the result is announced.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> -  **Rank 3 — Silver Tongue** You gain a +2 power bonus to the rerolled Bluff check.
>> - **Rank 6 — Layered Falsehood** If the rerolled Bluff check succeeds, you gain a +2 power bonus to your next Bluff or Diplomacy check before the end of the encounter.
>> - **Rank 9 — Unshakable Mask** If the rerolled Bluff check fails by 2 or less, treat it as a success against creatures of your level or lower.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** You may use this power **twice per encounter**, but only once per round.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** When you succeed on the rerolled Bluff check, the target takes a –2 penalty to Insight checks against you until the end of the encounter.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** If you succeed on the rerolled Bluff check by 5 or more, you may impose a minor false belief that lasts until contradicted by clear evidence (DM adjudication).
