# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Attack ✦ Core Concept

**AT-WILL ARCANE FORCE IMPLEMENT**

**Eligible Essences:** Force, Echo, Light, Magic  

**Slot:** 1 – Core Concept  
**Tier:** At-Will

A precise bolt of force streaks unerringly toward its target.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Stable
**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Range:** Ranged 20
**Target:** One creature

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Intelligence vs. Reflex

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 2d4 + Intelligence modifier force damage. This power counts as a ranged basic attack.

> [!Miss|background-color-yellow]
> - No mark.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Focused Force** Increase the damage to 2d6 + your Intelligence modifier.
>> - **Rank 6 — Echoing Impact** If you hit the same target with Magic Missile on consecutive turns, the second hit deals additional force damage equal to your Intelligence modifier (once per round).
>> - **Rank 9 — Unerring Bolt** If the attack roll misses by 2 or less, it instead deals damage equal to your Intelligence modifier.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Damage increases to 3d4 + Intelligence modifier.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** Damage increases to 4d4 + Intelligence modifier. Once per turn, when you hit, you may push the target 1 square.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Damage increases to 5d4 + Intelligence modifier. If the target is bloodied, this power deals additional force damage equal to your Intelligence modifier.
