# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Utility ✦ Utility / Passive

**DAILY DIVINE**

**Eligible Essences:** Oath, Shield, Life, Zeal, Bear, Dog, Dominion, Harmony

**Slot:** 2 – Utility / Passive  
**Tier:** Daily

You interpose yourself without hesitation, taking upon yourself the suffering meant for another.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Escalating (self-sacrifice draws Dungeon attention)
**Action Type:** Immediate Interrupt  
**Range:** Close burst 1
**Trigger:**  An adjacent ally is hit by a melee or ranged attack
**Cost:** 1 Mana

> [!Effect|background-color-blue]
> You are hit by the attack instead of the ally. You take the damage and effects as though you were the original target.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Shared Burden** Reduce the damage taken by an amount equal to your Constitution modifier.
>> - **Rank 6 — Devoted Intercession** After using this power, you gain temporary hit points equal to your Charisma modifier.
>> - **Rank 9 — Unbreakable Sacrifice** If the damage would reduce you to 0 HP, you remain at 1 HP instead (once per use).
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** You may shift 1 square before resolving the effect to reach the ally.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** After taking the attack, you gain resistance to all damage equal to your Charisma modifier until the end of your next turn.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** The protected ally gains immunity to the triggering attack’s effects entirely.
