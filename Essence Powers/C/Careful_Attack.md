# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`

Attack ✦ Core Concept

**At-Will Martial Weapon Precision**

**Eligible Essences:** Hunt, Adept, Blade, Ranged, Snake  

**Slot:** 1 – Core Concept  
**Tier:** At-Will

You wait for the perfect opening, striking only when the enemy’s guard slips.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Neutral
**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Attack Type and Range:** Melee or Ranged weapon
**Requirement:** You must be wielding either two melee weapons or a ranged weapon.
**Target:** One creature
**Special:** This power cannot gain additional weapon damage dice beyond its listed [W] value. Flat modifiers, penalties, and vulnerability still apply.

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - **Melee:** Essence Attack +2 vs. AC (Strength or Dexterity chosen when unlocked) 
> - **Ranged:** Essence Attack +2 vs. AC (Dexterity)

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 1[W] damage.

> [!Miss|background-color-yellow]
> - No damage.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Patient Aim** Increase the attack bonus to +3 vs. AC.
>> - **Rank 6 — Exploit Opening** If you hit with this power, you gain a +1 power bonus to your next attack roll before the end of your next turn.
>> - **Rank 9 — Perfect Timing** If you miss with this power, you gain a +2 power bonus to your next attack roll against the same target before the end of your next turn.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Damage increases to 2[W].
>> - **Silver / Epic:** If the attack roll misses by 2 or less, treat it as a hit.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** This attack ignores penalties from concealment and partial cover.
