# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Attack ✦ Signature Strike  

**ENCOUNTER MARTIAL WEAPON**

**Eligible Essences:** Bludgeon, Might, Steel, Bear, Iron, Momentum  

**Slot:** 3 – Signature Strike  
**Tier:** Encounter

You commit your full weight to a brutal strike meant to crush armor, bone, and resolve alike.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Neutral
**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Range:** Melee weapon  
**Cost:** 1 Stamina
**Target:** One creature
**Requirement:** You must be wielding a melee weapon.

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Strength vs. AC

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 2[W] + Strength modifier damage. If you are wielding an axe, a hammer, or a mace, you gain a bonus to the damage roll equal to your Constitution modifier.

> [!Miss|background-color-yellow]
> - Half damage.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Heavy Follow-Through** On a hit, add +2 damage.
>> - **Rank 6 — Bone-Rattling Impact** If you are wielding an axe, hammer, or mace, the target takes a –2 penalty to attack rolls until the end of its next turn.
>> - **Rank 9 — Overwhelming Force** If the target is bloodied, you may push it 1 square on a hit.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Damage increases to 3[W] + Strength modifier. If wielding an axe, hammer, or mace, add Constitution modifier to the damage roll.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** Damage increases to 4[W] + Strength modifier. If wielding an axe, hammer, or mace, the target is knocked prone on a hit.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Damage increases to 5[W] + Strength modifier. If wielding an axe, hammer, or mace and the target is bloodied, it is also dazed until the end of your next turn.
