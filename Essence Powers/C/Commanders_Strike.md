# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Attack ✦ Core Concept

**AT-WILL MARTIAL WEAPON**

**Eligible Essences:** Oath, Zeal, Eye, Shield, Momentum, Harmony

**Slot:** 1 – Core Concept  
**Tier:** At-Will

With a sharp command, you turn your ally’s blade into your own weapon.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Stable
**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Range:** Melee weapon
**Target:** One creature

> [!Effect|background-color-blue]
> Choose one ally adjacent to you or the target. That ally makes a melee basic attack against the target as a free action. If the attack hits, it deals extra damage equal to your Intelligence modifier.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Tactical Precision** Increase the extra damage to Intelligence modifier + 2.
>> - **Rank 6 — Coordinated Assault** If the ally hits with the granted attack, you gain a +1 power bonus to your next attack roll before the end of your next turn.
>> - **Rank 9 — Relentless Command** If the ally misses with the granted attack, it gains a +2 power bonus to its next attack roll against the same target before the end of its next turn.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Extra damage increases to Intelligence modifier + 4.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** If the granted attack hits, the target grants combat advantage to the ally until the end of its next turn.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Once per encounter, when the granted attack hits, the ally may shift 1 square.
