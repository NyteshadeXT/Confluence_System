# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Utility ✦ Tactical

**Encounter Martial**

**Eligible Essences:** Hunt, Eye, Knowledge, Wolf, Mind, Harmony

**Slot:** 2 – Utility / Passive  
**Tier:** Encounter

Your insight cuts through doubt at the exact moment it matters most.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Neutral
**Action Type:** Immediate Reaction  
**Range:** Ranged 5
**Trigger:** An ally you can see or hear makes a skill check using a skill in which you are trained.

> [!Effect|background-color-blue]
> The ally may reroll the skill check and gains a power bonus equal to your Wisdom modifier to the new roll. An ally can benefit from Crucial Advice only once per check.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Focused Instruction** Increase the power bonus to Wisdom modifier + 2.
>> - **Rank 6 — Shared Insight** If the reroll succeeds, you gain a +1 power bonus to your next skill check before the end of your next turn.
>> - **Rank 9 — Perfect Timing** If the ally fails the reroll, it still gains a +2 power bonus to its next check using the same skill before the end of the encounter. (Does not stack with itself.)
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** The bonus becomes Wisdom modifier + 4.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** The power may also be used on a saving throw (once per encounter).
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Once per encounter, if the reroll succeeds by 5 or more, the ally may shift 1 square.
