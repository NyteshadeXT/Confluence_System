# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Attack ✦ Signature Strike

**ENCOUNTER MARTIAL WEAPON**

**Eligible Essences:** Hunt, Swift, Wind, Momentum, Blade, Ranged, Eye

**Slot:** 3 – Signature Strike  
**Tier:** Encounter

You strike in motion, repositioning fluidly between blows to keep the advantage.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Neutral
**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Range:** Melee or Ranged weapon  
**Cost:** 1 Stamina
**Requirement:** You must be wielding two melee weapons or a ranged weapon.
**Target:** One or two creatures

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Essence Attack vs. AC (two attacks); Strength (melee; main-hand and off-hand)/Dexterity (ranged)

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 1[W] + your Essence ability modifier damage per hit. After the first or second attack, you may shift a number of squares equal to 1 + your Wisdom modifier.

> [!Miss|background-color-yellow]
> - No damage.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Fluid Positioning** Increase the shift distance by 1.
>> - **Rank 6 — Evasive Flow** After shifting, you gain a +1 bonus to AC until the start of your next turn.
>> - **Rank 9 — Relentless Pursuit** If you hit the same target with both attacks, you may shift again (1 square).

>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Damage increases to 2[W] + ability modifier per hit.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** Damage increases to 3[W] + ability modifier per hit. You may shift before the first attack.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Damage increases to 4[W] + ability modifier per hit. If both attacks hit, the target is slowed until the end of your next turn.
