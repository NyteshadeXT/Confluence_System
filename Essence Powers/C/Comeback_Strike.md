# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Attack ✦ Apex Ability

**Daily Martial Weapon Healing Reliable**

**Eligible Essences:** Bear, Blood, Life, Shield, Resolute, Zeal, Renewal  

**Slot:** 5 – Apex Ability  
**Tier:** Daily

You strike through pain and doubt, drawing strength from the act of defiance itself.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Escalating
**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Attack Type and Range:** Melee weapon
**Target:** One creature
**Cost:** Cost: 2 Stamina

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Power Rank vs. AC

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 2[W] + your Essence ability modifier damage. You may immediately spend a healing surge. If this power misses, it is not expended.

> [!Miss|background-color-yellow]
> - Half damage (round down). You may spend a healing surge, but regain only half the normal hit points.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Defiant Blow** Increase the damage to 3[W] + ability modifier.
>> - **Rank 6 — Renewed Resolve** If you are bloodied when you use this power, you gain temporary hit points equal to your Constitution modifier after resolving the attack.
>> - **Rank 9 — Iron Recovery** If the attack hits, increase the hit points regained from the healing surge by your Wisdom modifier. (Does not apply on a miss.)
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Damage becomes 4[W] + ability modifier.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** If you are bloodied when you use this power, you gain resistance to all damage equal to your Constitution modifier until the end of your next turn.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** When you reduce a creature to 0 HP with this power, you regain hit points equal to your Constitution modifier.
