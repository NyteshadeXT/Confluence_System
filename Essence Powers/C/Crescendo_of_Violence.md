# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Utility ✦ Reactive Support

**ENCOUNTER MARTIAL**

Eligible Essences: Zeal, Momentum, Blood, Hunt, Blade

**Slot:** 2 – Utility / Passive  
**Tier:** Encounter

A decisive strike becomes a rallying cry.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Stable
**Action Type:** Immediate Reaction  
**Range:** Ranged 5
**Cost:** 1 Stamina
**Trigger:** An ally within range scores a critical hit

> [!Effect|background-color-blue]
> The ally gains temporary hit points equal to your Charisma modifier.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Rallying Surge** Increase the temporary hit points to Charisma modifier + 2.
>> - **Rank 6 — Shared Momentum** You gain temporary hit points equal to half your Charisma modifier (rounded down) when this power triggers.
>> - **Rank 9 — Unbroken Rhythm** If the ally is bloodied when this power triggers, increase the temporary hit points granted by your Charisma modifier. (Stacks with Rank 3 increase.)
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Temporary hit points equal 5 + your Charisma modifier.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** The ally also gains a +1 power bonus to attack rolls until the end of their next turn.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Once per encounter, when this power triggers, you may shift 2 squares.
