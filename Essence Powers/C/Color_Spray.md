# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Attack ✦ Tactical Strike  

**ENCOUNTER ARCANE RADIANT IMPLEMENT**

**Eligible Essences:** Light, Sound, Magic, Eye, Revelation, Equilibrium, Mind

**Slot:** 4 – Tactical Strike  
**Tier:** Encounter

A cascade of blinding, discordant colors erupts from your hands, overwhelming the senses of those caught within.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Erratic
**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Range:** Close blast 5  
**Cost:** 1 Mana
**Target:** Each creature in blast

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Power Rank vs. Will

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 1d6 + your Essence ability modifier radiant damage, and the target is dazed until the end of your next turn.

> [!Miss|background-color-yellow]
> - Half damage. No daze.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Intensified Spectrum** Increase damage to 2d6 + ability modifier.
>> - **Rank 6 — Disorienting Burst** Targets hit take a –2 penalty to attack rolls until the end of your next turn.
>> - **Rank 9 — Overwhelming Light** If a target is already dazed, it is instead stunned until the end of your next turn.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Damage increases to 3d6 + ability modifier.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** Damage increases to 4d6 + ability modifier. Daze lasts until the end of the target’s next turn after yours.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Damage increases to 5d6 + ability modifier. Targets also grant combat advantage until the end of your next turn.
