# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Attack ✦ Signature Strike

**ENCOUNTER ARCANE COLD IMPLEMENT**

**Eligible Essences:** Ice, Water, Omen, Balance  

**Slot:** 3 – Signature Strike  
**Tier:** Encounter

A bolt of killing frost crashes into your foe, numbing mind and body.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Stable
**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Range:** Ranged 10
**Cost:** 1 Mana
**Target:** One creature

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Intelligence vs. Fortitude

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 2d8 + Intelligence modifier cold damage. The target is dazed until the end of your next turn.

>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Deepening Frost** Increase the damage to 2d10 + Intelligence modifier.
>> - **Rank 6 — Numbing Chill** After the daze ends, the target is slowed until the end of its next turn. (Note: This is sequential, not overlapping.)
>> - **Rank 9 — Crippling Cold** The first time the target misses an attack while dazed by this power, it takes cold damage equal to your Intelligence modifier.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Damage increases to 3d8 + Intelligence modifier.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** While slowed from this power, the target takes a –2 penalty to saving throws.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** If the target fails a saving throw against this power, it is also knocked prone.
