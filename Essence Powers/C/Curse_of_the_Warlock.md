# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Utility ✦ At-Will

**At-Will Arcane**

**Eligible Essences:** Omen, Echo, Gathering, Blood, Shadow, Reckoning

**Slot:** 2 – Utility / Passive  
**Tier:** At-will

You bind a foe to your pact, marking them for inevitable consequence.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Latent
**Action Type:** Minor Action  
**Target:** One creature

> [!Effect|background-color-blue]
> Once per turn, you place a Warlock’s Curse on the nearest enemy you can see that is not already cursed. A cursed enemy remains cursed until:
> - The end of the encounter, or
> - It drops to 0 HP or fewer.
> 
> You may have multiple enemies cursed during an encounter, but you may only apply one curse per turn. You cannot curse a creature already affected by another Warlock’s Curse.
> 
> **Curse Effect** Once per round, when you damage a cursed enemy, you may deal 1d6 extra damage. You decide whether to apply this extra damage after rolling damage.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Dark Precision** Increase the extra damage to 1d8.
>> - **Rank 6 — Lingering Malice** If the cursed enemy is bloodied, increase the extra damage by your Charisma modifier. (Only applies once per round.)
>> - **Rank 9 — Relentless Hex** When a cursed enemy drops to 0 HP, you may immediately place your curse on another enemy you can see without using a minor action. (Still only one curse application per turn.)
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Extra damage becomes 2d6.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** Once per encounter, when you apply your curse damage, the target takes a –2 penalty to saving throws until the end of your next turn.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** If a cursed target fails a saving throw against one of your powers, it takes psychic damage equal to your Charisma modifier.
