# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Attack ✦ Apex Ability

**DAILY DIVINE RADIANT IMPLEMENT**

**Eligible Essences:** Light, Reckoning, Omen, Zeal  

**Slot:** 5 – Apex Ability  
**Tier:** Daily

Radiant judgment sears your foe and exposes them to divine retribution.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Escalating
**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Range:** Ranged 10
**Cost:** 2 Mana
**Target:** One creature

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Wisdom vs. Will

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 3d8 + Wisdom modifier radiant damage. The target gains vulnerability 5 to all your attacks (save ends).

> [!Miss|background-color-yellow]
> - Half damage; no vulnerability.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Intensified Radiance** Increase the initial damage to **3d10 + Wisdom modifier**.
>> - **Rank 6 — Relentless Exposure** Increase the vulnerability to **7**.
>> - **Rank 9 — Judgment Amplified** The first time each round you hit the target while it is vulnerable from this power, it takes additional radiant damage equal to your Wisdom modifier.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Vulnerability increases to 10.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** While vulnerable from this power, the target takes a –2 penalty to saving throws against divine powers.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** If the target fails its first saving throw, it is also dazed until the end of its next turn.
