# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Attack ✦ Signature Strike  

**ENCOUNTER DIVINE CHARM IMPLEMENT**

**Eligible Essences:** Order, Oath, Chain, Sound, Rune, Revelation, Dominion, Equilibrium  

**Slot:** 3 – Signature Strike  
**Tier:** Encounter

Your word carries divine authority that cannot be ignored.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Escalating
**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Range:** Ranged 10  
**Cost:** 1 Mana
**Target:** One creature

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Wisdom vs. Will

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - The target is dazed until the end of your next turn.You choose one:
> 	- The target falls prone.
> 	- You slide the target up to 3 squares.

> [!Miss|background-color-yellow]
> - The target is slowed until the end of your next turn.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Sovereign Rebuke** You may slide the target up to 5 squares instead.
>> - **Rank 6 — Crushing Authority** If the target is bloodied, it is also weakened until the end of your next turn.
>> - **Rank 9 — Absolute Word** If the target fails its first saving throw against the dazed condition, it is stunned until the end of your next turn.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** The dazed condition becomes (save ends).
>> - **Silver / Epic:** On hit, the target is stunned until the end of your next turn instead of dazed.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** The target is dominated until the end of your next turn (save ends at end of turn).
