# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Attack ✦ Core Concept

**At-Will Martial  Weapon**

**Eligible Essences:** Blade, Steel, Might, Momentum  

**Slot:** 1 – Core Concept  
**Tier:** At-Will

You strike through one foe and let the momentum of the blow spill into another nearby enemy.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Neutral
**Action Type:** Standard Action
**Attack Type and Range:** Melee weapon
**Target:** One creature

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Essence Attack vs. AC

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 1[W] + your Essence ability modifier damage, and one enemy adjacent to you takes damage equal to your Essence ability modifier.

> [!Miss|background-color-yellow]
> - No damage.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Forceful Swing** Increase the secondary damage to ability modifier + 2.
>> - **Rank 6 — Sweeping Follow-Through** If the primary attack reduces a creature to 0 HP, you may shift 1 square.
>> - **Rank 9 — Relentless Momentum** If you hit the primary target, you gain a +1 power bonus to damage rolls against enemies adjacent to you until the end of your next turn.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Secondary damage becomes equal to your full ability modifier + 3.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** If both primary and secondary damage hit bloodied enemies, you gain temporary HP equal to your Constitution modifier.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic:** When you reduce a creature to 0 HP with Cleave, one adjacent enemy takes damage equal to your ability modifier.
