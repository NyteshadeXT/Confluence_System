# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`

Attack ✦ Predatory Compulsion

**ENCOUNTER PSYCHIC CHARM**

**Eligible Essences:** Hunt, Pursuit, Mind, Subjugation, Wolf, Cat, Fox

**Slot:** 3 – Signature Strike
**Tier:** Encounter

Your predatory presence overwhelms your prey with the certainty that it has been singled out, drawing it helplessly toward you.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Stable
**Action Type:** Standard Action
**Range:** Ranged 5
**Cost:** 1 Mana
**Target:** One creature

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Power Rank vs. Will

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 2d8 + Wisdom modifier psychic damage, and you pull the target 3 squares.

> [!Miss|background-color-yellow]
> - No damage.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Isolated Prey** If the target ends the pull with no allies adjacent to it, it grants Combat Advantage until the end of your next turn.
>> - **Rank 6 — Relentless Call** Increase the pull to 4 squares. You may shift 1 square toward the target after resolving the pull.
>> - **Rank 9 — Marked for the Cull** If the target ends the pull adjacent to you, it is Slowed until the end of your next turn.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Increase the damage to 3d8 + Wisdom modifier.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** On a hit, you may pull the target up to 5 squares, and the target cannot shift until the end of your next turn.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Increase the damage to 4d8 + Wisdom modifier. If the target ends adjacent to you, it grants Combat Advantage to all creatures until the end of your next turn.
