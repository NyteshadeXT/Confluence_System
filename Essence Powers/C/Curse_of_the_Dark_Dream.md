# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Attack ✦ Apex Ability

**Daily Arcane Implement**

**Eligible Essences:** Veil, Shadow, Eye, Moon, Revelation

**Slot:** 5 – Apex Ability  
**Tier:** Daily

You fracture your enemy’s perception, forcing waking nightmare upon them.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Distorting
**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Attack Type and Range:** Ranged 10
**Target:** One creature
**Cost:** 2 Mana

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Essence Attack vs. Will

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 3d8 + your Charisma modifier psychic damage, and you slide the target 3 squares.
> 
> - **Sustain Minor:** You slide the target 1 square (save ends).

> [!Miss|background-color-yellow]
> - Half damage. You may sustain to slide 1 square once.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Shattered Perception** Increase the initial slide to 4 squares.
>> - **Rank 6 — Lingering Hallucination** When the target fails its first saving throw against this power, it takes psychic damage equal to your Charisma modifier. (Once per target.)
>> - **Rank 9 — Warped Reality** When you sustain this power, you may also shift 1 square.
>
