# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Utility ✦ Sacred Restoration

**DAILY DIVINE HEALING**

**Eligible Essences:** Life, Light, Oath, Renewal

**Slot:** 2 – Utility / Passive  
**Tier:** Daily

A quiet prayer closes wounds and restores strength.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Stable
**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Range:** Melee touch
**Cost:** 2 Mana
**Target:** You or one creature

> [!Effect|background-color-blue]
> The target regains hit points as if it had spent a Healing Surge.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Strengthened Prayer** Increase the hit points regained by +5.
>> - **Rank 6 — Timely Restoration** If the target is bloodied, it also gains temporary hit points equal to your Wisdom modifier.
>> - **Rank 9 — Renewed Resolve** If the target has no healing surges remaining, it may still benefit from this power and regains hit points equal to half its surge value instead.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Healing becomes surge value + 10.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** The target may also make a saving throw.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Once per encounter, when you use this power, you may target yourself and one ally.
