# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Attack ✦ Core Concept

**AT-WILL ARCANE FORCE IMPLEMENT**

**Eligible Essences:** Force, Wind, Glass, Reckoning, Echo  

**Slot:** 1 – Core Concept  
**Tier:** At-Will

A suspended lattice of force blades tears at anything that lingers within it.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Stable
**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Range:** Area 1 square within 10 squares
**Target:** Each creature in the square

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Intelligence vs. Reflex

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 1d6 + Intelligence modifier force damage.The square becomes a zone of whirling force until the end of your next turn. Any creature that enters the zone or starts its turn there takes force damage equal to your Wisdom modifier (minimum 1). You may dismiss the zone early as a minor action.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Sharpened Blades** Increase the initial damage to 1d8 + Intelligence modifier.
>> - **Rank 6 — Persistent Edges** Increase the zone damage to Wisdom modifier + 2.
>> - **Rank 9 — Tactical Placement** When you create the zone, you may shift 1 square.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Initial damage increases to 2d6 + Intelligence modifier.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** Creatures that start their turn in the zone take a –1 penalty to attack rolls until the end of their turn.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** When you dismiss the zone, creatures within it take force damage equal to your Intelligence modifier.
