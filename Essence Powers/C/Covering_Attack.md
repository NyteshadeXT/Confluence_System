# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Attack ✦ Tactical Strike

**Encounter Martial Weapon**

**Eligible Essences:** Blade, Shield, Order, Momentum, Wolf, Harmony

**Slot:** 4 – Tactical Strike  
**Tier:** Encounter

You unleash a relentless flurry of strikes, forcing your enemy to focus entirely on you while an ally exploits the opening to disengage safely.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Neutral
**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Attack Type and Range:** Melee weapon
**Target:** One creature
**Cost:** Cost: 1 Stamina

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Power Rank vs. AC

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 2[W] + your Essence ability modifier damage. One ally adjacent to the target may shift up to 2 squares as a free action. This movement does not provoke opportunity attacks from the target.

> [!Miss|background-color-yellow]
> - No damage. One ally adjacent to the target may shift 1 square.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Relentless Pressure** Increase the damage to 3[W] + ability modifier.
>> - **Rank 6 — Shielding Footwork** If the ally shifts the full distance granted by this power, it gains a +1 power bonus to AC against the target until the end of its next turn.
>> - **Rank 9 — Commanding Advance** If the attack hits, you may shift 1 square after the ally resolves its movement.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Shift distance increases to 3 squares.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** The ally gains resistance equal to your Constitution modifier against the target’s next attack.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** If the ally reduces the target to 0 HP before the end of its next turn, it may shift an additional 2 squares.
