# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Attack ✦ Elemental Control

**AT-WILL COLD**

**Eligible Essences:** Wind, Ice, Winter, Storm, Elemental

**Slot:** 1 – Core Concept  
**Tier:** At-Will

A howling gust of bitter wind tears through your enemies, coating them in frost and scattering them before its force.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Stable  
**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Range:** Area burst 1 within 10 squares  
**Cost:** None  
**Target:** Each creature in the burst

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Power Attack vs. Fortitude

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 1d6 cold damage, and you slide the target 1 square.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Biting Gale** Increase the slide to 2 squares.
>> - **Rank 6 — Numbing Cold** A target you slide is also slowed until the end of your next turn.
>> - **Rank 9 — Howling Squall** You may slide each target up to 3 squares. Each target may be moved in a different direction.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Increase the damage to **2d6 cold damage**.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** A target slid by Chill Wind that ends the movement adjacent to blocking terrain falls prone.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Increase the damage to **3d6 cold damage**, and the burst creates a zone of freezing wind until the end of your next turn. The zone is difficult terrain for enemies.
