# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Attack ✦ Signature Strike

**ENCOUNTER DIVINE FEAR IMPLEMENT**

**Eligible Essences:** Omen, Reckoning, Shadow, Oath, Dominion

**Slot:** 3 – Signature Strike  
**Tier:** Encounter

Holy terror grips your foe and drives them from your presence.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Neutral
**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Range:** Ranged 10
**Cost:** 1 Mana
**Target:** One creature

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Wisdom vs. Will

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - The target moves its speed + your Charisma modifier away from you. The target avoids unsafe squares and difficult terrain if possible. This movement provokes opportunity attacks.

> [!Miss|background-color-yellow]
> - The target shifts 1 square away from you.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Heightened Panic** Increase the movement distance by 2 squares.
>> - **Rank 6 — Shaken Resolve** After completing the forced movement, the target takes a –2 penalty to its next attack roll before the end of its next turn.
>> - **Rank 9 — Overwhelming Dread** If the target ends its movement adjacent to one of your allies, it grants combat advantage until the end of its next turn.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** If the target ends its movement more than 5 squares away from you, it is slowed until the end of its next turn.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** If the target fails its next saving throw before the end of your next turn, it is also knocked prone.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** The first time the target re-enters a square within 5 squares of you before the end of the encounter, it takes radiant damage equal to your Wisdom modifier.
