# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Attack ✦ Compulsion

**AT-WILL PSYCHIC CHARM**

**Eligible Essences:** Hunt, Wolf, Bear, Ape, Fox, Cat, Dog, Claw

**Slot:** 1 – Core Concept  
**Tier:** At-Will

You awaken the predatory instinct buried beneath conscious thought, compelling your enemies to abandon caution and turn upon the nearest threat.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Stable  
**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Range:** Area burst 1 within 10 squares  
**Cost:** None  
**Target:** Each enemy in the burst


> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Power Rank vs. Will 

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - The target cannot gain Combat Advantage until the end of your next turn. In addition, on its next turn, if the target makes an attack that does not include the ally of yours nearest to it as a target, it takes psychic damage equal to 5 + your Wisdom modifier.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Primal Distraction** The target also takes a -1 penalty to Perception and Insight until the end of your next turn.
>> - **Rank 6 — Predatory Fixation** Increase the psychic damage for ignoring the nearest ally to 7 + your Wisdom modifier.
>> - **Rank 9 — Savage Compulsion** The target also grants Combat Advantage to your nearest ally until the end of the target's next turn.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Increase the psychic damage for ignoring the nearest ally to 10 + your Wisdom modifier.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** A target that triggers the psychic damage also grants Combat Advantage until the end of its next turn.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** The target takes the psychic damage the first time on its next turn that it attacks a creature other than your nearest ally or willingly moves farther away from that ally.
