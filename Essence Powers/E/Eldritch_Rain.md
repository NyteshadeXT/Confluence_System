# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`

Attack ✦ Signature Strike  

**ENCOUNTER ARCANE IMPLEMENT**

**Eligible Essences:** Air, Wind, Magic, Ranged, Swift, Gathering, Echo

**Slot:** 3 – Signature Strike  
**Tier:** Encounter

You loose a scattering of violet eldritch bolts that arc unpredictably between your foes.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Neutral → Erratic (unpredictable targeting patterns)
**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Range:** Ranged 10  
**Cost:** 1 Mana
**Targets:** One creature, or two creatures no more than 5 squares apart

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Essence Attack vs. Reflex

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 1d10 + your Essence ability modifier damage. You gain a bonus to each damage roll equal to your Intelligence modifier.

> [!Miss|background-color-yellow]
> - Half damage.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Flickering Bolts** If you target two creatures, gain a +1 bonus to both attack rolls.
>> - **Rank 6 — Unpredictable Arcana** If you hit two different targets, you gain a +1 bonus to AC until the start of your next turn.
>> - **Rank 9 — Fey Scatter** If both attacks hit, you may slide one of the targets 1 square.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Damage increases to 2d10 + ability modifier.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** Damage increases to 3d10 + ability modifier. If both targets are hit, they grant combat advantage until the end of your next turn.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Damage increases to 4d10 + ability modifier. If both attacks hit, you may teleport 3 squares.
