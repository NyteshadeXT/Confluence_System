# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Attack ✦ Core Concept

**At-Will Arcane Implement**

**Eligible Essences:** Magic, Force, Shadow, Lightning, Star, Mind, Dimension

**Slot:** 1 – Core Concept  
**Tier:** At-Will

You channel raw energy into a focused lance of destructive power.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Neutral
**Action Type:** Single Action  
**Range:** Ranged 10  
**Target:** One creature

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Power Rank vs. Reflex. This power counts as a ranged basic attack. When you gain this power, choose whether it uses Charisma or Constitution. That choice cannot be changed.

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 1d10 + your chosen ability modifier damage.

> [!Miss|background-color-yellow]
> - No damage.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Potent Channel** Increase the damage to 1d12 + your chosen ability modifier.
>> - **Rank 6 — Lingering Echo** If you hit the same target with Eldritch Blast on consecutive turns, the second hit deals additional damage equal to your chosen ability modifier (once per round).
>> - **Rank 9 — Forceful Surge** When you hit with this power, you may push the target 1 square.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Damage increases to 2d10 + chosen ability modifier.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** Damage increases to 3d10 + chosen ability modifier. Once per turn, when you hit, you may slide the target 1 square instead of pushing it.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Damage increases to 4d10 + chosen ability modifier. If the target is bloodied, this power deals additional force damage equal to your chosen ability modifier.
