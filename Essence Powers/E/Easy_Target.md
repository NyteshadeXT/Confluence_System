# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Attack ✦ Apex Ability

**Daily Martial Weapon Setup**

**Eligible Essences:** Fox, Hunt, Blade, Ranged  

**Slot:** 5 – Apex Ability  
**Tier:** Daily

A brutal, disorienting strike leaves your enemy reeling—perfectly positioned for everything that comes next.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Escalating
**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Range:** Melee or Ranged weapon
**Requirement:** You must be wielding a crossbow, a light blade, or a sling.
**Target:** One creature
**Cost:** 2 Stamina

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Essence Attack vs. AC

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 2[W] + your Dexterity modifier damage.  The target is slowed and grants combat advantage to you (save ends both).

> [!Miss|background-color-yellow]
> - Half damage (round down).  The target grants combat advantage to you until the end of your next turn.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Calculated Exposure** Increase the weapon damage to 3[W] + your Dexterity modifier.
>> - **Rank 6 — Predatory Focus** While the target grants combat advantage to you from this power, your attacks against it deal additional damage equal to your Dexterity modifier (once per turn).
>> - **Rank 9 — Unavoidable Quarry** If the target fails its first saving throw against this power’s effects, it is also knocked prone
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Damage increases to 4[W] + Dexterity modifier. The target grants combat advantage to you and your allies (save ends).
>> - **Silver / Epic:** Damage increases to 5[W] + Dexterity modifier. If the target is slowed by this power and moves, it takes damage equal to your Dexterity modifier (once per turn).
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Damage increases to 6[W] + Dexterity modifier. If the target fails two saving throws against this power’s effects, it becomes immobilized until the end of its next turn.
