# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Utility ✦ Arcane Mobility

**DAILY ARCANE**

**Eligible Essences:** Wind, Light, Momentum, Echo  

**Slot:** 2 – Utility / Passive  
**Tier:** Daily

Your form blurs and bends away from danger.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Neutral
**Action Type:** Move Action  
**Range:** Personal
**Cost:** 2 Mana

> [!Effect|background-color-blue]
> You shift up to twice your speed.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Blurred Motion** After shifting, you gain a +2 power bonus to AC and Reflex until the start of your next turn.
>> - **Rank 6 — Unbound Momentum** During this movement, you ignore difficult terrain.
>> - **Rank 9 — Slip the Net** When you use this power, you may make a saving throw against one effect that a save can end before or after the movement.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** You may shift up to **three times your speed**.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** After shifting, you are insubstantial until the start of your next turn.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** After shifting, you may immediately take a standard action, but you cannot use a Daily power as part of that action.
