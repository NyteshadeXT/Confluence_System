# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Attack ✦ Signature Strike

**Encounter Martial Weapon Mobility**

**Eligible Essences:** Swift, Hunt, Cat, Fox, Ranged  

**Slot:** 3 – Signature Strike  
**Tier:** Encounter

You flow through the battlefield with practiced ease, striking while never remaining where danger expects you to be.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Neutral
**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Range:** Melee or Ranged weapon
**Target:** One creature
**Cost:** Cost: 1 Stamina

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Melee: Power Rank vs. AC
> - Ranged: Power Rank vs. AC

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 2[W] + your Essence ability modifier damage. You may shift a number of squares equal to 1 + your Wisdom modifier either before or after the attack.

> [!Miss|background-color-yellow]
> - Half damage (round down).

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Fluid Assault** If you shift at least 2 squares as part of this power, you gain a +2 power bonus to the attack roll.
>> - **Rank 6 — Elusive Footwork** After resolving the attack, you gain a +2 power bonus to AC against opportunity attacks until the end of your turn.
>> - **Rank 9 — Vanishing Vector** If you hit, you may shift an additional 1 square. If you miss, you may still shift 1 square.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Damage increases to 3[W] + Essence ability modifier.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** Damage increases to 4[W] + Essence ability modifier. If you shift before the attack, you gain combat advantage for that attack.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Damage increases to 5[W] + Essence ability modifier. If the attack hits, you may shift up to half your speed instead of the normal shift granted by this power.
