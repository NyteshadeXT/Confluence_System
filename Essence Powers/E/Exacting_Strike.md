# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Attack ✦ Core Concept

**Encounter Martial Weapon Precision**

**Eligible Essences:** Blade, Steel, Adept, Hunt  

**Slot:** 3 – Signature Strike  
**Tier:** Encounter

You commit fully to a precise, disciplined strike, sacrificing power for certainty.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Neutral
**Action Type:** Standard Action
**Range:** Melee weapon
**Target:** One creature
**Cost:** 1 Stamina

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Power Rank +2 vs. AC (Strength or Dexterity modifier chosen when this power is unlocked)

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 2[W] + your Essence ability modifier damage. This power cannot gain additional weapon damage dice beyond its listed [W] value.  Flat modifiers, penalties to the target, and vulnerability still apply.

> [!Miss|background-color-yellow]
> - No damage.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Disciplined Aim** Increase the attack bonus to +3 instead of +2.
>> - **Rank 6 — Perfect Form** If this power hits, you gain a +2 power bonus to your next attack roll against the same target before the end of your next turn.
>> - **Rank 9 — Unerring Blow** If this power misses, you deal damage equal to your Essence ability modifier.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Damage increases to 3[W] + Essence ability modifier.  The attack bonus remains +3.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** Damage increases to 4[W] + Essence ability modifier. If the attack hits, the target takes a –2 penalty to AC against your next attack before the end of your next turn.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Damage increases to 5[W] + Essence ability modifier. If this power hits a bloodied target, it deals additional damage equal to your Essence ability modifier (once per use).
