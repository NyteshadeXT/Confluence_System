# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Utility ✦ Mobility

**Encounter Arcane Teleportation**

**Eligible Essences:** Veil, Shadow, Star, Moon, Freedom, Ascendence

**Slot:** 2 – Utility / Passive  
**Tier:** Encounter

You slip momentarily out of phase with reality before reappearing elsewhere.

**Action Type:** Move Action  
**Range:** Personal

> [!Effect|background-color-blue]
> You teleport 3 squares. You gain a +2 power bonus to all defenses until the end of your next turn.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Phased Step** Increase the teleport distance to 4 squares.
>> - **Rank 6 — Untouchable Transit** After teleporting, you gain resistance to all damage equal to your Intelligence or Charisma modifier (choose when you gain this power) until the end of your next turn.
>> - **Rank 9 — Slipping Between Moments** You may teleport through occupied squares and difficult terrain without restriction. If you teleport adjacent to an enemy, you gain combat advantage against that enemy until the end of your turn.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Teleport distance increases to 5 squares. Defense bonus increases to +3.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** Teleport distance increases to 6 squares. You may teleport as a free action once per encounter when an enemy misses you with an attack.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Teleport distance increases to 8 squares. After teleporting, you are insubstantial until the start of your next turn.
