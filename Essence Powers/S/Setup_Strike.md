# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Attack ✦ Signature Strike  

**ENCOUNTER MARTIAL WEAPON**

**Eligible Essences:** Blade, Shadow, Eye, Hunt, Ambush

**Slot:** 3 – Signature Strike  
**Tier:** Encounter

You strike with calculated intent, opening your foe’s defenses for a decisive follow-up.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Neutral
**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Range:** Melee weapon  
**Cost:** 1 Stamina
**Requirement:** You must be wielding a light blade.
**Target:** One creature

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Essence Attack vs. AC (Dexterity-based)

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 2d8 + Intelligence modifier acid damage, and ongoing 5 acid damage (save ends).
> 
> - **Secondary Attack:** 2[W] + your Essence ability modifier damage. The target grants combat advantage to you until the end of your next turn.

> [!Miss|background-color-yellow]
> - Half damage. No advantage.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Opened Guard** While the target grants you combat advantage from this power, you gain a +1 bonus to attack rolls against it.
>> - **Rank 6 — Exploit Weakness** Your first attack against the target while it grants you combat advantage deals additional damage equal to your Dexterity modifier.
>> - **Rank 9 — Perfect Setup** If you hit the target again before the end of your next turn, it continues to grant combat advantage until the end of that turn.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Damage increases to 3[W] + ability modifier.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** Damage increases to 4[W] + ability modifier. The target grants combat advantage to all allies until the end of your next turn.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Damage increases to 5[W] + ability modifier. If the target is already granting combat advantage, it instead takes a –2 penalty to AC.
