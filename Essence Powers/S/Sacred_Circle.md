# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Utility ✦ Utility / Passive  

**DAILY DIVINE ZONE**

**Eligible Essences:** Rune, Light, Order, Dog, Dominion, Equilibrium

**Slot:** 2 – Utility / Passive  
**Tier:** Daily

You inscribe a radiant boundary of divine protection, sanctifying the ground itself.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Escalating (persistent zone alters Dungeon flow)

**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Range:** Close burst 3

**Cost:** 1 Mana

> [!Effect|background-color-blue]
> The burst creates a zone that lasts until the end of the encounter. You and allies within the zone gain a +1 power bonus to AC.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Reinforced Circle** Increase the bonus to AC to +2.
>> - **Rank 6 — Anchored Ground** Allies in the zone gain a +1 bonus to saving throws.
>> - **Rank 9 — Sanctified Defense** Enemies entering the zone for the first time each turn take radiant damage equal to your Charisma modifier.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** AC bonus increases to +3.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** Allies in the zone gain resistance to damage equal to your Charisma modifier.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Enemies in the zone take a –2 penalty to attack rolls.
