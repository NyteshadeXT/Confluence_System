# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Attack ✦ Signature Strike

**Encounter Martial Weapon Control**

**Eligible Essences:** Blade, Snake, Steel, Hunt, Adept  

**Slot:** 3 – Signature Strike  
**Tier:** Encounter

You drive your weapon into a joint, tendon, or pressure point, crippling your foe’s movement with precise, vicious intent.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Neutral
**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Range:** Melee weapon
**Target:** One creature
**Cost:** Cost: 1 Stamina

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Power Rank vs. AC

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 2[W] + your Essence ability modifier damage. The target is slowed and cannot shift until the end of your next turn.

> [!Miss|background-color-yellow]
> - Half damage (round down). The target is slowed until the end of your next turn.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Piercing Disablement** Increase the weapon damage to 3[W] + your Essence ability modifier.
>> - **Rank 6 — Tendon Lock** If the target is bloodied when hit, it also takes a –2 penalty to attack rolls until the end of your next turn.
>> - **Rank 9 — Serpent’s Grasp** If the target fails its first saving throw against an effect you impose before the end of your next turn, it remains slowed until the end of its next turn after that save.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Damage increases to 4[W] + Essence ability modifier.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** Damage increases to 5[W] + Essence ability modifier. If the target is slowed by this power, its speed is reduced to 2 until the end of your next turn.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Damage increases to 6[W] + Essence ability modifier. If the target is bloodied and hit, it is immobilized until the end of your next turn instead of slowed.
