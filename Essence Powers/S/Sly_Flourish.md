**At-Will Martial Weapon Deception**

**Eligible Essences:** Fox, Blade, Cat, Adept, Eye  

**Slot:** 1 – Core Concept  
**Tier:** At-Will

A flourish, a feint, and a smile—your foe never notices the real strike until it’s too late.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Neutral
**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Range:** Melee or Ranged weapon
**Requirement:** You must be wielding a crossbow, a light blade, or a sling.
**Target:** One creature

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Essence Attack vs. AC (Dexterity)

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 1[W] + your Dexterity modifier + your secondary ability modifier damage  
(secondary ability chosen when this power is unlocked).

> [!Miss|background-color-yellow]
> - No damage.

>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Polished Deception** Increase the damage to 1[W] + Dexterity modifier + secondary ability modifier + 2.
>> - **Rank 6 — Cutting Wit** If you have combat advantage against the target, this attack deals additional damage equal to your secondary ability modifier.
>> - **Rank 9 — Perfect Misdirection** If this attack hits while you have combat advantage, the target grants combat advantage to you until the end of your next turn.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Damage increases to 2[W] + Dexterity modifier + secondary ability modifier.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** Damage increases to 3[W] + Dexterity modifier + secondary ability modifier. If you have combat advantage, the target takes a –2 penalty to attack rolls until the end of its next turn.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Damage increases to 4[W] + Dexterity modifier + secondary ability modifier. If this attack reduces the target to 0 HP, you may shift up to half your speed.
