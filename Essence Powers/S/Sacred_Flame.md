# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Attack ✦ Core Concept

**AT-WILL DIVINE RADIANT IMPLEMENT**

**Eligible Essences:** Light, Life, Oath, Balance  

**Slot:** 1 – Core Concept  
**Tier:** At-Will

Radiant light sears the wicked and sustains the faithful.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Stable
**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Range:** Ranged 5
**Target:** One creature

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Wisdom vs. Reflex

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 1d6 + Wisdom modifier radiant damage. Choose one ally you can see. That ally either:
> 	- Gains temporary hit points equal to your Charisma modifier, or
> 	- Immediately makes a saving throw.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Brightened Grace** Increase the damage to 1d8 + your Wisdom modifier.
>> - **Rank 6 — Sustaining Light** If the chosen ally gains temporary hit points, increase the amount by your Wisdom modifier.
>> - **Rank 9 — Cleansing Radiance** If the chosen ally makes a saving throw and succeeds, it gains a +2 power bonus to its next attack roll before the end of its next turn.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Damage increases to 2d6 + Wisdom modifier.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** Damage increases to 3d6 + Wisdom modifier. If the chosen ally is bloodied, it gains both temporary hit points and a saving throw.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Damage increases to 4d6 + Wisdom modifier. If the attack reduces the target to 0 HP, one ally within 5 squares regains hit points equal to your Wisdom modifier.
