# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Attack ✦ Signature Strike  

**ENCOUNTER MARTIAL WEAPON**

**Eligible Essences:** Hunt, Venom, Shadow, Swift, Eye, Wasp

**Slot:** 3 – Signature Strike  
**Tier:** Encounter

You strike with sudden, lethal precision, exploiting the exact moment your quarry is exposed.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Neutral
**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Range:** Melee or Ranged weapon  
**Cost:** 1 Stamina
**Target:** One creature that is your quarry

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Essence Attack vs. Reflex; Strength (melee); Dexterity (ranged)

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 2[W] + your Essence ability modifier damage.

> [!Miss|background-color-yellow]
> - Half damage.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Lethal Precision** Gain a +1 bonus to the attack roll against your quarry.
>> - **Rank 6 — Exposed Vital Point** If the target is your quarry, it takes a –2 penalty to AC until the start of your next turn.
>> - **Rank 9 — Relentless Sting** If the target is bloodied, deal additional damage equal to your Wisdom modifier.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Damage increases to 3[W] + ability modifier.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** Damage increases to 4[W] + ability modifier. Against your quarry, this attack gains a +2 bonus to hit.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Damage increases to 5[W] + ability modifier. If the target is your quarry and bloodied, it is slowed until the end of your next turn.
