# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Attack ✦ Tactical Strike

**Encounter Martial Weapon**

**Eligible Essences:** Blade, Bludgeon, Might, Momentum, Swift, Storm

**Slot:** 4 – Tactical Strike  
**Tier:** Encounter

You slip beneath your enemy’s guard with a low, sweeping strike, cutting and then hooking their legs out from under them in one fluid motion.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Neutral
**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Range:** Melee weapon
**Target:** One creature
**Cost:** Cost: 1 Stamina

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Essence Attack vs. AC

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 1[W] + your Essence ability modifier damage, and the target is knocked prone.

> [!Miss|background-color-yellow]
> - No damage. The target takes a –2 penalty to its next attack roll before the end of its next turn.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Driving Arc** Increase the weapon damage to 2[W] + your Essence ability modifier.
>> - **Rank 6 — Crushing Follow-Through** If the target is prone after the hit, it takes additional damage equal to your Essence ability modifier.
>> - **Rank 9 — Ground Control** If the target stands before the end of its next turn, it provokes an opportunity attack from you.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Damage increases to 3[W] + Essence ability modifier.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** Damage increases to 4[W] + Essence ability modifier. If the target is bloodied and hit, it is slowed until the end of its next turn after standing.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Damage increases to 5[W] + Essence ability modifier. If the target fails its first saving throw against an effect you impose while prone from this power, it remains prone until the end of its next turn.
