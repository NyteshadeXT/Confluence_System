# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Utility ✦ Utility / Passive  

**PSIONIC  MIND  TELEPORTATION**

**Eligible Essences:** Mind, Dimension, Wind, Lightning, Gravity

**Slot:** 2 – Utility / Passive  
**Tier:** Conditional

Your mind moves before the battle begins, placing your body where it is needed before your foes can react.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Dimension

**Action Type:** Free Action  
**Range:** Personal  
**Cost:** None

**Trigger:** You roll initiative.

> [!Effect|background-color-blue]
> Before the first creature takes a turn, you Shift up to 3 squares. You can use this power even if you are surprised.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Swift Thought** Increase the distance to 4 squares.
>> - **Rank 6 — Thought Step** You may teleport instead of Shifting.
>> - **Rank 9 — First Into Battle** Increase the distance to 6 squares.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** After moving with this power, you may mark one creature you can see within 5 squares.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** You may bring one willing adjacent ally when you teleport with this power; that ally appears in an unoccupied space adjacent to you.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** After you move with this power, you may make one weapon Strike or use one Slot 1 power as a free action.
