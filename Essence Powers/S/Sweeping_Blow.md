# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Attack ✦ Tactical Strike

**ENCOUNTER MARTIAL WEAPON**

**Eligible Essences:** Bludgeon, Blade, Might, Momentum, Force, Steel, Storm

**Slot:** 4 – Tactical Strike  
**Tier:** Encounter

You commit to a wide, crushing swing that batters all foes within reach.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Neutral
**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Range:** Close burst 1
**Target:** Each enemy in burst you can see
**Cost:** 1 Stamina

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Essence Attack vs. AC. If you are wielding an axe, flail, heavy blade, or pick, you gain a bonus to the attack roll equal to one-half your Strength modifier.

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 1[W] + your Essence ability modifier damage.

> [!Miss|background-color-yellow]
> - No damage.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Driven Arc** Gain a +1 bonus to attack rolls against targets adjacent to at least one ally.
>> - **Rank 6 — Disrupting Sweep** Creatures hit take a –2 penalty to attack rolls until the start of your next turn.
>> - **Rank 9 — Overbearing Swing** If you hit two or more creatures, you may push one of them 1 square.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Damage increases to 2[W] + ability modifier.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** Damage increases to 3[W] + ability modifier. Creatures hit are knocked prone if they are bloodied.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Damage increases to 4[W] + ability modifier. You may shift 1 square after resolving the attack.
