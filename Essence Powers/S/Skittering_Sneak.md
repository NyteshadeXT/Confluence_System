# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`

Utility ✦ Infiltration Transformation

**DAILY POLYMORPH STEALTH**

**Eligible Essences:** Spider, Bat, Cat, Fox, Snake, Lizard, Wasp

**Slot:** 2 – Utility / Passive  
**Tier:** Daily

You compress your physical presence into a small, unobtrusive manifestation of your Essence, sacrificing combat capability to slip through places where your normal form would draw attention.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Stable  
**Action Type:** Free Action  
**Range:** Personal  
**Cost:** 1 Stamina  
**Target:** Self

> [!Effect|background-color-blue]
> Until the end of the encounter, you may assume or dismiss a Skittering Form as a Single Action.
> 
> Your Skittering Form is a dramatically reduced, bestial manifestation appropriate to the Essence expressing this power. A Spider Essence might produce an arachnid-like form, Snake a small serpentine form, Bat a hunched winged form, and Cat a compact feline form. This is an Essence-shaped transformation rather than a complete transformation into a natural animal.
> 
> While in Skittering Form:
> - Your size becomes **Tiny**.
> - You gain a **+5 power bonus to Stealth checks**.
> - You cannot make attacks.
> - You cannot use attack powers.
> - You cannot pick up or carry objects.
> - You cannot manipulate weapons, implements, tools, or complex objects.
> - Your equipment merges into your form and cannot be accessed or activated.
> - You continue to gain passive benefits from merged equipment.
> - Your attributes, defenses, Hit Points, Mana, Stamina, Speed, and movement modes otherwise remain unchanged.
> - Skittering Form does not inherently grant flight, climbing, swimming, special senses, or other capabilities associated with the creature represented by your Essence.
> 
> You may dismiss Skittering Form at any time during your turn as a Minor Action, returning to your normal form.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Diminished Presence** Creatures take a -2 penalty to Perception checks made to detect you while you remain still.
>> - **Rank 6 — Scurrying Passage** You may move through spaces occupied by creatures larger than you, though you cannot end your movement in an occupied square.
>> - **Rank 9 — Almost Unseen** When you end a move with cover or concealment, you may immediately make a Stealth check to become Hidden.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon — True Skittering Form:** Your transformation becomes complete. Instead of an Essence-shaped approximation, you may fully transform into a Tiny natural or Essence-infused creature appropriate to the Essence expressing this power. Spider may become a small spider, Cat a house cat, Bat a tiny bat, Snake a small serpent, and similar forms may be chosen for other eligible Essences. All other restrictions of Skittering Form remain.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** While in Skittering Form, mundane terrain appropriate to your creature does not count as Difficult Terrain. This does not grant movement modes you do not otherwise possess.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Assuming or dismissing Skittering Form becomes a Free Action, though you may still change forms only once per round.
