# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Utility ✦ Sacred Bulwark

**DAILY DIVINE**

**Eligible Essences:** Oath, Shield, Light, Life, Harmony 

**Slot:** 2 – Utility / Passive  
**Tier:** Daily

Radiant force surrounds you and your allies like a luminous shield wall.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Neutral
**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Range:** Close burst 5
**Cost:** 2 Mana
**Targets:** You and each ally in burst

> [!Effect|background-color-blue]
> The targets gain a +2 power bonus to AC until the end of the encounter.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Strengthened Aegis** Increase the AC bonus to +3.
>> - **Rank 6 — Radiant Intercession** Once per round, when a protected ally is hit by an attack, reduce the damage by your Charisma modifier.
>> - **Rank 9 — Consecrated Bulwark** If a protected ally is bloodied, their AC bonus increases by +1 until they are no longer bloodied.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** The AC bonus increases to +4.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** The AC bonus increases to +5. All affected allies gain a +2 power bonus to saving throws.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** The AC bonus increases to +6. Once per encounter, when an affected ally would be reduced to 0 HP, they instead remain at 1 HP and the AC bonus for that ally ends.
