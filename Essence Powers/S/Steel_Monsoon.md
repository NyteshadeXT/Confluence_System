# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Attack ✦ Tactical Strike  

**ENCOUNTER MARTIAL WEAPON**

**Eligible Essences:** Blade, Steel, Swift, Momentum, Order, Sound, Gathering

**Slot:** 4 – Tactical Strike  
**Tier:** Encounter

You plunge into the fray in a whirling storm of steel, your motions drawing enemy focus just long enough for your allies to reposition.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Neutral
**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Range:** Melee weapon  
**Cost:** 1 Stamina
**Target:** One creature

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Essence Attack vs. AC (Strength-based)

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 2[W] + your Essence ability modifier damage. One ally within 5 squares may shift 1 square.
> 
> **Special (Intelligence-aligned builds):**  A number of allies equal to your Intelligence modifier may shift instead.

> [!Miss|background-color-yellow]
> - Half damage. One ally may still shift 1 square.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Flowing Advance** Each affected ally gains a +1 bonus to AC until the start of your next turn after shifting.
>> - **Rank 6 — Coordinated Movement** Affected allies may shift an additional 1 square if they end adjacent to an enemy.
>> - **Rank 9 — Relentless Tempo** If two or more allies shift, you gain a +1 bonus to attack rolls until the end of your next turn.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Damage increases to 3[W] + ability modifier.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** Damage increases to 4[W] + ability modifier. Affected allies may shift before or after your attack resolves.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Damage increases to 5[W] + ability modifier. Affected allies gain a +1 bonus to attack rolls until the end of your next turn.
