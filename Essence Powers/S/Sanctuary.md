# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Utility ✦ Sacred Ward

**ENCOUNTER DIVINE**

**Eligible Essences:** Oath, Light, Balance, Harmony

**Slot:** 2 – Utility / Passive  
**Tier:** Encounter

A divine ward shields the faithful from harm.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Stable
**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Range:** Ranged 10
**Cost:** 1 Mana
**Target:** You or one creature

> [!Effect|background-color-blue]
> The target gains a +5 power bonus to all defenses. The effect lasts until the target makes an attack or until the end of your next turn.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Radiant Shelter** If the effect ends because the target makes an attack, the target gains resistance to all damage equal to your Charisma modifier until the end of its turn.
>> - **Rank 6 — Steadfast Ward** While the effect is active, the target gains a +2 power bonus to saving throws.
>> - **Rank 9 — Consecrated Haven** If the target is bloodied when you use this power, increase the defense bonus to +6.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** The defense bonus increases to +6.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** The defense bonus increases to +7. If the effect ends because the target is attacked (hit or miss), the attacker takes radiant damage equal to your Wisdom modifier (once per use).
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** The defense bonus increases to +8. If the target does not make an attack before the effect ends naturally, they regain hit points equal to your Charisma modifier.
