# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Utility ✦ Arcane Defense

**ENCOUNTER ARCANE FORCE**

**Eligible Essences:** Force, Light, Balance, Echo  

**Slot:** 2 – Utility / Passive  
**Tier:** Encounter

A disc of shimmering force interposes itself between you and harm.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Stable
**Action Type:** Immediate Interrupt  
**Range:** Personal
**Cost:** 1 Mana
**Trigger:** You are hit by an attack

> [!Effect|background-color-blue]
> You gain a +4 power bonus to AC and Reflex defense until the end of your next turn.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Reinforced Barrier** Increase the bonus to +5.
>> - **Rank 6 — Reactive Force** If the triggering attack still hits after applying the bonus, you gain resistance to all damage from that attack equal to your Intelligence modifier.
>> - **Rank 9 — Deflecting Disc** If the triggering attack misses because of this power, the attacker takes force damage equal to your Intelligence modifier.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** The defense bonus increases to +6.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** The defense bonus increases to +7. If the triggering attack misses, you may shift 1 square as a free action.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** The defense bonus increases to **+8**. If the triggering attack misses, the attacker is pushed 1 square.
