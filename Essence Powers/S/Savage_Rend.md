# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`

Attack ✦ Bestial Assault

**AT-WILL BEAST**

**Eligible Essences:** Bear, Cat, Ape, Lizard, Hunt, Might

**Slot:** 1 – Core Concept  
**Tier:** At-Will

Savage claws manifest as you rake across your foe, using the force of the attack to drive your prey exactly where you want it.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Stable  
**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Range:** Melee touch  
**Cost:** None  
**Target:** One creature

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Power Attack vs. Reflex

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 1d8 + Wisdom modifier damage, and you slide the target 1 square.
> 
> If your current form does not possess claws or equivalent natural weapons, they manifest as part of the attack and disappear immediately afterward. Savage Rend can be used as a Melee Basic Attack.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Driving Rend** You may slide the target up to 2 squares.
>> - **Rank 6 — Raking Advance** After sliding the target, you may shift 1 square into a square it vacated.
>> - **Rank 9 — Savage Positioning** Increase the slide to 3 squares. You may shift 1 square after the slide, provided you end adjacent to the target.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Increase the damage to **2d8 + Wisdom modifier**.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** If you end the attack adjacent to the target after sliding it, the target grants Combat Advantage to you until the end of your next turn.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Increase the damage to **3d8 + Wisdom modifier**, and you may shift up to 2 squares after sliding the target.
