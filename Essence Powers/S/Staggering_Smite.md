# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Attack ✦ Tactical Strike  

**ENCOUNTER DIVINE WEAPON**

**Eligible Essences:** Might, Force, Oath, Dominion, Momentum

**Slot:** 4 – Tactical Strike  
**Tier:** Encounter

You deliver a crushing blow that drives your enemy backward under the force of divine judgment.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Neutral → Escalating (repeated forced movement draws response)
**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Range:** Melee weapon  
**Cost:** 1 Mana
**Target:** One creature

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Essence Attack vs. AC (Strength-based)

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 2d8 + Intelligence modifier acid damage, and ongoing 5 acid damage (save ends).
> 
> - **Secondary Attack:** 2[W] + your Essence ability modifier damage, and you push the target a number of squares equal to your Wisdom modifier.

> [!Miss|background-color-yellow]
> - Half damage. No push.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Driving Force** Increase the push distance by 1 square.
>> - **Rank 6 — Disruptive Impact** If the target is pushed 3 or more squares, it takes a –2 penalty to attack rolls until the end of its next turn.
>> - **Rank 9 — Relentless Momentum** If the target collides with a creature or obstacle during the push, it takes additional damage equal to your Strength modifier.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Damage increases to 3[W] + ability modifier.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** Damage increases to 4[W] + ability modifier. If the target is pushed at least 2 squares, it is knocked prone.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Damage increases to 5[W] + ability modifier. If the target is bloodied and pushed, it is also dazed until the end of your next turn.
