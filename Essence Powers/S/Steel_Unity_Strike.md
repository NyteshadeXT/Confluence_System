# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Attack ✦ Apex Ability  

**PSIONIC • STANCE • WEAPON**

**Eligible Essences:** Blade, Dog, Iron, Oath, Polearm, Resolute, Shield, Steel

**Slot:** 5 – Apex Ability
**Tier:** Daily

You become one with your weapon, delivering a devastating stroke and adopting a stance that punishes any foe that tries to slip past you.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Iron

**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Range:** Melee weapon  
**Cost:** 3 Mana

**Target:** One creature

> [!Effect|background-color-blue]
> - You assume the steel unity stance until the end of the encounter.

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Power Rank vs. AC

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 3[W] + your Essence ability modifier damage.

> [!Miss|background-color-yellow]
> - Half damage.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Guarded Unity** While in the stance, you gain a +1 bonus to AC against attacks made by adjacent enemies.
>> - **Rank 6 — Hammering Reprisal** A creature hit by the stance's reprisal Strike is pushed 1 square.
>> - **Rank 9 — Relentless Unity** Increase the reprisal Strike's damage to 3[W] + your Essence ability modifier.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** You may make the stance's reprisal Strike even if the triggering enemy began its movement 2 squares away from you.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** A creature hit by the reprisal Strike is off-guard until the end of its next turn.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** If the reprisal Strike is a critical hit, the target is stunned until the end of its next turn.

> [!note] Steel Unity Reprisal
> **Opportunity Action** — **Trigger:** An adjacent enemy moves away from you without Shifting.  
> **Target:** The triggering enemy.  
> **Attack:** Power Rank vs. AC.  
> **Hit:** 2[W] + your Essence ability modifier damage.
