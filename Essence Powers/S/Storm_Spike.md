# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`

Attack ✦ Elemental Assault

**AT-WILL LIGHTNING**

**Eligible Essences:** Lightning, Storm, Air, Wind, Elemental

**Slot:** 1 – Core Concept  
**Tier:** At-Will

A concentrated bolt of lightning spears your foe and leaves the surrounding air dangerously charged. Remaining in place invites a second discharge.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Stable  
**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Range:** Ranged 10  
**Cost:** None  
**Target:** One creature

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Power Rank vs. Reflex

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 1d8 + Wisdom modifier lightning damage. If the target does not move at least 2 squares from its current position during its next turn, it takes additional lightning damage equal to your Wisdom modifier at the end of that turn.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Charged Air** Increase the secondary lightning damage to 2 + your Wisdom modifier.
>> - **Rank 6 — Gathering Charge** The target must move at least 3 squares during its next turn to avoid the secondary damage.
>> - **Rank 9 — Violent Discharge** If the secondary damage is triggered, each enemy adjacent to the target takes lightning damage equal to your Wisdom modifier.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Increase the initial damage to 2d8 + Wisdom modifier.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** A target that triggers the secondary damage also grants Combat Advantage until the end of its next turn.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Increase the initial damage to 3d8 + Wisdom modifier. The secondary discharge affects each enemy adjacent to the target for the full secondary damage.
