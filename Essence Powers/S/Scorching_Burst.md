# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Attack ✦ Core Concept

**AT-WILL ARCANE FIRE IMPLEMENT**

**Eligible Essences:** Fire, Light, Omen, Reckoning, Momentum, Storm

**Slot:** 1 – Core Concept  
**Tier:** At-Will

A column of flame erupts and sears all within.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Stable
**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Range:** Area burst 1 within 10 squares
**Target:** Each creature in burst

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Intelligence vs. Reflex

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 1d6 + Intelligence modifier fire damage.

> [!Miss|background-color-yellow]
> - Half damage to the primary target and ongoing 2 acid damage (save ends). No secondary attack.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Intensified Spark** Increase the damage to 1d8 + your Intelligence modifier.
>> - **Rank 6 — Lingering Heat** Creatures hit by this power take fire damage equal to your Intelligence modifier the first time they enter the burst area before the start of your next turn.
>> - **Rank 9 — Blazing Surge** If at least two creatures are hit, one creature of your choice takes additional fire damage equal to your Intelligence modifier.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Damage increases to 2d6 + Intelligence modifier.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** CDamage increases to 3d6 + Intelligence modifier. Creatures hit are slowed until the end of their next turn.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Damage increases to 4d6 + Intelligence modifier. If three or more creatures are hit, the burst creates difficult terrain until the end of your next turn.
