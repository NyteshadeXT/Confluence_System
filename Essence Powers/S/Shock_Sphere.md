# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Attack ✦ Signature Strike

**ENCOUNTER ARCANE LIGHTNING IMPLEMENT**

**Eligible Essences:** Lightning, Air, Sound, Magic, Force, Gathering

**Slot:** 3 – Signature Strike  
**Tier:** Encounter

You hurl a crackling sphere of lightning that detonates in a violent surge, electrifying everything nearby.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Escalating
**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Range:** Area burst 2 within 10 squares  
**Cost:** 1 Mana
**Target:** Each creature in burst

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Essence Attack vs. Reflex (Intelligence-based)

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 2d6 + your Essence ability modifier lightning damage.

> [!Miss|background-color-yellow]
> - Half damage.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Charged Detonation** Increase damage to 3d6 + ability modifier.
>> - **Rank 6 — Lingering Charge** Creatures hit take a –2 penalty to Reflex until the start of your next turn.
>> - **Rank 9 — Conductive Surge** If two or more creatures are hit, one target takes additional lightning damage equal to your Intelligence modifier.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Damage increases to 4d6 + ability modifier.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** Damage increases to 5d6 + ability modifier. Creatures hit grant combat advantage until the end of your next turn.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Damage increases to 6d6 + ability modifier. The burst becomes lightly electrified terrain until the end of your next turn.
