# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`

Attack ✦ Bestial Onslaught

**DAILY BEAST**

**Eligible Essences:** Bear, Wolf, Cat, Ape, Crocodile, Dog, Hunt, Might, Devouring, Claw

**Slot:** 5 – Apex Ability
**Tier:** Daily

Instinct overwhelms restraint. In a blur of claw, fang, and brutal motion, you tear through every enemy within reach before they can recover.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Elevated
**Action Type:** Standard Action
**Range:** Close burst 1
**Cost:** 1 Stamina
**Target:** Each enemy in the burst you can see

If your current form does not possess appropriate natural weapons, claws, fangs, or equivalent; bestial features manifest for the duration of the attack.

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Power Rank vs. Reflex

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 1d6 + Wisdom modifier damage, and the target is Dazed and Slowed (save ends both).

> [!Miss|background-color-yellow]
> - Half damage, and the target is Slowed until the end of your next turn.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Unrestrained Assault** After resolving Savage Frenzy, you may shift 1 square for each target you hit, to a maximum of 3 squares.
>> - **Rank 6 — Predatory Violence** A target hit by Savage Frenzy grants Combat Advantage while it remains Dazed by the power.
>> - **Rank 9 — Frenzied Predator** When a target fails its first saving throw against Savage Frenzy, it takes additional damage equal to your Wisdom modifier.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Increase the damage to 2d6 + Wisdom modifier.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** Increase the burst to Close burst 2. A target that saves against the Dazed and Slowed conditions remains Slowed until the end of its next turn.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Increase the damage to 3d6 + Wisdom modifier. After resolving the attack, you may shift up to your Speed, provided every square of the movement brings you closer to an enemy.
