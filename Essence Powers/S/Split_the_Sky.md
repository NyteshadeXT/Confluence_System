# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Attack ✦ Signature Strike  

**ENCOUNTER DIVINE THUNDER WEAPON**

**Eligible Essences:** Sound, Force, Might, Sun, Oath, Dominion  

**Slot:** 3 – Signature Strike  
**Tier:** Encounter

You call down thunder as your weapon strikes, blasting your foe from their footing.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Neutral
**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Range:** Melee weapon  
**Cost:** 1 Stamina
**Target:** One creature

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Strength vs. Fortitude

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 1[W] + Strength modifier thunder damage.  You push the target 2 squares and knock it prone.

> [!Miss|background-color-yellow]
> - You push the target 1 square.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Rolling Thunder** Increase damage to 2[W] + Strength modifier.
>> - **Rank 6 — Reverberating Shock** Increase push to 3 squares.
>> - **Rank 9 — Shattering Impact** If the target collides with a wall or creature during the push, it takes additional thunder damage equal to your Strength modifier.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Damage increases to 3[W] + Strength modifier.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** The target is dazed until the end of your next turn after being knocked prone.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** You create a close burst 1 thunder shockwave centered on the target after the push; enemies in the burst take thunder damage equal to your Strength modifier and are knocked prone.
