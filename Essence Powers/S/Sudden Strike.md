# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Attack ✦ Apex Ability

**Daily Martial Weapon Combo**

**Eligible Essences:** Blade, Fox, Momentum, Wolf  

**Slot:** 5 – Apex Ability  
**Tier:** Daily

You stagger your foe with a vicious feint, then roll aside and drive your real strike home as their guard collapses.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Escalating
**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Range:** Melee weapon
**Requirement:** You must be wielding two melee weapons.
**Target:** One creature
**Cost:** 2 Stamina

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Essence Attack vs. AC (off-hand weapon)
> - **Secondary Attack:** Essence Attack vs. AC (main-hand weapon)

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 1[W] damage. You shift 1 square and make a secondary attack against the target.
> 
> - **Secondary Attack:** 2[W] + your Essence ability modifier damage. The target is weakened until the end of your next turn.

> [!Miss|background-color-yellow]
> - Half damage for that attack (round down).  Applies to either attack.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Fluid Execution** Increase the secondary attack damage to 3[W] + your Essence ability modifier.
>> - **Rank 6 — Collapsing Guard** If both attacks hit, the target grants combat advantage until the end of your next turn.
>> - **Rank 9 — Predator’s Sequence** If both attacks hit, the target is also slowed until the end of your next turn.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Secondary attack damage increases to 4[W] + Essence ability modifier.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** Secondary attack damage increases to 5[W] + Essence ability modifier. If both attacks hit, the target is weakened (save ends) instead of until the end of your next turn.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Secondary attack damage increases to 6[W] + Essence ability modifier. If both attacks hit, you may immediately shift up to your speed after resolving the attack sequence.
