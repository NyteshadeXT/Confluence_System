# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Attack ✦ Apex Ability

**DAILY ARCANE SLEEP IMPLEMENT**

**Eligible Essences:** Omen, Shadow, Dream, Mind

**Slot:** 5 – Apex Ability  
**Tier:** Daily

A wave of magical exhaustion crashes over your foes.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Escalating
**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Range:** Area burst 2 within 20 squares
**Cost:** 2 Mana
**Target:** Each creature in burst

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Intelligence vs. Will

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - The target is slowed (save ends). If the target fails its first saving throw against this power, it becomes unconscious (save ends).

> [!Miss|background-color-yellow]
> - The target is slowed (save ends).

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Deepening Lull** Creatures that fail their first saving throw against this power take psychic damage equal to your Intelligence modifier when they fall unconscious.
>> - **Rank 6 — Heavy Slumber** Increase the burst to burst 3.
>> - **Rank 9 — Crushing Fatigue** Creatures slowed by this power take a –2 penalty to attack rolls until they succeed on their first saving throw.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Creatures that fail their first saving throw fall unconscious and are also weakened until the end of their next turn after waking.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** If a creature fails two consecutive saving throws against this power, it remains unconscious until the end of its next turn after succeeding.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** When a creature falls unconscious from this power, you may slide it 2 squares.
