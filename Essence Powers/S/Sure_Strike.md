At-Will         Martial        Weapon       Precision

You sacrifice raw power to ensure your blow lands where it must.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Neutral
**Action Type:** Standard Action
**Range:** Melee weapon
**Target:** One creature

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Essence Attack +2 vs. AC

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 1[W] damage. This power cannot gain additional weapon damage dice beyond its listed [W] value. Flat modifiers, penalties to the target, and vulnerability still apply.

> [!Miss|background-color-yellow]
> - No damage.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Corrosive Potency** Measured Strike** Increase the attack bonus to +3.
>> - **Rank 6 — Unerring Form** On a hit, add your Essence ability modifier to the damage.
>> - **Rank 9 — Relentless Accuracy** If this power misses, you gain a +2 power bonus to your next attack roll against the same target before the end of your next turn.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Attack bonus increases to +4.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** Attack bonus increases to +5. On a hit, the target takes a –2 penalty to AC against your next attack before the end of your next turn.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Attack bonus increases to +6. If this attack hits a bloodied target, it deals additional damage equal to your Essence ability modifier.
