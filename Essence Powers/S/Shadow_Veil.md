# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Utility ✦ Concealment

**ENCOUNTER ARCANE ILLUSION**

**Eligible Essences:** Shadow, Veil, Eye, Moon, Suppression

**Slot:** 2 – Utility / Passive  
**Tier:** Encounter

A murky shroud of darkness cloaks your presence.

**Action Type:** Minor Action  
**Range:** Personal
**Cost:** 1 Mana

> [!Effect|background-color-blue]
> You gain a +5 power bonus to Stealth checks until the end of your next turn.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Deepening Shade** Increase the Stealth bonus to +6.
>> - **Rank 6 — Cloaked Form** While this power is active, you gain concealment.
>> - **Rank 9 — Veiled Presence** If you become hidden while this power is active, you gain a +2 power bonus to your next attack roll before the end of your turn.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** The Stealth bonus increases to +7.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** The Stealth bonus increases to +8. If you are hidden at the end of your turn, you remain hidden until you attack or move more than 2 squares.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** The Stealth bonus increases to +9. Once per use, you may become invisible until the end of your next turn instead of gaining the Stealth bonus.
