# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Utility ✦ Rallying Command

**ENCOUNTER MARTIAL**

**Eligible Essences:** Oath, Zeal, Balance, Shield, Life  

**Slot:** 2 – Utility / Passive  
**Tier:** Encounter

You refuse to let weakness take hold.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Stable
**Action Type:** Minor Action  
**Range:** Ranged 10
**Cost:** 1 Stamina
**Target:** You or one ally

> [!Effect|background-color-blue]
> The target makes a saving throw with a power bonus equal to your Charisma modifier.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Grit and Resolve** If the saving throw succeeds, the target gains temporary hit points equal to your Charisma modifier.
>> - **Rank 6 — Refuse the Pain** If the saving throw fails, the target gains a +2 power bonus to its next saving throw before the end of your next turn.
>> - **Rank 9 — Iron Will** If the saving throw fails by 2 or less, the target may reroll the saving throw but must use the second result.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** The saving throw gains a minimum result equal to your Charisma modifier (if the die result is lower, treat it as that value before modifiers).
>> - **Silver / Epic:** If the saving throw succeeds, the target may also shift 1 square as a free action.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** If the saving throw succeeds against a charm, fear, or stun effect, the target gains a +2 power bonus to attack rolls until the end of its next turn.
