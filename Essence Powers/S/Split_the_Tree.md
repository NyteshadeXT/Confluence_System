# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Attack ✦ Apex Ability

**Daily Martial Weapon Precision**

**Eligible Essences:** Ranged, Hunt, Adept, Eye, Wood

**Slot:** 5 – Apex Ability  
**Tier:** Daily

You loose two arrows at once; they separate in flight and strike as if guided by intent itself.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Escalating
**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Range:** Ranged weapon
**Targets:** Two creatures within 3 squares of each other
**Cost:** 2 Stamina

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Power Rank vs. AC. Make two attack rolls, take the higher result, and apply it to both targets.

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 2[W] + your Essence ability modifier damage.

> [!Miss|background-color-yellow]
> - Half damage.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Perfect Draw** Increase the damage to 3[W] + your Essence ability modifier.
>> - **Rank 6 — Dividing Trajectory** If both targets are hit, each takes additional damage equal to your secondary ability modifier (chosen when this power is unlocked).
>> - **Rank 9 — Unerring Pair** If one target is bloodied, both targets take additional damage equal to your Essence ability modifier.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Damage increases to 4[W] + Essence ability modifier.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** Damage increases to 5[W] + Essence ability modifier. If both targets are hit, they grant combat advantage until the end of your next turn.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Damage increases to 6[W] + Essence ability modifier. If both targets are reduced to 0 HP by this power, you may immediately make a ranged basic attack against a third creature within range (once per use).
