# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Utility ✦ At-Will

**At-Will Martial**

**Eligible Essences:** Hunt, Swift, Eye, Momentum, Pursuit

**Slot:** 2 – Utility / Passive  
**Tier:** At-Will

> [!Effect|background-color-blue]
> You single out your prey, tracking weakness and opportunity with relentless focus.
> 
> **Minor Action:** Once per turn, designate the nearest enemy you can see as your quarry. You may have only one quarry at a time. Your quarry remains designated until the end of the encounter, the quarry drops to 0 HP or you designate a different creature as your quarry.
> 
> **Quarry Effect:** Once per round, when you damage your quarry, you deal 1d6 extra damage. If you make multiple attacks in a round, you decide which attack gains the extra damage after all attacks are rolled.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Focused Hunter** Increase the extra damage to 1d8.
>> - **Rank 6 — Relentless Tracker** If your quarry moves away from you on its turn, increase the extra damage by your Wisdom modifier (applies once per round).
>> - **Rank 9 — Merciless Finish** If your quarry is bloodied, increase the extra damage by your Dexterity modifier (applies once per round).
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Extra damage becomes 2d6.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** Extra damage becomes 3d6. If your quarry is the only enemy adjacent to you, you gain a +2 power bonus to attack rolls against it.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Extra damage becomes 4d6. If your quarry drops to 0 HP, you may immediately designate a new quarry without using a minor action (still once per turn).
