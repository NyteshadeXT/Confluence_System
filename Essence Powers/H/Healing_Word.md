# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Utility ✦ Channel Divinity

**ENCOUNTER DIVINE HEALING**

**Eligible Essences:** Life, Oath, Light, Balance  

**Slot:** 2 – Utility / Passive  
**Tier:** Encounter

A whispered prayer restores strength and resolve.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Stable
**Action Type:** Minor Action  
**Range:** Close burst 5
**Cost:** 1 Mana
**Target:** You or one ally

> [!Effect|background-color-blue]
> The target can spend a Healing Surge and regain additional hit points equal to 1d6 + your Wisdom modifier. You may use this power twice per encounter, but only once per round.  

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Deepened Prayer** Increase the additional healing to 1d8 + your Wisdom modifier.
>> - **Rank 6 — Comforting Light** The target gains temporary hit points equal to your Charisma modifier.
>> - **Rank 9 — Sustaining Grace** If the target is bloodied when they receive this healing, they regain additional hit points equal to your Wisdom modifier.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Additional healing becomes 2d6 + Wisdom modifier.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** You may use this power three times per encounter but only once per round. Additional healing becomes 3d6 + Wisdom modifier.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Additional healing becomes 4d6 + Wisdom modifier. If the target is below half HP after the healing is resolved, they gain a +2 power bonus to all defenses until the start of your next turn.
