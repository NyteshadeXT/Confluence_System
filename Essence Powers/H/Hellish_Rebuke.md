# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Attack ✦ Core Concept

**At-Will Arcane Fire Implement**

**Eligible Essences:** Fire, Blood, Zeal, Omen, Reckoning

**Slot:** 1 – Core Concept  
**Tier:** At-Will

You brand your foe with flames that burn hotter when fed by your suffering.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Reactive
**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Range:** Ranged 10
**Target:** One creature

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Power Rank vs. Reflex (Constitution)

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 1d6 + your Constitution modifier fire damage. If you take damage before the start of your next turn, the target takes an additional 1d6 + your Constitution modifier fire damage.

> [!Miss|background-color-yellow]
> - No damage.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Smoldering Mark** Increase the initial damage to 1d8 + your Constitution modifier.
>> - **Rank 6 — Fueled by Pain** If the additional damage is triggered, increase it by your Constitution modifier (applies once per turn).
>> - **Rank 9 — Scorching Retaliation** If the additional damage is triggered, the target takes a –2 penalty to attack rolls until the end of its next turn.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Initial damage becomes 2d6 + Constitution modifier. Triggered damage becomes 2d6 + Constitution modifier.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** Initial damage becomes 3d6 + Constitution modifier. If the triggered damage occurs, the target grants combat advantage until the end of your next turn.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Initial damage becomes 4d6 + Constitution modifier. If the triggered damage reduces the target to 0 HP, you gain temporary hit points equal to your Constitution modifier.
