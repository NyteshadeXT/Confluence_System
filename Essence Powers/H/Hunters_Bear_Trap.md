# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`


Attack ✦ Apex Ability

**Daily Martial Weapon Bleed**

**Eligible Essences:** Hunt, Trap, Wolf, Snake, Ranged  

**Slot:** 5 – Apex Ability  
**Tier:** Daily

A carefully placed strike cripples your foe’s movement, leaving them limping, bleeding, and unable to escape the hunt.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Escalating
**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Range:** Melee or Ranged weapon
**Target:** One creature
**Cost:** 2 Stamina

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Melee: Essence Attack vs. AC (Strength)
> - Ranged: Essence Attack vs. AC (Dexterity)

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 2[W] + your Essence ability modifier damage. The target is slowed and takes ongoing damage equal to your Tier bleed value (save ends both).

> [!Miss|background-color-yellow]
> - Half damage.  The target is slowed until the end of your next turn.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Deep Cut** Increase the weapon damage to 3[W] + your Essence ability modifier.
>> - **Rank 6 — Crippling Wound** While the target is slowed by this power, it cannot shift.
>> - **Rank 9 — Relentless Pursuit** If the target fails its first saving throw against the ongoing damage, it falls prone.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Weapon damage becomes 4[W] + Essence ability modifier. Increase the ongoing damage to your Bronze Tier bleed value.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** Weapon damage becomes 5[W] + Essence ability modifier. If the target fails two saving throws against the bleed, it is immobilized until the end of its next turn.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Weapon damage becomes 6[W] + Essence ability modifier. If the target is bloodied while still affected by this power’s bleed, it grants combat advantage to you and your allies until the bleed ends.
