# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Attack ✦ Tactical Strike  

**ENCOUNTER MARTIAL WEAPON**

**Eligible Essences:** Shield, Order, Chain, Resolute, Steel, Bond, Dominion, Harmony

**Slot:** 4 – Tactical Strike  
**Tier:** Encounter

With sharp commands and disciplined presence, you anchor your allies in place and deny the enemy any chance to break your line.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Stable → Escalating (formation enforcement resists dungeon pressure)
**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Range:** Melee weapon  
**Cost:** 1 Stamina
**Target:** One creature

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Power Rank vs. AC (Strength-based)

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 1[W] + your Essence ability modifier damage. Until the end of your next turn allies adjacent to you gain a +2 bonus to AC and those allies cannot be pulled, pushed, or slid.

> [!Miss|background-color-yellow]
> - No damage, but the effect still applies to you (not allies).

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Reinforced Formation** Affected allies gain a +1 bonus to saving throws.
>> - **Rank 6 — Anchored Line** Affected allies gain resistance to forced movement effects (if a creature would move them, reduce the distance by 1 square).
>> - **Rank 9 — Unbreakable Front** If an affected ally is attacked while adjacent to you, they gain a +1 bonus to all defenses against that attack.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Damage increases to 2[W] + ability modifier. AC bonus increases to +3.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** Damage increases to 3[W] + ability modifier. Affected allies also gain a +1 bonus to Fortitude.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Damage increases to 4[W] + ability modifier. Affected allies cannot be knocked prone.
