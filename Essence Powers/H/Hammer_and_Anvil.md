# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Attack ✦ Signature Strike

**ENCOUNTER MARTIAL WEAPON**

**Eligible Essences:** Zeal, Oath, Momentum, Hunt, Might  

**Slot:** 3 – Signature Strike  
**Tier:** Encounter

You strike the anvil blow that sets your ally’s hammer in motion.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Stable
**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Range:** Melee weapon
**Cost:** 1 Stamina
**Target:** One creature

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Strength vs. Reflex

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 1[W] + Strength modifier damage. One ally adjacent to the target makes a melee basic attack against it as a free action. The ally adds your Charisma modifier to the damage roll.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Driving Impact** Increase your damage to 2[W] + Strength modifier.
>> - **Rank 6 — Perfect Timing** If the ally’s melee basic attack hits, you may slide the target 1 square.
>> - **Rank 9 — Crushing Combination** If both your attack and the ally’s attack hit, the target takes additional damage equal to your Charisma modifier.
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Your damage becomes 3[W] + Strength modifier. The ally’s attack gains a +1 power bonus to the attack roll.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** Your damage becomes 4[W] + Strength modifier. If the ally’s attack hits, the target is slowed until the end of its next turn.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Your damage becomes 5[W] + Strength modifier. If both attacks hit a bloodied target, it is knocked prone.
