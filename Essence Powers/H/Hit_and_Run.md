# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Attack ✦ Core Concept

**At-Will Martial Weapon Mobility**

**Eligible Essences:** Swift, Hunt, Cat, Wolf, Blade  

**Slot:** 1 – Core Concept  
**Tier:** At-Will

You strike and immediately disengage, refusing to be pinned down.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Neutral
**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Range:** Melee weapon
**Target:** One creature

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Power Rank vs. AC

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 1[W] + your Essence ability modifier damage. If you move later in the same turn, leaving the first square adjacent to the target does not provoke an opportunity attack from that target.

> [!Miss|background-color-yellow]
> - No damage..

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Clean Withdrawal** Increase the damage to 1[W] + Essence ability modifier + 2.
>> - **Rank 6 — Fluid Disengagement** If you hit, you may shift 1 square as a free action.
>> - **Rank 9 — Uncatchable Strike** If you move at least 2 squares after using this power, you gain a +2 power bonus to AC against the target until the start of your next turn.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Damage increases to 2[W] + Essence ability modifier.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** Damage increases to 3[W] + Essence ability modifier. If you move after the attack, you gain combat advantage against the target until the end of your turn.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Damage increases to 4[W] + Essence ability modifier. If you reduce the target to 0 HP with this power, you may immediately shift up to your speed.
