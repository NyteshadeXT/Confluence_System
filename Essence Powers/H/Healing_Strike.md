# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Attack ✦ Signature Strike

**ENCOUNTER DIVINE HEALING RADIANT WEAPON**

**Eligible Essences:** Life, Oath, Light, Shield  

**Slot:** 3 – Signature Strike  
**Tier:** Encounter

Your weapon gleams with divine grace, striking and restoring in the same breath.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Stable
**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Range:** Melee weapon
**Cost:** 1 Mana
**Target:** One creature

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Strength vs. AC

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 2[W] + Strength modifier radiant damage. The target is marked until the end of your next turn. You or one ally within 5 squares may spend a Healing Surge.

> [!Miss|background-color-yellow]
> - The target is marked until the end of your next turn.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Restorative Blow** Increase the weapon damage to 3[W] + your Strength modifier.
>> - **Rank 6 — Overflowing Grace** When an ally spends a Healing Surge from this power, they gain additional hit points equal to your Charisma modifier.
>> - **Rank 9 — Consecrated Challenge** If the marked target attacks an ally before the end of your next turn, that ally gains temporary hit points equal to your Wisdom modifier.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Damage increases to 4[W] + Strength modifier. If the Healing Surge is spent by an ally, that ally gains a +2 power bonus to AC until the end of your next turn.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** Damage increases to 5[W] + Strength modifier. The chosen creature may spend a Healing Surge and regain additional hit points equal to your Charisma modifier.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Damage increases to 6[W] + Strength modifier. If the marked target drops to 0 HP before the end of your next turn, you or one ally within 5 squares may immediately spend a Healing Surge as a free action.
