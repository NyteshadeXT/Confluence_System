# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Utility ✦ Survival

**Daily Martial Healing**

**Eligible Essences:** Bear, Might, Blood  

**Slot:** 2 – Utility / Passive  
**Tier:** Daily

You push past exhaustion and injury, fueled by sheer refusal to fall.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Escalating
**Action Type:** Minor Action  
**Range:** Personal
**Cost:** 2 Stamina

> [!Effect|background-color-blue]
> You gain temporary hit points equal to 2d6 + your Constitution modifier.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Relentless Vitality** Increase the temporary hit points to 3d6 + your Constitution modifier.
>> - **Rank 6 — Ignore the Wound** While you have temporary hit points from this power, you gain a +2 power bonus to Fortitude.
>> - **Rank 9 — Refuse to Yield** If you are bloodied when you use this power, add your Constitution modifier again to the temporary hit points gained.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Temporary hit points increase to 4d6 + Constitution modifier.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** Temporary hit points increase to 5d6 + Constitution modifier. While you have temporary hit points from this power, you gain resistance to all damage equal to your Constitution modifier.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Temporary hit points increase to 6d6 + Constitution modifier. If you are reduced to 0 HP while these temporary hit points remain, you instead remain at 1 HP and this power ends.
