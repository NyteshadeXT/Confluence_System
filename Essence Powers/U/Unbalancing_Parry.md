# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Utility ✦ Tactical

**Encounter Martial Weapon**

**Eligible Essences:** Fox, Cat, Blade, Swift  

**Slot:** 2 – Utility / Passive  
**Tier:** Encounter

You turn a failed strike into a moment of dangerous imbalance.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Neutral
**Action Type:** Immediate Reaction  
**Range:** Melee 1
**Trigger:** An enemy misses you with a melee attack.

> [!Effect|background-color-blue]
> Slide the enemy 1 square into a space adjacent to you.  You gain combat advantage against that enemy until the end of your next turn.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Off-Balance Footing** Increase the slide distance to 2 squares.
>> - **Rank 6 — Opportunist’s Edge** While you have combat advantage from this power, your first attack against the target deals additional damage equal to your Dexterity modifier.
>> - **Rank 9 — Overextended** If the target was bloodied when it missed you, it also takes a –2 penalty to AC until the end of your next turn.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** You may shift 1 square after sliding the enemy.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** If the enemy misses you by 5 or more, it falls prone after the slide.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** If the enemy misses you while bloodied, it is slowed until the end of its next turn.
