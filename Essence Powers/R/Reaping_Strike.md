# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Attack ✦ Core Concept

**At-Will  Martial Weapon**

**Eligible Essences:** Blade, Steel, Adept  

**Slot:** 1 – Core Concept  
**Tier:** At-Will

Even when your main blow fails, smaller cuts and jabs slip through your enemy’s defenses.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Neutral
**Action Type:** Standard Action
**Range:** Melee weapon
**Target:** One creature

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Power Rank vs. AC

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 1[W] + your Essence ability modifier damage.

> [!Miss|background-color-yellow]
> - The target takes damage equal to your Essence ability modifier.   If you are wielding a two-handed weapon, this damage equals your Essence ability modifier + Tier bonus.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Relentless Edge** Increase the hit damage to 1[W] + your Essence ability modifier + 2.
>> - **Rank 6 — Grinding Assault** If the attack misses, increase the miss damage by +2.
>> - **Rank 9 — Cleaving Pressure** If you are wielding a two-handed weapon and hit, add your Tier bonus to the damage (instead of only applying it on a miss).
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> -  **Bronze / Paragon:** Hit damage increases to 2[W] + Essence ability modifier. Miss damage becomes Essence ability modifier + Tier bonus (regardless of weapon).
>> - **Silver / Epic:** Hit damage increases to 3[W] + Essence ability modifier. If the attack misses, the target takes Essence ability modifier + Tier bonus + 2.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Hit damage increases to 4[W] + Essence ability modifier. If the attack misses, the target takes Essence ability modifier + Tier bonus, and you gain a +2 power bonus to your next attack roll against that target before the end of your next turn.
