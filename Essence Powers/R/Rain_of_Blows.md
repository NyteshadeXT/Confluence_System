# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Attack ✦ Signature Strike

**ENCOUNTER MARTIAL WEAPON**

**Eligible Essences:** Blade, Polearm, Swift, Momentum, Hunt, Steel

**Slot:** 3 – Signature Strike  
**Tier:** Encounter

You become a blur of motion, striking in rapid succession before your foe can recover.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Neutral → Escalating (if repeatedly used for burst damage focus)

**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Range:** Melee weapon  
**Cost:** 1 Stamina
**Primary Target:** One creature

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Essence Attack vs. AC (make two attacks)

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 1[W] + your Essence ability modifier damage per hit. If you are wielding a light blade, spear, or flail and have Dexterity 15 or higher, you may make a third attack.
> 
> - **Secondary Targets:** May be the same target or a different creature within reach.  Make a second (or third) attack Essence Attack vs. AC.  On hit, 1[W] + your Essence ability modifier damage.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Relentless Assault** If both primary attacks hit the same target, deal +2 damage to that target.
>> - **Rank 6 — Press the Advantage** If at least one attack hits, gain a +1 bonus to your next attack roll before the end of your next turn.
>> - **Rank 9 — Flurry Precision** If two or more attacks hit the same target, that target takes a –2 penalty to AC until the start of your next turn.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Damage increases to 2[W] + ability modifier per hit.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** Damage increases to 3[W] + ability modifier per hit. If you hit the same target twice, it grants combat advantage.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Damage increases to 4[W] + ability modifier per hit. If you hit with all attacks, the target is dazed until the end of your next turn.
