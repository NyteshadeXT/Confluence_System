# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Attack ✦ Core Concept

**AT-WILL ARCANE COLD IMPLEMENT**

**Eligible Essences:** Ice, Water, Wind

**Slot:** 1 – Core Concept  
**Tier:** At-Will

A beam of killing cold locks your foe in creeping frost.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Stable
**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Range:** Ranged 10
**Target:** One creature

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Intelligence vs. Fortitude

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 1d6 + Intelligence modifier cold damage.  The target is slowed until the end of your next turn.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Biting Chill** Increase the damage to 1d8 + your Intelligence modifier.
>> - **Rank 6 — Creeping Frost** If the target is already slowed when hit, it takes a –2 penalty to attack rolls until the end of its next turn.
>> - **Rank 9 — Numbing Cold** If the target is bloodied, it also takes a –2 penalty to Reflex until the end of your next turn.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Damage increases to 2d6 + Intelligence modifier.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** Damage increases to 3d6 + Intelligence modifier. If the target is slowed by this power, its speed is reduced to 2 until the end of your next turn.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Damage increases to 4d6 + Intelligence modifier. If the target is slowed and attempts to move more than 2 squares on its turn, it takes cold damage equal to your Intelligence modifier (once per turn).
