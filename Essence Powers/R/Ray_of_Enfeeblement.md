# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Attack ✦ Signature Strike

**ENCOUNTER ARCANE NECROTIC IMPLEMENT**

**Eligible Essences:** Omen, Shadow, Blood, Reckoning  

**Slot:** 3 – Signature Strike  
**Tier:** Encounter

Sickly green mist drains strength from your foe.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Neutral
**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Range:** Ranged 10
**Cost:** 1 Mana
**Target:** One creature

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Intelligence vs. Fortitude

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 1d10 + Intelligence modifier necrotic damage. The target is weakened until the end of your next turn.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Deepening Blight** Increase the damage to 2d10 + your Intelligence modifier.
>> - **Rank 6 — Lingering Frailty** If the target is bloodied, the weakened condition lasts until the end of your next turn + 1 round.
>> - **Rank 9 — Draining Collapse** If the target fails a saving throw against one of your necrotic effects before the end of your next turn, it takes additional necrotic damage equal to your Intelligence modifier.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Damage increases to 3d10 + Intelligence modifier.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** Damage increases to 4d10 + Intelligence modifier. If the target is bloodied, it also takes a –2 penalty to damage rolls until the end of your next turn.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Damage increases to 5d10 + Intelligence modifier. If the target fails its first saving throw against weakened from this power, it remains weakened until the end of its next turn after that save.
