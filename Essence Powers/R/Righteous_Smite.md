# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Attack ✦ Signature Strike

**ENCOUNTER DIVINE WEAPON**

**Eligible Essences:** Oath, Light, Zeal, Life, Renewal, Equilibrium

**Slot:** 3 – Signature Strike  
**Tier:** Encounter

Your strike carries the weight of righteous conviction, bolstering the spirits and resolve of those who stand with you.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Neutral
**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Range:** Melee weapon  
**Cost:** 1 Mana
**Target:** One creature

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Power Rank vs. AC (Charisma-based)

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 2[W] + your Essence ability modifier damage. You and each ally within 5 squares gain temporary hit points equal to 5 + your Wisdom modifier.

> [!Miss|background-color-yellow]
> - Half damage. No temporary hit points.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Strength of Conviction** Increase the temporary hit points to 7 + Wisdom modifier.
>> - **Rank 6 — Shared Resolve** Affected allies gain a +1 bonus to saving throws until the end of your next turn.
>> - **Rank 9 — Unbreakable Faith** If you are bloodied, increase the temporary hit points granted by your Charisma modifier.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Damage increases to 3[W] + ability modifier. Temporary hit points increase to 10 + Wisdom modifier.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** Damage increases to 4[W] + ability modifier. Affected allies also gain a +1 bonus to AC until the end of your next turn.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Damage increases to 5[W] + ability modifier. Temporary hit points also grant resistance equal to your Wisdom modifier until the end of your next turn.
