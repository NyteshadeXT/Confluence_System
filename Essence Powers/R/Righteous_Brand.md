# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Attack ✦ Core Concept

**AT-WILL DIVINE WEAPON**

Eligible Essences: Oath, Light, Zeal, Reckoning  

**Slot:** 1 – Core Concept  
**Tier:** At-Will

You mark your foe with divine judgment and empower an ally to strike true.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Stable
**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Range:** Melee weapon
**Target:** One creature


> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Strength vs. AC

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 1[W] + Strength modifier damage. Choose one ally within 5 squares. That ally gains a power bonus to melee attack rolls against the target equal to your Strength modifier until the end of your next turn.

> [!Miss|background-color-yellow]
> - Half damage to the primary target and ongoing 2 acid damage (save ends). No secondary attack.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Burning Seal** Increase the weapon damage to 1[W] + Strength modifier + 2.
>> - **Rank 6 — Faith-Driven Assault** If the chosen ally hits the target with a melee attack before the end of your next turn, that ally deals additional radiant damage equal to your Charisma modifier.
>> - **Rank 9 — Unyielding Judgment** If the chosen ally hits the target while bloodied, the target takes a –2 penalty to attack rolls until the end of its next turn.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Damage increases to 2[W] + Strength modifier.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** Damage increases to 3[W] + Strength modifier. If the empowered ally hits the target, it is marked until the end of your next turn.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Damage increases to 4[W] + Strength modifier. If the empowered ally reduces the target to 0 HP, another ally within 5 squares gains the same melee attack bonus against a creature of your choice until the end of your next turn.
