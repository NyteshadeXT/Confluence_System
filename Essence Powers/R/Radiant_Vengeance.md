# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
Attack ✦ Core Concept

**AT-WILL DIVINE IMPLEMENT**

**Eligible Essences:** Radiant Vengeance, Renewal, Order, Revelation

**Slot:** 1 – Core Concept
**Tier:** At-Will

You reflect divine power through your wounds, turning pain into restoration and harm.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Neutral
**Action Type:** Standard Action
**Range:** Ranged 10
**Cost:** —
**Target:** One creature

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Essence Attack vs Reflex

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 1d8 + your Essence ability modifier light damage. You gain temporary hit points equal to your Essence ability modifier

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Guided Strike:** Gain a +1 bonus to the attack roll
>> - **Rank 6 — Reinforced Spirit:** Increase temporary hit points by +2
>> - **Rank 9 — Sustained Vengeance:** Temporary hit points increase to modifier + 5
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Damage increases to 2d8 + modifier. Temporary hit points become modifier + 1d6.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** Damage increases to 3d8 + modifier. You gain a +2 bonus to all defenses until the start of your next turn if you gain temporary HP.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Damage increases to 4d8 + modifier. When you gain temporary HP, you may transfer half of it to an ally within 5 squares.
