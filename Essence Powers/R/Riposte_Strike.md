# `VIEW[{alias}][text]`
**Attack ✦ Core Concept**

**At-Will Martial Weapon Counter**

**Eligible Essences:** Blade, Fox, Cat, Adept  

**Slot:** 1 – Core Concept  
**Tier:** At-Will

You strike in a way that dares your foe to respond—and punish them if they do.

**Dungeon Resonance:** Neutral
**Action Type:** Standard Action  
**Range:** Melee weapon
**Requirement:** You must be wielding a light blade.
**Target:** One creature

> [!Attack|background-color-red]
> - Power Rank vs. AC (Dexterity)

> [!Hit|background-color-green]
> - 1[W] + your Dexterity modifier damage. Until the start of your next turn, if the target attacks you, you may make a riposte against it as an immediate interrupt.
> 
> - **Riposte Attack:** Power Rank vs. AC (Strength or Dexterity chosen when unlocked) on hit 1[W] + your ability modifier damage.

> [!column]-
>> [!info] ### RANK EXPRESSIONS (IRON TIER)
>> - **Rank 3 — Provoking Edge** Increase the initial strike damage to 1[W] + Dexterity modifier + 2.
>> - **Rank 6 — Punishing Riposte** If the riposte attack hits, the target takes a –2 penalty to attack rolls until the end of its turn.
>> - **Rank 9 — Perfect Timing** If the triggering attack misses you, the riposte deals additional damage equal to your Dexterity.
>
>> [!note] ### TIER EXPRESSIONS
>> - **Bronze / Paragon:** Initial and riposte damage increase to 2[W] + ability modifier.
>> - **Silver / Epic:** Initial and riposte damage increase to 3[W] + ability modifier. If the riposte hits, the target grants combat advantage to you until the end of your next turn.
>> - **Gold / Post-Epic (Optional):** Initial and riposte damage increase to 4[W] + ability modifier. If the riposte reduces the target to 0 HP, you may immediately shift up to half your speed.
