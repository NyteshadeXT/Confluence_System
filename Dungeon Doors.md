Dungeon Doors have runes etched along the outside of the Door.  These runes provide hints as to the strength, stability, and dangers found within. Every Door serves as a message and a puzzle.  Players can spend as much or as little time examining a Door as they like. Different skills reveal different pieces of the puzzle. The difficulty class of a skill check is determined by the Rank of the dungeon. Every action reveals something different.  For example, 
- Arcana examines the magical lattice connecting the runes.
- Religion recognizes divine iconography.
- Nature identifies symbols representing ecological concepts.
- Occultism notices metaphysical relationships.
- Crafting studies the construction techniques.
- Society compares it to known ruins.
- Even Perception can spot worn or damaged glyphs.

Unlike a puzzle, however, the runes are not always static.  Runes can shift position, change or even disappear.  When a Dungeon becomes unstable, one rune physically alters. When corruption spreads, another darkens.  Should the Seed is removed the entire ring goes blank.

The Rank symbol is a general indicator of the level of challenge players should expect to find beyond the Door.  They are Iron, Bronze, Sliver, Gold and Platinum. It's important to note that a rune is not absolute.  A Bronze rank Dungeon is most likely to contain Bronze rank challenges, but other factors can influence the development of a Dungeon and cause there to be some variation.  The same Bronze rank Dungeon could have both Iron or Silver rank encounters, but they are the exception.

Attunement symbols align to Essences.  They indicate the general theme of a given Dungeon.  A Dungeon with a Fire attunement likely has many challenges involve heat, flames, magma and/or burning while a Time attuned Dungeon might find the players sped up or slowed down at times or there might be strange time dilations. Dungeons with one or more Confluence Attunements are particularly dangerous and unpredictable as in addition to the them derived from the Confluence Essence the Dungeon can also leverage themes from any of the Essences which can be combined into that Confluence in addition to any other Attunements the Dungeon might have.

Dungeon Stability is reflected in the Stability rune.  Dungeons can be Stable, Unstable, Collapsing, Dormant or Corrupted just as a few examples.  Conditions within the Dungeon will heavily reflect in not only it's appearance but the overall functioning of the Dungeon as well.  A Collapsing Dungeon might have whole rooms which appear to be missing or are actually falling apart as the players enter them.

Whereas Attunement symbols align to Essences and indicate theme, Hazard symbols are slightly more direct indicators of the dangers in the Dungeon. The poison Hazard symbol likely indicates poisoned traps and/or creatures who leverage poison in their attacks.  The Flying symbol could indicate aerial combat, flying creatures or open air challenges of some kind.

|Hazard Rune|Implies|
|---|---|
|Organized|Intelligent defenders, tactics, patrols|
|Brood|Nests, hatcheries, swarms|
|Predator|Apex hunters, ambush predators|
|Flying|Vertical combat, aerial threats|
|Poison|Venom, toxins, spores|
|Traps|Mechanical or magical traps|
|Darkness|Concealment, light suppression|
|Regeneration|Creatures or Dungeon repairs|
|Corruption|Taint, mutation, insanity|
|Flood|Water hazards|
|Flame|Fire hazards|
|Frost|Cold hazards|
|Mind|Mental attacks, illusions|
|Shifting|Moving rooms, teleportation|
|Construct|Artificial guardians|
|Undead|Death magic|
|Ooze|Corrosive creatures and environments|

A final, fifth category of Dungeon Runes exists, but is very rare - Special Traits.  Special traits, fall outside of the normal Hazard runes. A such, any Dungeon with Special traits is considered highly prized or dangerous depending on your overall outlook regarding Dungeons. Some examples could include, but are not limited to, time flows differently, death is temporary, reality is unstable or Essence Amplification of some sort.
