**Core Concept:** Reach, constriction, multidirectional control

Tentacle extends the body beyond its natural limits to seize control of physical space. It favors grappling, restraining, and striking from unexpected angles rather than decisive killing blows or nimble pursuit.

**Associated Ability Score:** Strength

**Durability:** Tentacle expects to remain close to danger while holding enemies in place. Its durability is solid but not defensive.
- **Iron Tier:** +2 HP per Essence Rank
- **Bronze Tier:** +2 HP per Essence Rank

**Recovery:** Tentacle does not rely on recovery. It wins by denying enemies the space and freedom to regain control.
- **Iron Tier:** No additional Healing Surges
- **Bronze Tier:** No additional Healing Surges

**Exertion:** Tentacle strongly supports stamina-heavy control and repeated physical exertion.
- **Iron Tier:** Gain +1 Stamina at Rank 1 and again at Rank 6
- **Bronze Tier:** Gain +1 Stamina at Bronze Rank 1. No further Stamina gained from Tentacle in Bronze

**Focus:** Tentacle has no natural affinity for Mana.
- **Iron Tier:** No Mana gained from Tentacle
- **Bronze Tier:** No Mana gained from Tentacle

---

### Essence Mastery Milestones
**Rank 3 — Anchoring Grip** Tentacle holds fast against opposition. Gain +1 Fortitude.  
**Rank 5 — Constricting Strength** Increase Strength by +1.  
**Rank 9 — Many-Armed Control** Gain +1 Fortitude AND +1 to Attack Rolls with Unarmed or Polearm weapon powers.
