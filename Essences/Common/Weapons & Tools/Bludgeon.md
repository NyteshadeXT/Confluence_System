**Core Concept:** Impact, disruption, force  

Bludgeon overwhelms through sheer momentum. It excels at breaking defenses, staggering opposition, and reshaping engagements through force rather than finesse.

**Associated Ability Score:** Strength

**Durability**

Bludgeon expects to trade blows. Its durability reflects willingness to stay in the exchange.
- **Iron Tier:** +2 HP per Essence Rank
- **Bronze Tier:** +2 HP per Essence Rank

**Recovery:** Bludgeon relies on toughness and rhythm rather than recovery.
- **Iron Tier:** No additional Healing Surges
- **Bronze Tier:** Gain +1 Healing Surge at Bronze Rank 1

**Exertion:** Bludgeon strongly favors physical exertion and repeated forceful actions.
- **Iron Tier:** Gain +1 Stamina at Rank 1 and again at Rank 6
- **Bronze Tier:** Gain +1 Stamina at Bronze Rank 1. No further Stamina gained from Bludgeon in Bronze

**Focus:** Bludgeon has little affinity for Mana.
- **Iron Tier:** No Mana gained from Bludgeon
- **Bronze Tier:** No Mana gained from Bludgeon

---

### Training Interaction
Bludgeon grants impact weapon training. At Rank 6, Bludgeon grants one of the following **Hammer Group Proficiency, Mace Group Proficiency OR Two-Handed Bludgeon Proficiency.**

Bludgeon does NOT grant armor.

---

### Essence Mastery Milestones
**Rank 3 — Crushing Force** Bludgeon fighters withstand impact and recoil. Gain +1 Fortitude.
**Rank 5 — Overwhelming Strength** Increase Strength by +1.
**Rank 9 — Relentless Impact** Gain +1 Fortitude AND +1 to Attack Rolls with Bludgeon weapons.
