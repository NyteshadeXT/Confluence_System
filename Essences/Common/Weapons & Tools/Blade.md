**Core Concept:** Precision, lethality, commitment  

Blade rewards decisive action and close engagement. It favors accuracy, follow-through, and choosing the moment of contact rather than endurance or disruption.

**Associated Ability Score:** Dexterity

**Durability:** Blade survives by ending fights quickly, not by taking hits.
-  **Iron Tier:** +1 HP per Essence Rank
- **Bronze Tier:** +1 HP per Essence Rank

**Recovery:** Blade does not plan for drawn-out conflict. It expects resolution through execution.
- **Iron Tier:** No additional Healing Surges
- **Bronze Tier:** No additional Healing Surges

**Exertion:** Blade strongly supports stamina-driven offense and repeated attacks.
- **Iron Tier:** Gain +1 Stamina at Rank 1 and again at Rank 5
- **Bronze Tier:** Gain +1 Stamina at Bronze Rank 1. No further Stamina gained from Blade in Bronze

**Focus:** Blade has minimal magical reliance.
- **Iron Tier:** No Mana gained from Blade
- **Bronze Tier:** No Mana gained from Blade

---

### Training Interaction
Blade grants blade specialization training. At Rank 6, Blade grants one of the following **Light Blade Group Proficiency OR Heavy Blade Group**.

Blade does NOT grant armor.

---

### Essence Mastery Milestones
**Rank 3 — Committed Strike** Gain +1 to Attack Rolls with Blade weapons.
**Rank 5 — Lethal Agility** Increase Dexterity by +1.
**Rank 9 — Killing Precision** Gain +1 to Attack Rolls with Blade weapons AND +1 Reflex.
