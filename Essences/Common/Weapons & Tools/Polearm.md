**Core Concept:** Reach, formation, dominance  

Polearm controls distance and engagement lines. It rewards positioning, discipline, and battlefield awareness over raw aggression or mobility.

**Associate Ability Score:** Strength

**Durability:** Polearm expects to be near danger but not surrounded. Its durability is moderate.
- **Iron Tier:** +2 HP per Essence Rank
- **Bronze Tier:** +2 HP per Essence Rank

**Recovery:** Polearm relies on spacing and formation rather than recovery.
- **Iron Tier:** No additional Healing Surges
- **Bronze Tier:** No additional Healing Surges

**Exertion:** Polearm strongly supports stamina-driven control and repeated positional actions.
- **Iron Tier:** Gain +1 Stamina at Rank 1 and again at Rank 5
- **Bronze Tier:** Gain +1 Stamina at Bronze Rank 1. No further Stamina gained from Polearm in Bronze

**Focus:** Polearm has little affinity for magic.
-  **Iron Tier:** No Mana gained from Polearm
- **Bronze Tier:** No Mana gained from Polearm

---

### Training Interaction
Polearm grants reach weapon specialization. At Rank 6, Polearm grants one of the following **Polearm Group Proficiency, Spear Group Proficiency OR Military Melee Proficiency.**

Polearm does NOT grant armor.

---

### Essence Mastery Milestones
**Rank 3 — Controlled Engagement** Polearm fighters rely on awareness and spacing. Gain +1 Reflex.
**Rank 5 — Anchored Grip** Increase Strength by +1.
**Rank 9 — Dominant Reach** Gain +1 Reflex AND +1 to Attack Rolls with Polearm weapons.
