**Core Concept:** Rending, precision, predatory aggression

Claw emphasizes close, decisive violence and the instinct to exploit an opening. It favors rapid strikes, tearing wounds, and relentless pressure against vulnerable foes rather than brute force or defensive control.

**Associated Ability Score:** Dexterity

**Durability:** Claw avoids prolonged exchanges through aggression and movement. Its durability is modest.
- **Iron Tier:** +1 HP per Essence Rank
- **Bronze Tier:** +1 HP per Essence Rank

**Recovery:** Claw does not rely on recovery. It wins by maintaining pressure and ending a fight before the enemy can regain control.
- **Iron Tier:** No additional Healing Surges
- **Bronze Tier:** No additional Healing Surges

**Exertion:** Claw supports repeated, stamina-heavy attacks and rapid movement.
- **Iron Tier:** Gain +1 Stamina at Rank 1 and again at Rank 6
- **Bronze Tier:** Gain +1 Stamina at Bronze Rank 1. No further Stamina gained from Claw in Bronze

**Focus:** Claw has no natural affinity for Mana.
- **Iron Tier:** No Mana gained from Claw
- **Bronze Tier:** No Mana gained from Claw

---

### Essence Mastery Milestones
**Rank 1 — Manifested Claws** You can make weapon attacks with your claws. Your claws are one-handed melee weapons in the Unarmed group, dealing 1d6 slashing damage.
**Rank 3 — Predator's Reflexes** Claw responds instantly to danger. Gain +1 Reflex.  
**Rank 5 — Rending Precision** Increase Dexterity by +1.  
**Rank 9 — Relentless Talons** Gain +1 Reflex AND +1 to Attack Rolls with Unarmed or Light Blade weapon powers.
