**Core Concept:** Distance, patience, positioning  

Ranged emphasizes control through separation. It favors preparation, timing, and spatial awareness over direct confrontation.

**Associated Ability Score:** Dexterity

**Durability:** Ranged avoids damage through distance. Its durability is intentionally low.
- **Iron Tier:** +1 HP per Essence Rank
- **Bronze Tier:** +1 HP per Essence Rank

**Recovery:** Ranged does not rely on recovery. It disengages instead.
- **Iron Tier:** No additional Healing Surges
- **Bronze Tier:** No additional Healing Surges

**Exertion:** Ranged supports sustained stamina use tied to repeated attacks and repositioning.
- **Iron Tier:** Gain +1 Stamina at Rank 1 and again at Rank 6
- **Bronze Tier:** Gain +1 Stamina at Bronze Rank 1. No further Stamina gained from Ranged in Bronze

**Focus:** Ranged has minimal magical expression.
- **Iron Tier:** No Mana gained from Ranged
- **Bronze Tier:** No Mana gained from Ranged

---

### Essence Mastery Milestones
**Rank 1 — Weapon Training** Ranged grants ranged weapon specialization. At Rank 1, Ranged grants one of the following Crossbow Weapon Group Proficiency OR Bow Weapon Group Proficiency.  If the character is already Trained in the selected weapon group, increase to Expert.
**Rank 3 — Patient Aim** Ranged fighters rely on awareness and positioning. Gain +1 Reflex.
**Rank 5 — Steady Hand** Increase Dexterity by +1.
**Rank 9 — Perfect Shot** Gain +1 to Attack Rolls with Crossbow or Bow Weapon Group AND +1 Reflex.
