**Core Concept:** Defense, refusal, endurance  

Shield represents the will to hold the line. It favors protection, counterpressure, and denying progress over proactive offense.

**Associated Ability Score:** Constitution

**Durability:** Shield is built to absorb punishment directly. Its durability is high and dependable.
- **Iron Tier:** +4 HP per Essence Rank
- **Bronze Tier:** +4 HP per Essence Rank

**Recovery:** Shield recovers better than most defensive Essences, reflecting trained endurance and defensive rhythm.
-  **Iron Tier:** Gain +1 Healing Surge at Rank 3 and Rank 6
- **Bronze Tier:** Gain +1 Healing Surge at Bronze Rank 1

**Exertion:** Shield supports exertion tied to bracing, blocking, and countering, but not sustained aggression.
- **Iron Tier:** Gain +1 Stamina at Rank 3
- **Bronze Tier:** No additional Stamina gained from Shield in Bronze

**Focus:** Shield has little magical expression. Its power is physical and positional.
- **Iron Tier:** No Mana gained from Shield
- **Bronze Tier:** No Mana gained from Shield

---

### Training Interaction
Shield grants advanced shield discipline. At Rank 6, Shield grants one of the following: **Heavy Shield Proficiency OR Shield Mastery (increase shield AC bonus by +1).**

Shield does NOT grant weapon proficiency.

---

### Essence Mastery Milestones
**Rank 3 — Braced Defense** Gain +1 AC.
**Rank 5 — Unyielding Stance** Increase Constitution by +1.
**Rank 9 — Hold the Line** Gain +1 AC AND +1 Fortitude.
