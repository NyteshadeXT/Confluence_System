**Core Concept:** Pursuit, tracking, inevitability  

Hunt excels at finding, following, and wearing down prey. It rewards preparation, persistence, and denying escape rather than raw confrontation.

**Associated Ability Score:** Wisdom

**Durability:** Hunt survives through awareness and positioning rather than direct endurance.
- **Iron Tier:** +2 HP per Essence Rank
- **Bronze Tier:** +2 HP per Essence Rank

**Recovery:** Hunt sustains long pursuits through discipline and measured recovery.
- **Iron Tier:** Gain +1 Healing Surge at Rank 3
- **Bronze Tier:** Gain +1 Healing Surge at Bronze Rank 1

**Exertion:** Hunt strongly supports stamina use tied to movement, tracking, and sustained pressure.
- **Iron Tier:** Gain +1 Stamina at Rank 1 and again at Rank 5
- **Bronze Tier:** Gain +1 Stamina at Bronze Rank 1. No further Stamina gained from Hunt in Bronze

**Focus:** Hunt has limited magical support tied to perception, tracking, and marking.
- **Iron Tier:** Gain +1 Mana at Rank 3
- **Bronze Tier:** No additional Mana gained from Hunt in Bronze

---

### Training Interaction
Hunt follows signs relentlessly tracking at range.  It prefers to hunt with reach. At Rank 6, Hunt grants one of the following **Bow Group Proficiency, Spear Group Proficiency OR +2 bonus to Perception and Nature checks made to track creatures.**

Hunt does NOT grant armor.

---

### Essence Mastery Milestones
**Rank 3 — Keen Senses** Hunt reacts quickly when prey appears. Gain +1 Initiative.
**Rank 5 — Patient Tracker** Increase Wisdom by +1.
**Rank 9 — Inevitable Quarry** Gain +1 Will AND +1 to attack rolls against a target you have hit earlier this encounter.
