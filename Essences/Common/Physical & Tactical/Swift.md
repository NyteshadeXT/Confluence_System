**Core Concept:** Speed, initiative, reaction  

Swift favors acting first and repositioning constantly. It rewards timing, mobility, and decisive movement over endurance or sustained pressure.

**Associated Ability Score:** Dexterity

**Durability:** Swift avoids danger through speed rather than toughness.
- **Iron Tier:** +1 HP per Essence Rank
- **Bronze Tier:** +1 HP per Essence Rank

**Recovery:** Swift does not rely on recovery. It disengages before damage accumulates.
- **Iron Tier:** No additional Healing Surges
- **Bronze Tier:** No additional Healing Surges

**Exertion:** Swift strongly supports stamina-driven movement, reactions, and initiative-based play.
- **Iron Tier:** Gain +1 Stamina at Rank 1 and again at Rank 5
- **Bronze Tier:** Gain +1 Stamina at Bronze Rank 1. No further Stamina gained from Swift in Bronze

**Focus:** Swift has minimal magical inclination.
- **Iron Tier:** No Mana gained from Swift
- **Bronze Tier:** No Mana gained from Swift

---

### Essence Mastery Milestones
**Rank 3 — Lightning Reflex** Swift consistently acts early. Gain +1 Initiative.
**Rank 5 — Rapid Form** Increase Dexterity by +1.
**Rank 9 — First to Act** Gain +1 Initiative AND +1 to attack rolls against enemies that have not yet acted this encounter.
