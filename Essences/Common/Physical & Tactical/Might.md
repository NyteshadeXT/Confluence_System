**Core Concept:** Force, exertion, dominance  

Might expresses strength without subtlety. It favors decisive impact, breaking resistance, and imposing will through sheer physical capability rather than endurance or finesse.

**Associated Ability Score:** Strength

**Durability:** Might expects to trade blows and win through superiority of force.
-  **Iron Tier:** +3 HP per Essence Rank
- **Bronze Tier:** +3 HP per Essence Rank

**Recovery:** Might does not rely on recovery. It aims to end opposition through dominance before attrition matters.
- **Iron Tier:** No additional Healing Surges
- **Bronze Tier:** No additional Healing Surges

**Exertion:** Might strongly supports stamina-heavy actions and repeated displays of strength.
- **Iron Tier:** Gain +1 Stamina at Rank 1 and again at Rank 6
- **Bronze Tier:** Gain +1 Stamina at Bronze Rank 1. No further Stamina gained from Might in Bronze

**Focus:** Might has no affinity for Mana.
- **Iron Tier:** No Mana gained from Might
- **Bronze Tier:** No Mana gained from Might

---

### Training Interaction
Might commits fully to physical dominance using powerful weapons.  It breaks objects, grapples shoves and climbs. At Rank 6, Might grants one of the following **Military Melee Proficiency, Two-Handed Weapon Proficiency OR +2 bonus to Athletics checks.**

Might does NOT grant armor.

---

### Essence Mastery Milestones
**Rank 3 — Imposing Presence** Might withstands physical challenge. Gain +1 Fortitude.
**Rank 5 — Crushing Strength** Increase Strength by +1.
**Rank 9 — Overwhelming Force** Gain +1 Fortitude AND +1 to attack rolls with Strength-based powers.
