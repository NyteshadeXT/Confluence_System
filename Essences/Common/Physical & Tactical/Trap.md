**Core Concept:** Preparation, denial, punishment  

Trap wins before the fight begins. It rewards foresight, patience, and positioning, turning enemy movement and overconfidence into liability.

**Associated Ability Score:** Intelligence

**Durability:** Trap survives by avoiding fair fights altogether. Its durability is intentionally modest.
- **Iron Tier:** +1 HP per Essence Rank
- **Bronze Tier:** +1 HP per Essence Rank

**Recovery:** Trap does not rely on recovery. It expects damage to be prevented through preparation.
- **Iron Tier:** No additional Healing Surges
- **Bronze Tier:** No additional Healing Surges

**Exertion:** Trap supports exertion tied to setup, maintenance, and triggering prepared effects.
- **Iron Tier:** Gain +1 Stamina at Rank 3 and again at Rank 6
- **Bronze Tier:** Gain +1 Stamina at Bronze Rank 1. No further Stamina gained from Trap in Bronze

**Focus:** Trap relies on deliberate, structured magic to establish and sustain preparations.
- **Iron Tier:** Gain +1 Mana at Rank 3
- **Bronze Tier:** Gain +1 Mana at Bronze Rank 1. No further Mana gained from Trap in Bronze

---

### Essence Mastery Milestones
**Rank 3 — Prepared Position** Trap anticipates danger and avoids retaliation. Gain +1 Reflex.
**Rank 5 — Calculated Mind** Increase Intelligence by +1.
**Rank 9 — Punishing Setup** Gain +1 Will AND +1 to attack rolls against enemies that moved 2 or more squares on their last turn.
