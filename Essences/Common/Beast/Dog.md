**Core Concept:** Loyalty, vigilance, persistence  

Dog endures through commitment and awareness. It favors protection, detection, and holding ground against persistent threats rather than burst damage or clever misdirection.

**Associated Ability Score:** Wisdom

**Durability:** Dog survives by staying present and absorbing pressure meant for others.
- **Iron Tier:** +2 HP per Essence Rank
- **Bronze Tier:** +2 HP per Essence Rank

**Recovery:** Dog recovers through steadiness and care, reflecting long-term endurance rather than rapid healing.
- **Iron Tier:** Gain +1 Healing Surge at Rank 3
- **Bronze Tier:** Gain +1 Healing Surge at Bronze Rank 1

**Exertion:** Dog supports sustained exertion tied to guarding, responding, and staying engaged.
- **Iron Tier:** Gain +1 Stamina at Rank 1 and again at Rank 6
- **Bronze Tier:** Gain **+**1 Stamina at Bronze Rank 1. No further Stamina gained from Dog in Bronze

**Focus:** Dog has minimal magical inclination.
- **Iron Tier:** No Mana gained from Dog
- **Bronze Tier:** No Mana gained from Dog

---

### Essence Mastery Milestones
**Rank 3 — Watchful Instinct** Dog stands firm against fear and mental pressure. Gain +1 Will.
**Rank 5 — Steady Resolve** Increase Wisdom by +1.
**Rank 9 — Loyal Defender** Gain +1 Fortitude AND +1 to attack rolls against enemies adjacent to your allies.
