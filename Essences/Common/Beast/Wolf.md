**Core Concept:** Coordination, pursuit, pack tactics  

Wolf excels through cooperation and sustained pressure. It rewards positioning, teamwork, and relentless pursuit over solitary strength or sudden bursts.

**Associated Ability Score:** Dexterity

**Durability:** Wolf survives by sharing pressure across the pack rather than absorbing it alone.
- **Iron Tier:** +2 HP per Essence Rank
- **Bronze Tier:** +2 HP per Essence Rank

**Recovery:** Wolf relies on momentum and coordination rather than recovery.
- **Iron Tier:** No additional Healing Surges
- **Bronze Tier:** Gain +1 Healing Surge at Bronze Rank 1

**Exertion:** Wolf strongly supports repeated exertion tied to movement, flanking, and sustained engagement.
- **Iron Tier:** Gain +1 Stamina at Rank 1 and again at Rank 5
- **Bronze Tier:** Gain +1 Stamina at Bronze Rank 1. No further Stamina gained from Wolf in Bronze

**Focus:** Wolf has minimal magical expression.
- **Iron Tier:** No Mana gained from Wolf
- **Bronze Tier:** No Mana gained from Wolf

---

### Training Interaction
Wolf circles and strikes. It likes to hunt with reach and formation. It ultimately runs down it’s prey. At Rank 6, Wolf grants one of the following **Light Blade Group Proficiency**, **Spear Group Proficiency OR Speed +1.**

---

### Essence Mastery Milestones
**Rank 3 — Pack Instinct** Wolf acts when the pack moves. Gain +1 Initiative.

**Rank 5 — Hunter’s Agility** Increase Dexterity by +1.

**Rank 9 — Relentless Pursuit** Gain +1 Reflex AND +1 to attack rolls when you have combat advantage.
