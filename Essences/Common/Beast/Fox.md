**Core Concept:** Cunning, misdirection, escape  

Fox avoids direct confrontation through clever positioning. It excels at deception, evasion, and turning enemy mistakes into advantages rather than applying sustained pressure.

**Associated Ability Score:** Charisma

**Durability:** Fox survives by not being where danger lands.
- **Iron Tier:** +1 HP per Essence Rank
- **Bronze Tier:** +1 HP per Essence Rank

**Recovery:** Fox does not plan to be hurt often enough to need recovery.
- **Iron Tier:** No additional Healing Surges
- **Bronze Tier:** No additional Healing Surges

**Exertion:** Fox strongly supports stamina use tied to repositioning, feints, and disengagement.
- **Iron Tier:** Gain +1 Stamina at Rank 1 and again at Rank 5
- **Bronze Tier:** Gain +1 Stamina at Bronze Rank 1. No further Stamina gained from Fox in Bronze

**Focus:** Fox has limited but flexible magical expression tied to illusion, trickery, and escape.
- **Iron Tier:** Gain +1 Mana at Rank 3
- **Bronze Tier:** No additional Mana gained from Fox in Bronze

---

### Essence Mastery Milestones
**Rank 3 — Slippery Instinct** Fox acts before the trap closes. Gain +1 Initiative.
**Rank 5 — Silver Tongue** Increase Charisma by +1.
**Rank 9 — Turn the Tables** Gain +1 Reflex AND +1 to attack rolls against enemies that missed you since the end of your last turn.
