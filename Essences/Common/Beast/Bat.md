**Core Concept:** Awareness, mobility, disorientation  

Bat thrives where others are blind. It favors perception, unpredictable movement, and disrupting enemy coordination rather than endurance or raw force.

**Associated Ability Score:** Wisdom

**Durability:** Bat avoids danger through awareness and mobility, not toughness.
- **Iron Tier:** +1 HP per Essence Rank
- **Bronze Tier:** +1 HP per Essence Rank

**Recovery:** Bat does not linger in danger long enough to rely on recovery.
- **Iron Tier:** No additional Healing Surges
- **Bronze Tier:** No additional Healing Surges

**Exertion:** Bat strongly supports stamina-driven movement, repositioning, and reactive actions.
- **Iron Tier:** Gain +1 Stamina at Rank 1 and again at Rank 5
- **Bronze Tier:** Gain +1 Stamina at Bronze Rank 1. No further Stamina gained from Bat in Bronze

**Focus:** Bat has limited but functional magical support tied to perception and disruption.
- **Iron Tier:** Gain +1 Mana at Rank 3
- **Bronze Tier:** No additional Mana gained from Bat in Bronze

---

### Essence Mastery Milestones
**Rank 3 — Echo Awareness** Bat acts when others hesitate. Gain +1 Initiative.
**Rank 5 — Heightened Senses** Increase Wisdom by +1.
**Rank 9 — Disorienting Presence** Gain +1 Reflex AND +1 to attack rolls against enemies granting combat advantage or that have not yet acted this encounter.
