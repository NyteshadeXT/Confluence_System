**Core Concept:** Agility, balance, predation  

Cat strikes with speed and precision. It rewards mobility, control of footing, and choosing the moment of engagement rather than endurance or deception.

**Associated Ability Score:** Dexterity

**Durability:** Cat avoids damage through agility and positioning, not toughness.
- **Iron Tier:** +1 HP per Essence Rank
- **Bronze Tier:** +1 HP per Essence Rank

**Recovery:** Cat does not rely on recovery. It disengages and repositions instead.
- **Iron Tier:** No additional Healing Surges
- **Bronze Tier:** No additional Healing Surges

**Exertion:** Cat strongly supports repeated, agile exertion and short bursts of action.
- **Iron Tier:** Gain +1 Stamina at Rank 1 and again at Rank 5
- **Bronze Tier:** Gain +1 Stamina at Bronze Rank 1. No further Stamina gained from Cat in Bronze

**Focus:** Cat has minimal magical inclination.
- **Iron Tier:** No Mana gained from Cat
- **Bronze Tier:** No Mana gained from Cat

---

### Training Interaction
Cat is agile and moves consistently.  It maintains balance and defensive posture without the need for armor. At Rank 6, Cat grants one of the following **Light Blade Group Proficiency**, **Gain +1 Speed OR Gain +1 AC while wearing cloth or no armor.**

---

### Essence Mastery Milestones
**Rank 3 — Quickstep** Cat acts before others commit. Gain +1 Initiative.
**Rank 5 — Feline Grace** Increase Dexterity by +1.
**Rank 9 — Perfect Balance** Gain +1 Reflex AND +1 to attack rolls when you moved at least 2 squares this turn.
