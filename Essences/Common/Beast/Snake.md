**Core Concept:** Precision, patience, inevitability  

Snake waits for the decisive moment. It favors careful positioning, single decisive strikes, and consequences that unfold over time rather than sustained aggression.

**Associated Ability Score:** Dexterity

**Durability:** Snake does not endure prolonged combat. It avoids damage through timing and restraint.
- **Iron Tier:** +1 HP per Essence Rank
- **Bronze Tier:** +1 HP per Essence Rank

**Recovery:** Snake does not plan for recovery. It expects engagements to end decisively.
- **Iron Tier:** No additional Healing Surges
- **Bronze Tier:** No additional Healing Surges

**Exertion:** Snake supports exertion tied to deliberate, well-timed actions rather than repeated effort.
- **Iron Tier:** Gain +1 Stamina at Rank 3
- **Bronze Tier:** No additional Stamina gained from Snake in Bronze

**Focus:** Snake supports limited, precise magical expression tied to delayed consequence.
- **Iron Tier:** Gain +1 Mana at Rank 3
- **Bronze Tier:** No additional Mana gained from Snake in Bronze

---

### Essence Mastery Milestones
**Rank 3 — Patient Watch** Snake survives through timing and awareness. Gain +1 Reflex.
**Rank 5 — Coiled Precision** Increase Dexterity by +1.
**Rank 9 — Inevitable Strike** Gain +1 Reflex AND +1 to attack rolls against targets granting combat advantage.
