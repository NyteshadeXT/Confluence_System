**Core Concept:** Endurance, fury, protection  

Bear absorbs punishment and answers with relentless force. It favors staying power, retaliation, and refusing to yield ground.

**Associated Ability Score:** Constitution

**Durability:** Bear is one of the most physically durable Beast Essences.
- **Iron Tier:** +3 HP per Essence Rank
- **Bronze Tier:** +3 HP per Essence Rank

**Recovery:** Bear recovers through grit and resilience rather than finesse.
- **Iron Tier:** Gain +1 Healing Surge at Rank 3
- **Bronze Tier:** Gain +1 Healing Surge at Bronze Rank 1

**Exertion:** Bear supports sustained exertion and repeated heavy actions.
- **Iron Tier:** Gain +1 Stamina at Rank 1 and again at Rank 5
- **Bronze Tier:** Gain +1 Stamina at Bronze Rank 1. No further Stamina gained from Bear in Bronze

**Focus:** Bear has little magical inclination.
- **Iron Tier:** No Mana gained from Bear
- **Bronze Tier:** No Mana gained from Bear

---

### Essence Mastery Milestones
**Rank 3 — Thick Hide** Bear endures physical punishment. Gain +1 Fortitude.
**Rank 5 — Primal Bulk** Increase Constitution by +1.
**Rank 9 — Unyielding Fury** Gain +1 Fortitude AND +1 to Attack Rolls with Unarmed or Two-Handed weapon powers.
