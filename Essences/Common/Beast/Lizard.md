**Core Concept:** Resilience, adaptation, recovery  

Lizard survives where others fail. It favors endurance through regeneration, environmental tolerance, and the ability to recover from setbacks rather than resisting them outright.

**Associated Ability Score:** Constitution

**Durability:** Lizard bodies are resilient but flexible, able to endure damage without collapsing.
- **Iron Tier:** +2 HP per Essence Rank
- **Bronze Tier:** +2 HP per Essence Rank

**Recovery:** Lizard is one of the most recovery-focused Beast Essences, emphasizing regeneration and survival after harm.
- **Iron Tier:** Gain +1 Healing Surge at Rank 1
- **Bronze Tier:** Gain +1 Healing Surge at Bronze Rank 1

**Exertion:** Lizard supports moderate exertion tied to sustained survival and environmental response.
- **Iron Tier:** Gain +1 Stamina at Rank 3
- **Bronze Tier:** Gain +1 Stamina at Bronze Rank 1. No further Stamina gained from Lizard in Bronze

**Focus:** Lizard has limited but functional magical expression tied to adaptation and recovery.
- **Iron Tier:** Gain +1 Mana at Rank 3
- **Bronze Tier:** No additional Mana gained from Lizard in Bronze

---

### Essence Mastery Milestones
**Rank 3 — Adaptive Instinct** Lizard withstands physical stress. Gain +1 Fortitude.
**Rank 5 — Regenerative Frame** Increase Constitution by +1.
**Rank 9 — Persistent Survivor** Gain +1 Fortitude AND +1 to saving throws.
