**Core Concept:** Ambush, grip, territorial dominance  

Crocodile overwhelms through sudden control. It excels at seizing enemies, denying escape, and enforcing dominance within its territory.

**Associated Ability Score:** Strength

**Durability:** Crocodile is heavily built to survive the initial clash and maintain its grip.
- **Iron Tier:** +3 HP per Essence Rank
- **Bronze Tier:** +3 HP per Essence Rank

**Recovery:** Crocodile relies on ending fights quickly after the ambush rather than extended recovery.
- **Iron Tier:** +1 Healing Surge at Iron Rank 7
- **Bronze Tier:** No additional Healing Surges

**Exertion:** Crocodile strongly supports stamina-heavy exertion during the opening moments of combat.
- **Iron Tier:** Gain +1 Stamina at Rank 1 and again at Rank 5
- **Bronze Tier:** Gain +1 Stamina at Bronze Rank 1. No further Stamina gained from Crocodile in Bronze

**Focus:** Crocodile has no natural affinity for Mana.
- **Iron Tier:** No Mana gained from Crocodile
- **Bronze Tier:** No Mana gained from Crocodile

---

### Training Interaction
Crocodile bites, crushes and hacks with heavy force. It seeks to seize its target and drag it away. At Rank 6, Crocodile grants one of the following **Unarmed Weapon Group Enhancement** (Your unarmed strike damage increases to 1d8 and gains a +1 proficiency bonus.), **Axxe Weapon Proficiency OR Gain +2 bonus to Athletics checks for grabbing and maintaining grabs.**

---

### Essence Mastery Milestones
**Rank 3 — Ambush Instinct** Crocodile strikes first from concealment. Gain +1 Initiative.
**Rank 5 — Crushing Frame** Increase Strength by +1.
**Rank 9 — Death Roll** Gain +1 Fortitude AND +1 to attack rolls against grabbed, immobilized, or prone targets.
