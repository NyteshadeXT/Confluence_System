**Core Concept:** Harassment, escalation, swarming  

Wasp punishes intrusion through persistence. It excels at repeated pressure, disruption, and turning minor threats into mounting danger rather than delivering decisive blows.

**Associated Ability Score**: Dexterity

**Durability:** Wasp survives by being difficult to ignore and harder to pin down, not by absorbing punishment.
- **Iron Tier:** +1 HP per Essence Rank
- **Bronze Tier:** +1 HP per Essence Rank

**Recovery:** Wasp does not disengage to recover. It stays present and keeps pressure constant.
- **Iron Tier:** No additional Healing Surges
- **Bronze Tier:** No additional Healing Surges

**Exertion:** Wasp strongly supports frequent exertion and repeated actions over the course of an encounter.
- **Iron Tier:** Gain +1 Stamina at Rank 1 and again at Rank 5
- **Bronze Tier:** Gain +1 Stamina at Bronze Rank 1. No further Stamina gained from Wasp in Bronze

**Focus:** Wasp has limited magical support, usually tied to venom, irritation, or escalation effects.
- **Iron Tier:** Gain +1 Mana at Rank 3
- **Bronze Tier:** No additional Mana gained from Wasp in Bronze

---

### Essence Mastery Milestones
**Rank 3 — Agitating Presence** Wasp acts early and often. Gain +1 Initiative.
**Rank 5 — Quickened Reflexes** Increase Dexterity by +1.
**Rank 9 — Escalating Threat** Gain +1 Reflex AND +1 to attack rolls against targets you have already hit this encounter.
