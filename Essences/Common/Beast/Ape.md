**Core Concept:** Strength, dominance, climbing  

Ape emphasizes raw physical power and vertical mobility. It favors overwhelming force, control through strength, and commanding physical space rather than endurance or finesse.

**Associated Ability Score:** Strength

**Durability:** Ape expects to take hits while forcing its way through opposition. Its durability is solid but not defensive.
- **Iron Tier:** +2 HP per Essence Rank
- **Bronze Tier:** +2 HP per Essence Rank

**Recovery:** Ape does not rely on recovery. It wins by overpowering resistance before attrition matters.
- **Iron Tier:** No additional Healing Surges
- **Bronze Tier:** No additional Healing Surges

**Exertion:** Ape strongly supports stamina-heavy, physical exertion.
- **Iron Tier:** Gain +1 Stamina at Rank 1 and again at Rank 6
- **Bronze Tier:** Gain +1 Stamina at Bronze Rank 1. No further Stamina gained from Ape in Bronze

**Focus:** Ape has no natural affinity for Mana.
- **Iron Tier:** No Mana gained from Ape
- **Bronze Tier:** No Mana gained from Ape

---

### Training Interaction
Ape grants training aligned with brute physical dominance. At Rank 6, Ape grants one of the following **Unarmed Weapon Group Enhancement** (Your unarmed strike damage increases to 1d8 and gains a +1 proficiency bonus.), **Two-Handed Weapon Proficiency OR Climb Speed (equal to base speed).**

---

### Essence Mastery Milestones
**Rank 3 — Primal Presence** Ape withstands physical opposition. Gain +1 Fortitude.
**Rank 5 — Crushing Strength** Increase Strength by +1.
**Rank 9 — Alpha Force** Gain +1 Fortitude AND +1 to Attack Rolls with Unarmed or Two-Handed weapon powers.
