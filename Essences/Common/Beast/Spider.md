**Core Concept:** Traps, positioning, inevitability  

Spider controls encounters before they begin. It reshapes space, restricts movement, and punishes those who advance carelessly, favoring preparation and inevitability over reaction.

**Associated Ability Score:** Intelligence

**Durability:** Spider survives by avoiding direct confrontation and forcing enemies into disadvantage.
- **Iron Tier:** +1 HP per Essence Rank
- **Bronze Tier:** +1 HP per Essence Rank

**Recovery:** Spider does not rely on recovery. It prevents damage through preparation and denial.
- **Iron Tier:** No additional Healing Surges
- **Bronze Tier:** No additional Healing Surges

**Exertion:** Spider supports exertion tied to maintaining control and layered setup.
- **Iron Tier:** Gain +1 Stamina at Rank 3 and again at Rank 6
- **Bronze Tier:** Gain +1 Stamina at Bronze Rank 1. No further Stamina gained from Spider in Bronze

**Focus:** Spider relies on deliberate, controlling magic to shape the battlefield.
- **Iron Tier:** Gain +1 Mana at Rank 1
- **Bronze Tier:** Gain +1 Mana at Bronze Rank 1. No further Mana gained from Spider in Bronze

---

### Essence Mastery Milestones
**Rank 3 — Web Sense** Spider anticipates movement. Gain +1 Reflex.
**Rank 5 — Calculated Patience** Increase Intelligence by +1.
**Rank 9 — Layered Control** Gain +1 Reflex AND +1 to attack rolls against targets that are slowed, immobilized, restrained, or granting combat advantage.
