**Core Concept:** Defense, patience, attrition  

Turtle endures by waiting out opposition. It emphasizes protection, restraint, and winning through exhaustion rather than force.

**Associated Ability Score: Constitution**

**Durability:** Turtle is exceptionally durable, built to absorb punishment over long engagements.
- **Iron Tier:** +4 HP per Essence Rank
- **Bronze Tier:** +4 HP per Essence Rank

**Recovery:** Turtle relies more on protection than recovery, but eventually learns to sustain long sieges.
- **Iron Tier:** No additional Healing Surges
- **Bronze Tier:** Gain +1 Healing Surge at Bronze Rank 1

**Exertion:** Turtle does not favor exertion. It acts deliberately and infrequently.
- **Iron Tier:** No additional Stamina gained from Turtle
- **Bronze Tier:** Gain +1 Stamina at Bronze Rank 1

**Focus:** Turtle has no affinity for Mana.
- **Iron Tier:** No Mana gained from Turtle
- **Bronze Tier:** No Mana gained from Turtle

---

### Essence Mastery Milestones
**Rank 3 — Hardened Shell** Turtle reinforces its defensive posture early. Gain +1 AC.
**Rank 5 — Enduring Body** Increase Constitution by +1.
**Rank 9 — Unbreakable Patience** Gain +1 Fortitude AND +1 AC.
