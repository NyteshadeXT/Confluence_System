**Core Concept:** Binding, control, enforced order  

Chain restricts movement and dictates positioning. It excels at limiting options and imposing structure on chaotic situations.

**Associated Ability Score:** Strength

**Durability:** Chain is durable, but not meant to absorb punishment indefinitely.
- **Iron Tier:** +2 HP per Essence Rank
- **Bronze Tier:** +2 HP per Essence Rank

**Recovery:** Chain does not recover well. It prevents damage by denying movement instead.
- **Iron Tier:** +1 Healing Surge at Iron Rank 3
- **Bronze Tier:** No additional Healing Surges

**Exertion:** Chain supports exertion tied to restraint, pulling, and maintaining control.
- **Iron Tier:** Gain +1 Stamina at Rank 1 and again at Rank 5
- **Bronze Tier:** Gain +1 Stamina at Bronze Rank 1. No further Stamina gained from Chain in Bronze

**Focus:** Chain has limited but functional magical support.
- **Iron Tier:** Gain +1 Mana at Rank 3
- **Bronze Tier:** No additional Mana gained from Chain in Bronze

---

### Essence Mastery Milestones
**Rank 3 — Imposed Structure** Chain resists being moved or disrupted. Gain +1 Fortitude.
**Rank 5 — Iron Grip** Increase Strength by +1.
**Rank 9 — Enforced Order** Gain +1 AC AND +1 to Saving Throws.
