**Core Concept:** Containment, denial, imprisonment  

Cage defines space by what it forbids. It isolates threats, prevents escape, and turns positioning into a liability rather than an advantage.

**Associated Ability Score:** Intelligence

**Durability:** Cage must withstand resistance long enough to hold. Its durability is solid but not absolute.
- **Iron Tier:** +3 HP per Essence Rank
- **Bronze Tier:** +3 HP per Essence Rank

**Recovery:** Cage does not heal well. Once strained, containment weakens rather than recovers.
- **Iron Tier:** No additional Healing Surges
- **Bronze Tier:** No additional Healing Surges

**Exertion:** Cage supports exertion tied to maintaining restraint and enforcing boundaries.
- **Iron Tier:** Gain +1 Stamina at Rank 1 and again at Rank 6
- **Bronze Tier:** Gain +1 Stamina at Bronze Rank 1. No further Stamina gained from Cage in Bronze

**Focus:** Cage relies on deliberate, structured magic to enforce confinement.
- **Iron Tier:** Gain +1 Mana at Rank 1
- **Bronze Tier:** Gain +1 Mana at Bronze Rank 1. No further Mana gained from Cage in Bronze

---

### Essence Mastery Milestones
**Rank 3 — Structured Defense** Cage resists disruption of its imposed order. Gain +1 Will.
**Rank 5 — Calculated Containment** Increase Intelligence by +1.
**Rank 9 — Absolute Containment** Gain +1 AC OR +1 Implement attack AND +1 Will.
