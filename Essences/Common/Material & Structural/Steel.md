**Core Concept:** Discipline, reliability, lethal refinement  

Steel embodies controlled force applied with intent. It favors consistency, precision, and sustained effectiveness over raw power or improvisation.

**Associated Ability Score:** Strength

**Durability:** Steel survives through technique and structure rather than mass. Its durability is solid but not exceptional.
- **Iron Tier:** +2 HP per Essence Rank
- **Bronze Tier:** +2 HP per Essence Rank

**Recovery:** Steel relies on discipline rather than recuperation. It expects fights to be managed, not endured.
- **Iron Tier:** No additional Healing Surges
- **Bronze Tier:** No additional Healing Surges

**Exertion:** Steel strongly supports repeated, deliberate exertion without spikes or exhaustion.
- **Iron Tier:** Gain +1 Stamina at Rank 1 and again at Rank 6
- **Bronze Tier:** Gain +1 Stamina at Bronze Rank 1. No further Stamina gained from Steel in Bronze

**Focus:** Steel has minimal magical reliance. When Mana is present, it is tightly controlled.
- **Iron Tier:** No Mana gained from Steel
- **Bronze Tier:** Gain +1 Mana at Bronze Rank 1

---

### Training Interaction
Steel grants weapon mastery training. At Rank 6, Steel grants one of the following: **Heavy Blade Group Proficiency, Light Blade Group Proficiency OR Military Melee Proficiency.**

Steel does not grant armor proficiency.

---

### Essence Mastery Milestones
**Rank 3 — Measured Strike** Gain +1 to Attack Rolls with Weapon powers.
**Rank 5 — Tempered Form** Increase Strength by +1.
**Rank 9 — Perfect Technique** Gain +1 to Attack Rolls with Weapon powers AND +1 Reflex.
