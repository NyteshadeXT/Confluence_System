**Core Concept:** Durability, relentlessness, weight  

Iron endures punishment and presses forward regardless of resistance. It favors steady pressure, resilience, and inevitability over speed or flexibility.

**Associated Ability Score:** Constitution

**Durability:** Iron is built to be struck and keep moving. Its durability is high, but not absolute.
- **Iron Tier:** +3 HP per Essence Rank
- **Bronze Tier:** +3 HP per Essence Rank

**Recovery:** Iron does not heal easily, but it learns to endure longer engagements through discipline.
- **Iron Tier:** No additional Healing Surges
- **Bronze Tier:** Gain +1 Healing Surge at Bronze Rank 1

**Exertion:** Iron supports continuous physical exertion without spikes or bursts.
- **Iron Tier:** Gain +1 Stamina at Rank 1 and again at Rank 6
- **Bronze Tier:** Gain +1 Stamina at Bronze Rank 1. No further Stamina gained from Iron in Bronze

**Focus:** Iron has little affinity for Mana. When it uses magic, it is deliberate and minimal.
- **Iron Tier:** No Mana gained from Iron
- **Bronze Tier:** Gain +1 Mana at Bronze Rank 1

---

### Training Interaction

Iron represents disciplined martial protection. At Rank 6, Iron grants one of the following proficiencies **Plate Armor Proficiency, Military Melee Proficiency OR Heavy Shield Proficiency.**

---

### Essence Mastery Milestones
**Rank 3 — Hardened Frame** Gain +1 Fortitude.
**Rank 5 — Tempered Body** Increase Constitution by +1.
**Rank 9 — Relentless Advance** Gain +1 AC AND +1 Fortitude.
