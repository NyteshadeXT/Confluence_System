**Core Concept:** Protection, absorption, defiance  

Armor exists to be struck and endure. It mitigates harm through layered defense, rewarding patience and steadfastness rather than aggression or control.

**Associated Ability Score:** Constitution

**Durability:** Armor is designed to take hits directly. Its durability is high and reliable.
- **Iron Tier:** +4 HP per Essence Rank
- **Bronze Tier:** +4 HP per Essence Rank

**Recovery:** Armor does not heal quickly. Its value is in preventing loss rather than restoring it.
- **Iron Tier:** No additional Healing Surges
- **Bronze Tier:** Gain +1 Healing Surge at Bronze Rank 1

**Exertion:** Armor discourages exertion. It favors endurance over activity.
- **Iron Tier:** Gain +1 Stamina at Rank 3
- **Bronze Tier:** No additional Stamina gained from Armor in Bronze

**Focus:** Armor has minimal magical expression.
- **Iron Tier:** No Mana gained from Armor
- **Bronze Tier:** No Mana gained from Armor

---

### Training Interaction
Armor grants the broadest armor proficiency access. At Rank 6, Armor grants one of the following: **Scale Armor Proficiency, Plate Armor Proficiency OR Heavy Shield Proficiency.**

Armor does NOT grant weapon proficiency.

---

### Essence Mastery Milestones
**Rank 3 — Reinforced Plating** Gain +1 AC.
**Rank 5 — Hardened Frame** Increase Constitution by +1.
**Rank 9 — Unyielding Bulwark** Gain +1 AC AND +1 Fortitude.
