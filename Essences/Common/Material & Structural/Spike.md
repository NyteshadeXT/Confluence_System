**Core Concept:** Punishment, deterrence, hazard  

Spike makes movement costly and mistakes painful. It controls territory by threatening consequence rather than by blocking access.

**Associated Ability Score:** Strength

**Durability:** Spike survives by discouraging contact, not by absorbing damage.
- **Iron Tier:** +2 HP per Essence Rank
- **Bronze Tier:** +2 HP per Essence Rank

**Recovery:** Spike does not favor recovery. It expects damage to be avoided through deterrence.
- **Iron Tier:** No additional Healing Surges
- **Bronze Tier:** No additional Healing Surges

**Exertion:** Spike strongly supports repeated exertion tied to reactive punishment and area denial.
-  **Iron Tier:** Gain +1 Stamina at Rank 1 and again at Rank 5
- **Bronze Tier:** Gain +1 Stamina at Bronze Rank 1. No further Stamina gained from Spike in Bronze

**Focus:** Spike’s magic is secondary and situational.
- **Iron Tier:** Gain +1 Mana at Rank 3
- **Bronze Tier:** No additional Mana gained from Spike in Bronze

---

### Essence Mastery Milestones
**Rank 3 — Threatening Posture** Spike withstands backlash and counter-force. Gain +1 Fortitude.
**Rank 5 — Barbed Strength** Increase Strength by +1.
**Rank 9 — Painful Presence** Gain +1 Reflex AND +1 Fortitude.
