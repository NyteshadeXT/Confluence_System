**Core Concept:** Flexibility, resilience, crafted growth

Wood is strength that bends rather than breaks. It favors reinforcement, adaptation, and shaping physical space through living or worked timber rather than raw force or rigid defense.

**Associated Ability Score:** Strength

**Durability:** Wood endures through flexibility and layered resilience. Its durability is solid but not defensive.
- **Iron Tier:** +2 HP per Essence Rank
- **Bronze Tier:** +2 HP per Essence Rank

**Recovery:** Wood does not rely on recovery. It wins by absorbing pressure, adapting to opposition, and remaining useful when rigid defenses would fail.
- **Iron Tier:** No additional Healing Surges
- **Bronze Tier:** No additional Healing Surges

**Exertion:** Wood supports sustained stamina-heavy exertion and the shaping of physical defenses.
- **Iron Tier:** Gain +1 Stamina at Rank 1 and again at Rank 6
- **Bronze Tier:** Gain +1 Stamina at Bronze Rank 1. No further Stamina gained from Wood in Bronze

**Focus:** Wood has no natural affinity for Mana.
- **Iron Tier:** No Mana gained from Wood
- **Bronze Tier:** No Mana gained from Wood

---

### Essence Mastery Milestones

**Rank 3 — Flexible Grain** Wood yields without giving way. Gain +1 Fortitude.  
**Rank 5 — Heartwood Strength** Increase Strength by +1.  
**Rank 9 — Living Framework** Gain +1 Fortitude AND +1 to Attack Rolls with Staff or Ranged weapon powers.
