**Core Concept:** Endurance, stability, denial  

Stone represents permanence and refusal. It is harder to move, harder to break, and slower to change than Earth. Stone survives by outlasting opposition.

**Associated Ability Score:** Constitution

**Durability:** Stone provides the highest raw durability among Common Essences.
- **Iron Tier:** +4 HP per Essence Rank
- **Bronze Tier:** +4 HP per Essence Rank

**Recovery:** Stone is slow to heal but inexhaustible once set. Recovery comes slowly, if at all.
- **Iron Tier:** +1 Healing Surge at Iron Rank 3
- **Bronze Tier:** +1 Healing Surge at Bronze

**Exertion:** Stone does not favor exertion. It acts deliberately and infrequently.
- **Iron Tier:** Gain +1 Stamina at Rank 3
- **Bronze Tier:** No additional Stamina gained from Stone in Bronze

**Focus:** Stone has no natural affinity for Mana.
- **Iron Tier:** No Mana gained from Stone
- **Bronze Tier:** No Mana gained from Stone

Training Interaction

Stone grants the heaviest forms of protection. At Rank 6, Stone grants one of the following proficiencies **Plate Armor Proficiency** OR **Heavy Shield Proficiency.**

Stone does not grant weapon category proficiency.

Essence Mastery Milestones

**Rank 3 — Granite Frame** Gain +1 Fortitude.
**Rank 5 — Immutable Body** Increase Constitution by +1.
**Rank 9 — Unbreakable Form** Choose one: +1 AC OR +1 to Saving Throws against forced movement effects.
