**Core Concept:** Growth, entanglement, persistence  

Plant overwhelms through spread and inevitability. It restricts movement, reshapes space over time, and thrives in prolonged conflicts where pressure accumulates.

**Associated Ability Score:** Wisdom

**Durability:** Plant survives by absorbing minor harm repeatedly rather than resisting major blows.
- **Iron Tier:** +2 HP per Essence Rank
- **Bronze Tier:** +2 HP per Essence Rank

**Recovery:** Plant is resilient and self-sustaining, favoring long engagements with gradual recovery.
- **Iron Tier:** Gain +1 Healing Surge at Rank 3
- **Bronze Tier:** Gain +1 Healing Surge at Bronze Rank 1

**Exertion:** Plant supports sustained physical exertion tied to control and persistence.
- **Iron Tier:** Gain +1 Stamina at Rank 1 and again at Rank 6
- **Bronze Tier:** No additional Stamina gained from Plant in Bronze

**Focus:** Plant’s magic is steady and accumulative rather than explosive.
- **Iron Tier:** Gain +1 Mana at Rank 3
- **Bronze Tier:** Gain +1 Mana at Bronze Rank 1

---

### Training Interaction
Plant grants defensive training aligned with natural protection and grounded positioning. At Rank 6, Plant grants one of the following proficiencies **Hide Armor Proficiency** OR **Spear Group Proficiency.**

---

### Essence Mastery Milestones
**Rank 3 — Rooted Presence** Plant endures through organic resilience. Gain +1 Fortitude.
**Rank 5 — Verdant Growth** Increase Wisdom by +1.
**Rank 9 — Creeping Inevitability** Gain +1 to Saving Throws AND +1 Fortitude.
