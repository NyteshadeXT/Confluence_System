**Core Concept:** Pressure, consumption, inevitability  

Fire emphasizes endurance through aggression and damage persistence, accepting risk in exchange for escalation.

**Associated Ability Score:** Constitution

**Durability:** Fire expects the character to remain engaged while consequences unfold. Durability supports staying power.
- **Iron Tier:** +2 HP per Essence Rank
- **Bronze Tier:** +2 HP per Essence Rank

**Recovery:** Fire does not recover easily early, but Bronze represents learned control over burn and backlash.
- **Iron Tier:** No additional Healing Surges
- **Bronze Tier:** Gain +1 Healing Surge at Bronze Rank 1

**Exertion:** Fire supports repeated exertion, but not endlessly. Iron favors escalation; Bronze favors control.
- **Iron Tier:** Gain +1 Stamina at Rank 1 and again at Rank 6.
- **Bronze Tier:** Gain +1 Stamina at Bronze Rank 1. No further Stamina gained from Fire in Bronze

**Focus:** Fire is one of the few Common Essences that meaningfully fuels both Mana and Stamina.
- **Iron Tier:** Gain +1 Mana at Rank 1 and again at Rank 6.
- **Bronze Tier:** Gain +1 Mana at Bronze Rank 1. No further Mana gained from Fire in Bronze.

---

### Essence Mastery Milestones
**Rank 3 — Combat Pressure** +1 Fortitude. Fire stands its ground in heat and strain.
**Rank 5 — Tempered Core** Increase Constitution by +1
**Rank 6 — Martial Exposure** Fire is aggressive and offensive. Choose one: Gain Military Melee proficiency, gain Military Ranged proficiency OR gain Implement Accuracy +1.
**Rank 9 — Relentless Presence** Reflecting experienced combat. Gain +1 Will to Saving Thows AND +1 to attack rolls with Fire powers.
