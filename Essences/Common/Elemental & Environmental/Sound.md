**Core Concept:** Vibration, disruption, presence  

Sound reshapes space through resonance and impact. It disrupts coordination, punishes clustering, and asserts presence rather than control or endurance.

> [!Associated Ability Score]-
> Charisma

# Iron
> [!Information]-
> ## Durability
> Sound does not endure through toughness. It survives by keeping enemies off-balance.
> - **Iron Tier:** +1 HP per Essence Rank
> 
> ## Recovery
> Sound favors tempo over recovery. It expects conflicts to remain unstable rather than prolonged.
> - **Iron Tier:** No additional Healing Surges
> 
> ## Exertion
> Sound strongly supports repeated exertion, especially for disruptive and area-focused actions.
> - **Iron Tier:** Gain +1 Stamina at Rank 1 and again at Rank 5
>
> ## Focus
> Sound’s magic relies on sustained resonance rather than raw intensity.
> - **Iron Tier:** Gain +1 Mana at Rank 1
