**Core Concept:** Vibration, disruption, presence  

Sound reshapes space through resonance and impact. It disrupts coordination, punishes clustering, and asserts presence rather than control or endurance.

**Associated Ability Score:** Constitution

**Durability:** Sound does not endure through toughness. It survives by keeping enemies off-balance.
- **Iron Tier:** +1 HP per Essence Rank
- **Bronze Tier:** +1 HP per Essence Rank

**Recovery:** Sound favors tempo over recovery. It expects conflicts to remain unstable rather than prolonged.
- **Iron Tier:** No additional Healing Surges
- **Bronze Tier:** No additional Healing Surges

**Exertion:** Sound strongly supports repeated exertion, especially for disruptive and area-focused actions.
- **Iron Tier:** Gain +1 Stamina at Rank 1 and again at Rank 5
- **Bronze Tier:** Gain +1 Stamina at Bronze Rank 1. No further Stamina gained from Sound in Bronze

**Focus:** Sound’s magic relies on sustained resonance rather than raw intensity.
- **Iron Tier:** Gain +1 Mana at Rank 1
- **Bronze Tier:** Gain +1 Mana at Bronze Rank 1. No further Mana gained from Sound in Bronze

---

### Training Interaction
Sound grants training aligned with presence and projection. At Rank 6, Sound grants one of the following proficiencies **Implement Proficiency (choose one: Staff, Wand, Instrument, etc.), Military Ranged Proficiency OR Light Shield Proficiency.**

---

### Essence Mastery Milestones
**Rank 3 — Resonant Presence** Sound asserts presence and resists mental suppression. Gain +1 Will.
**Rank 5 — Projected Force** Increase Charisma by +1.
**Rank 9 — Reverberation** Gain +1 Reflex AND +1 to Implement Attacks.
