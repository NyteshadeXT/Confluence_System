**Core Concept:** Freedom, displacement, reach  

Air emphasizes avoidance over endurance and control through movement, not durability or recovery.

**Associated Ability Score:** Dexterity

**Durability:** Air users are not meant to absorb punishment. Durability remains intentionally low even after Tier advancement.
- **Iron Tier:** +1 HP per Essence Rank
- **Bronze Tier:** +1 HP per Essence Rank (no increase)

**Recovery:** Air avoids damage rather than recovers from it. Healing capacity does not scale with this Essence.
- **Iron Tier:** No additional Healing Surges
- **Bronze Tier:** No additional Healing Surges

**Exertion:** Air provides early, light stamina access to support mobility and tactical exertion, but does not scale aggressively.
- **Iron Tier:** Gain +1 Stamina at Rank 1. No further Stamina gained from Air in Iron
- **Bronze Tier:** Gain +1 additional Stamina at Bronze Rank 1. No further Stamina gained from Air in Bronze

**Focus:** Mana mirrors Stamina: Air supports occasional exertion, not sustained casting.
- **Iron Tier:** Gain +1 Mana at Rank 1**.** No further Mana gained from Air in Iron
- **Bronze Tier:** Gain +1 additional Mana at Bronze Rank 1. No further Mana gained from Air in Bronze

### Essence Mastery Milestones
**Rank 3 — Evasion Instinct** Air moves first. Gain +1 Initiative. 
**Rank 5 — Kinetic Refinement** Air refines reaction speed, not resilience. Increase Dexterity by +1.
**Rank 6 — Fleet Body** Air users rely on mobility, gain +1 AC when using cloth or no armor.
**Rank 9 — Untouchable Flow** +1 Reflex
