**Core Concept:** Restraint, control, inevitability  

Ice limits options and dictates pace. It slows, binds, and constrains enemies, forcing engagements to unfold on its terms rather than through endurance or burst.

**Associated Ability Score:** Intelligence

**Durability:** Ice survives by preventing damage rather than absorbing it. Its durability is modest and secondary to control.
- **Iron Tier:** +1 HP per Essence Rank
- **Bronze Tier:** +1 HP per Essence Rank

**Recovery:** Ice favors control over recovery. It does not heal well, but it prevents situations from worsening.
- **Iron Tier:** No additional Healing Surges
- **Bronze Tier:** Gain +1 Healing Surge at Bronze Rank 1

**Exertion:** Ice supports exertion tied to maintaining control, but not repeated aggression.
- **Iron Tier:** Gain +1 Stamina at Rank 3
- **Bronze Tier:** Gain +1 Stamina at Bronze Rank 1. No further Stamina gained from Ice in Bronze

**Focus:** Ice is strongly magical and rewards careful, deliberate casting.
- **Iron Tier:** Gain +1 Mana at Rank 1 and again at Rank 6
- **Bronze Tier:** Gain +1 Mana at Bronze Rank 1. No further Mana gained from Ice in Bronze

---

### Training Interaction
Ice is not martial and not heavily armored. At Rank 6, Ice grants one of the following proficiencies: **Implement Focus** (choose one implement category: Orb, Staff, Wand, etc.) OR **Light Shield Proficiency.** Ice does not grant armor beyond cloth.

Ice does not grant weapon category proficiency.

---

### Essence Mastery Milestones
**Rank 3 — Calculated Defense** Ice resists mental disruption through composure. Gain +1 Will.
**Rank 5 — Cold Precision** Increase Intelligence by +1.
**Rank 9 — Inevitable Presence** Gain +1 to attack rolls with Implement powers.
