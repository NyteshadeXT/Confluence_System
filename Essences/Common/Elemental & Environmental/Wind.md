**Core Concept:** Momentum, positioning, evasion  

Wind emphasizes control through speed and redirection. It survives by acting first, disengaging cleanly, and avoiding prolonged exposure.

**Associated Ability Score:** Dexterity

**Durability:** Wind does not endure damage. It avoids it. Durability gains are intentionally minimal.
- **Iron Tier:** +1 HP per Essence Rank
- **Bronze Tier:** +1 HP per Essence Rank

**Recovery** Wind does not linger long enough to rely on recovery. It disengages instead of healing.
- **Iron Tier:** No additional Healing Surges
- **Bronze Tier:** No additional Healing Surges

**Exertion** Wind strongly supports stamina-based play, enabling repeated movement, repositioning, and tactical exertion.
- **Iron Tier:** Gain +1 Stamina at Rank 1 and again at Rank 5
- **Bronze Tier:** Gain +1 Stamina at Bronze Rank 1. No further Stamina gained from Wind in Bronze

**Focus:** Wind’s magic is situational and secondary. Mana is available, but limited.
- **Iron Tier:** Gain +1 Mana at Rank 1
- **Bronze Tier:** No additional Mana gained from Wind in Bronze

---

### Essence Mastery Milestones
**Rank 3 — Rapid Engagement** Wind is often first to act. Gain +1 Initiative.
**Rank 5 — Accelerated Reflexes** Increase Dexterity by +1.
**Rank 9 — Uncatchable** Gain +1 AC when using cloth or no armor.
