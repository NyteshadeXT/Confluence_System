**Core Concept:** Adaptation, flow, persistence  

Water emphasizes endurance through adjustment rather than resistance. It survives by redirecting force, smoothing damage over time, and remaining effective under pressure.

**Associated Ability Score:** Wisdom

**Durability:** Water characters are difficult to wear down. They do not absorb punishment like stone or fire, but they lose effectiveness more slowly over time.
- **Iron Tier:** +1 HP per Essence Rank
- **Bronze Tier:** +1 HP per Essence Rank

**Recovery:** Water is the primary recovery-oriented Common Essence. It favors sustained operations and repeated engagements.
- **Iron Tier:** Gain +1 Healing Surge at Rank 7
- **Bronze Tier:** Gain +1 additional Healing Surge at Bronze Rank 1

**Exertion:** Water supports steady exertion but discourages explosive output. Its stamina comes online early but does not scale aggressively.
- **Iron Tier:** Gain +1 Stamina at Rank 1
- **Bronze Tier:** No additional Stamina gained from Water in Bronze

**Focus:** Water fuels magic through continuity rather than intensity. Mana growth is gradual and stabilizing.
- **Iron Tier:** Gain +1 Mana at Rank 3
- **Bronze Tier:** Gain +1 Mana at Bronze Rank 1

---

### Training Interaction
Water grants defensive training aligned with adaptive protection rather than rigid armor. At Rank 6, Water grants one of the following proficiencies **Hide Armor Proficiency** OR **Light** **Shield Proficiency.**

Water does not grant weapon category proficiency.

---

### Essence Mastery Milestones
**Rank 3 — Steady Current** Water maintains composure under sustained pressure. Gain +1 Will. +1 to Implement attack rolls with Healing or Restoration powers.
**Rank 5 — Fluid Awareness** Increase Wisdom by +1.
**Rank 9 — Persistent Flow** Water endures through continuity, shedding conditions gradually rather than resisting them outright. Gain +1 to Saving Throws.
