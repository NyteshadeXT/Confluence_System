**Core Concept:** Shelter, patience, enduring strength  

Tree represents slow resilience and protection. It favors defense, longevity, and stability over adaptation or aggression.

**Associated Ability Score:** Constitution

**Durability:** Tree provides strong durability, but less than Stone. It is built to protect and endure.
- **Iron Tier:** +3 HP per Essence Rank
- **Bronze Tier:** +3 HP per Essence Rank

**Recovery:** Tree is deeply restorative, emphasizing protection through recovery rather than denial.
- **Iron Tier:** Gain +1 Healing Surge at Rank 1 and Rank 3
- **Bronze Tier:** Gain +1 Healing Surge at Bronze Rank 1

**Exertion:** Tree does not favor repeated exertion. It acts deliberately and holds position.
- **Iron Tier:** Gain +1 Stamina at Rank 3
- **Bronze Tier:** No additional Stamina gained from Tree in Bronze

**Focus:** Tree supports supportive and protective magic, but not sustained casting.
- **Iron Tier:** Gain +1 Mana at Rank 1
- **Bronze Tier:** No additional Mana gained from Tree in Bronze

---

### Essence Mastery Milestones
**Rank 3 — Barkskin Resolve** Tree withstands physical force. Gain +1 Fortitude.
**Rank 5 — Deep Roots** Increase Constitution by +1.
**Rank 9 — Living Shelter** Gain +1 AC AND +1 to Saving Throws.
