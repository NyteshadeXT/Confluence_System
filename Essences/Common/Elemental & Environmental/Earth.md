**Core Concept:** Stability, resistance, foundation  

Earth emphasizes holding ground and enduring pressure through sheer stability. It absorbs force, resists displacement, and remains functional under sustained assault.

**Associated Ability Score:** Constitution

**Durability:** Earth is the most reliably durable Common Essence. It is built to take hits without collapsing.
- **Iron Tier:** +3 HP per Essence Rank
- **Bronze Tier:** +3 HP per Essence Rank

**Recovery:** Earth recovers slowly. It expects to survive through toughness rather than rapid healing.
- **Iron Tier:** +1 Healing Surge at Iron Rank 3 and 7
- **Bronze Tier:** Gain **+**1 Healing Surge at Bronze Rank 1

**Exertion:** Earth supports physical exertion tied to holding position and resisting force, but not repeated bursts.
- **Iron Tier:** Gain +1 Stamina at Rank 1
- **Bronze Tier:** Gain +1 Stamina at Bronze Rank 1. No further Stamina gained from Earth in Bronze

**Focus:** Earth’s relationship with magic is minimal and utilitarian.
- **Iron Tier:** No Mana gained from Earth
- **Bronze Tier:** Gain +1 Mana at Bronze Rank 1

---

### Essence Mastery Milestones
**Rank 3 — Rooted Stance** Earth resists physical force and bodily harm. Gain +1 Fortitude.
**Rank 5 — Enduring Frame** Increase Constitution by +1.
**Rank 9 — Unyielding Foundation** Gain +1 AC.
