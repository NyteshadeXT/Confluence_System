**Core Concept:** Speed, precision, sudden force  

Lightning emphasizes immediacy and decisive action. It rewards acting first, hitting hard, and ending conflicts quickly rather than enduring them.

**Associated Ability Score:** Dexterity

**Durability:** Lightning does not linger in danger. Its durability gains are intentionally low.
- **Iron Tier:** +1 HP per Essence Rank
- **Bronze Tier:** +1 HP per Essence Rank

**Recovery:** Lightning does not plan for recovery. It expects resolution.
- **Iron Tier:** No additional Healing Surges
- **Bronze Tier:** No additional Healing Surges

**Exertion:** Lightning strongly favors repeated exertion, especially in short bursts.
- **Iron Tier:** Gain +1 Stamina at Rank 1 and again at Rank 5
- **Bronze Tier:** Gain +1 Stamina at Bronze Rank 1. No further Stamina gained from Lightning in Bronze

**Focus:** Lightning fuels magic explosively rather than sustainably.
- **Iron Tier:** Gain +1 Mana at Rank 1
- **Bronze Tier:** Gain +1 Mana at Bronze Rank 1. No further Mana gained from Lightning in Bronze

---

### Training Interaction
Lightning does not grant armor proficiency.

Lightning grants weapon training aligned with speed and decisive action. At Rank 6, Lightning grants one of the following proficiencies: **Light Blade Group Proficiency** OR **Military Ranged Proficiency.**

---

### Essence Mastery Milestones
**Rank 3 — Quickened Reflex** Gain +1 Initiative.
**Rank 5 — Precise Motion** Increase Dexterity by +1.
**Rank 9 — Enhanced Reaction** Gain +1 to Reflex saves.
