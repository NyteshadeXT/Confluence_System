**Core Concept:** Speed, precision, sudden force  

Lightning emphasizes immediacy and decisive action. It rewards acting first, hitting hard, and ending conflicts quickly rather than enduring them.

> [!Associated Ability Score]-
> Dexterity

# Iron
> [!Information]-
> ## Durability
> Lightning does not linger in danger. Its durability gains are intentionally low.
> - **Iron Tier:** +1 HP per Essence Rank
> 
> ## Recovery
> Lightning does not plan for recovery. It expects resolution.
> - **Iron Tier:** No additional Healing Surges
> 
> ## Exertion
> Lightning strongly favors repeated exertion, especially in short bursts.
> - **Iron Tier:** Gain +1 Stamina at Rank 1 and again at Rank 5
>
> ## Focus
> Lightning fuels magic explosively rather than sustainably.
> - **Iron Tier:** Gain +1 Mana at Rank 1
