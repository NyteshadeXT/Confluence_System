**Core Concept:** Decay, spread, resilience  

Fungus thrives in hostile environments and persists through neglect. It survives by converting damage, time, and inattention into advantage, favoring attrition over confrontation.

**Associated Ability Score:** Constitution

**Durability:** Fungus bodies are resilient but soft. They endure by continuing to function after damage rather than preventing it.
- **Iron Tier:** +2 HP per Essence Rank
- **Bronze Tier:** +2 HP per Essence Rank

**Recovery:** Fungus excels at long-term survival. Its recovery represents regeneration and slow reconstitution rather than burst healing.
- **Iron Tier:** Gain +1 Healing Surge at Rank 3
- **Bronze Tier:** Gain +1 Healing Surge at Bronze Rank 1

**Exertion:** Fungus does not favor physical exertion. It spreads passively and endures.
- **Iron Tier:** No additional Stamina gained from Fungus
- **Bronze Tier:** Gain +1 Stamina at Bronze Rank 1

**Focus** Fungus supports magic tied to decay, persistence, and environmental influence.
- **Iron Tier:** Gain +1 Mana at Rank 3
- **Bronze Tier:** Gain +1 Mana at Bronze Rank 1

---

### Essence Mastery Milestones
**Rank 3 — Rotting Persistence** Fungus survives through condition resistance and slow reconstitution. Gain +1 to Saving Throws.
**Rank 5 — Spreading Body** Increase Constitution by +1.
**Rank 9 — Indifferent to Decay** Gain +1 Fortitude AND +1 to Saving Throws.
