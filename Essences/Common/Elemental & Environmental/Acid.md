**Core Concept:** Corrosion, erosion, persistent harm

Acid breaks down what resists it. It favors persistent damage, weakened defenses, and the gradual destruction of armor, barriers, and certainty rather than immediate force or overwhelming impact.

**Associated Ability Score:** Intelligence

**Durability:** Acid relies on distance, positioning, and the inevitability of corrosion. Its durability is modest.
- **Iron Tier:** +1 HP per Essence Rank
- **Bronze Tier:** +1 HP per Essence Rank

**Recovery:** Acid does not rely on recovery. It wins by making every exchange increasingly costly for its opponent.
- **Iron Tier:** No additional Healing Surges
- **Bronze Tier:** No additional Healing Surges

**Exertion:** Acid does not favor sustained physical exertion.
- **Iron Tier:** No Stamina gained from Acid
- **Bronze Tier:** No Stamina gained from Acid

**Focus:** Acid has a strong natural affinity for Mana, using it to sustain corrosive pressure and shape destructive effects.
- **Iron Tier:** Gain +1 Mana at Rank 1 and again at Rank 6
- **Bronze Tier:** Gain +1 Mana at Bronze Rank 1. No further Mana gained from Acid in Bronze

---

### Essence Mastery Milestones

**Rank 3 — Caustic Resistance** Acid endures hostile substances and degrading effects. Gain +1 Fortitude.  
**Rank 5 — Corrosive Intellect** Increase Intelligence by +1.  
**Rank 9 — Dissolving Strike** Gain +1 Fortitude AND +1 to Attack Rolls with Acid or Implement powers.
