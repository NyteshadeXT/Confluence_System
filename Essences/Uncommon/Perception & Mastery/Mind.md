**Core Concept:** Patient, calculated, and manipulative

Mind dominates through superior perception, foresight, and control of thought. It wins by unraveling an opponent's intentions, exploiting hesitation, and shaping the battlefield through influence rather than brute force.

> [!Associated Ability Score]-
> Intelligence

# Iron
> [!Information]-
> ## Durability
> Mind avoids direct confrontation whenever possible. Its resilience comes from preventing threats rather than enduring them.
> - **Iron Tier:** +2 HP per Essence Rank
> 
> ## Recovery
> Mental discipline allows Mind to recover from psychic strain, but it places little emphasis on physical endurance.
> - **Iron Tier:** Gain +1 Healing Surge at Iron Rank 3
> 
> ## Exertion
> Mind favors deliberate action over relentless offense. It expends stamina only when positioning or timing demands it.
> - **Iron Tier:** Gain +1 Stamina at Rank 3
>
> ## Focus
> Mind's greatest strength is disciplined concentration. Nearly every expression of the Essence relies on precise mental control and sustained focus.
> - **Iron Tier:** Gain +2 Mana at Rank 3
