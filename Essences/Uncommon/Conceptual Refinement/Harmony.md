## **Core Concept:** Coordinated, supportive, and resonant

Harmony triumphs by bringing disparate forces into perfect alignment. It strengthens allies, amplifies shared purpose, and transforms individual actions into coordinated excellence through resonance and synchronization.

**Associated Ability Score:** Charisma

---

### Durability

Harmony avoids conflict through cooperation rather than personal resilience. Its greatest protection comes from strengthening those around it.

- **Iron Tier:** +2 HP per Essence Rank
    
- **Bronze Tier:** +3 HP per Essence Rank
    

---

### Recovery

Harmony naturally restores balance and reinforces allies, making recovery one of its defining strengths.

- **Iron Tier:** Gain +2 Healing Surges at Iron Rank 3
    
- **Bronze Tier:** +3 Healing Surges at Bronze
    

---

### Exertion

Harmony favors measured, deliberate actions over relentless offense. It conserves stamina while maintaining a constant supportive presence.

- **Iron Tier:** Gain +1 Stamina at Rank 3
    
- **Bronze Tier:** +2 Stamina at Bronze
    

---

### Focus

Directing resonance requires exceptional concentration. Harmony channels magical energy into sustaining allies and maintaining perfect synchronization.

- **Iron Tier:** Gain +2 Mana at Rank 3
    
- **Bronze Tier:** +3 Mana at Bronze
    

---

### Essence Mastery Milestones

**Rank 3 — Resonant Presence**  
Gain **+1 Will**.

**Rank 5 — Harmonious Spirit**  
Increase **Charisma** **OR** **Wisdom** by **+1**.

**Rank 9 — Perfect Synchronization**  
Choose one:

- Gain a **+1 bonus to all saving throws while adjacent to an ally**, **OR**
    
- Whenever you grant a bonus to an ally, increase that bonus by **1** (to a maximum of +3 from this effect).

## Design Philosophy

Harmony is **not** "the bard Essence." It is the Essence of **resonance and synchronization**. Music is simply one of its most recognizable expressions.

Harmony naturally excels at:

- Buffs
    
- Healing
    
- Temporary Hit Points
    
- Morale
    
- Shared defenses
    
- Coordinated movement
    
- Action-granting effects
    
- Auras
    
- Inspiration
    
- Resonance
    
- Sonic magic used to unite rather than destroy
    

It should avoid:

- Direct battlefield control
    
- Domination
    
- Heavy personal offense
    
- Brutal force
    
- Solitary combat styles
    
- Chaotic or unpredictable effects
    

---

### Why Harmony instead of Music?

"Music" describes a **medium**. "Harmony" describes a **principle**.

A choir, synchronized soldiers, interlocking gears, magical resonance, celestial choirs, crystalline vibrations, coordinated spellcasting, and perfectly timed attacks all express **Harmony** without requiring literal music.

That gives the Essence a much broader identity and makes it feel at home alongside conceptual Essences like **Order**, **Renewal**, and **Reflection**. It also opens interesting Confluence space—**Storm + Harmony** could become a perfectly coordinated tempest, while **Mind + Harmony** could represent a psychic network or shared consciousness—without every power needing to involve songs or instruments.
