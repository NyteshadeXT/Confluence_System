#### Force (Needs a rewrite for new uncommon location and concept)

**Force represents pure arcane pressure**

**Core Concept:** Direct application of power  
Force imposes outcomes without negotiation. It favors clarity, inevitability, and direct resolution over setup, attrition, or endurance.

**Durability:** Force expects conflicts to be resolved decisively. It does not plan to endure prolonged engagements.

- **Iron Tier:** +2 HP per Essence Rank
- **Bronze Tier:** +2 HP per Essence Rank

**Recovery:** Force does not rely on recovery. It expects resolution through decisive action.

- **Iron Tier:** No additional Healing Surges
- **Bronze Tier:** No additional Healing Surges

**Exertion:** Force strongly supports repeated exertion tied to direct, decisive actions.

- **Iron Tier:** Gain +1 Stamina at Rank 1 and again at Rank 6
- **Bronze Tier:** Gain +1 Stamina at Bronze Rank 1. No further Stamina gained from Force in Bronze

**Focus:** Force has a strong magical identity tied to direct manifestation and control.

- **Iron Tier:** Gain +1 Mana at Rank 1 and again at Rank 6
- **Bronze Tier:** Gain +1 Mana at Bronze Rank 1. No further Mana gained from Force in Bronze
