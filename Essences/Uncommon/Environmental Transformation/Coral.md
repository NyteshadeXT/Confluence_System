**Core Concept:** Accretion, defense, hostile terrain  

Coral grows through accumulation and resistance. It reshapes space into dangerous environments that punish intrusion and reward control.

**Associated Ability Score:** Constitution

**Durability:** Coral is hard and layered, but brittle when overextended. Its durability reflects accretion rather than flexibility.
- **Iron Tier:** +3 HP per Essence Rank
- **Bronze Tier:** +3 HP per Essence Rank

**Recovery:** Coral does not heal quickly. Once broken, it takes time to rebuild.
- **Iron Tier:** +1 Healing Surge at Iron Rank 7
- **Bronze Tier:** Gain +1 Healing Surge at Bronze Rank 1

**Exertion:** Coral favors exertion tied to holding space and resisting removal, not repeated action.
- **Iron Tier:** Gain +1 Stamina at Rank 3
- **Bronze Tier:** Gain +1 Stamina at Bronze Rank 1

**Focus:** Coral’s magic is structural and environmental rather than expressive.
-  **Iron Tier:** No Mana gained from Coral
- **Bronze Tier:** Gain +1 Mana at Bronze Rank 1

---

### Essence Mastery Milestones
**Rank 3 — Layered Carapace** Coral hardens through accretion. Gain +1 AC.
**Rank 5 — Growing Structure** Increase Constitution by +1.
**Rank 9 — Hostile Foundation** Gain +1 Fortitude AND +1 AC.
