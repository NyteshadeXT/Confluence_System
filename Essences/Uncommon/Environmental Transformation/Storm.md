**Core Concept:** Aggressive, escalating, and relentless

Storm overwhelms by unleashing relentless, dynamic force that is difficult to predict or withstand. It wins by disrupting the battlefield and forcing opponents to react rather than execute their own plans.

**Associated Ability Score:** Strength

**Durability:** Storm is difficult to pin down, but it does not rely on exceptional toughness. Its durability reflects an active combat style rather than endurance.
- **Iron Tier:** +3 HP per Essence Rank
- **Bronze Tier:** +4 HP per Essence Rank

**Recovery:** Storm survives through momentum and battlefield control more than sustained recovery.
- **Iron Tier:** +1 Healing Surge at Iron Rank 3
- **Bronze Tier:** +2 Healing Surge at Bronze

**Exertion:** Storm favors repeated offensive actions and frequent repositioning. It naturally supports an active, high-tempo fighting style.
- **Iron Tier:** Gain +2 Stamina at Rank 3
- **Bronze Tier:** +3 Stamina at Bronze

**Focus:** Although rooted in elemental power, Storm is more than physical force. Directing the fury of a tempest requires significant magical focus, making it a balanced hybrid between martial exertion and elemental manipulation.
- **Iron Tier:** Gain +1 Mana at Rank 3
- **Bronze Tier:** +1 Stamina at Bronze

### Essence Mastery Milestones
**Rank 3 — Eye of the Tempest**  Gain +1 Reflex.
**Rank 5 — Gathering Tempest**  Increase Dexterity **OR** Strength by +1.
**Rank 9 — Stormborne**  Choose one: Gain a +1 bonus to Speed, **OR** gain +1 to attack rolls made after moving at least 2 squares on your turn.
