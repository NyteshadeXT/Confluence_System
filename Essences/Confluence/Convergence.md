**Nature:** Unity / Fusion  
**Core Concept:** Merging, shared identity, many becoming one

Convergence defines power as the dissolution of boundaries between separate forces. It does not consume or dominate. Instead, it merges distinct elements into unified expression. Identity is not erased — it is harmonized into something greater than the sum of its parts.

Convergence is not cooperation. It is singularity through union.

**Associated Ability Score:** Wisdom or Charisma (choose at acquisition)

## COMMON CONTRIBUTING ESSENCE PATTERNS

Convergence forms when unity, resonance, and alignment intersect. Representative combinations include:

|   |   |
|---|---|
|**Bond + Balance + Light**|**Harmony + Life + Order**|
|**Light + Sound + Awareness**|Light + Order + Harmony|

Any triad that dissolves boundaries between participants, aligns separate forces into singular purpose, and strengthens through unity rather than hierarchy naturally resolves into Convergence.

A triad resolves into Convergence only if unity is mutual and symmetrical. If one identity absorbs another, it resolves into Assimilation. If obligation remains distinct, it resolves into Bond.

**Durability** Convergence favors shared resilience.
·         **Iron Tier:** +2 HP per Essence Rank
·         **Bronze Tier:** +2 HP per Essence Rank

**Recovery** Convergence stabilizes through unified structure.
·         **Iron Tier:** Gain +1 Healing Surge at Rank 3
·         **Bronze Tier:** Gain +1 Healing Surge at Bronze Rank 1

**Exertion** Convergence supports sustained alignment.
·         **Iron Tier:** Gain +1 Stamina at Rank 5
·         **Bronze Tier:** No additional Stamina

**Focus** Convergence strongly favors magical resonance.
·         **Iron Tier:** Gain +1 Mana at Rank 3 and Rank 7
·         **Bronze Tier:** Gain +1 Mana at Bronze Rank 1

## RANK MILESTONES
**Rank 3 — Unified Will** You gain a +1 bonus to Will defense.
**Rank 5 — Harmonized Presence** Increase Wisdom or Charisma by +1.
**Rank 7 — Shared Fortitude** You gain a +1 bonus to Fortitude defense.
**Rank 9 — Singular Purpose** You gain a +1 bonus to saving throws.

## CONFLUENCE BACKLASH
**Backlash: Identity Bleed** When you use an Encounter power while Strained that benefits multiple allies, you grant combat advantage until the end of your next turn. Increase Dungeon Resonance by one category (to a maximum of Escalating).

Unity can blur boundaries — including your own.

## DUNGEON RESONANCE BIAS
Convergence is **Neutral** **→ Escalating**. Repeated Convergence behavior may cause collective enemies acting as single entities, linked environmental effects, shared damage pools in Dungeon manifestations and zones where individual actions ripple outward.

The Dungeon tolerates unity. It resists singularity.
