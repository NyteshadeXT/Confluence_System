**Nature:** Instability / Inversion
**Core Concept:** Contradiction, mutual reinforcement of opposites, balance through tension

Paradox embodies the coexistence of opposing truths. It strengthens through contradiction and destabilizes through certainty. Where others pursue purity or escalation, Paradox binds strength to weakness and advantage to cost.

Paradox does not seek resolution. It thrives in tension.

**Associated Ability Score** Charisma or Intelligence (choose at acquisition)

## Common Contributing Essence Patterns

|   |   |
|---|---|
|Order + Entropy + Force|Stone + Wind + Shield|
|Life + Entropy + Flesh|Order + Wind + Force|
|Iron + Wind + Rune|Growth + Stone + Force|
|Life + Stone + Entropy|Shield + Force + Entropy|

A triad resolves into Paradox only if it contains two opposing conceptual Essences, gains strength through contradiction and becomes weaker when unified or simplified.

**Durability** Paradox does not favor endurance.
·         Iron Tier: +1 HP per Essence Rank
·         Bronze Tier: +1 HP per Essence Rank

**Recovery** Paradox does not emphasize resilience.
·         Iron Tier: Gain +1 Healing Surge at Rank 3
·         Bronze Tier: No additional Healing Surges

**Exertion** Paradox favors volatile effort.
·         Iron Tier: Gain +1 Stamina at Rank 1
·         Bronze Tier: No additional Stamina

**Focus** Paradox supports unstable magical expression.
·         Iron Tier: Gain +1 Mana at Rank 3 and Rank 7
·         Bronze Tier: Gain +1 Mana at Bronze Rank 1

## TRAINING INTERACTION
At Rank 6, choose +2 bonus to Arcana checks involving magical instability, +2 bonus to Insight checks OR Light Shield Proficiency.

Paradox does not grant weapon or armor proficiency.

## RANK MILESTONES
**Rank 3 — Tension Awareness** Gain +1 to Reflex.
**Rank 5 — Sharpened Contradiction** Increase Charisma or Intelligence by +1 (based on selection during Essence acquisition).
**Rank 7 — Edge of Imbalance** Gain +1 to attack rolls while bloodied.
**Rank 9 — Reinforced Opposition** Gain +1 to all defenses while not bloodied.

## CONFLUENCE BACKLASH
**Backlash: Symmetry Collapse** When you use an Encounter power while Strained you suffer –1 to attack rolls and –1 to all defenses until the end of your next turn. Increase Dungeon Resonance by one category.

Paradox punishes imbalance without control.

## DUNGEON RESONANCE BIAS
Paradox is Escalating → Provocative. Repeated Paradox use causes environmental inversions, damage type reversal, sudden resistance shifts, gravity or positioning anomalies and creatures to gain resistance to one type and vulnerability to another.

The Dungeon dislikes unresolved contradiction.
