**Nature:** Authority / Control
**Core Concept** Territorial authority, imposed order, ownership of space

Dominion asserts the right to decide what may exist within a defined boundary. It transforms presence into authority, enforcing structure through occupation rather than motion. Dominion favors control, fortification, and inevitability over flexibility or pursuit.

Dominion does not chase opposition. It waits for opposition to submit or break.

**Associated Ability Score** Constitution or Strength (choose at acquisition)

Any triad that defines space as _owned, e_nforces behavior through presence and converts resistance into attrition naturally resolves into Dominion.

**Durability** Dominion is highly durable.
·         Iron Tier: +2 HP per Essence Rank
·         Bronze Tier: +2 HP per Essence Rank

**Recovery** Dominion sustains through endurance.
·         Iron Tier: Gain +1 Healing Surge at Rank 3
·         Bronze Tier: Gain +1 Healing Surge at Bronze Rank 1

**Exertion** Dominion favors sustained presence over mobility.
·         Iron Tier: Gain +1 Stamina at Rank 1
·         Bronze Tier: Gain +1 Stamina at Bronze Rank 1

**Focus** Dominion has limited magical bias.
·         Iron Tier: Gain +1 Mana at Rank 7
·         Bronze Tier: No additional Mana

## RANK MILESTONES
**Rank 3 — Established Presence** Gain +1 to Fortitude.
**Rank 5 — Imposing Form** Increase Constitution or Strength by +1 (based on selection during Essence acquisition).
**Rank 7 — Unyielding Ground** Gain +1 to AC while you did not move more than 1 square on your previous turn.
**Rank 9 — Authority of Presence** Gain +1 to attack rolls against creatures adjacent to you.

## CONFLUENCE BACKLASH
**Backlash: Overextension of Authority** When you use an Encounter power while Strained you take –1 to all defenses until the end of your next turn. If you moved more than 3 squares this round, increase Dungeon Resonance by one category.

Dominion punishes abandoning ground.

## DUNGEON RESONANCE BIAS
Dominion is Neutral → Escalating (if entrenched). Repeated Dominion behavior causes enemies to begin combats spread and target the Dominion wielder first.  Dungeon creatures to gain bonuses to forced movement. Environmental effects that disrupt static positioning. The Dungeon resists monopolization of space.
