**Nature:** Agency destruction
**Core Concept** Domination, erasure of will, imposed obedience

Subjugation defines power as the right to decide for others. It does not negotiate or persuade—it overwrites. Identity, choice, and resistance are reduced to obstacles to be removed. Power gained through Subjugation corrodes both victim and wielder. Subjugation leaves nothing voluntary behind.

All essence combinations resulting in the Subjugation Essence are considered forbidden by the Seekers guild.

**Associated Ability Score:** Charisma or Intelligence (choose at acquisition)

## Common Contributing Essence Patterns

|   |   |
|---|---|
|Oath + Structure + Power|Oath + Dominion + Force|
|Oath + Order + Force|Oath + Rune + Force|

Any triad that removes agency as a function of power, enforces obedience without consent, or treats will as a resource to be spent naturally risks resolving into Subjugation.

A triad resolves into Subjugation only if it contains a binding or structural Essence (Oath, Chain, Order, Cage) and a coercive or forceful Essence. If authority is mutual or freely chosen, it resolves into Bond. If control is territorial rather than personal, it resolves into Dominion.

Many Dungeons will react violently to its presence.

**Durability** Subjugation relies on imposed control, not endurance.
·         **Iron Tier:** +2 HP per Essence Rank
·         **Bronze Tier:** +2 HP per Essence Rank

**Recovery** Subjugation assumes dominance prevents retaliation rather than absorbing it.
·         **Iron Tier:** Gain +1 Healing Surge at Rank 5
·         **Bronze Tier:** No additional Healing Surges

**Exertion** Subjugation favors force of will and structured exertion.
·         **Iron Tier:** Gain +1 Stamina at Rank 3
·         **Bronze Tier:** Gain +1 Stamina at Bronze Rank 1

**Focus** Subjugation strongly favors mental dominance.
·         **Iron Tier:** Gain +1 Mana at Rank 3 and Rank 7
·         **Bronze Tier:** Gain +1 Mana at Bronze Rank 1

## RANK MILESTONES
**Rank 3 — Hardened Will** You gain a +1 bonus to Will defense.
**Rank 5 — Imposing Presence** Increase Charisma or Intelligence by +1 (based on selection during Essence acquisition).
**Rank 7 — Unyielding Authority** You gain a +1 bonus to saving throws against charm and fear effects.
**Rank 9 — Absolute Bearing** You gain a +1 bonus to Fortitude defense.

## CONFLUENCE BACKLASH
**Backlash: Rebound Defiance** When you use an Encounter power while Strained and that power imposes a condition that restricts or controls a target, you take –1 to Will until the end of your next turn. If the power forces movement or denies actions, you also take 1 psychic damage at the start of your next turn. Increase Dungeon Resonance by one category (to a maximum of Provocative).

## DUNGEON RESONANCE BIAS
Subjugation is **Escalating** **→ Provocative** by default. Repeated Subjugation behavior may cause enemies to gain temporary resistance to control effects, zones that resist compulsion, dungeon manifestations that attack the Subjugator directly and autonomous constructs that cannot be dominated.

The Dungeon tolerates power. It does not tolerate stolen agency.
