**Nature:** Accumulated consequence
**Core Concept:** Delayed judgment, stored consequence, inevitable release

Reckoning gathers unresolved actions and defers their resolution until balance must be paid. It rewards patience and foresight, but once released, consequence cannot be avoided or mitigated.

Reckoning is never sudden. It is overdue.

**Associated Ability Score:** Wisdom or Charisma (choose at acquisition)

## Common Contributing Essence Patterns

|   |   |
|---|---|
|Omen + Echo + Order|Eye + Echo + Balance|
|Oath + Omen + Order|Echo + Balance + Knowledge|
|Omen + Order + Rune||

Any triad that stores consequence rather than resolving it, converts history into pressure, or delays outcome until release is unavoidable naturally resolves into Reckoning.

A triad resolves into Reckoning only if it contains **Echo or Omen**, and at least one structural or moral stabilizer (Order, Balance, Oath). If consequence is returned immediately, it resolves into Reflection. If agency is overwritten, it resolves into Subjugation.

**Durability** Reckoning is steady but not fortified.
·         **Iron Tier:** +2 HP per Essence Rank
·         **Bronze Tier:** +2 HP per Essence Rank

**Recovery** Reckoning assumes endurance through inevitability.
·         **Iron Tier:** Gain +1 Healing Surge at Rank 5
·         **Bronze Tier:** Gain +1 Healing Surge at Bronze Rank 1

**Exertion** Reckoning supports deliberate restraint.
·         **Iron Tier:** Gain +1 Stamina at Rank 3
·         **Bronze Tier:** No additional Stamina

**Focus** Reckoning favors stored intent and measured release.
·         **Iron Tier:** Gain +1 Mana at Rank 3 and Rank 7
·         **Bronze Tier:** Gain +1 Mana at Bronze Rank 1

## RANK MILESTONES
**Rank 3 — Unavoidable Memory** You gain a +1 bonus to Will defense.
**Rank 5 — Measured Judgment** Increase Wisdom or Charisma by +1 (based on selection during Essence acquisition).
**Rank 7 — Patient Resolve** You gain a +1 bonus to saving throws.
**Rank 9 — Inevitable Presence** You gain a +1 bonus to Fortitude defense.

## CONFLUENCE BACKLASH
**Backlash: Premature Release** When you use an Encounter power while Strained and it imposes a delayed or ongoing effect, that effect ends at the start of your next turn. You take 1 psychic damage at the start of your next turn. Increase Dungeon Resonance by one category (to a maximum of Escalating).

## DUNGEON RESONANCE BIAS
Reckoning is **Neutral** **→ Escalating**. Repeated Reckoning behavior may cause time-distortion zones, delayed effects resolving unpredictably, consequences manifesting earlier than intended and environmental hazards that “remember” past actions.

The Dungeon tolerates justice. It resists inevitability imposed without context.
