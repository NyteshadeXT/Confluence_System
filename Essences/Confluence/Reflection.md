**Nature:** Reactive consequence
**Core Concept** Response, inversion, earned consequence

Reflection defines power as something returned rather than initiated. It turns aggression, force, or intent back upon its source, enforcing consequence through response. Reflection rewards awareness, restraint, and timing over initiative.

Reflection does nothing—until it does everything.

**Associated Ability Score:** Wisdom or Intelligence (choose at acquisition)

## Common Contributing Essence Patterns

|   |   |
|---|---|
|Mirror + Shield + Order|Eye + Force + Balance|
|Mirror + Rune + Order|Shield + Eye + Balance|
|Eye + Sound + Balance||

Any triad that prioritizes response over action, converts enemy intent into liability, or enforces consequence through inversion rather than dominance naturally resolves into Reflection.

A triad resolves into Reflection only if it contains **Mirror or Eye**, and at least one stabilizing Essence (Shield, Order, Balance). If consequence is stored for later release, it resolves into Reckoning. If options are removed outright, it resolves into Suppression.

**Durability** Reflection favors resilience through control, not fortification.
·         **Iron Tier:** +2 HP per Essence Rank
·         **Bronze Tier:** +2 HP per Essence Rank

**Recovery** Reflection assumes calculated restraint.
·         **Iron Tier:** Gain +1 Healing Surge at Rank 5
·         **Bronze Tier:** Gain +1 Healing Surge at Bronze Rank 1

**Exertion** Reflection is measured and controlled.
·         **Iron Tier:** Gain +1 Stamina at Rank 3
·         **Bronze Tier:** No additional Stamina

**Focus** Reflection benefits from mental clarity.
·         **Iron Tier:** Gain +1 Mana at Rank 3 and Rank 7
·         **Bronze Tier:** Gain +1 Mana at Bronze Rank 1

## RANK MILESTONES
**Rank 3 — Composed Awareness** You gain a +1 bonus to Will saving throws.
**Rank 5 — Measured Mind** Increase Wisdom or Intelligence by +1 (based on selection during Essence acquisition).
**Rank 7 — Unshaken Poise** You gain a +1 bonus to saving throws against forced movement.
**Rank 9 — Perfect Timing** You gain a +1 bonus to initiative rolls when you are not surprised.

## CONFLUENCE BACKLASH
**Backlash: Misjudged Response** When you use an Encounter power while Strained and it is triggered by an enemy’s action, you take –1 to all defenses until the end of your next turn. If the power negates or redirects damage, you also take 1 damage at the start of your next turn. Increase Dungeon Resonance by one category (to a maximum of Escalating).

## DUNGEON RESONANCE BIAS
Reflection is **Neutral**. Excessive reactive behavior may cause enemies to act unpredictably, effects to become non-reflectable, zones to distort symmetry and outcomes to resist inversion.

The Dungeon tolerates consequence. It resists perfection.
