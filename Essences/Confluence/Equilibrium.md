**Nature:** Dynamic balance / self-correction
**Core Concept** Balance, correction, opposition held in tension

Equilibrium defines power as the ability to maintain balance in the presence of opposing forces. It does not suppress extremes, nor does it allow them to dominate. Instead, it corrects deviation, redistributing pressure until stability is restored.

Equilibrium does not prevent change. It prevents collapse.

**Associated Ability Score:** Wisdom or Constitution (choose at acquisition)

## Common Contributing Essence Patterns

|   |   |
|---|---|
|Balance + Order + Force|Balance + Light + Shadow|
|Shield + Balance + Awareness|Balance + Stone + Awareness|
|Light + Shadow + Structure||

Any triad that contains opposing or contradictory impulses, seeks stability without dominance and responds to excess with correction rather than punishment naturally resolves into **Equilibrium**.

A triad resolves into Equilibrium only if it contains **Balance**, and at least one stabilizing Essence (Order, Shield, Stone, Awareness). If excess is punished rather than corrected, it resolves into Reflection or Reckoning. If dominance is imposed, it resolves into Dominion.

**Durability** Equilibrium favors steady resilience.
·         **Iron Tier:** +2 HP per Essence Rank
·         **Bronze Tier:** +2 HP per Essence Rank

**Recovery** Equilibrium restores stability through endurance.
·         **Iron Tier:** Gain +1 Healing Surge at Rank 3
·         **Bronze Tier:** Gain +1 Healing Surge at Bronze Rank 1

**Exertion** Equilibrium requires measured effort.
·         **Iron Tier:** Gain +1 Stamina at Rank 5
·         **Bronze Tier:** No additional Stamina

**Focus** Equilibrium favors mindful correction.
·         **Iron Tier:** Gain +1 Mana at Rank 3
·         **Bronze Tier:** Gain +1 Mana at Bronze Rank 1

## TRAINING INTERACTION
At Rank 6, choose one gain proficiency with **Chain Armor, g**ain proficiency with **Light Shields,** gain proficiency with Staff as an Implement **OR g**ain +2 bonus to Insight checks involving conflict resolution or detecting imbalance.

## RANK MILESTONES
**Rank 3 — Centered Form** You gain a +1 bonus to Will defense.
**Rank 5 — Balanced Mind** Increase Wisdom or Constitution by +1.
**Rank 7 — Stabilized Body** You gain a +1 bonus to Fortitude defense.
**Rank 9 — Measured Response** You gain a +1 bonus to saving throws.

## CONFLUENCE BACKLASH
**Backlash: Overcorrection** When you use an Encounter power while Strained that reduces or redistributes damage, you take 1 damage at the start of your next turn. Increase Dungeon Resonance by one category (to a maximum of Escalating).

Equilibrium must not be forced. Correction imposed recklessly destabilizes.

## DUNGEON RESONANCE BIAS
Equilibrium is **Neutral**. Repeated Equilibrium behavior may cause extremes to manifest more strongly, oscillating environmental effects, cyclical hazards and forces that test balance repeatedly.

The Dungeon tolerates balance. It resists stagnation.
