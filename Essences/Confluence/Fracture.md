**Nature:** Structural instability
**Core Concept** Breakage, cascading failure, loss of cohesion

Fracture weakens systems until collapse becomes self-propagating. It undermines structure, certainty, and integrity, often spreading faster than it can be contained. Fracture is powerful—but rarely precise.

Fracture never breaks only one thing.

**Associated Ability Score:** Intelligence or Strength (choose at acquisition)

## Common Contributing Essence Patterns

|   |   |
|---|---|
|Ruin + Glass + Force|Glass + Momentum + Ruin|
|Ruin + Knowledge + Force|Ruin + Force + Balance|
|Glass + Ruin + Order||

Any triad that causes cascading failure, weakens integrity faster than repair and turns damage into spread tends to resolve into **Fracture**.

A triad resolves into Fracture only if it contains **Glass or Ruin**, and at least one pressure or force-aligned Essence. If collapse is slow and inevitable, it resolves into Entropy. If collapse is territorial control, it resolves into Dominion.

**Durability** Fracture is unstable and does not favor endurance.
·         **Iron Tier:** +1 HP per Essence Rank
·         **Bronze Tier:** +1 HP per Essence Rank

**Recovery** Fracture survives through momentum, not resilience.
·         **Iron Tier:** Gain +1 Healing Surge at Rank 7
·         **Bronze Tier:** No additional Healing Surges

**Exertion** Fracture favors forceful application.
·         **Iron Tier:** Gain +1 Stamina at Rank 3
·         **Bronze Tier:** Gain +1 Stamina at Bronze Rank 1

**Focus** Fracture supports destabilizing magic.
·         **Iron Tier:** Gain +1 Mana at Rank 3
·         **Bronze Tier:** Gain +1 Mana at Bronze Rank 1

## RANK MILESTONES
**Rank 3 — Cracked Foundation** You gain a +1 bonus to Reflex defense.
**Rank 5 — Weaponized Insight** Increase Intelligence or Strength by +1 (based on selection during Essence acquisition).
**Rank 7 — Unstable Footing** You gain a +1 bonus to saving throws against effects that would knock you prone.
**Rank 9 — Cascading Force** You gain a +1 bonus to Fortitude defense.

## CONFLUENCE BACKLASH
**Backlash: Uncontrolled Collapse** When you use an Encounter power while Strained and that power damages multiple targets or creates a zone, you take 1 damage and grant combat advantage until the end of your next turn. Increase Dungeon Resonance by one category (to a maximum of Escalating).

Fracture spreads — including into the wielder.

## DUNGEON RESONANCE BIAS
Fracture is **Escalating** by default. Repeated Fracture behavior may cause structural hazards to propagate unpredictably, cracks in terrain to widen, magical effects to destabilize and environmental damage to chain outward.

The Dungeon tolerates breakage. It resists collapse without purpose.
