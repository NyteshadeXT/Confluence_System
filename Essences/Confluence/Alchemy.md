**Nature:** Process / Transformation
**Core Concept:** Conversion, leverage, dangerous refinement

Alchemy turns one state into another through intent and process. It values preparation, experimentation, and transformation, but demands cost, risk, and control. Power gained through Alchemy is never free—it is delayed, conditional, and often volatile.

Alchemy rewards those who understand what they are sacrificing.

**Associated Ability Score:** Intelligence

## Common Contributing Essence Patterns
Alchemy forms when knowledge, mutable substance, and deliberate alteration intersect. Representative combinations include:

|   |   |
|---|---|
|Adept + Venom + Water|Knowledge + Venom + Glass|
|Adept + Blood + Water|Magic + Venom + Flesh|
|Glass + Water + Knowledge|Knowledge + Water + Magic|
|Adept + Glass + Water|Knowledge + Flesh + Water|
|Adept + Magic + Glass||

Any triad that converts one resource or state into another, requires preparation or refinement and risks instability when rushed is prone to resolve into Alchemy. A triad resolves into Alchemy only if it contains Knowledge or Adept and at least one mutable substance Essence (Water, Glass, Flesh, Venom, Blood).

**Durability** Alchemy does not emphasize endurance.
·         Iron Tier: +1 HP per Essence Rank
·         Bronze Tier: +1 HP per Essence Rank

**Recovery** Alchemy sustains itself through preparation rather than resilience.
·         Iron Tier: Gain +1 Healing Surge at Rank 3
·         Bronze Tier: No additional Healing Surges

**Exertion** Alchemy supports calculated expenditure.
·         Iron Tier: Gain +1 Stamina at Rank 1
·         Bronze Tier: Gain +1 Stamina at Bronze Rank 1

**Focus** Alchemy strongly favors magical refinement.
·         Iron Tier: Gain +1 Mana at Rank 3 and Rank 7
·         Bronze Tier: Gain +1 Mana at Bronze Rank 1

## RANK MILESTONES
**Rank 3 — Measured Preparation** Gain +1 to saving throws against ongoing damage effects.
**Rank 5 — Refined Mind** Increase Intelligence by +1.
**Rank 7 — Efficient Conversion** +1 to attack rolls with powers that require Mana or Stamina.
**Rank 9 — Controlled Volatility** When you deal ongoing damage, increase that damage by +1. When you remove ongoing damage from yourself, gain +1 to the next saving throw you make before the end of your next turn.

## CONFLUENCE BACKLASH
**Backlash: Volatile Mixture** When you use an Encounter power while Strained you take –1 to all defenses until the end of your next turn. If that power deals ongoing damage, you also take 1 damage at the start of your next turn. Increase Dungeon Resonance by one category (to a maximum of Forbidden).

## DUNGEON RESONANCE BIAS
Alchemy is Neutral → Escalating. Repeated Alchemical behavior causes magical zones to fluctuate unpredictably, ongoing damage effects to randomly change damage types and environmental hazards to become volatile or unstable.

The Dungeon tolerates refinement—until manipulation becomes exploitation.
