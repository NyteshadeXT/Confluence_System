**Nature:** Escalation / Flow
**Core Concept** Continuation, carried force, unstoppable progression

Momentum transforms action into inevitability. Each success reinforces the next, rewarding commitment and follow-through. Momentum favors sustained motion, chaining actions, and refusing disengagement over precision or patience.

Momentum ends only when forcibly stopped.

**Associated Ability Score** Dexterity or Strength (choose at acquisition)

## Common Contributing Essence Patterns

|   |   |
|---|---|
|Swift + Might + Hunt|Hunt + Wind + Force|
|Swift + Hunt + Force|Swift + Might + Wind|
|Hunt + Might + Wind||

Any triad that rewards continuation, converts success into further action and loses power when halted resolves cleanly into Momentum. Conversely pure speed, evasion and repositioning without follow-through do not resolve into Momentum.

A triad resolves into Momentum only if it contains at least one motion Essence (Swift or Wind) and at least one continuation or commitment Essence (Hunt or Might). If it instead feeds upon destruction or consumption, it resolves into Devouring.

**Durability** Momentum survives through motion rather than endurance.
·         Iron Tier: +1 HP per Essence Rank
·         Bronze Tier: +1 HP per Essence Rank

**Recovery** Momentum does not favor long attrition.
·         Iron Tier: Gain +1 Healing Surge at Rank 3
·         Bronze Tier: No additional Healing Surges

**Exertion** Momentum strongly favors stamina and physical flow.
·         Iron Tier: Gain +1 Stamina at Rank 1 and Rank 5
·         Bronze Tier: Gain +1 Stamina at Bronze Rank 1

**Focus** Momentum has minimal magical bias.
·         Iron Tier: Gain +1 Mana at Rank 7
·         Bronze Tier: No additional Mana

## RANK MILESTONES
**Rank 3 — Driving Start** Gain +1 Initiative.
**Rank 5 — Carried Force** Increase Dexterity or Strength by +1 (based on selection during Essence acquisition).
**Rank 7 — Sustained Pressure** Gain +1 to attack rolls against a creature you have hit on your previous turn.
**Rank 9 — Relentless Advance** If you moved 4 or more squares on your turn, gain +1 to attack rolls until the end of that turn.

## CONFLUENCE BACKLASH
**Backlash: Broken Flow** When you use an Encounter power while Strained you suffer –1 to attack rolls until the end of your next turn. If you are slowed, immobilized, or knocked prone before the end of your next turn, increase Dungeon Resonance by one category.

Momentum collapses when interrupted.

## DUNGEON RESONANCE BIAS
Momentum is Escalating. Repeated Momentum behavior causes Dungeon creatures to gain bonuses against forced movement, environmental hazards that restrict movement to appear and enemies to adopt hit-and-run tactics.

The Dungeon seeks to break your flow.
