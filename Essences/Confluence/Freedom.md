**Nature:** Movement / Autonomy
**Core Concept** Unbound movement, disengagement, refusal to be contained

Freedom defines power as the ability to move without obligation. It prioritizes repositioning, escape, and autonomy over pursuit, pressure, or escalation. Freedom does not chase, trap, or overwhelm—it simply cannot be held. Freedom is motion without intent to dominate.

**Associated Ability Score:** Dexterity or Wisdom (choose at acquisition)

## Common Contributing Essence Patterns

|   |   |
|---|---|
|Wind + Swift + Wing|Wind + Water + Balance|
|Swift + Wing + Light|Wind + Cloud + Swift|
|Wind + Light + Swift|Swift + Balance + Air|
|Air + Swift + Wing|Air + Water + Balance|
|Air + Light + Swift|Air + Cloud + Swift|
|||

Any triad that enables movement without pursuit, values disengagement and autonomy and avoids escalation or follow-through resolves into **Freedom**.

A triad resolves into Freedom only if it contains at least one movement Essence (Wind, Swift, Wing) and avoids pressure-oriented Essences like Hunt or Momentum. If movement reinforces engagement, it resolves into Pursuit. If movement escalates action chains, it resolves into Momentum.

**Durability** Freedom does not emphasize endurance.
·         **Iron Tier:** +1 HP per Essence Rank
·         **Bronze Tier:** +1 HP per Essence Rank

**Recovery** Freedom assumes escape reduces attrition.
·         **Iron Tier:** Gain +1 Healing Surge at Rank 5
·         **Bronze Tier:** No additional Healing Surges

**Exertion** Freedom favors physical agility and repositioning.
·         **Iron Tier:** Gain +1 Stamina at Rank 3 and Rank 7
·         **Bronze Tier:** Gain +1 Stamina at Bronze Rank 1

**Focus** Freedom is not strongly magical.
·         **Iron Tier:** Gain +1 Mana at Rank 7
·         **Bronze Tier:** No additional Mana

## RANK MILESTONES
**Rank 3 — Fluid Reflexes** You gain a +1 bonus to Reflex defense.
**Rank 5 — Unbound Spirit** Increase Dexterity or Wisdom by +1 (based on selection during Essence acquisition).
**Rank 7 — Unhindered Motion** You gain a +1 bonus to saving throws against immobilized or restrained effects.
**Rank 9 — Elusive Form** You gain a +1 bonus to Will defense.

## CONFLUENCE BACKLASH
**Backlash: Overextension** When you use an Encounter power while Strained and it involves movement, you grant combat advantage until the end of your next turn. Increase Dungeon Resonance by one category (to a maximum of Escalating).

Freedom moves first — and may leave itself exposed.

## DUNGEON RESONANCE BIAS
Freedom is **Neutral**. Repeated Freedom behavior may cause zones that restrict teleportation or flight, wind-suppressing terrain, gravity distortions and enemies that anchor space.

The Dungeon tolerates movement. It resists abandonment.
