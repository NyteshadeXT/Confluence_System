**Nature:** Awareness / Truth Pressure
**Core Concept** Exposure, certainty, the impossibility of hiding

Revelation defines power as awareness made unavoidable. It strips away secrecy, misdirection, and ambiguity, forcing truth into the open simply by existing. Revelation does not judge, punish, or act—it ensures that nothing remains unseen or unknown.

Revelation changes outcomes by removing uncertainty, not by applying force.

**Associated Ability Score:** Wisdom or Intelligence (choose at acquisition)

## Common Contributing Essence Patterns

|   |   |
|---|---|
|Eye + Light + Knowledge|Eye + Sound + Wind|
|Eye + Knowledge + Balance|Eye + Sound + Magic|
|Eye + Rune + Knowledge||

Any triad that prioritizes awareness over action, denies concealment or secrecy and forces truth without enforcing consequence resolves into **Revelation**.

A triad resolves into Revelation only if it contains **Eye**, and at least one clarifying Essence (Light, Knowledge, Sound). If awareness immediately converts into punishment, it resolves into Reflection. If awareness stores consequence, it resolves into Reckoning.

**Durability** Revelation does not emphasize endurance.
·         **Iron Tier:** +1 HP per Essence Rank
·         **Bronze Tier:** +1 HP per Essence Rank

**Recovery** Revelation survives through clarity rather than resilience.
·         **Iron Tier:** Gain +1 Healing Surge at Rank 5
·         **Bronze Tier:** No additional Healing Surges

**Exertion** Revelation supports sustained vigilance.
·         **Iron Tier:** Gain +1 Stamina at Rank 7
·         **Bronze Tier:** No additional Stamina

**Focus** Revelation strongly favors mental acuity.
·         **Iron Tier:** Gain +1 Mana at Rank 3 and Rank 7
·         **Bronze Tier:** Gain +1 Mana at Bronze Rank 1

## RANK MILESTONES
**Rank 3 — Clear Sight** You gain a +1 bonus to Will defense.
**Rank 5 — Enlightened Mind** Increase Wisdom or Intelligence by ++1 (based on selection during Essence acquisition).
**Rank 7 — Steady Perception** You gain a +1 bonus to saving throws.
**Rank 9 — Unclouded Awareness** You gain a +1 bonus to Reflex defense.

## CONFLUENCE BACKLASH
**Backlash: Overexposure** When you use an Encounter power while Strained that removes concealment or reveals hidden information, you grant combat advantage until the end of your next turn. Increase Dungeon Resonance by one category (to a maximum of Escalating).

Revelation exposes everything — including the self.

## DUNGEON RESONANCE BIAS
Revelation is **Neutral** **→ Escalating**. Repeated Revelation behavior may cause illusions to become unstable, truth-distortion zones, enemies to gain concealment through environmental manipulation and Dungeon manifestations that obscure perception.

The Dungeon tolerates truth. It resists certainty imposed too often.
