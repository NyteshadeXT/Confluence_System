**Nature:** Denial / Control
**Core Concept** Restriction, exhaustion, enforced limitation

Suppression reduces conflict by removing options. It limits movement, expression, and escalation, forcing opponents into constrained, inefficient choices. Suppression favors control through denial rather than damage or dominance. Suppression wins by leaving nothing left to do.

**Associated Ability Score** Wisdom or Constitution (choose at acquisition)

## Common Contributing Essence Patterns

|   |   |
|---|---|
|Cage + Chain + Force|**Cage + Chain + Stone**|
|Chain + Shield + Order|Cage + Order + Shield|
|Chain + Order + Stone|Cage + Force + Order|
|Chain + Force + Shield||

Any triad that removes options instead of adding threats, converts effort into exhaustion and favors containment over destruction resolves into Suppression.

A triad resolves into Suppression only if it contains at least one direct restriction Essence (Chain or Cage) and prioritizes removing options rather than asserting ownership.

**Durability** Suppression favors steady endurance.
·         Iron Tier: +2 HP per Essence Rank
·         Bronze Tier: +2 HP per Essence Rank

**Recovery** Suppression sustains long engagements.
·         Iron Tier: Gain +1 Healing Surge at Rank 3
·         Bronze Tier: Gain +1 Healing Surge at Bronze Rank 1

**Exertion** Suppression supports steady pressure rather than burst.
·         Iron Tier: Gain +1 Stamina at Rank 1
·         Bronze Tier: Gain +1 Stamina at Bronze Rank 1

**Focus** Suppression has minimal magical bias.
·         Iron Tier: Gain +1 Mana at Rank 7
·         Bronze Tier: No additional Mana

## RANK MILESTONES
**Rank 3 — Restrictive Presence** Gain +1 to Fortitude.
**Rank 5 — Controlled Endurance** Increase Wisdom or Constitution by +1 (based on selection during Essence acquisition).
**Rank 7 — Fatiguing Pressure** Gain +1 to attack rolls against slowed, immobilized, or restrained creatures.
**Rank 9 — Exhaustive Engagement** Gain +1 to all defenses against creatures adjacent to you.

## CONFLUENCE BACKLASH
**Backlash: Overcontrol** When you use an Encounter power while Strained you suffer –1 to all defenses until the end of your next turn. If you are pushed, pulled, or slid more than 2 squares before then, increase Dungeon Resonance by one category.

Suppression collapses when its control is broken.

## DUNGEON RESONANCE BIAS
Suppression is Neutral → Escalating. Enemies to gain bonuses against forced movement, environmental effects that disrupt static positioning and Dungeon creatures to adopt unpredictable movement patterns.
