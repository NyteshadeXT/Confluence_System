**Nature:** Relentless engagement
**Core Concept** Tracking, pressure, refusal to disengage

Pursuit defines conflict as something that cannot be escaped. It transforms awareness and movement into inevitability, closing distance through persistence rather than speed. Pursuit rewards patience, observation, and sustained pressure over sudden aggression.

Pursuit does not rush. It arrives eventually.

**Associated Ability Score:** Wisdom or Dexterity (choose at acquisition)

## Common Contributing Essence Patterns

|   |   |
|---|---|
|Hunt + Wolf + Swift|Hunt + Wind + Eye|
|Wolf + Momentum + Hunt|Hunt + Chain + Wind|
|Dog + Hunt + Swift|Eye + Hunt + Wind|
|Hunt + Snake + Swift|Wolf + Hunt + Momentum|

Any triad that prevents escape over time, converts awareness into pressure and rewards continued engagement naturally resolves into **Pursuit**.

A triad resolves into Pursuit only if it contains **Hunt** or **Wolf** and at least one movement-aligned Essence (Swift, Wind, Momentum) or awareness-aligned Essence (Eye, Dog). Triads that fully remove movement resolve into **Suppression**, not Pursuit.

**Durability** Pursuit favors endurance through continued presence rather than fortification.
·         **Iron Tier:** +2 HP per Essence Rank
·         **Bronze Tier:** +2 HP per Essence Rank

**Recovery** Pursuit assumes extended engagement and grants moderate staying power.
·         **Iron Tier:** Gain +1 Healing Surge at Rank 5
·         **Bronze Tier:** Gain +1 Healing Surge at Bronze Rank 1

**Exertion** Pursuit rewards physical continuation.
·         **Iron Tier:** Gain +1 Stamina at Rank 3 and Rank 7
·         **Bronze Tier:** Gain +1 Stamina at Bronze Rank 1

**Focus** Pursuit is not primarily magical but benefits from heightened awareness.
·         **Iron Tier:** Gain +1 Mana at Rank 7
·         **Bronze Tier:** No additional Mana

## TRAINING INTERACTION
At Rank 6, choose gain proficiency with the Light Blade Weapon Group, gain proficiency with the Bows Weapon Group, gain Leather Armor Proficiency OR gain +2 bonus to Perception checks made to track or oppose Stealth.

## RANK MILESTONES
**Rank 3 — Focused Quarry** You gain a +1 bonus to Perception checks made to track a creature you have previously encountered within the last 24 hours.
**Rank 5 — Predator’s Discipline** Increase Wisdom or Dexterity by +1 (based on selection during Essence acquisition).
**Rank 7 — Sustained Presence** You gain a +1 bonus to saving throws against effects that would forcibly move you.
**Rank 9 — Relentless Awareness** You gain a +1 bonus to initiative rolls.

## CONFLUENCE BACKLASH
**Backlash: Overextension** When you use an Encounter power while Strained and that power involves movement toward a target, you must move at least 1 square closer to the nearest enemy after the attack resolves (if possible). Until the end of your next turn, you take –1 to AC against creatures other than the one you targeted. Increase Dungeon Resonance by one category (to a maximum of Escalating).

## DUNGEON RESONANCE BIAS
Pursuit is **Neutral**. Repeated or obsessive use may shift it toward **Escalating** in predator-aligned Dungeons. Such Dungeons may cause prey creatures to mutate toward evasive adaptations, create terrain that elongates chase sequences and spawn rival hunters drawn to your pressure.

The Dungeon accepts pursuit as natural law. It resists only when inevitability becomes obsession.
