**Nature:** Attrition / Structural pressure
**Core Concept** Endurance, erosion, victory through exhaustion

Siege reframes conflict as a test of time rather than force. It applies steady, unavoidable pressure, eroding defenses, morale, and structure until collapse becomes inevitable. Siege favors patience, resource denial, and inevitability over decisive strikes.

Siege wins by lasting longer than resistance.

**Associated Ability Score:** Constitution or Strength (choose at acquisition)

## Common Contributing Essence Patterns

|   |   |
|---|---|
|Stone + Iron + Force|Ruin + Earth + Order|
|Stone + Ruin + Iron|Iron + Force + Earth|
|Chain + Stone + Iron|Earth + Order + Iron|
|Stone + Order + Force||

Any triad that degrades rather than overwhelms, converts time into advantage, or makes defense unsustainable naturally resolves into Siege.

A triad resolves into Siege only if it contains at least one structural endurance Essence (Stone, Iron, Earth) and one pressure or degradation Essence (Force, Ruin, Order). If the triad removes options entirely, it resolves into Suppression. If it asserts territorial authority, it resolves into Dominion.

**Durability** Siege heavily favors endurance.
·         **Iron Tier:** +3 HP per Essence Rank
·         **Bronze Tier:** +3 HP per Essence Rank

**Recovery** Siege assumes prolonged engagement and reinforces recovery.
·         **Iron Tier:** Gain +1 Healing Surge at Rank 3 and Rank 7
·         **Bronze Tier:** Gain +1 Healing Surge at Bronze Rank 1

**Exertion** Siege supports sustained physical application of force.
·         **Iron Tier:** Gain +1 Stamina at Rank 3
·         **Bronze Tier:** Gain +1 Stamina at Bronze Rank 1

**Focus** Siege is not primarily magical.
·         **Iron Tier:** No Mana granted
·         **Bronze Tier:** No additional Mana

## TRAINING INTERACTION
At Rank 6, choose gain proficiency with Scale Armor Proficiency, gain proficiency with Heavy Shields, gain proficiency with Hammer Weapon Group OR gain +2 bonus to Endurance checks involving prolonged strain or exhaustion.

## RANK MILESTONES
**Rank 3 — Unyielding Frame** You gain a +1 bonus to Fortitude saving throws.
**Rank 5 — Structural Resolve** Increase Constitution or Strength by +1 (based on selection during Essence acquisition).
**Rank 7 — Slow Pressure** You gain a +1 bonus to checks made to damage objects, barriers, or structures.
**Rank 9 — Endurance Beyond Impulse** You gain a +1 bonus to saving throws against fear effects.

## CONFLUENCE BACKLASH
**Backlash: Grinding Overreach** When you use an Encounter power while Strained, your speed is reduced by 1 until the end of your next turn. If the power deals ongoing damage or creates a zone, you also take 1 damage at the start of your next turn. Increase Dungeon Resonance by one category (to a maximum of Escalating).

## DUNGEON RESONANCE BIAS
Siege is **Neutral** **→ Escalating**. Repeated Siege behavior may cause terrain to harden or become fortified, defensive creatures to gain temporary resilience, structural elements of the Dungeon to reinforce or counter-siege and environmental resistance to become adaptive.

The Dungeon respects endurance. It resents erosion.
