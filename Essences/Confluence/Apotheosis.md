**Nature:** Self-deification
**Core Concept** Supremacy, transcendence through domination, stolen divinity

Apotheosis defines power as the right to rise above all constraint. It seeks godhood not through harmony or sponsorship, but through accumulation and imposition. Apotheosis reshapes identity around supremacy and inevitability.

Apotheosis does not ask permission—from gods or reality.

**Associated Ability Score:** Charisma or Constitution (choose at acquisition)

## Common Contributing Essence Patterns

|   |   |
|---|---|
|Ascendance + Dominion + Force|Ascendance + Dominion + Force|
|Dominion, Order, Oath||

Any triad that elevates self above consequence, rejects external authority and claims supremacy without responsibility risks resolving into **Apotheosis**.

A triad resolves into Apotheosis only if it contains a self-elevating Essence (Ascendance, Growth, Zeal, Will-aligned) and an authority or domination Essence (Dominion, Order, Oath bent inward). If transcendence rejects domination, it resolves into Ascendance. If authority governs territory rather than self, it resolves into Dominion.

Apotheosis is not merely powerful. It is self-exalting.

**Durability** Apotheosis reinforces the self through force of identity.
·         **Iron Tier:** +3 HP per Essence Rank
·         **Bronze Tier:** +3 HP per Essence Rank

**Recovery** Apotheosis assumes the self cannot be easily undone.
·         **Iron Tier:** Gain +1 Healing Surge at Rank 3 and Rank 7
·         **Bronze Tier:** Gain +1 Healing Surge at Bronze Rank 1

**Exertion** Apotheosis fuels imposed dominance.
·         **Iron Tier:** Gain +1 Stamina at Rank 3
·         **Bronze Tier:** Gain +1 Stamina at Bronze Rank 1

**Focus** Apotheosis bends reality through force of will.
·         **Iron Tier:** Gain +1 Mana at Rank 3 and Rank 7
·         **Bronze Tier:** Gain +1 Mana at Bronze Rank 1

## RANK MILESTONES
 **Rank 3 — Sovereign Presence** You gain a +1 bonus to Will defense.
**Rank 5 — Self-Exaltation** Increase Charisma or Constitution by +1.
**Rank 7 — Unchallenged Form** You gain a +1 bonus to Fortitude defense.
**Rank 9 — Unquestioned Authority** You gain a +1 bonus to saving throws.

## CONFLUENCE BACKLASH
**Backlash: Divine Rejection** When you use an Encounter power while Strained, you take –1 to all defenses until the end of your next turn. If the power imposes a condition on another creature, you also take 1 radiant damage at the start of your next turn. Increase Dungeon Resonance by one category (to a maximum of **Provocative**). If already Provocative, the Dungeon may escalate to **Forbidden** behavior at the GM’s discretion.

Apotheosis provokes cosmic attention.

## DUNGEON RESONANCE BIAS
Apotheosis is **Escalating** **→ Provocative** by default. Repeated Apotheosis behavior may cause divine-aligned manifestations, anti-authority constructs, reality distortion zones, suppression of external divine influence and direct Dungeon hostility.

The Dungeon tolerates power. It does not tolerate stolen godhood.
