**Nature:** Erasure / Finality
**Core Concept** Negation, loss of consequence, annihilation of meaning

Oblivion does not destroy—it removes. Actions, identities, and outcomes cease to matter once claimed by Oblivion. Power expressed through Oblivion leaves nothing behind to react, remember, or resist.

Oblivion ends stories.

All essence combinations resulting in the Oblivion Essence are considered forbidden by the Seekers guild.

**Associated Ability Score** Intelligence or Constitution (choose at acquisition)

## Common Contributing Essence Patterns

|   |   |
|---|---|
|Entropy + Death + Force|Entropy + Pure + Death|
|Entropy + Death + Rune|Entropy + Death + Void|

Any triad that removes rather than damages, prevents recovery or continuation and denies consequence itself risks resolving into Oblivion. A triad resolves into Oblivion only if it contains Entropy and Death.

Many Dungeons will seek to actively exterminate bearers of this Confluence.

**Durability** Oblivion is steady but not regenerative.
·         Iron Tier: +2 HP per Essence Rank
·         Bronze Tier: +2 HP per Essence Rank

**Recovery** Oblivion does not emphasize renewal.
·         Iron Tier: Gain +1 Healing Surge at Rank 3
·         Bronze Tier: No additional Healing Surges

**Exertion** Oblivion favors decisive effort.
·         Iron Tier: Gain +1 Stamina at Rank 1
·         Bronze Tier: Gain +1 Stamina at Bronze Rank 1

**Focus** Oblivion supports necrotic or destructive focus.
·         Iron Tier: Gain +1 Mana at Rank 3
·         Bronze Tier: Gain +1 Mana at Bronze Rank 1

## ESSENCE MASTERY MILESTONES
**Rank 3 — Final Strike** Gain +1 to damage rolls against bloodied creatures.
**Rank 5 — Cold Resolve** Increase Intelligence or Constitution by +1 (based on selection during Essence acquisition).
**Rank 7 — Unyielding End** Gain +1 to attack rolls against bloodied creatures.
**Rank 9 — Denial of Recovery** Creatures you hit cannot regain HP until the start of your next turn.

## CONFLUENCE BACKLASH
**Backlash: Existential Recoil** When you use an Encounter power while Strained you cannot regain HP until the end of your next turn. • Increase Dungeon Resonance by one category.

Oblivion denies recovery — including your own.

## DUNGEON RESONANCE BIAS
Oblivion is Provocative → Forbidden rapidly. Repeated Oblivion behavior causes Dungeon creatures to gain resistance to necrotic damage, environmental effects that suppress healing and constructs or anti-entropy entities begin to appear.

Many Dungeons attempt to eliminate bearers of Oblivion.
