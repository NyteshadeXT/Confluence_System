**Nature:** Tactical / Preparatory
**Core Concept:** Preparation, inevitability, first strike

Ambush resolves conflict before it begins. It transforms space, timing, and expectation into weapons, ensuring that when violence occurs, it does so on predetermined terms. Ambush favors foresight, patience, and positioning over reaction or endurance.

Ambush does not seek prolonged conflict. It seeks decisive advantage.

**Associated Ability Score:** Dexterity

## Common Contributing Essence Patterns
Ambush emerges when control of space, delayed action, and restricted movement converge. Representative combinations include:

|   |   |
|---|---|
|Trap + Spider + Rune|Spider + Hunt + Wind|
|Trap + Cat + Blade|Rune + Hunt + Spider|
|Trap + Earth + Spike|Chain + Trap + Swift|
|Trap + Hunt + Swift|Hunt + Wolf + Trap|
|Rune + Chain + Trap||

Any triad that prepares consequences in advance, punishes movement or ignorance and rewards acting first naturally resolves into **Ambush**.

**Durability** Ambush survives through avoidance rather than endurance.
·         Iron Tier: +1 HP per Essence Rank
·         Bronze Tier: +1 HP per Essence Rank

**Recovery** Ambush favors mobility over resilience.
·         Iron Tier: Gain +1 Healing Surge at Rank 3
·         Bronze Tier: No additional Healing Surges

**Exertion** Ambush strongly supports stamina tied to movement and opening engagement.
·         Iron Tier: Gain +1 Stamina at Rank 1 and Rank 5
·         Bronze Tier: Gain +1 Stamina at Bronze Rank 1

**Focus** Ambush has minimal magical bias.
·         Iron Tier: Gain +1 Mana at Rank 3
·         Bronze Tier: No additional Mana

## Rank Milestones
**Rank 3 — First to Act** Gain +1 Initiative.
**Rank 5 — Precise Strike** Increase Dexterity by +1.
**Rank 7 — Opening Accuracy** Gain +1 to attack rolls during the first round of combat.
**Rank 9 — Decisive Engagement** Gain +1 to attack rolls against creatures that have not yet acted this encounter.

## CONFLUENCE BACKLASH
**Backlash: Premature Commitment** When you use an Encounter power while Strained take –1 to all defenses until the end of your next turn. If you act before at least one enemy in the initiative order this round and miss an attack, increase Dungeon Resonance by one category.

Ambush punishes mistimed aggression.

## DUNGEON RESONANCE BIAS
Ambush is Escalating. Repeated Ambush behavior causes enemies to begin encounters spread out, increased trap density and Dungeon creatures gain bonuses to Initiative.

The Dungeon adapts to repeated first-strike tactics.
