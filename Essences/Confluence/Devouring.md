**Nature:** Predatory / Escalatory
**Core Concept:** Consumption, escalation, identity erosion

Devouring is need without limit. It defines the self through what is taken and what is erased. Power gained through Devouring accelerates its own demand, reshaping identity around absence rather than purpose. Devouring does not end. It only grows.

All essence combinations resulting in the Devouring Essence are considered forbidden by the Seekers guild.

**Associated Ability Score** Strength or Constitution (choose at acquisition)

## Common Contributing Essence Patterns

Devouring manifests when self-reinforcing need, growth without restraint, and direct force converge. Representative combinations include:

|   |   |
|---|---|
|Hunger + Growth + Force|Hunger + Might + Life|
|Hunger + Blood + Growth|Entropy + Hunger + Might|
|Hunger + Flesh + Momentum|Hunger + Force + Flesh|
|Hunger + Blood + Might|Entropy + Hunger + Growth|

Any triad that feeds upon its own success, lacks an internal stopping condition and converts consumption directly into escalation is likely to resolve into Devouring, whether intended or not.

**Durability** Devouring survives through endurance and aggression.
·         Iron Tier: +2 HP per Essence Rank
·         Bronze Tier: +2 HP per Essence Rank

**Recovery** Devouring pushes through damage by consuming strength.
·         Iron Tier: Gain +1 Healing Surge at Rank 3
·         Bronze Tier: Gain +1 Healing Surge at Bronze Rank 1

**Exertion** Devouring favors physical escalation.
·         Iron Tier: Gain +1 Stamina at Rank 1 and Rank 5
·         Bronze Tier: Gain +1 Stamina at Bronze Rank 1

**Focus** Devouring has minimal magical bias. It is physical escalation.
·         Iron Tier: No Mana gained
·         Bronze Tier: No Mana gained

## RANK MILESTONES
**Rank 3 — Ravenous Assault** Gain +1 to damage rolls against bloodied creatures.
**Rank 5 — Growing Hunger** Increase Strength or Constitution by +1 (based on selection during Essence acquisition).
**Rank 7 — Escalating Pressure** Gain +1 to attack rolls against bloodied creatures.
**Rank 9 — Predatory Momentum** When you reduce a creature to 0 HP, gain +1 to attack rolls until the end of your next turn.

## CONFLUENCE BACKLASH
**Backlash: Unchecked Hunger** When you use an Encounter power while Strained you take –1 to all defenses until the end of your next turn. If you do not deal damage before the end of your next turn, increase Dungeon Resonance by one category.

Devouring punishes stalled aggression.

Dungeon Resonance Bias

Devouring is Provocative → Forbidden rapidly.

Repeated Devouring behavior causes enemies to fight more defensively and withdraw, apex predators to appear in response and corpses to dissolve before looting.

Many Dungeons attempt to eliminate Devouring bearers.
