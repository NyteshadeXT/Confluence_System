> _"Essence shapes what you can become. Training shapes what you know."_

Training Manuals are the primary means by which characters develop practical expertise. Unlike Essences, which awaken supernatural potential, Training Manuals represent years of study, disciplined practice, and accumulated knowledge preserved in written form.

Within Dungeons, forgotten libraries, martial academies, ruined monasteries, and ancient workshops often contain these invaluable texts. Some are simple instructional guides, while others are legendary treatises penned by history's greatest masters.

Reading a Training Manual permanently improves a character's proficiency in a specific discipline.

Training Manuals represent learned skill, not supernatural power while a character's Essences determine the kinds of powers they can wield. They determine how effectively they wield weapons, wear armor, defend themselves, and perform specialized tasks.

# Rarity
Training Manuals are found throughout the world, although higher levels of mastery become increasingly rare. The greatest manuals are often unique works created by famous masters, hidden within forgotten ruins, guarded by powerful creatures, or preserved inside Dungeons.

|Manual|Typical Availability|
|---|---|
|I (Trained)|Common|
|II (Expert)|Uncommon|
|III (Master)|Rare|
|IV (Legendary)|Legendary|

 # Proficiency Ranks and Caps
Every discipline progresses through five levels of mastery.

|Rank|Description|
|---|---|
|**Untrained**|Little or no formal instruction.|
|**Trained**|Basic professional competence.|
|**Expert**|Considerable experience and refined technique.|
|**Master**|Among the finest practitioners in the region.|
|**Legendary**|Extraordinary skill bordering on the impossible.|
|**Mythic**|Skill on par with that of higher order beings.|

Training Manuals always improve a discipline by one proficiency rank and a character may never exceed Legendary proficiency.  The characters proficiency is capped by his or her rank as follows.

|Rank|Maximum Training|
|---|---|
|Iron|Expert|
|Bronze|Master|
|Silver|Grandmaster|
|Gold|Legendary|
|Platinum|Mythic|

# Learning Training Manuals
A Training Manual can only be studied if the character meets its prerequisites.

For example:
- **Heavy Blade II** requires Heavy Blade (Trained).
- **Heavy Blade III** requires Heavy Blade (Expert).
- **Heavy Blade IV** requires Heavy Blade (Master).

Once successfully studied, the benefits are permanent. Unless otherwise noted, a manual can only benefit a character once.

# Weapon Proficiency
Weapon proficiency reflects both familiarity and disciplined technique. Characters may wield weapons without proficiency, but do so awkwardly and inefficiently. Weapon skills are capped at skill rank 10 per character tier.  So a Iron tier character can get to 10 in any given weapon group skill but can't advance to 1 until Silver tier.  Once Silver, that character then can progress to 20 before being capped again.

**Untrained** Suffer a –2 penalty to attack and damage rolls (minimum of 1 damage) made with weapons in this group and the character cannot benefit from Weapon Mastery features associated with the weapon group.
**Trained** Remove the Untrained penalty. Character gains proficiency with all weapons in the group.
**Expert** Character receives the weapon training bonus for all weapons in the group. (See weapon proficiency bonus in 4e.)
**Master** Unlock the weapon group's **Master Mastery** feature.
**Legendary** Unlock the weapon group's **Legendary Mastery** feature.
**Mythic** Currently unknown

> **Note:** Each weapon group has its own unique Mastery progression that reflects its fighting style. Heavy Blades, Spears, Bows, and other groups do not share mastery abilities.

# Armor Proficiency
Armor proficiency represents learning to fight without allowing armor to hinder movement or awareness. Armor skills are capped at skill rank 5 per character tier.  So a Iron tier character can get to 5 in all defense skills but can't advance to 6 until Silver tier.  Once Silver, that character then can progress to 10 before being capped again.

**Untrained** When wearing armor without proficiency the character suffers -2 penalty to attack rolls, -2 penalty to reflex saves, increase any Speed penalty by 1 or gains a speed penalty of 1 if the armor does not already have a speed penalty and cannot benefit from armor-specific abilities.
**Trained** Wear armor normally. Ignore all Untrained penalties.
**Expert** The player may choose one: add +3 to any saving throw defense OR gain resistance 3 to bludgeoning, slashing or piercing weapons.
**Master**  The player may choose one: add +3 to any saving throw defense OR gain resistance 3 to bludgeoning, slashing or piercing weapons.
**Legendary** Critical hits occur on a 12+ instead of a 10+.
**Mythic**

# Shield Proficiency
Shield proficiency teaches defensive timing, positioning, and battlefield awareness. Characters may not wield shields without proficiency. At this time Shields do not get a score only a proficiency rank.

**Untrained** The character is unable to effectively use shields.
**Trained** Gain the shield's basic AC bonus. Cannot use Shield Mastery abilities.
**Expert** Unlock Expert Shield Mastery.
**Master** Unlock Master Shield Mastery.
**Legendary** Unlock Legendary Shield Mastery.
**Mythic**

# Skill Proficiency
Skill Manuals represent formal education, apprenticeship, or exceptional practical experience. Characters may use skills without proficiency, but do so some what accidentally and do not have a full breadth of knowledge. Skills are capped at skill rank 10 per character tier.  So a Iron tier character can get to 10 in any given skill but can't advance to 1 until Silver tier.  Once Silver, that character then can progress to 20 before being capped again.

**Untrained** you may attempt routine tasks but lack specialized knowledge.
**Trained** Gain full proficiency in the skill.
**Expert** Perform difficult tasks consistently and unlock advanced applications.
**Master** Achieve extraordinary results beyond ordinary practitioners.
**Legendary** Become one of the world's foremost experts in the discipline.
**Mythic** You have achieved a cosmic level of knowledge.


# Crafting Proficiency
Crafting Manuals represent formal education, apprenticeship, or exceptional practical experience. Characters may not use crafting skills without proficiency as they do not have a full breadth of knowledge. Skills are capped at skill rank 10 per character tier.  So a Iron tier character can get to 10 in any given skill but can't advance to 1 until Silver tier.  Once Silver, that character then can progress to 20 before being capped again. A character's Crafting Rank determines the most advanced recipes they are capable of completing.

**Untrained** Cannot craft items within this discipline.
**Trained** May craft Iron Rank items.
**Expert** May craft Bronze Rank items.
**Master** May craft Silver Rank items.
**Legendary** May Gold Iron Rank items.
**Mythic** Required for artifacts and creations beyond mortal craftsmanship.

# Defense Proficiency
Defense Skill proficiency represents a character’s training in resisting threats through physical conditioning, practiced reactions, and mental discipline. They are capped at skill rank 5 per character tier.  So a Iron tier character can get to 5 in all defense skills but can't advance to 6 until Silver tier.  Once Silver, that character then can progress to 10 before being capped again.

**Untrained** The character has no formal defensive training. The Defense Skill cannot be increased above 0.
**Trained** The character may increase the Defense Skill to the Trained maximum. Unlock basic Defense Skill abilities.
**Expert** The character may increase the Defense Skill to the Expert maximum. Unlock Expert Defense Mastery abilities.
**Master** The character may increase the Defense Skill to the Master maximum. Unlock Master Defense Mastery abilities.
**Legendary** The character may increase the Defense Skill to the Legendary maximum. Unlock Legendary Defense Mastery abilities.
**Mythic** The character may increase the Defense Skill to the Mythic maximum. Unlock Mythic Defense Mastery abilities.
