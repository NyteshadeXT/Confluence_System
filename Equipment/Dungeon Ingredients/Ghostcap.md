# Ghostcap
> [!metadata]  
> **Iron** • **Ingredient** • **Common** • **Variable Value**

_A pale, nearly translucent mushroom whose thin white cap seems to fade into its surroundings when viewed from certain angles. When disturbed, the flesh briefly becomes almost completely transparent before slowly returning to its milky coloration. Ghostcap is prized by alchemists for its unusual ability to obscure both physical form and magical presence._

> [!power|passive]-  
> **Crafting Resource**
> Ghostcap is consumed when brewing concealment potions and other alchemical creations. It counts as an Ingredient for any recipe requiring obscuring or concealment-producing fungi.
> 
> **Harvest** A successful Nature (DC 15) check allows the mushroom to be harvested intact. On a failure, the delicate cap is damaged, yielding only half the normal value.

> [!Info|sources & usage]-
> ## Sources
> Ghostcap thrives in dim, humid environments rich with ambient magic, often growing beneath ancient roots, within dense undergrowth, and in secluded regions of Fungus-attuned Dungeons.
> 
> Freshly harvested caps retain their potency for several weeks when properly dried and stored.
> 
> ## Uses
> - Brewing Potions of Invisibility
> - Concealment elixirs
> - Stealth-enhancing alchemical preparations
> - Advanced illusion and obscuration alchemical recipes

> [!info|notes]-
> ## Notes
> Ghostcap is a common alchemical fungus found within Iron Rank Dungeons. Experienced gatherers often locate patches by searching for small gaps in otherwise dense vegetation where the nearly invisible mushrooms have displaced surrounding growth.
