# Bloodberry

> [!metadata]  
> **Iron** • **Ingredient** • **Common** • **Variable Value**

_A glossy crimson berry that grows along creeping, thorn-covered vines in places where predatory plants have recently fed. Its dark juice is strangely warm to the touch and carries a sharp metallic scent when the skin is broken. Bloodberry is prized by alchemists for its ability to stimulate vitality and accelerate the body's natural recovery._

> [!power|passive]-  
> **Crafting Resource**
> > Bloodberry is consumed when brewing regenerative potions and other alchemical creations. It counts as an Ingredient for any recipe requiring vitality-restorative herbs.
> 
> **Harvest** A successful Nature (DC 15) check allows the berries to be harvested intact. On a failure, the delicate fruits are damaged, yielding only half the normal value.

> [!Info|sources & usage]-
> ## Sources
> Bloodberry thrives in areas where magically empowered predatory plants have recently fed, often growing among the roots of Bloodthorn Vines and other carnivorous Dungeon flora.
> 
> Freshly harvested berries retain their potency for several weeks when properly dried and stored.
> 
> ## Uses
> - Brewing regenerative potions
> - Vitality-enhancing elixirs
> - Blood-restorative alchemical preparations
> - Advanced regenerative alchemical recipes

> [!info|notes]-
> ## Notes
> Bloodberry is a common alchemical fruit harvested from Iron Rank Dungeons and is valued by adventurers and alchemists for restorative preparations that encourage the body to recover over time.
