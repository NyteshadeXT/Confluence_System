# Fleetfern

> [!metadata]  
> **Iron** • **Ingredient** • **Common** • **Variable Value**

_A delicate silver-green fern whose narrow fronds twitch and unfurl even in perfectly still air. When touched, the entire plant snaps closed with startling speed before gradually reopening. Fleetfern is prized by alchemists for compounds that heighten reflexes, quicken movement, and temporarily push the body beyond its normal limits._

> [!power|passive]-  
> **Crafting Resource**
> Fleetfern is consumed when brewing speed-enhancing potions and other alchemical creations. It counts as an Ingredient for any recipe requiring herbs associated with speed, mobility, or heightened reflexes.
> 
> **Harvest** A successful Nature (DC 15) check allows the fern to be harvested intact. On a failure, the delicate fronds are damaged, yielding only half the normal value.

> [!Info|sources & usage]-
> ## Sources
> 
> Fleetfern thrives in magically enriched forests and Dungeons where dense vegetation competes fiercely for light and space. It is frequently found along well-traveled game trails, beneath crystal formations, and among rapidly growing Dungeon flora.
> 
> Freshly harvested fronds retain their potency for several weeks when properly dried and stored.
> 
> ## Uses
> - Brewing Potions of Speed
> - Mobility-enhancing elixirs
> - Reflex-enhancing alchemical preparations

> [!info|notes]-
> ## Notes
> Fleetfern is a common alchemical plant harvested from Iron Rank Dungeons. Its characteristic twitching movement makes mature specimens surprisingly easy to identify once an adventurer knows what to watch for.
